# Vendor Support

## Purpose

Vendor Support assists onboarding, verification, storefront, product/service, order, fulfillment, customer service, fee, tax, settlement, reserve, statement, payout, and dispute workflows.

## Procedure

Authenticate and scope the vendor/entity/country; classify issue; inspect agreement, compliance tasks, orders, financial projections, provider evidence, and reconciliation state; provide guidance; initiate approved workflow or escalate to finance/tax/risk/compliance/treasury; record outcome and follow-up.

## Financial Safety

Support cannot edit vendor balances, change fee/tax/accounting treatment, mark settlement/payout paid, release reserves, override holds, or expose customer/member data. Earnings distinguish gross/net, fees, taxes, reserves, pending, available, settled, and paid states.

## Vendor Care

Provide accessible and localized explanations, payout/refund/chargeback timelines, agreement and policy references, complaint/appeal paths, prohibited-product escalation, privacy safeguards, and evidence for material decisions.
