# Customer Support

## Purpose

Customer Support resolves questions and cases involving discovery, orders, payments, invoices, delivery, refunds, disputes, accounts, privacy, rewards, and wallet status.

## Procedure

Authenticate the requester; scope the account/order/country; classify the issue; inspect authorized projections and source references; explain current status and uncertainty; use approved commands or escalate; communicate expected timing; record evidence, correlation, action, and resolution.

## Financial Safety

Support cannot mark payment/refund/payout complete, edit balances, release holds, alter tax/revenue/accounting, disclose another party's data, or bypass risk, compliance, identity, dispute, or approval controls. Timeouts and provider uncertainty are explained as pending/unknown and routed to reconciliation.

## Customer Care

Provide clear fee/tax/refund/return terms, accessible communication, privacy-safe identity verification, language/localization, complaint and appeal routes, and escalation for fraud, account takeover, privacy, legal, vulnerable-person, or security concerns.
