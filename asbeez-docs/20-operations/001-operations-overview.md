# Operations Overview

## Purpose

AsBeez Operations keeps customer, member, vendor, partner, platform, and financial workflows reliable, understandable, controlled, and recoverable.

## Operating Principles

Assign owner, scope, status, evidence, authority, due date, escalation, and service target to every operational item. Preserve pending, held, failed, reversed, exception, provider-evidence, and unknown states. Use approved APIs/runbooks; never manually edit authoritative records.

## Operating Areas

Customer/vendor support; order/payment/refund/payout assistance; fraud/risk review; compliance/KYC/AML/sanctions; reconciliation and close coordination; incident management; privacy/security response; notifications; provider escalation; business continuity; and disaster recovery.

## Quality and Trust

Operations must protect personal/financial data, provide accessible and localized communication, explain source/freshness and next steps, maintain separation of duties, link actions to correlation/audit evidence, and reconcile external outcomes before closure.
