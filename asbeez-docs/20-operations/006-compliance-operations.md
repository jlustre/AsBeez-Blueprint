# Compliance Operations

## Purpose

Compliance Operations executes and monitors KYC/KYB, AML, sanctions, source-of-funds, tax, country, privacy, retention, approval, and regulatory control workflows.

## Procedure

Classify case and jurisdiction; verify identity/business and legal basis; screen/monitor; preserve provider evidence; apply policy/version and effective date; place hold/review where required; obtain authorized decision; manage notice/appeal/reporting; retain evidence; test and remediate.

## Rules

Provider or AI output is evidence unless an approved deterministic control applies. Compliance operations cannot make unsupported final legal conclusions, expose sensitive evidence, release restricted funds, alter financial history, or override country/tax/privacy requirements. Uncertainty becomes review, hold, exception, or launch block.

## Governance

Track owner, jurisdiction, case/status, policy/version, subject scope, expiry/re-screen date, decision authority, reason, access, retention/legal hold, escalation, incident, and audit. Coordinate with finance, tax, risk, support, security, and legal.
