# Purpose

This document defines the AsBeez Ecosystem.

The ecosystem is the complete network of participants, technology, business processes, and value exchanges that together create the AsBeez marketplace.

Unlike a traditional online store, AsBeez is designed as a living ecosystem where every participant contributes to the growth and sustainability of the Hive.

This document establishes the business architecture that will guide every future software module, operational process, and strategic initiative.

---

# Definition

The AsBeez Ecosystem is a community-powered commerce platform where customers, members, vendors, partners, employees, investors, and technology work together to create value through genuine marketplace activity.

Each participant has a unique role.

Each role contributes differently.

Together they strengthen the Hive.

---

# Ecosystem Philosophy

The AsBeez Ecosystem is founded on one simple belief:

> A healthy ecosystem benefits every participant.

Customers receive products.

Vendors build businesses.

Members participate in loyalty.

Partners expand capabilities.

Employees create innovation.

Investors enable growth.

AsBeez provides the platform that connects everyone together.

Every component of the ecosystem must satisfy the following principles.
