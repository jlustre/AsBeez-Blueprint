# Book 02 – Business Blueprint

---

## Overview

This book defines the business foundation of the AsBeez platform.

It describes the business vision, operating model, stakeholders, competitive advantages, ecosystem, and value proposition. It answers the question:

> **"What business are we building and how does it operate?"**

The Business Blueprint serves as the foundation for all subsequent books, including Business Capabilities, System Architecture, Product Requirements, and Database Design.

# Purpose of Each Document

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Executive Summary | High-level summary of the AsBeez business. |
| Business Overview | Describes what AsBeez is and why it exists. |
| Business Model | Explains how AsBeez creates, delivers, and captures value. |
| The AsBeez Ecosystem | Defines the participants and how they interact. |
| Stakeholders | Identifies everyone involved in the platform. |
| Competitive Advantage | Explains what differentiates AsBeez from competitors. |
| The AsBeez Hive Flywheel | Describes the self-reinforcing growth model. |
| Customer Segments | Defines the target audiences. |
| Value Proposition | Explains the value delivered to each stakeholder. |
