# Purpose

The AsBeez Book of Principles is the philosophical foundation of the company.

While the Constitution defines **what AsBeez believes**, this book explains **how those beliefs influence every decision** made throughout the organization.

Every employee, executive, developer, vendor, partner, and future leader should understand and apply these principles.

Technology will evolve.

Markets will change.

Business models will improve.

These principles should endure.

---

# Why This Book Exists

Many companies create mission statements.

Some create values.

Very few create enduring principles.

The purpose of this book is to establish timeless guidance that helps future generations make decisions consistent with the original vision of AsBeez.

Whenever uncertainty exists, these principles should provide direction.

---

# How to Use This Book

Every principle contains:

- The Principle
- Why It Exists
- Practical Meaning
- Business Applications
- Examples
- Questions to Ask
- Things to Avoid

These principles are intended to guide decision-making rather than replace good judgment.
