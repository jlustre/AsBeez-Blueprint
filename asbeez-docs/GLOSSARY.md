# AsBeez Glossary

This glossary defines the official terminology used throughout the AsBeez Enterprise Knowledge System.

---

## ABC

**AsBeez Business Cell**

A digital business asset created whenever a Member accumulates **120 Reward Points**.

An ABC participates in the Beehive Matrix and earns AsBeez Hive Credits.

---

## AHC

**AsBeez Hive Credits**

Credits earned by qualified ABCs whenever a new ABC is placed beneath them in the Beehive Matrix.

10 AHC = $1.00 (Current Business Rule)

---

## Beehive Matrix

A country-specific 3 × 12 forced matrix where every newly created ABC is automatically placed.

---

## Customer

A person who purchases products from AsBeez.

Customers may or may not be Members.

---

## Member

A registered participant who can earn Reward Points, create ABCs, participate in the Beehive Matrix, and refer new Members.

---

## Vendor

A business or individual authorized to publish and sell products on AsBeez.

---

## Reward Points (RP)

Qualification points earned from qualifying purchases.

Reward Points are **not money**.

120 RP creates one ABC.

---

## Qualified Referral

A referred Member who has successfully created their first ABC.

---

## Wallet

The financial account where converted earnings are stored.

Funds may later be:

- Withdrawn
- Used to purchase products
- Transferred to another Member
- Used for future platform services

---

## Hive

The AsBeez community ecosystem.

---

## Country Hive

An independent Beehive Matrix assigned to a specific country.

Examples:

- USA Hive
- Canada Hive
- Philippines Hive

---

## Revenue Share

The percentage voluntarily contributed by Vendors from qualifying sales to fund the AsBeez ecosystem.

---

## Founder Principle

A guiding statement placed at the beginning of every major documentation volume.

---

This glossary will continuously evolve as AsBeez grows.
