# Matrix Business Rules

## Purpose

Matrix rules govern Beehive Matrix structures, placement, qualification inputs, distributions, historical positions, and country behavior without turning the Matrix into a financial ledger.

## Rules

- placement follows published deterministic rules and versioned policy;
- a member's country context is explicit; historical placement is not silently moved;
- qualification and placement are distinct from monetary reward treatment;
- RP/ABC/AHC and matrix cells are program/domain records, not money, equity, ownership, or guaranteed income;
- changes, reversals, refunds, chargebacks, fraud, expiry, and policy breaches create explicit events and audit history; and
- cross-context financial consequences are evaluated by Reward Finance and posted only through approved financial workflows.

## Controls

Use stable member/cell/matrix IDs, immutable placement history, effective policy version, idempotency, country/entity scope, privacy, audit, maximum liability, reconciliation, and appeal/review paths. No user, UI, AI, or administrator may edit placement to create a financial effect.
