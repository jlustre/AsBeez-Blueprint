# Rewards Business Rules

## Purpose

Rewards rules define qualifying activity, RP, ABC, AHC, referrals, distribution, expiry, reversal, holds, disclosure, and approved monetary treatment.

## Rules

- qualifying rewards are tied to genuine, documented marketplace activity;
- recruitment, joining fees, inventory loading, debt, or paid rank advancement are not required for ordinary customer benefits;
- RP/ABC/AHC are platform-defined units and not money, currency, investment, equity, ownership, or guaranteed returns unless approved by jurisdiction-specific legal analysis;
- qualification, placement, distribution, display balance, and monetary treatment are separate decisions;
- refund, chargeback, fraud, cancellation, expiry, and policy breach create explicit reversal/suspension events; and
- country availability, funding source, maximum liability, reserve, tax, disclosures, and payout rules are approved before launch.

## Controls

Use versioned policy, source order/payment/member/country references, idempotency, audit, privacy, reconciliation, appeals, and clear UI/API disclosure. Reward Finance evaluates monetary effects; Matrix/Rewards own mechanics.
