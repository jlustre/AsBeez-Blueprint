# Business Rules

## Purpose

This domain records AsBeez business rules for commerce, financial integrity, Matrix, membership, platform roles, rewards, and vendors.

## Authority

Business rules define domain behavior but do not override law, signed agreements, accounting policy, the General Ledger, security, privacy, or country controls. Each rule has an owner, scope, status, effective version, source, and test evidence. Conflicts are resolved by the responsible domain authority and recorded.

## Rule Format

Use rule ID, statement, scope, trigger, inputs, outcome, exceptions, owner, effective date/version, dependencies, acceptance tests, audit/evidence, and related policy. Historical decisions retain the rule/policy version used.

## Cross-Cutting Principles

Commerce is genuine and customer-accessible; membership is voluntary; rewards are not money by default; vendor/customer/member/platform responsibilities are distinct; financial effects are authorized, idempotent, balanced, immutable, and reconciled; and country/entity/currency/privacy/legal scope is explicit.
