# Financial Business Rules

## Purpose

Financial rules govern approved monetary consequences of commerce, payments, wallets, rewards, vendors, partners, tax, reserves, treasury, settlement, payouts, refunds, disputes, reconciliation, and close.

## Core Rules

- customer payment is not automatically recognized revenue;
- GMV, recognized revenue, vendor/partner entitlement, tax, fees, reserves, cash, liabilities, expenses, and rewards remain distinct;
- every monetary effect is authorized, idempotent, scoped by entity/country/currency/period/policy, and posted through balanced immutable ledger entries;
- corrections use reversal/compensating entries rather than balance edits;
- provider/bank status is evidence until mapped and reconciled; and
- RP/ABC/AHC are not money by default and do not create financial value without approved classification and funding policy.

## Control Rules

Subledgers reconcile to GL control accounts; refunds/chargebacks/fraud/expiry propagate corrections; reserves are not free cash; reports disclose actual/estimate/pending/uncertified states; close cannot hide material differences; and financial commands require authorization, approval, audit, and idempotency.
