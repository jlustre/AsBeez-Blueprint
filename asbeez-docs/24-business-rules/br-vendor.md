# Vendor Business Rules

## Purpose

Vendor rules govern qualification, onboarding, agreements, products/services, pricing, orders, customer care, fulfillment, fees, tax, settlement, reserves, payouts, disputes, privacy, and termination.

## Rules

- vendor identity/business and required registrations are verified before activation;
- products/services, claims, pricing, promotions, fulfillment, support, and prohibited categories are reviewed;
- vendor/customer payment, platform revenue, fees, tax, entitlement, reserves, settlement, and payout remain distinct;
- vendor statements disclose pending, available, held, reserved, settled, and paid states;
- refunds, chargebacks, fraud, disputes, tax changes, and policy breaches create explicit adjustments; and
- vendor data and customer data are scoped, minimized, protected, retained, and auditable.

## Controls

Vendor APIs and portals cannot edit balances, mark settlement/payout complete, release reserves, or change accounting/tax treatment. Agreements, country/entity, risk/compliance, support, reconciliation, and payout controls apply before material action.
