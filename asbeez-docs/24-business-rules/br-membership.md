# Membership Business Rules

## Purpose

Membership rules define eligibility, account status, country assignment, participation, referrals, benefits, privacy, suspension, migration, and termination.

## Rules

- membership is voluntary and governed by accepted terms;
- one current country assignment is maintained by platform process, but does not prove legal/tax residence;
- country migration requires residency evidence, review, approval, privacy/tax/compliance checks, and preserves historical country/currency/policy;
- referrals do not require recruitment for ordinary customer benefits and do not guarantee rewards or income;
- member, customer, vendor, and partner roles have distinct permissions and agreements; and
- suspension, holds, appeals, termination, and data rights follow documented policy and applicable law.

## Financial Boundary

Membership status does not create revenue, wallet value, liability, ownership, or payout. Reward, wallet, payment, tax, and financial effects require separate approved workflows and disclosures.
