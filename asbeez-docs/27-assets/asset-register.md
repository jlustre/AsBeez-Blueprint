# Asset Register

## Purpose

The asset register tracks every managed asset and its ownership, provenance, rights, status, and use.

## Required Fields

Asset ID, title, category, file/path, format, dimensions/duration, creator/source, license/consent, owner, project/domain, audience, country/channel scope, confidentiality/privacy class, version, status, related document/decision/requirement, approval, expiration/review date, and checksum where integrity matters.

## Statuses

Draft, in review, approved, published, restricted, superseded, archived, expired, or removed. Superseded assets remain traceable when they support historical decisions or published materials.

## Controls

No asset is published without rights, privacy, accessibility, quality, brand, legal, and owner review appropriate to its use. External financial, investor, legal, reward, or income-related assets require the relevant Finance/Legal/Compliance approvals.
