# Pitch Deck Assets

## Purpose

Pitch-deck assets include approved slides, charts, diagrams, logos, product visuals, financial exhibits, market exhibits, and disclosure pages.

## Requirements

Record source, period, methodology, currency/FX, actual/forecast status, claims approval, confidentiality, audience, version, owner, and expiration/review date.

## Controls

Do not imply guaranteed income, investment return, valuation, ownership, revenue, growth, or customer/vendor outcomes. Legal, Finance, Compliance, and business owners approve material external-use assets.
