## Asset Authority

Assets support documentation, product, marketing, operations, investor relations, legal, and implementation work; they do not override written requirements, policies, contracts, architecture decisions, business rules, financial records, or approved API/event contracts. Published assets must link to their source and approval.

## Global Requirements

Every managed asset has an owner, source/provenance, rights or consent, version/status, use scope, privacy/confidentiality classification, accessibility treatment, review/expiry date, and related document or decision. Sensitive, personal, financial, payment, identity, legal, investor, or provider data is minimized, masked, access-controlled, and retained under policy.

## Publication Gate

Before publication verify rights, privacy, accessibility, brand/product accuracy, localization, legal/compliance, financial claim accuracy, security, and audience/channel scope. Preserve superseded assets when they support historical evidence.
