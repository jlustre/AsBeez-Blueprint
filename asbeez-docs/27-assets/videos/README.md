# Video Assets

## Purpose

Video assets include demos, training, onboarding, product education, support, investor, campaign, and operational recordings.

## Requirements

Record script/storyboard, owner, participants/consent, source/version, captions/transcript, language, accessibility, music/image rights, privacy classification, audience, usage scope, and review/expiry date.

## Controls

Do not demonstrate unsupported financial, reward, payout, AI, or compliance behavior as available. Label prototypes and forecasts. Retain rights and consent evidence with the source project.
