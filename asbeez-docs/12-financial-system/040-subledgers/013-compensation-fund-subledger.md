# Compensation Fund Subledger

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/040-subledgers/013-compensation-fund-subledger.md

---

## Purpose

The Compensation Fund Subledger records approved funds designated for participant, partner, member, or program compensation. It does not authorize a compensation plan or determine whether a participant qualifies.

## Required Detail

Fund ID, funding source, program and policy version, country, legal entity, currency, approved allocation, committed amount, paid amount, returned/expired amount, reserve, beneficiary class, approval, and remaining balance.

## Rules

Funding must come from a documented source such as approved marketplace allocation, platform revenue, promotion budget, or other lawful source. Recruitment or participant registration alone cannot create a compensation entitlement. Use is limited to the approved program and jurisdiction.

## Controls

Fund balance, commitments, payouts, reversals, and expirations reconcile to GL accounts, commission/reward subledgers, settlement batches, and approval evidence. A fund cannot be overspent or silently transferred to another purpose.
