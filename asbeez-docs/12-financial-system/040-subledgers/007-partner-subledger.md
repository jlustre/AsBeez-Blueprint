# Partner Subledger

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/040-subledgers/007-partner-subledger.md

---

## Purpose

The Partner Subledger records approved partner-referred activity, allocations, fees, commissions, holds, reserves, tax, settlement, and payout obligations.

## Qualification Boundary

A partner record is not a payable merely because a partner registered, referred a person, or appears in a genealogy. A payable requires a qualifying source transaction, approved program rule, attribution, compliance status, and settlement approval.

## Required Detail

Partner ID, program ID/version, source order or activity, attribution evidence, customer/member reference where permitted, gross basis, fee, tax, reserve, adjustment, net obligation, country, currency, settlement window, approval, payout, and reversal links.

## Rules

Refunds, chargebacks, fraud, and attribution disputes create explicit holds or adjustments. Partner obligations remain distinct from platform revenue and vendor entitlement. Totals reconcile to commission subledger, settlement batches, and GL partner payable accounts.
