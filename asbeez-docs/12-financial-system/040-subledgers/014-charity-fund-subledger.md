# Charity Fund Subledger

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/040-subledgers/014-charity-fund-subledger.md

---

## Purpose

The Charity Fund Subledger records amounts that AsBeez has explicitly designated for approved charitable or community purposes, including commitments, disbursements, returns, and evidence.

## Required Detail

Fund ID, source transaction or allocation, beneficiary organization, purpose, country, legal entity, currency, designated amount, commitment, approval, disbursement, receipt/evidence, restricted status, and remaining balance.

## Rules

Charity funds are segregated from vendor, partner, member, reward, promotional, and operating funds. Designation does not become a charitable contribution or tax-deductible donation claim without applicable legal approval and evidence. Disbursements require beneficiary verification and dual control.

## Reconciliation

Fund commitments and disbursements reconcile to approved allocations, bank or payout evidence, fund GL accounts, and beneficiary acknowledgements. Unused or returned funds follow the approved designation policy.
