# AHC Subledger

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/040-subledgers/004-ahc-subledger.md

---

## Purpose

The AHC Subledger records AsBeez Hive Credit events generated by approved Beehive Matrix distribution rules. AHC is a platform-defined reward unit; its financial treatment is separate from its program quantity.

## Record Contract

Each AHC event references the member, ABC, matrix node/cycle, source descendant or qualifying event, level/rule version, country, event quantity, status, and any approved financial classification. The event states include proposed, validated, distributed, held, reversed, expired, redeemed, and financially recognized where applicable.

## Rules

- Matrix placement or an AHC quantity does not automatically create money or a liability.
- Financial recognition requires approved Legal and Finance classification, funding source, jurisdiction, and account mapping.
- AHC events remain traceable after reversal, expiry, redemption, or payout.
- AHC cannot be purchased directly or used to bypass marketplace qualification rules.
- Any wallet credit or payout requires a separate approved monetary event.

## Control Totals

Report AHC issued, held, reversed, expired, redeemed, financially recognized, and outstanding by member, ABC, country, matrix cycle, and policy version. Monetary totals reconcile only for events explicitly classified as monetary.
