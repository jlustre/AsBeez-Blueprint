# Commission Subledger

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/040-subledgers/008-commission-subledger.md

---

## Purpose

The Commission Subledger records commission calculations and obligations created by approved partner, referral, vendor, or other compensation programs.

## Lifecycle

```text
Proposed -> Qualified -> Calculated -> Held/Reviewed -> Approved -> Payable -> Paid
						 \-> Reversed or Rejected
```

## Required Detail

Commission ID, program/version, beneficiary, source transaction, qualification evidence, calculation basis, rate or fixed amount, currency, tax/withholding, reserve, hold, country, legal entity, approval, settlement, payout, and reversal references.

## Rules

Commission is not created from recruitment or registration alone. The program must identify the genuine qualifying commerce or approved activity, funding source, maximum liability, and refund/chargeback treatment. Commission subledger totals reconcile to partner/vendor payables and approved expense or allocation accounts.
