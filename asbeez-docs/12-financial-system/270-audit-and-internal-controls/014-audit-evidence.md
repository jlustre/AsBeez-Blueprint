# Audit Evidence

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/270-audit-and-internal-controls/014-audit-evidence.md

---

## Purpose

Audit evidence proves that financial events, controls, policies, approvals, calculations, reconciliations, reports, and corrections occurred as represented.

## Evidence Requirements

Evidence has source, scope, date/time, actor/system, entity/country/currency, policy/rule/model version, integrity/hash, access classification, retention, chain of custody, and relationship to control/audit request.

## Rules

Evidence is complete, relevant, reliable, timely, protected, and reproducible. Redaction minimizes personal/sensitive data without destroying required financial meaning. Evidence cannot be fabricated, silently replaced, or deleted under legal hold.
