# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/270-audit-and-internal-controls/019-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve risks, control objectives, owners, materiality, evidence, retention, and audit authority;
- implement preventive/detective/corrective, approval, access, change, reconciliation, and close controls;
- implement control testing, evidence, exceptions, remediation, external audit support, reporting, API, and events; and
- connect all financial domains, country controls, fraud/risk, and reporting.

## Medium Term

- automate evidence collection, control testing, exception aging, remediation tracking, and audit requests;
- strengthen continuous controls, access reviews, policy/version impact, and country compliance; and
- add explainable AI assistance with human approval.

## Long Term

- support regional audit/control services and continuous close;
- provide governed regulator, auditor, provider, and partner integrations;
- strengthen control simulation, resilience, and model governance; and
- preserve portable evidence and control history across jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No financial feature may launch, change, certify, or automate material decisions without control owner, risk assessment, evidence, testing, approval, retention, exception, remediation, and audit rules.
