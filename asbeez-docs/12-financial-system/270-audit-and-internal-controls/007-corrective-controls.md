# Corrective Controls

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/270-audit-and-internal-controls/007-corrective-controls.md

---

## Purpose

Corrective controls contain, correct, compensate, restore, and prevent recurrence after a control failure, financial discrepancy, fraud event, policy breach, or operational incident.

## Examples

Source correction, compensating entry, reversal, reserve/hold, account lock, payout stop, policy update, access removal, provider correction, tax amendment, reward adjustment, vendor/partner settlement correction, and incident remediation.

## Rules

Corrections preserve original records, identify root cause and impact, require authority, and reconcile downstream effects. Corrective control does not conceal loss, erase audit history, or convert an unresolved issue into a zero balance.
