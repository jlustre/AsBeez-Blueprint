## Purpose

Audit and Internal Controls protect the integrity, authorization, confidentiality, availability, and traceability of AsBeez financial activity and reporting.

## AsBeez Boundary

Controls and audit trace orders, payments, invoices, refunds, disputes, wallets, rewards, vendors, partners, tax, reserves, treasury, FX, budgets, reconciliations, payouts, and ledgers. They provide evidence and governance but do not replace domain ownership or rewrite historical records.

## Principles

- prevent, detect, correct, and evidence control failures;
- preserve immutable financial and operational history;
- separate initiation, approval, execution, reconciliation, and reporting;
- make policy, model, rule, account, source, entity, country, currency, and period versions traceable;
- protect privacy and sensitive financial/risk data; and
- require fair, explainable, reviewable, and appealable control decisions.
