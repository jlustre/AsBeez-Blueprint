# Reconciliation Controls

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/270-audit-and-internal-controls/011-reconciliation-controls.md

---

## Purpose

Reconciliation controls ensure ledger, subledger, source, provider, bank, tax, treasury, reward, vendor/partner, reserve, payout, and report populations are compared and exceptions resolved.

## Rules

Control scope, frequency, match rules, tolerance, source/target, owner, evidence, exception severity, approval, and close impact are explicit. Balanced totals do not excuse missing attribution, duplicate records, stale data, or unresolved exceptions.

## Testing

Test population completeness, matching accuracy, tolerance, exception routing, aging, reviewer approval, source integrity, and close/reporting impact at the defined frequency.
