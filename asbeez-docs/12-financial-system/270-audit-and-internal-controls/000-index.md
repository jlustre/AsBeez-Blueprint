## Design Authority

Audit and Internal Controls owns the control framework, audit trail, evidence, testing, exceptions, remediation, audit support, and control reporting. Compliance/Governance owns policy authority; domain teams own operational controls; Internal/External Audit retain independent assurance roles.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Approve risks, control objectives, owners, materiality, evidence, retention, and audit authority.
2. Implement preventive/detective/corrective, approval, access, change, reconciliation, and close controls.
3. Implement control testing, evidence, exceptions, remediation, external audit support, reporting, API, and events.
4. Connect all financial domains, country controls, fraud/risk, policy versions, and governed AI.
