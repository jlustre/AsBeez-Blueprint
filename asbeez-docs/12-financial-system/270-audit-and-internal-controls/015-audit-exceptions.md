# Audit Exceptions

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/270-audit-and-internal-controls/015-audit-exceptions.md

---

## Purpose

Audit exceptions record control failures, evidence gaps, policy breaches, reconciliation differences, unauthorized access, reporting limitations, or audit findings.

## Required Data

Exception/finding ID, control/risk, scope, evidence, amount/unit/currency, entity/country, severity, root cause, owner, due date, interim control, risk acceptance, remediation, retest, closure, and approval.

## Rules

Exceptions cannot be closed by changing source data, suppressing reports, or accepting risk without authority. Material, repeated, overdue, fraud, tax, privacy, payout, reward, or financial-statement issues escalate under governance.

## Reporting

Report exceptions by control, risk, entity, country, currency, severity, owner, age, root cause, remediation status, financial impact, and risk acceptance.
