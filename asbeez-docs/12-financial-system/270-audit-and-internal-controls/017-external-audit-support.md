# External Audit Support

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/270-audit-and-internal-controls/017-external-audit-support.md

---

## Purpose

External audit support provides authorized auditors with complete, controlled, reproducible evidence for financial statements, controls, policies, reconciliations, tax, risk, technology, and compliance.

## Support Package

Trial balance, statements, ledger/subledger mappings, source-to-report samples, close files, reconciliations, policies, approvals, access/change logs, control tests, exceptions, remediation, tax, treasury, reserves, rewards, vendor/partner, payment/provider, and audit exports.

## Rules

Requests are scoped, authorized, logged, privacy-reviewed, and delivered through secure channels. Evidence is read-only, versioned, integrity-protected, and retained. Management explanations do not replace source evidence.
