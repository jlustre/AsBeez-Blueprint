# Period Close Controls

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/270-audit-and-internal-controls/012-period-close-controls.md

---

## Purpose

Period-close controls verify cut-off, postings, adjustments, accruals, deferrals, trial balance, reconciliations, tax, treasury, reserves, rewards, vendor/partner, payouts, FX, budgets, approvals, and reports before lock.

## Rules

Close checklist, evidence, owners, exceptions, approvals, period state, late entries, reopenings, and restatements are recorded. Close does not hide unresolved issues or authorize future movement.

## Testing

Test cut-off, period locks, trial balance, reconciliations, accruals/deferrals, adjustments, approvals, report certification, and reopening controls before close certification.
