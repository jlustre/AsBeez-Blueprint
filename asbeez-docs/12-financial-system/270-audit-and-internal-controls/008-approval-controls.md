# Approval Controls

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/270-audit-and-internal-controls/008-approval-controls.md

---

## Purpose

Approval controls authorize material financial, risk, policy, accounting, tax, treasury, reward, payout, reserve, reconciliation, and reporting actions.

## Required Evidence

Request, scope, amount/unit/currency, entity/country, operation, policy/version, risk, requester, reviewer, approver, delegation/quorum, conditions, timestamp, decision, and downstream action.

## Rules

Approval is separate from execution and does not itself create a journal, payable, payout, reward, or reserve release. Self-approval is prohibited where separation of duties applies; emergency approvals are time-limited and reviewed.
