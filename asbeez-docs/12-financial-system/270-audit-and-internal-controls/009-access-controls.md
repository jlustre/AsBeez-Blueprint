# Access Controls

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/270-audit-and-internal-controls/009-access-controls.md

---

## Purpose

Access controls protect financial data, operations, source records, reports, credentials, evidence, and administrative authority through least privilege, authentication, authorization, review, and monitoring.

## Rules

Access is scoped by role, entity, country, operation, data classification, environment, and time. Privileged access requires strong authentication, approval, session logging, periodic review, and rapid revocation. Service accounts are unique, scoped, secret-managed, and monitored.

## Protected Actions

Posting, account changes, refunds, payouts, transfers, reserve release, tax/remittance, policy, risk override, reconciliation, close, reporting certification, export, and evidence destruction.
