# Audit Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/270-audit-and-internal-controls/018-audit-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Audit reporting presents control status, testing, findings, exceptions, remediation, risk acceptance, evidence completeness, financial impact, and external audit responses.

## Report Families

- control design/operating effectiveness;
- financial statement, close, ledger, subledger, reconciliation, tax, treasury, reserve, reward, vendor/partner, payout, and fraud controls;
- open/overdue/severity/root-cause/remediation reports;
- access/change/approval/policy/retention reports; and
- audit request, evidence, response, finding, and certification reports.

## Rules

Reports state scope, period, entity/country, currency, control/version, evidence status, limitations, owner, approval, and confidentiality. Audit reports are not financial statements unless separately certified.
