# Remediation

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/270-audit-and-internal-controls/016-remediation.md

---

## Purpose

Remediation resolves control deficiencies, audit findings, fraud/risk weaknesses, reconciliation differences, policy gaps, access issues, or financial errors and prevents recurrence.

## Required Plan

Finding/root cause, risk, affected scope, containment, corrective action, owner, resources, due date, dependency, evidence, validation, retest, residual risk, acceptance, and closure approval.

## Rules

Remediation may require source correction, compensating entry, policy change, access removal, control redesign, training, provider fix, reserve/hold, or disclosure. It does not delete history or close until effectiveness is retested.

## Closure Evidence

Closure requires completed action, supporting evidence, retest result, residual-risk decision, owner confirmation, approver, date, affected reports/periods, and linkage to the original finding.
