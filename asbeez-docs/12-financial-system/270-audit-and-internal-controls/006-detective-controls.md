# Detective Controls

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/270-audit-and-internal-controls/006-detective-controls.md

---

## Purpose

Detective controls identify activity or conditions that should be reviewed after or during processing.

## Examples

Bank/provider reconciliation, ledger/subledger reconciliation, duplicate detection, variance analysis, fraud monitoring, exception aging, tax/report review, payout monitoring, reserve coverage, access review, model drift, and close control checks.

## Rules

Detection creates evidence, alert, exception, or case; it does not prove fault or automatically alter financial records. Alerts have owner, severity, SLA, rule/model version, evidence, action, and resolution.

## Testing

Tests assess coverage, alert quality, timeliness, false positives, missed events, escalation, owner response, and closure evidence. Material or repeated failures become audit findings.
