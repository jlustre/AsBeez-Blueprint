# Encryption

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/280-security/009-encryption.md

---

## Purpose

Encryption protects financial, identity, tax, payment, risk, audit, policy, and provider data in transit, at rest, in backups, and in exports.

## Rules

Use approved modern protocols, authenticated transport, strong storage encryption, field-level protection for sensitive data, key separation, rotation, revocation, backup protection, and access logging. Encryption does not replace authorization, masking, tokenization, retention, or secure deletion.

## Scope

APIs, databases, queues, logs, files, reports, evidence packages, provider connections, bank data, secrets, disaster recovery, and cross-country transfers are classified and protected according to policy.

## Assurance

Encryption coverage, algorithm/protocol, key version, rotation, exception, backup protection, access, and test results are monitored and reviewed by environment and country.
