# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/280-security/018-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- classify financial assets, data, threats, trust boundaries, countries, entities, and control owners;
- implement authentication, authorization, RBAC, separation, transaction/MFA approval, encryption, tokenization, keys, secrets, PCI, API, masking, monitoring, and incident response;
- connect fraud/risk, compliance, audit, reconciliation, payouts, wallets, payments, rewards, and reporting; and
- establish security testing, access review, provider controls, evidence, retention, and recovery.

## Medium Term

- automate control monitoring, vulnerability management, access certification, secret/key rotation, and incident evidence;
- strengthen country/data-residency, provider, supply-chain, and resilience controls; and
- add explainable security AI with human approval.

## Long Term

- support regional security services and high-volume financial operations;
- provide governed provider, bank, auditor, partner, and regulator integrations;
- strengthen continuous authentication, privacy-preserving analytics, and recovery; and
- preserve portable security and audit evidence across jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No financial feature may launch or change without threat assessment, data classification, authentication, authorization, encryption, secrets, logging, incident, retention, recovery, and audit controls.
