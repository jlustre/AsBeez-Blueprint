## Purpose

Financial Security protects AsBeez financial data, accounts, transactions, wallets, payouts, payments, rewards, vendors, partners, providers, policies, audit evidence, and control authority.

## AsBeez Boundary

Security controls protect access and integrity but do not decide accounting, tax, reward qualification, vendor/partner liability, or customer rights. Security events and controls must integrate with fraud/risk, compliance, audit, reconciliation, incident, and financial correction workflows.

## Principles

- defense in depth, least privilege, separation of duties, and secure defaults;
- strong authentication and transaction-level authorization;
- tokenization, encryption, masking, key/secrets protection, and data minimization;
- immutable audit logs and evidence for material access and changes;
- country/entity/privacy/retention-aware controls; and
- incident containment preserves financial history and supports recovery without unauthorized edits.
