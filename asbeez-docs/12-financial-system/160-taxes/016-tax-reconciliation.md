# Tax Reconciliation

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/160-taxes/016-tax-reconciliation.md

---

## Purpose

Tax reconciliation proves that tax assessments agree with order/invoice lines, payments, vendor/partner settlements, withholding, tax subledger, GL payable/recoverable accounts, filings, and remittances.

## Required Checks

- taxable basis, rate, authority, currency, and jurisdiction agree;
- exemptions, registrations, and place-of-supply evidence are valid;
- refunds, returns, chargebacks, discounts, cancellations, notes, and withholding propagate;
- invoice, settlement, filing, remittance, and GL populations agree;
- rounding residuals are within approved policy; and
- amended, late, failed, duplicate, or unpaid remittances are explicit exceptions.

## Outcomes

Matched, timing difference, assessment mismatch, missing evidence, duplicate, filing difference, remittance difference, provider mismatch, or approved adjustment. Every exception has owner, age, evidence, severity, and resolution.
