# Goods And Services Tax

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/160-taxes/006-goods-and-services-tax.md

---

## Purpose

GST records goods and services tax treatment for applicable jurisdictions, including taxable supplies, zero-rated supplies, exemptions, input credits, registration, collection, and remittance.

## Rules

GST basis, rate, authority, registration, place of supply, product/service category, customer/vendor status, and evidence are explicit. GST collected or withheld is not AsBeez revenue. Marketplace, digital product, service, subscription, vendor, and cross-border treatment follows local policy.

## Reconciliation

GST assessments reconcile to invoice lines, payments, refunds, vendor/partner settlements, tax payable/recoverable accounts, filings, and remittance confirmations. Historical assessments remain immutable.
