## Purpose

Taxes governs tax determination, collection, withholding, exemption, registration, invoicing, remittance, reporting, reconciliation, provider integration, and audit across AsBeez countries and legal entities.

## AsBeez Boundary

Tax is determined by jurisdiction, transaction, product/service, party role, place of supply, registration, and effective law. Tax collected or withheld for an authority is not AsBeez revenue, and tax calculation does not decide order pricing, vendor entitlement, reward qualification, or platform revenue.

## Principles

- tax facts and rules are jurisdiction- and effective-date-specific;
- taxable basis, rate, authority, currency, exemption, evidence, and liability are explicit;
- tax is calculated per applicable line and preserved with invoice/order/settlement evidence;
- refunds, returns, chargebacks, discounts, cancellations, and notes create tax adjustments;
- filing and remittance status reconcile to tax subledger and GL payable accounts; and
- automated or AI recommendations require deterministic tax policy and human/tax approval where material.
