# Tax Invoices

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/160-taxes/013-tax-invoices.md

---

## Purpose

Tax invoices present required taxable supplies, tax lines, registration, authority, amounts, currency, exemptions, and statutory fields for an issued invoice.

## Rules

Tax invoice content is generated from immutable assessment and invoice versions. It distinguishes tax from consideration, fees, vendor entitlement, partner commission, rewards, and revenue. Credit/debit notes and amended documents reference the original and preserve audit evidence.

## Controls

Numbering, issuer registration, recipient details, place of supply, tax category, rate, rounding, currency, language, delivery, retention, and e-invoice/submission requirements follow effective jurisdictional policy.

## Reporting and Corrections

Issued tax invoices report assessment, authority, tax type, taxable basis, and collection status separately from revenue and payment. Credit/debit notes, amended invoices, and filing corrections reference the original document and retain the reason, approval, and tax impact.
