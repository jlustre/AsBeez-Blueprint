# Tax AI Capabilities

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/160-taxes/020-tax-ai-capabilities.md

---

## Purpose

AI may assist tax operations with classification, anomaly detection, exemption review, reconciliation, filing preparation, and exposure forecasting while tax law, deterministic policy, and human approval remain authoritative.

## Permitted Assistance

- identify missing jurisdiction, registration, product, or exemption facts;
- suggest tax category or reconciliation matches;
- detect unusual rate, basis, rounding, filing, or remittance patterns;
- prioritize exceptions and filing deadlines; and
- forecast tax payable, recoverable, withholding, and liquidity needs.

## Prohibited Actions

AI must not independently determine final tax, approve exemption, submit filing/remittance, alter historical assessment, bypass authority policy, classify tax as revenue, or expose sensitive tax identifiers.

## Governance

Recommendations include model/version, evidence scope, confidence, reason, provenance, policy context, reviewer decision, and amendment trail. Tax, identity, registration, and financial data is minimized and access-controlled.
