# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/160-taxes/021-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve launch jurisdictions, entities, registrations, tax types, products, currencies, and providers;
- implement deterministic calculation, exemption, invoice, withholding, filing, remittance, and reconciliation workflows;
- connect orders, invoices, payments, vendors, partners, fees, refunds, disputes, payouts, and GL mappings;
- implement reporting, API, events, security, evidence, retention, and audit; and
- establish amendment, exception, and human tax review operations.

## Medium Term

- support localized indirect tax and income reporting;
- automate provider evidence, filing preparation, reconciliation, and remittance monitoring;
- improve tax liability, cash, threshold, and registration forecasting; and
- add explainable AI assistance with tax/human approval.

## Long Term

- support regional tax services and multi-entity consolidation;
- provide governed authority/provider integrations;
- strengthen cross-border, marketplace, digital product, and partner tax treatment; and
- preserve portable assessment and filing evidence across jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No tax feature may calculate, collect, withhold, report, or remit without approved jurisdiction, registration, rule, evidence, currency, filing, reconciliation, retention, and audit requirements.
