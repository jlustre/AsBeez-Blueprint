# Value Added Tax

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/160-taxes/005-value-added-tax.md

---

## Purpose

VAT records output, input, reverse-charge, exempt, zero-rated, and recoverable tax treatment for applicable goods and services.

## Required Inputs

Supply type, seller/customer registration, place of supply, product/service category, taxable basis, rate, exemption/zero-rate evidence, invoice data, currency, jurisdiction, and effective rule.

## Rules

VAT collected is a tax payable, not revenue. Input VAT recovery depends on valid evidence and jurisdictional eligibility. Marketplace, vendor, partner, subscription, service, and cross-border treatment follows approved local policy.

## Corrections

Credit/debit notes, refunds, returns, cancellations, and amended returns reference original VAT assessment and update payable/recoverable balances through controlled events.
