# Tax Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/160-taxes/015-tax-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Tax reporting produces authority filings, information returns, management views, and audit packages from approved tax assessments, invoices, settlements, withholding, remittance, and registration records.

## Report Families

- sales tax, VAT, GST, and indirect tax returns;
- vendor/partner/member withholding and income reporting;
- exempt, zero-rated, reverse-charge, and recoverable tax populations;
- collected, payable, recoverable, withheld, remitted, overdue, and adjusted tax;
- country/entity/authority/currency and filing-period views; and
- exception, evidence, reconciliation, and amendment reports.

## Rules

Each report states population, authority, jurisdiction, period, currency, policy/rule version, source, filing status, approval, and reconciliation status. Tax is not reported as AsBeez revenue, reward income, vendor sales, or partner commission unless the law and approved classification require that treatment.
