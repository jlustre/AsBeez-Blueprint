# Tax Registration

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/160-taxes/010-tax-registration.md

---

## Purpose

Tax registration records the legal entity, vendor, partner, or other party’s registration with an authority and determines where collection, withholding, reporting, and remittance obligations may apply.

## Required Data

Registrant, legal entity, country/jurisdiction, authority, registration number, tax types, effective/expiry dates, threshold status, verification source, filing frequency, currency, and policy owner.

## Rules

Registration status is versioned and cannot be changed for historical assessments. A missing, expired, or revoked registration may block collection/withholding, change rate/treatment, or require review. Registration does not itself create revenue, payable, or tax amount.
