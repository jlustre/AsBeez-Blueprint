# Withholding Tax

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/160-taxes/007-withholding-tax.md

---

## Purpose

Withholding tax records amounts withheld from an approved vendor, partner, member, contractor, or other payable under jurisdictional law.

## Required Data

Payee, tax ID/residency, jurisdiction, authority, tax type, taxable basis, rate, amount, currency, exemption/treaty evidence, settlement/payout, filing period, remittance, and policy version.

## Rules

Withholding is not AsBeez revenue or an arbitrary fee. It reduces the approved payable, credits tax payable, is disclosed where required, and reconciles to filing and remittance evidence. A missing or stale tax profile blocks or holds the affected payout.

## Reporting States

Track calculated, reviewed, withheld, payable, filed, remitted, adjusted, refunded, and disputed withholding by payee, authority, country, entity, currency, period, and policy version.
