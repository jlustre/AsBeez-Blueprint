# Tax Exemptions

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/160-taxes/009-tax-exemptions.md

---

## Purpose

Tax exemptions, zero rates, reliefs, and treaty benefits reduce or alter tax only when a valid legal basis and evidence apply to the party, transaction, product/service, jurisdiction, and period.

## Required Evidence

Exemption ID/type, holder, tax ID, certificate or treaty, jurisdiction, covered products/services, effective/expiry dates, verification source, reviewer, and revocation status.

## Rules

An exemption is not inferred from a customer/vendor label or partner status. Missing, expired, revoked, or mismatched evidence applies ordinary tax treatment or routes the transaction to review. Historical assessments retain the evidence used at calculation time.

## Lifecycle

```text
Submitted -> Verified -> Active -> Expiring -> Expired | Revoked
```

Every approval, rejection, renewal, revocation, and affected assessment retains reviewer, source, effective dates, and authority evidence.
