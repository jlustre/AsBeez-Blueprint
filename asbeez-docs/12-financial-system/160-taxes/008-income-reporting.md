# Income Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/160-taxes/008-income-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Income reporting produces jurisdictional statements or information returns for approved vendor, partner, member, contractor, or other payee amounts where law requires.

## Required Data

Payee identity/tax ID, residency, reportable amount, withholding, payment date, source program, entity, country, currency, form/return type, consent, corrections, and filing evidence.

## Rules

Reportable earnings, commissions, bonuses, incentives, payouts, RP/ABC/AHC program quantities, and AsBeez revenue are distinct categories. RP, ABC, and AHC are not reported as income or cash unless an approved jurisdictional classification requires it.

## Corrections

Amended reports reference the original population, assessment, filing, and reason. Privacy, access, retention, and legal hold controls apply.
