# Sales Tax

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/160-taxes/004-sales-tax.md

---

## Purpose

Sales tax records jurisdictional consumption tax assessed on taxable sales of products or services under applicable law.

## Required Inputs

Seller/marketplace role, customer location, product/service category, taxable price, discounts, delivery, registration, exemption, jurisdiction, rate, currency, and effective policy.

## Rules

Sales tax is calculated per taxable line, disclosed on invoice, credited to tax payable, and excluded from AsBeez revenue. Marketplace facilitator, vendor, customer, and platform collection responsibilities are determined by jurisdiction and contract.

## Corrections

Returns, refunds, cancellations, chargebacks, discounts, and credit/debit notes recalculate or adjust tax under jurisdictional policy while preserving original assessment and filing evidence.
