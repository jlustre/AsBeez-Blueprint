# Report Management

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/380-administration/017-report-management.md

---

## Purpose

Report administration manages definitions, schedules, filters, datasets, snapshots, exports, delivery, certification, withdrawal, and access for financial and management reporting.

## Rules

Every report identifies source/version, actual versus projection/forecast, period, entity/country/currency/FX/tax basis, filters, freshness, rounding, certification, owner, and delivery. Administrators cannot alter source data, conceal exceptions, certify their own report, or present estimates as posted actuals.

## Permitted Actions

Create/version definition, request run/export, schedule, deliver, review lineage, certify under authority, withdraw certification, and resolve report exceptions. Large or sensitive exports require scoped access, approval, encryption, retention, and recipient evidence.
