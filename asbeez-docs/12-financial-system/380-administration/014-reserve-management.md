# Reserve Management

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/380-administration/014-reserve-management.md

---

## Purpose

Reserve administration manages purpose, owner, exposure, funding, holds, release conditions, status, approval, wallet/subledger, ledger, and reconciliation.

## Permitted Actions

Propose, review, approve, fund, increase, decrease, release, transfer, expire, close, or investigate reserves through policy-controlled workflows.

## Rules

Reserve funds are not free cash, revenue, or an untracked liability. Administrators cannot release, rewrite, or repurpose reserves directly. Funding/release require current version, amount/currency, entity/country, exposure, authority, idempotency, accounting, and reconciliation evidence.
