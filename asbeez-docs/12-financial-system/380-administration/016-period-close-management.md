# Period Close Management

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/380-administration/016-period-close-management.md

---

## Purpose

Period-close administration manages calendars, posting windows, cutoff, close checklists, accruals, deferrals, adjustments, reconciliation, trial balance, reports, approvals, locks, reopenings, and certification.

## Procedure

Review scope and readiness; complete reconciliations and checklist; inspect late data/exceptions; approve controlled accrual/deferral/adjustment; validate trial balance/reports; obtain separate approval; lock/close; publish evidence and unresolved risk.

## Rules

Administrators cannot force unresolved differences into close, post ordinary entries to a locked period, reopen without authority, or certify without evidence. Reopening creates an auditable reason, impact assessment, correction, and re-close requirement.
