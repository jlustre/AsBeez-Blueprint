# Tax Management

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/380-administration/012-tax-management.md

---

## Purpose

Tax administration manages registrations, jurisdictions, classifications, calculation policies, exemptions, collection, withholding, filings, remittance, provider evidence, and tax reconciliation.

## Permitted Actions

Review/approve registration and classification, validate exemption, inspect calculation, prepare filing/remittance, resolve exception, and request controlled adjustment with entity/country/period authority.

## Rules

Tax collected or withheld is not revenue. Historical decisions are immutable; corrections use notes/adjustments and linked ledger/tax records. Administrators cannot bypass registration, exemption, effective policy, rounding, approval, retention, or segregation controls.
