# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/380-administration/022-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- establish admin dashboard, roles, permissions, MFA, scope, approval, configuration, audit, privacy, retention, and evidence standards;
- implement account, chart, journal, wallet, payment, payout, invoice, refund, dispute, tax, currency, reserve, reconciliation, close, and report administration; and
- connect administration to APIs, observability, testing, operations, incidents, and policy versioning.

## Medium Term

- add workflow automation for safe triage, evidence collection, exception routing, reconciliation, close, and report review with approval gates;
- improve country/entity administration, access recertification, configuration impact analysis, audit portals, and operational capacity; and
- provide governed AI assistance without direct financial side effects.

## Long Term

- support independently operated contexts with portable role, policy, audit, and runbook administration;
- provide predictive control and exception-risk analysis; and
- preserve human accountability, immutable financial truth, reconciliation, security, privacy, and recoverability.

## Roadmap Gate

No administrative change may bypass authorization, separation of duties, ledger immutability, reconciliation, tax/compliance, audit, privacy, country/entity, security, or recovery controls.
