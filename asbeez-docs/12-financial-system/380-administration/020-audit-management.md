# Audit Management

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/380-administration/020-audit-management.md

---

## Purpose

Audit administration manages audit trails, evidence requests, control testing, access, retention, legal hold, findings, remediation, report certification, and internal/external audit coordination.

## Rules

Audit records are append-only, tamper-evident, access-controlled, time/correlation linked, and retained by legal, tax, dispute, security, and financial policy. Administrators cannot edit/delete evidence, suppress findings, certify their own control, or change posted truth to satisfy an audit request.

## Permitted Actions

Search authorized evidence, create request/population, map control, assign reviewer, record finding, approve remediation, preserve legal hold, withdraw/reissue certification under authority, and report status. Every action carries actor, purpose, scope, source, time, policy, and outcome.
