# Chart Of Accounts Management

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/380-administration/004-chart-of-accounts-management.md

---

## Purpose

Chart of Accounts administration governs account hierarchy, numbering, classes, normal balances, control accounts, dimensions, mappings, versioning, and entity/country applicability.

## Procedure

Propose change; assess postings, subledgers, reports, tax, revenue, reserves, reconciliation, and close impact; define effective date and migration; obtain accounting/approval authority; publish version; test mappings; monitor adoption; and retain evidence.

## Rules

Posted account meaning and historical chart versions remain immutable. No account or mapping change bypasses ledger validation, dimension policy, separation of duties, or report/reconciliation certification. Legacy accounts are closed/superseded rather than erased.
