# AI Assistance

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/380-administration/021-ai-assistance.md

---

## Purpose

AI may assist administrators by summarizing queues, explaining balances/reports, retrieving evidence, prioritizing exceptions, drafting configuration impact, recommending mappings, and preparing review packets.

## Output

Show model/prompt/rule version, source scope, freshness, entity/country/currency/period, confidence, rationale, alternatives, limitations, policy, recommendation, human owner, and disposition.

## Prohibited Actions

AI cannot directly post, approve, pay, release/freeze, alter balances/configuration/history, certify, close, grant permissions, make final fraud/compliance/legal decisions, or bypass MFA, separation of duties, approval, reconciliation, tax, security, privacy, or audit. Recommendations re-enter normal authorized workflows.
