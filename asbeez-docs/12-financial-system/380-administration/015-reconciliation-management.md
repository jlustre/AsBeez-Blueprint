# Reconciliation Management

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/380-administration/015-reconciliation-management.md

---

## Purpose

Reconciliation administration manages scopes, source snapshots, matching rules, exceptions, assignments, resolution proposals, approvals, certification, reopening, and control totals.

## Permitted Actions

Start/re-run authorized scope, inspect sources, assign exception, propose resolution, approve under segregation rules, certify, reopen with authority, and request controlled adjustment or source refresh.

## Rules

Differences cannot be hidden, manually balanced, or certified without scope, source version, control totals, reviewer, unresolved risk, and evidence. Provider/bank/operational state is not authoritative until matched. Adjustments are idempotent, linked, approved, and re-reconciled.
