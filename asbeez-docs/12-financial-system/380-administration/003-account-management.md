# Account Management

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/380-administration/003-account-management.md

---

## Purpose

Account management administers financial account definitions, lifecycle, ownership, scope, mappings, dimensions, access, and effective dates.

## Permitted Actions

Create, activate, close, supersede, review, map, change dimension policy, and investigate accounts through authorized workflows with chart version, entity/country, impact, approval, and audit evidence.

## Rules

Account codes are unique within chart version. An account used by posted history cannot be deleted, repurposed, or silently remapped. Administrators cannot write balances or select unrestricted mappings for journal posting. Changes are effective-dated and require downstream report/reconciliation review.
