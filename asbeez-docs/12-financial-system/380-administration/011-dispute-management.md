# Dispute Management

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/380-administration/011-dispute-management.md

---

## Purpose

Dispute administration manages chargeback cases, deadlines, evidence, representment, arbitration, liability, reserves, recovery, loss, fees, communications, and reconciliation.

## Permitted Actions

Review case and evidence, request/submit authorized evidence, assign owner, accept/escalate liability decision, manage deadlines, request reserve workflow, and reconcile outcome.

## Rules

Administrators cannot fabricate/replace evidence, suppress deadlines, change liability without authority, release reserves, or alter posted accounting directly. Provider status is evidence until mapped to an approved business/accounting outcome; decisions, hashes, approvals, and effects remain auditable.
