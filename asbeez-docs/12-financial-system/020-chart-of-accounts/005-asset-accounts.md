# Asset Accounts

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/020-chart-of-accounts/005-asset-accounts.md

---

## Purpose

Asset accounts represent resources controlled by AsBeez or amounts owed to it. Recognition requires evidence that the resource or receivable exists and can be measured under the applicable accounting policy.

## Account Families

| Family | Typical accounts | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cash and bank | operating, reserve, restricted cash | bank reconciliation |
| Payment processor | processor receivable, funds in transit | provider statement matching |
| Receivables | customer, vendor, partner, tax receivable | aging and allowance |
| Prepayments | insurance, services, vendor advances | amortization schedule |
| Fixed assets | equipment, leasehold, technology assets | capitalization and depreciation |
| Intangible assets | approved software or acquisition assets | impairment and amortization |

## Rules

Cash accounts require institution, currency, legal entity, and reconciliation ownership. Processor receivables must distinguish captured unsettled funds from failed or disputed amounts. A wallet balance is not an AsBeez asset merely because it is visible in a platform interface.
