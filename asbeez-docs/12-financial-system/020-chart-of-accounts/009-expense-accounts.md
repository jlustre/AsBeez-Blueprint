# Expense Accounts

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/020-chart-of-accounts/009-expense-accounts.md

---

## Purpose

Expense accounts record resources consumed to operate the business or generate revenue. Each expense must have an owner, cost center or dimension where required, supporting source, and period treatment.

## Account Families

- payment processing, banking, and payout fees;
- reward, loyalty, and approved promotional costs;
- vendor and partner acquisition or service costs;
- people, support, compliance, legal, and administration;
- cloud infrastructure, software, data, and security;
- sales, marketing, advertising, and customer success; and
- depreciation, amortization, impairment, and bad debt.

## Rules

An expense is not created merely because cash was paid; prepayments, deposits, and capitalizable assets follow their appropriate accounting treatment. Reward-related expense requires a traceable qualifying event and approved policy version. Refund costs and chargeback losses must remain distinguishable from ordinary operating expense.

## Controls

Spend limits, purchase approvals, vendor identity, tax treatment, and period cut-off are validated before posting. Accruals are reversed or settled through explicit workflow.
