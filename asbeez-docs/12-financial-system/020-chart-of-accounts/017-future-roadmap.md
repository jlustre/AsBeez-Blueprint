# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/020-chart-of-accounts/017-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve the launch legal entities and jurisdictions;
- publish the initial account register and reporting mappings;
- define reward liability treatment with Legal and Finance;
- connect payment, settlement, payout, tax, and reconciliation workflows; and
- automate account metadata validation and period-close checks.

## Medium Term

- support localized statutory charts mapped to a global chart;
- add controlled intercompany and consolidation accounts;
- introduce account-usage analytics and dormant-account detection;
- version report definitions alongside account hierarchy; and
- provide governed account-change workflows for finance administrators.

## Long Term

- support regional ledger services and multiple legal entities;
- provide policy-aware account recommendations with human approval;
- strengthen automated reconciliation and anomaly detection; and
- preserve full historical reporting across chart versions and reorganizations.

## Roadmap Gate

No new account family or automated account recommendation may bypass the governance process. The roadmap is subordinate to legal classification, accounting standards, audit requirements, and approved architecture decisions.
