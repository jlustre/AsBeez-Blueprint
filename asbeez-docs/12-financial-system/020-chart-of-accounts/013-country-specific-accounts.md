# Country Specific Accounts

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/020-chart-of-accounts/013-country-specific-accounts.md

---

## Purpose

Country configuration supports local tax, reporting, settlement, and legal-entity requirements without fragmenting the global account vocabulary.

## Localized Account Families

- indirect tax collected and payable;
- withholding and remittance obligations;
- local payment and bank clearing;
- country-specific regulatory fees;
- local payroll or statutory obligations; and
- approved local revenue or expense treatment.

## Rules

Country-specific accounts require a jurisdiction, legal entity, chart version, effective period, accounting policy reference, and reporting mapping. A country variation must be justified by law, accounting treatment, banking reality, or approved operating need; translation or currency alone is not sufficient.

## Global Reporting

Each local account maps to a global reporting category. Consolidation retains local statutory detail and does not erase the source country, entity, currency, or tax evidence.
