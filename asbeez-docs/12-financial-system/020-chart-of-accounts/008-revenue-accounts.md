# Revenue Accounts

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/020-chart-of-accounts/008-revenue-accounts.md

---

## Purpose

Revenue accounts record amounts recognized as earned from approved performance obligations. Cash received, invoice issuance, and reward creation do not automatically equal revenue.

## Account Families

| Family | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Marketplace services | transaction fees, listing fees, platform commissions |
| Subscriptions | vendor, member, enterprise, or service subscriptions |
| Advertising | sponsored placement, campaign, and promotion services |
| Technology services | API, analytics, SaaS, or integration services |
| Other approved services | documented, separately reviewed revenue streams |

## Rules

Revenue postings reference the contract or order, performance obligation, recognition policy, jurisdiction, and reporting dimensions. Gross and net presentation must be approved; vendor collections and taxes held for others are not automatically AsBeez revenue.

For marketplace orders, platform revenue is distinct from customer payment, vendor entitlement, partner commission, tax collected, reserves, and reward-related financial effects. The order allocation must identify each component before revenue is recognized.

Refunds, credits, discounts, and chargebacks use controlled contra-revenue or expense treatment according to the approved accounting policy and never overwrite original revenue entries.
