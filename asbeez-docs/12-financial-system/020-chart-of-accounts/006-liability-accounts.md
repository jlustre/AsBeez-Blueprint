# Liability Accounts

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/020-chart-of-accounts/006-liability-accounts.md

---

## Purpose

Liability accounts represent present obligations to customers, members, vendors, partners, governments, providers, employees, or other parties.

## Account Families

| Family | Typical accounts | Recognition source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participant payables | vendor, partner, member settlement payable | approved settlement |
| Customer funds | customer credits, gift or stored-value obligations | approved customer balance policy |
| Tax payables | sales tax, VAT/GST, withholding | tax assessment |
| Payment obligations | refunds payable, chargeback exposure | payment/dispute event |
| Reward liabilities | approved financial obligations arising from an RP, ABC, or AHC event | reward accounting policy |
| Deferred revenue | unearned subscriptions or services | billing and recognition schedule |
| Accruals | services received, payroll, vendor costs | close or accrual workflow |

## Rules

Every liability has an owner, settlement or release condition, currency, jurisdiction where relevant, and aging policy. A liability is not reduced because a UI balance changed; it is released, paid, expired, or reversed through a sourced financial event.

Reward liabilities require Legal and Finance approval of the applicable classification. RP, ABC, and AHC themselves are not money; ABC creation or matrix placement creates no liability unless an approved policy explicitly creates a monetary obligation.
