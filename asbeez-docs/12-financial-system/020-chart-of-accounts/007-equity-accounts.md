# Equity Accounts

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/020-chart-of-accounts/007-equity-accounts.md

---

## Purpose

Equity accounts represent the residual interest attributable to the legal entity after liabilities are deducted from assets. They must not be used to represent member wallet balances or ABC participation.

## Account Families

- contributed or issued capital;
- additional paid-in capital;
- retained earnings;
- current-period result;
- accumulated other comprehensive income where applicable; and
- approved distributions or withdrawals.

## Rules

Equity is maintained per legal entity and reporting currency policy. Closing entries transfer approved period results according to the financial close procedure. Equity postings require authorized corporate or accounting support and cannot be created by marketplace, reward, wallet, or payout workflows directly.

## Reporting

Equity reports must distinguish contributed capital, accumulated results, current-period activity, and distributions. Intercompany or owner transactions require explicit party and approval evidence.
