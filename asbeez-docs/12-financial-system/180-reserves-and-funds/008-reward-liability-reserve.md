# Reward Liability Reserve

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/180-reserves-and-funds/008-reward-liability-reserve.md

---

## Purpose

The Reward Liability Reserve protects against approved monetary obligations arising from classified RP, ABC, AHC, referral, compensation, redemption, or loyalty programs.

## Rules

RP, ABC, and AHC quantities are not reserves or liabilities by default. Reserve measurement requires approved legal/accounting classification, qualifying source, funding, liability ceiling, utilization assumptions, country/entity/currency, and release rules.

## Monitoring

Report program quantity, recognized liability, reserve target, funded, released, utilized, outstanding, expired, and variance separately by program, country, entity, currency, and policy version.
