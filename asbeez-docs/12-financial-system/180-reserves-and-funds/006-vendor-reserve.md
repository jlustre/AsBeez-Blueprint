# Vendor Reserve

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/180-reserves-and-funds/006-vendor-reserve.md

---

## Purpose

The Vendor Reserve restricts vendor entitlement against documented refund, return, chargeback, fraud, fulfillment, tax, or settlement exposure.

## Rules

Reserve remains attributable to the vendor and exposure population. It is not a fee, revenue, confiscation, or realized loss. Release or utilization requires policy, evidence, approval, and reconciliation to vendor settlement and payout.

## Required Detail

Vendor, order/population, exposure, basis, rate/amount, currency, country/entity, funding, review/release condition, owner, approval, utilization, and GL/subledger references.

## Monitoring

Track reserved, held, released, utilized, expired, and outstanding amounts by vendor, exposure type, settlement period, country, entity, currency, and policy version.
