# Charity Fund

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/180-reserves-and-funds/010-charity-fund.md

---

## Purpose

The Charity Fund is a designated pool for approved charitable or community allocations with documented beneficiary, purpose, jurisdiction, and disbursement evidence.

## Rules

Charity designation does not automatically create a donation, tax deduction, or charitable legal status. Funds are segregated from rewards, vendor/partner payables, promotional funds, and operating reserves. Disbursement requires verified beneficiary, purpose, approval, payment evidence, and applicable tax/legal review.

## Reconciliation

Designated, committed, disbursed, returned, and remaining amounts reconcile to fund GL accounts, bank/payout evidence, beneficiary acknowledgements, and country policy.
