# Liquidity Reserve

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/180-reserves-and-funds/013-liquidity-reserve.md

---

## Purpose

The Liquidity Reserve protects the cash and settlement capacity required for approved payments, refunds, payouts, chargebacks, taxes, operations, and cross-border timing differences.

## Rules

Liquidity reserve targets use cash forecasts, settlement windows, provider delays, refund/chargeback exposure, payout obligations, tax due dates, currency, entity, and risk assumptions. It is not customer, vendor, partner, reward, charity, or promotional money.

## Controls

Treasury approves funding, access, minimum, replenishment, currency, and release rules. Usage is recorded against the approved exposure and reconciles to bank, provider, payout, refund, tax, reserve, and GL evidence.
