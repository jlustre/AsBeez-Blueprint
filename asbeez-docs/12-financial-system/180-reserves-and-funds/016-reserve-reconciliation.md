# Reserve Reconciliation

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/180-reserves-and-funds/016-reserve-reconciliation.md

---

## Purpose

Reserve reconciliation proves that reserve/fund balances agree with exposure populations, funding, commitments, releases, utilization, returns, treasury/provider evidence, subledgers, and General Ledger accounts.

## Required Checks

- reserve basis/population and assumptions are current;
- funded amount agrees to source and destination accounts;
- commitments, holds, releases, utilization, expiry, and returns are idempotent;
- vendor, partner, reward, refund, chargeback, country, liquidity, and fund links are complete;
- currency/entity/policy dimensions agree; and
- outstanding exceptions are owned and aged.

## Outcomes

Matched, exposure variance, funding variance, stale assumption, duplicate movement, missing release, provider/bank mismatch, unauthorized use, or approved adjustment. A balanced total does not prove correct purpose or beneficiary attribution.
