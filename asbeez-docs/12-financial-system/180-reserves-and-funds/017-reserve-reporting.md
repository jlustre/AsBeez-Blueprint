# Reserve Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/180-reserves-and-funds/017-reserve-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Reserve reporting separates exposure, target, funded, committed, held, available, utilized, released, returned, expired, and outstanding amounts across reserves and designated funds.

## Report Families

- operating, refund, chargeback, vendor, partner, reward, country, and liquidity reserves;
- compensation, charity, and promotional funds;
- exposure, coverage, probability, assumptions, aging, and variance;
- funding, commitment, release, utilization, return, payout, settlement, treasury, and GL views; and
- reconciliation, exception, audit, and forecast reporting.

## Rules

Reports identify purpose, owner, beneficiary/exposure, country, entity, currency, source, policy version, and status. Reserve is not called loss, revenue, cash available, payable, or reward value without approved context.
