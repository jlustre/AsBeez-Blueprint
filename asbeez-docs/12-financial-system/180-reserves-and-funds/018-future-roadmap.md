# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/180-reserves-and-funds/018-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve reserve/fund types, purposes, owners, countries, entities, currencies, funding, exposure, and release policies;
- implement funding, commitment, hold, release, utilization, return, expiry, and reconciliation;
- connect refunds, chargebacks, vendor/partner settlement, rewards, wallets, payouts, tax, treasury, and GL;
- implement reporting, APIs, events, security, audit, and exception operations; and
- establish liquidity and program-liability monitoring.

## Medium Term

- automate exposure measurement, reserve recommendations, release, and provider/bank reconciliation;
- support localized fund and statutory restrictions;
- improve liquidity, refund, chargeback, reward, vendor, partner, and payout forecasting; and
- add explainable AI assistance with human approval.

## Long Term

- support regional reserve/fund services and high-volume event processing;
- provide governed treasury, vendor, partner, charity, and program integrations;
- strengthen scenario planning and risk controls; and
- preserve portable funding and utilization evidence across jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No reserve or fund feature may hold, release, utilize, transfer, or report value without approved purpose, owner, source, exposure, currency, entity, policy, authority, reconciliation, and audit rules.
