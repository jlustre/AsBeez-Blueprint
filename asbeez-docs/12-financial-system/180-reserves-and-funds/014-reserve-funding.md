# Reserve Funding

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/180-reserves-and-funds/014-reserve-funding.md

---

## Purpose

Reserve funding records approved value allocated into a reserve or designated fund from company cash, marketplace allocation, revenue, settlement, compensation budget, promotion budget, treasury transfer, or another lawful source.

## Required Data

Reserve/fund, source account, amount/currency, country/entity, purpose/exposure, transfer reference, approval, effective time, policy, destination account, and reconciliation status.

## Rules

Funding cannot be fabricated from an accounting reclassification or participant registration. Source ownership, restrictions, tax, entity, currency, and permitted use are explicit. Funding does not create reward, vendor, partner, customer, or payout value until a separate approved allocation event.
