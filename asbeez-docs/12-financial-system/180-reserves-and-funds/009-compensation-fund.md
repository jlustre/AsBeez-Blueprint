# Compensation Fund

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/180-reserves-and-funds/009-compensation-fund.md

---

## Purpose

The Compensation Fund is a designated pool for approved partner, member, vendor, referral, or program compensation under a defined funding source and jurisdictional plan.

## Rules

Funding cannot come from participant registration or recruitment alone without lawful qualifying value. The fund has purpose, beneficiary class, country/entity, currency, budget, cap, commitment, approval, payout, return, and expiry rules. It is separate from reward quantities, vendor payable, partner payable, revenue, and operating cash.

## Controls

Commitments and payouts reconcile to commission/reward subledgers, settlement, payout, fund GL accounts, and approval evidence. Overspend, unapproved transfer, or funding shortfall blocks use.
