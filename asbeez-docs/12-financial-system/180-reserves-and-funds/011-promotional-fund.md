# Promotional Fund

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/180-reserves-and-funds/011-promotional-fund.md

---

## Purpose

The Promotional Fund finances approved discounts, campaigns, credits, loyalty promotions, vendor acquisition, customer acquisition, or marketplace growth programs.

## Rules

Each program has owner, budget, funding source, country/entity, currency, eligibility, duration, cap, measurement, tax, vendor/partner allocation, reward effect, approval, and expiry/return policy. A promotional fund is not revenue, customer cash, reward liability, or vendor payable until a separate event creates that effect.

## Controls

Track budgeted, committed, used, reversed, expired, and remaining amounts. Promotion performance, refunds, chargebacks, fraud, and reward effects reconcile to fund, order, vendor/partner, and GL records.
