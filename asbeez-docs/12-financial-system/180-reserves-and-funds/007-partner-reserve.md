# Partner Reserve

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/180-reserves-and-funds/007-partner-reserve.md

---

## Purpose

The Partner Reserve restricts partner entitlement against documented refund, chargeback, attribution, fraud, service, tax, or settlement exposure.

## Rules

The reserve remains attributable to the partner and program. It is not a commission, revenue, confiscation, or realized loss. Release or utilization requires approved policy, evidence, funding, and reconciliation to partner settlement and payout.

## Required Detail

Partner/program, source population, exposure, basis, rate/amount, currency, country/entity, funding, review/release condition, owner, approval, utilization, and GL/subledger references.

## Monitoring

Track reserved, held, released, utilized, expired, and outstanding amounts by partner, program, exposure type, settlement period, country, entity, currency, and policy version.
