## Purpose

Reserves and Funds govern designated value held against a defined exposure or purpose, including operating, refund, chargeback, vendor, partner, reward, compensation, charity, promotional, country, and liquidity needs.

## Boundary

A reserve protects against an exposure and is measured against a population or risk. A fund is a designated pool with an approved purpose, funding source, commitment, and use policy. Neither is automatically revenue, free cash, a payable, a reward, or a guarantee of availability.

## Principles

- purpose, owner, beneficiary/exposure, currency, country, entity, funding, amount, and release/use rules are explicit;
- reserves and funds remain separate from customer/vendor/partner/reward balances unless a controlled event allocates them;
- funding, commitment, release, utilization, expiry, and return are immutable events;
- release or use requires evidence, authority, policy, and reconciliation; and
- balances are reported by designated pool, exposure, entity, currency, and source.
