# Country Reserve

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/180-reserves-and-funds/012-country-reserve.md

---

## Purpose

The Country Reserve protects or designates value for jurisdiction-specific tax, regulatory, settlement, liquidity, consumer, vendor, partner, reward, or operational exposure.

## Rules

Country reserve requires legal entity, authority/jurisdiction, currency, source, exposure, target, funding, release/use, owner, and effective policy. It cannot be created as an unexplained country surcharge or used for another jurisdiction without approved intercompany/transfer treatment.

## Reporting

Report funded, committed, available, utilized, released, returned, and outstanding amounts by country, entity, currency, purpose, authority, and policy version.
