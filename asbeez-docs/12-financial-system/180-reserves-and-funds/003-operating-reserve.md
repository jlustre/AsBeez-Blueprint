# Operating Reserve

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/180-reserves-and-funds/003-operating-reserve.md

---

## Purpose

The Operating Reserve protects AsBeez liquidity and continuity for approved operating, regulatory, provider, technology, security, and emergency exposures.

## Rules

The reserve has board/Finance-approved target, minimum, funding source, entity, currency, access authority, and release/use conditions. It is not vendor, partner, customer, reward, charity, or promotional money. Use requires documented exposure, approval, and subsequent reconciliation.

## Reporting

Report target, funded, committed, available, utilized, replenishment, and variance by entity, currency, country, purpose, and policy period.

## Lifecycle

```text
Proposed -> Approved -> Funded -> Available -> Committed -> Utilized/Released
```

Funding and use require authorized treasury movement, evidence, and GL/treasury reconciliation.
