## Reference Authority

Reference documents summarize and connect authoritative Financial System domains; they do not replace accounting policies, General Ledger records, legal/compliance requirements, API/event contracts, configuration, or operating procedures. Conflicts are resolved in favor of the authoritative source and recorded for correction.
