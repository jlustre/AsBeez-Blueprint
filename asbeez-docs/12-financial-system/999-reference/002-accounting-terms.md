# Accounting Terms

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/999-reference/002-accounting-terms.md

---

## Purpose

These terms explain the accounting concepts used by AsBeez. They are reference guidance; the General Ledger, accounting policies, tax rules, and legal requirements govern implementation.

## Terms

- **Asset:** controlled resource expected to provide future benefit.
- **Liability:** present obligation or amount owed.
- **Equity:** residual interest after liabilities.
- **Revenue:** recognized earning under applicable policy; distinct from cash, GMV, or gross billings.
- **Expense:** recognized consumption/cost under applicable policy.
- **Debit/Credit:** the two sides of double-entry recording; meaning depends on account class and normal balance.
- **Accrual:** recognized effect before cash movement when criteria are met.
- **Deferral:** postponement of recognition until criteria/time are met.
- **Unearned/deferred revenue:** consideration received or billed before recognition.
- **Trial balance:** account-level debit/credit listing used to validate equality.
- **Materiality:** significance threshold for decision, control, review, or disclosure.
- **Realized/unrealized FX:** gain/loss treatment based on settlement or valuation policy.

## AsBeez Distinctions

Customer payment is not automatically revenue; vendor/partner entitlement is not automatically AsBeez revenue; tax collected/withheld is not revenue; reserves are not free cash; settlement is not payout; and projections are not posted actuals.
