## Purpose

This glossary provides concise, AsBeez-specific meanings. Numbered domain documents remain authoritative when a definition is detailed or policy-bearing.

## Core Terms

- **General Ledger (GL):** authoritative record of posted monetary effects.
- **Subledger:** domain-level detail reconciled to a GL control account.
- **Journal:** balanced accounting header with immutable posted lines.
- **Control account:** GL account that summarizes a reconciled subledger.
- **Posted:** validated, approved, balanced, and immutable accounting state.
- **Compensating entry:** new entry that corrects an earlier effect without deleting it.
- **GMV:** gross merchandise value; not automatically revenue.
- **Recognized revenue:** revenue recorded under applicable accounting policy.
- **Reserve:** restricted value held for an approved exposure or purpose.
- **Settlement:** determination/allocation of amounts owed; not necessarily payout.
- **Payout:** disbursement to an approved recipient/destination.
- **RP/ABC/AHC:** AsBeez reward units with business/accounting treatment distinct from money unless approved conversion occurs.
- **Provider evidence:** external observation retained until mapped and reconciled.
- **Idempotency:** retry protection that limits an operation to one financial effect per scope.
- **Effective-dated policy:** version applied from a defined time without rewriting history.

## Authority Rule

When this glossary conflicts with a domain policy, accounting rule, legal requirement, or ledger record, the authoritative source governs and the glossary must be corrected.
