## Purpose

Common answers for users of the AsBeez Financial System. Detailed policy and domain documents govern where an answer requires authority.

## Questions

**Is GMV revenue?** No. Revenue follows recognition policy.

**Is payment capture revenue?** Not automatically; allocation, tax, entitlement, and recognition apply.

**Are RP/ABC/AHC money?** No, unless approved conversion creates a separate monetary effect.

**Can posted journals be edited?** No. Use reversal or compensating entries.

**Can a provider callback prove success?** No. Store evidence and reconcile.

**Can support or AI release money?** No. Authorized workflows and approvals are required.

**Can reserves be spent as cash?** No. They are restricted for approved purposes.

**What happens when a source disagrees?** Create an exception and reconcile; do not hide or manually balance it.

**Can a closed period be reopened?** Only under documented authority with auditable correction and re-close.

**What is the first troubleshooting step?** Preserve correlation/source/scope, inspect authoritative state, and use the approved runbook.
