## Purpose

The Chart of Accounts reference summarizes account classes, hierarchy, numbering, normal balance, control mappings, dimensions, scope, and versioning.

## Account Classes

Assets, liabilities, equity, revenue, expenses, contra accounts, and memorandum accounts. Account code/name/class/normal balance/parent/chart version are unique and effective-dated within the applicable entity/country scope.

## Dimensions and Controls

Use approved dimensions such as entity, country, currency, vendor, partner, member, wallet, order, payment, tax, reserve, product, channel, and region. Wallet, reward, payment, payout, vendor, partner, tax, reserve, treasury, and provider subledgers map to control accounts and reconcile to the GL.

## Rules

Accounts used by posted history cannot be deleted or repurposed. Mapping changes require accounting authority, impact analysis, tests, effective date, approval, and report/reconciliation review.
