## Purpose

This reference summarizes accounting, tax, AML/KYC/KYB, sanctions, source-of-funds, privacy, country/entity, retention, approval, audit, and reporting compliance concepts.

## Core Expectations

Risk-based controls, local registrations and policy, screening/monitoring, identity/business verification, source-of-funds review, separation of duties, approvals, evidence, retention/legal holds, appeals, incident reporting, independent testing, and policy versioning.

## Rules

Provider/AI output is evidence or recommendation unless an approved deterministic control applies. Restricted activity becomes hold/review/exception rather than silent approval. Tax collected/withheld is not revenue; historical decisions/evidence are not overwritten; local statutory obligations remain distinct from consolidation.
