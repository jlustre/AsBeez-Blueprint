# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/350-observability/017-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- establish financial, business, technical, logging, tracing, correlation, dashboards, alerting, SLO, incident, and domain-monitoring standards;
- instrument ledger, payments, payouts, reconciliation, close, events, outbox, projections, integrations, providers, and security; and
- create runbooks, ownership, escalation, control-total alerts, provider-unknown workflows, and incident evidence practices.

## Medium Term

- add continuous reconciliation/close observability, financial data quality, anomaly/drift detection, dependency maps, SLO/error budgets, and country/entity dashboards;
- improve trace sampling, privacy-preserving analytics, capacity forecasting, recovery exercises, and automated safe operational responses; and
- link observability to audit, compliance, risk, support, and management reporting.

## Long Term

- provide cross-region financial control towers and predictive operational risk analysis;
- support portable, context-aware observability across extracted services and providers; and
- preserve auditable lineage, financial correctness, privacy, and human accountability through automation.

## Roadmap Gate

No observability capability may hide uncertainty, weaken financial controls, expose sensitive data, suppress audit evidence, or create unauthorized financial effects.
