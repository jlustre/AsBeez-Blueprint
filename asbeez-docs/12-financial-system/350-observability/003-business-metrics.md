# Business Metrics

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/350-observability/003-business-metrics.md

---

## Purpose

Business metrics show commerce and stakeholder outcomes while preserving the distinction between GMV, customer payment, recognized revenue, vendor/partner entitlement, rewards, tax, and cash.

## Metric Families

Orders and GMV, net/platform/vendor/partner revenue, subscription/service/advertising revenue, payment conversion, refunds, disputes, payout completion, wallet activity, reward issuance/redemption, vendor/partner earnings, customer/member balances, fees, commissions, country performance, retention, and forecast variance.

## Rules

Every metric defines numerator, denominator, source, population, time basis, currency/FX, entity/country, exclusions, freshness, and certification. Business metrics do not imply accounting recognition or legal performance without the relevant financial source and policy.
