> **Document:** 12-financial-system/350-observability/014-dashboards.md

---

## Purpose

Dashboards provide role-based views for finance, treasury, operations, payments, payouts, reconciliation, close, risk, compliance, audit, engineering, and management.

## Dashboard Rules

Every panel identifies source, metric definition, period, entity/country/currency, FX/tax basis, freshness, certification, filter, owner, and drill-through lineage. Dashboards distinguish actual, posted, projected, forecast, pending, provider-evidence, exception, and uncertified states.

## Access and Action

Dashboards enforce resource, entity, country, privacy, and role scope. They are read-only; links to action create separately authorized commands with current validation, idempotency, approval, and audit. Export permissions, recipients, retention, and delivery are recorded.
