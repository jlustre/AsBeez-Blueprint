# Incident Management

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/350-observability/016-incident-management.md

---

## Purpose

Incident management coordinates detection, triage, containment, recovery, communication, reconciliation, evidence preservation, and learning for financial, technical, provider, security, compliance, and data incidents.

## Incident Requirements

Record severity, scope, entity/country/currency/period, affected services/providers, first detection, correlation/trace, financial/customer/regulatory impact, owner, timeline, actions, approvals, communications, decision log, evidence, and recovery status.

## Financial Response

Containment may pause traffic, queue work, isolate a provider, or place controlled operational holds under authority. Do not edit posted records, retry uncertain provider effects blindly, suppress exceptions, or declare recovery before reconciliation, control-total comparison, security/compliance review, and stakeholder approval.

## Closure

Close only after service recovery, financial reconciliation, customer/vendor/partner impact assessment, required reporting, evidence retention, root-cause analysis, corrective actions, and owner acceptance. Track post-incident tests and remediation.
