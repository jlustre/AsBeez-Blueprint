# Close Monitoring

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/350-observability/012-close-monitoring.md

---

## Purpose

Close monitoring makes period readiness, task completion, reconciliation, accruals, deferrals, adjustments, approvals, exceptions, reports, and certification visible.

## Signals

Track open/soft-closed/locked/closed status, checklist age, journal/posting exceptions, reconciliation completion, unresolved differences, accrual/deferral status, trial balance, control totals, report freshness, approval/segregation of duties, task owner, and close duration by entity/country/period.

## Response

Alerts identify blockers, missing evidence, overdue tasks, and material risk. AI or automation may summarize and prioritize; authorized finance owners decide adjustments, period lock/reopen, close approval, and certification. Monitoring cannot waive controls or declare close complete.
