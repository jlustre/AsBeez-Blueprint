# Financial Dashboards

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/017-financial-dashboards.md

---

## Purpose

Financial dashboards present role-appropriate metrics for Finance, Treasury, Operations, vendors, partners, leadership, compliance, and support.

## Dashboard Families

- executive revenue, profitability, liquidity, country, and risk;
- finance trial balance, close, reconciliation, tax, reserves, and reporting;
- treasury cash, provider, payout, refund, FX, and liquidity;
- vendor/partner earnings, settlements, commissions, and exceptions;
- rewards liability, wallet, payment, refund, dispute, and anomaly; and
- budget/forecast/actual, variance, scenario, and operational health.

## Rules

Each tile states metric definition, source, freshness, period, currency, entity, country, assumptions, and certification. Access is role-scoped. Dashboards do not write balances, certify reports, close periods, or authorize financial movement.
