# Refund Analytics

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/012-refund-analytics.md

---

## Purpose

Refund analytics measures request, eligibility, approval, amount, method, timing, return/cancellation, provider execution, wallet credit, credit note, vendor/partner adjustment, reward reversal, fraud, and reconciliation.

## Rules

Refund rate, amount, cost, and time-to-completion are calculated from immutable refund/payment/order records. Failed, pending, held, returned, and completed outcomes remain distinct; refund metrics do not automatically represent customer loss, vendor fault, revenue reversal, or reward liability.

## Measures

Track refund request/approval/completion rate, amount, reason, line/category, time-to-refund, method, wallet/credit-note use, vendor/partner adjustment, reward reversal, fraud review, and reconciliation exception.
