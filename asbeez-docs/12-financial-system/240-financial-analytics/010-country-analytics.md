# Country Analytics

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/010-country-analytics.md

---

## Purpose

Country analytics measures financial performance, cash, liquidity, tax, reserves, payments, payouts, vendors, partners, rewards, FX, compliance, and reconciliation by country and legal entity.

## Rules

Local currency, entity, tax authority, policy, bank/provider, regulatory status, and reporting mapping remain visible. Consolidated or converted values do not replace local source values or statutory evidence.

## Measures

Track country revenue/GMV, costs, cash, liquidity, tax, reserves, payouts, refunds, disputes, vendors, partners, rewards, FX, compliance, reconciliation, and forecast variance.

Country metrics retain local source currency and statutory/reporting policy before any consolidated conversion.
