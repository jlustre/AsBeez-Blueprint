# Payment Analytics

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/011-payment-analytics.md

---

## Purpose

Payment analytics measures intent, authorization, capture, failure, retry, refund, dispute, fee, settlement, provider, method, country, currency, and reconciliation performance.

## Rules

Authorization, capture, provider settlement, order completion, revenue, vendor/partner settlement, and rewards are separate states. Metrics label provider, attempt, amount, currency, timing, unknown outcomes, exceptions, and source freshness.

## Measures

Track authorization/capture success, failure/retry, latency, provider fee, settlement delay, refund/dispute rate, payment method, country, currency, provider concentration, and reconciliation exceptions.
