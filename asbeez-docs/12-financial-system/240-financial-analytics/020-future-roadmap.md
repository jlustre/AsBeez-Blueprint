# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/020-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve metric definitions, sources, dimensions, periods, entities, countries, currencies, and data governance;
- implement KPI, revenue, profitability, cash, wallet, reward, vendor/partner, country, payment, refund, dispute, tax, forecast, and anomaly analytics;
- implement dashboards, API, events, snapshots, exports, access, and audit; and
- connect reconciled reporting, budgets, treasury, reserves, rewards, settlements, and close.

## Medium Term

- automate data freshness, metric certification, anomaly triage, and forecast evaluation;
- support governed cohorts, scenario comparisons, country/entity analytics, and partner/vendor insights; and
- add explainable AI assistance with human review.

## Long Term

- support regional analytics services and high-volume financial data;
- provide governed partner, auditor, regulatory, and operational integrations;
- strengthen causal, risk, liquidity, reward-liability, and profitability modeling; and
- preserve portable metric definitions and evidence across systems.

## Roadmap Gate

No analytics feature may present an estimate as actual, a program quantity as money, a dashboard as a certified statement, or an anomaly as proven fault without definition, source, reconciliation, confidence, access, and audit controls.
