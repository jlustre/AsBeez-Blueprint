# Revenue Analytics

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/003-revenue-analytics.md

---

## Purpose

Revenue analytics explains GMV, customer consideration, platform fees, subscriptions, services, advertising, recognized revenue, deferred/accrued/unearned revenue, refunds, chargebacks, tax, vendor/partner obligations, and reward financial effects.

## Rules

GMV, customer collections, vendor/partner proceeds, platform revenue, tax, and reward quantities are separate measures. Analytics use reconciled order/invoice/payment/settlement/GL sources and disclose principal/agent treatment, period, currency, entity, country, policy, estimates, and exceptions.

## Metrics

Track GMV, orders, recognized gross/net revenue, platform fees, subscriptions, services, advertising, deferred/accrued/unearned revenue, refunds, chargebacks, revenue per order/customer/vendor, and reconciliation status by product, category, vendor, partner, channel, country, entity, currency, and period.
