# Financial KPIs

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/002-financial-kpis.md

---

## Purpose

Financial KPIs measure marketplace scale, financial performance, liquidity, risk, obligations, cost, and operating health using defined, reproducible calculations.

## KPI Families

- GMV, orders, customers, vendors, partners, conversion, and retention;
- recognized revenue, fees, costs, margin, and profitability;
- customer collections, vendor/partner payable, commissions, rewards, reserves, tax, and payouts;
- cash, liquidity, provider settlement, refunds, chargebacks, FX, and treasury;
- budget/forecast/actual, variance, close, reconciliation, and exception aging; and
- payment success, refund rate, dispute rate, tax timeliness, and operational SLA.

## KPI Contract

Every KPI defines numerator, denominator, exclusions, source, grain, time basis, currency, entity, country, dimensions, policy/model version, freshness, and certification status. GMV is not revenue; RP/ABC/AHC are not monetary KPI values without approved classification.
