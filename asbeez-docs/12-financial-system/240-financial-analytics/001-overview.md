## Purpose

Financial Analytics derives decision-support metrics, trends, forecasts, anomalies, dashboards, and insights from reconciled financial and operational data across the AsBeez ecosystem.

## AsBeez Boundary

Analytics are derived views, not General Ledger truth, certified financial statements, wallet balances, payable obligations, or cash authorization. GMV, reward quantities, vendor/partner activity, forecasts, and estimates must not be presented as revenue, cash, profit, income, or guaranteed outcome.

## Principles

- every metric has definition, source, grain, period, entity, country, currency, dimensions, policy/version, and freshness;
- certified reports and reconciled sources are preferred for financial facts;
- analytics preserve non-monetary populations and monetary control totals;
- estimates and model outputs disclose assumptions, confidence, and limitations; and
- insights cannot edit source records, close exceptions, authorize funds, or bypass controls.
