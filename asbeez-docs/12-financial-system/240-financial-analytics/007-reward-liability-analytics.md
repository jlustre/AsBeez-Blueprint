# Reward Liability Analytics

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/007-reward-liability-analytics.md

---

## Purpose

Reward liability analytics measures approved reward obligations, expense, reserve, redemption, wallet, payout, expiry, forfeiture, reversal, and outstanding exposure separately from RP/ABC/AHC quantities.

## Rules

No monetary liability is inferred from reward quantity, ABC creation, matrix placement, or AHC display. Analytics state legal/accounting classification, qualifying source, funding, country/entity/currency, policy, assumptions, liability ceiling, reserve coverage, and reconciliation status.

## Measures

Track program quantities, classified liability, expense, reserve target/coverage, redeemed, released, reversed, expired, forfeited, wallet credit, payout, and outstanding exposure by program, country, entity, member class, and period.
