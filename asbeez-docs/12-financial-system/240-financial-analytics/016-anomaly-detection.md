# Anomaly Detection

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/016-anomaly-detection.md

---

## Purpose

Anomaly detection identifies unusual financial, operational, reconciliation, payment, refund, dispute, tax, reward, vendor/partner, reserve, treasury, FX, or reporting patterns for review.

## Signals

Duplicate/missing activity, amount/volume spikes, rate/FX outliers, attribution mismatch, unusual refunds/chargebacks, payout velocity, reward liability, tax variance, reserve coverage, provider/bank differences, and stale data.

## Rules

Anomaly is a review signal, not proof of fraud, liability, accounting error, or policy breach. Each alert includes data scope, baseline, threshold/model, confidence, reason, owner, action, and resolution. Analytics cannot edit source or close exceptions automatically.
