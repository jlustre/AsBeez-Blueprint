# Forecast Analytics

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/015-forecast-analytics.md

---

## Purpose

Forecast analytics evaluates budget, forecast, actual, scenario, confidence, assumptions, variance, and model performance for revenue, costs, cash, reserves, rewards, vendor/partner obligations, tax, FX, and liquidity.

## Rules

Forecast values are not actual revenue, cash, profit, payable, or guarantee. Each metric states source, model/version, assumptions, confidence, freshness, currency, entity, country, scenario, and reconciliation to actuals.

## Measures

Track forecast accuracy, bias, confidence, revenue/expense/cash/liquidity variance, reserve and reward-liability forecast, FX sensitivity, payout/refund timing, scenario spread, and assumption stability.
