# Vendor Analytics

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/008-vendor-analytics.md

---

## Purpose

Vendor analytics measures vendor sales, earnings, fees, taxes, reserves, holds, refunds, chargebacks, settlement, payout, margin, and exception health.

## Rules

Vendor sales/entitlement are not automatically AsBeez revenue. Analytics derive from reconciled order, vendor subledger, settlement, payout, tax, refund, dispute, and GL data and label estimated, held, payable, paid, reversed, and disputed states.

## Measures

Track GMV, gross/net earnings, fees, tax, reserves, holds, refunds, chargebacks, settlement time, payout time, outstanding payable, margin, exceptions, and vendor concentration.

Each metric states reconciled source, freshness, policy version, and estimated versus certified status.
