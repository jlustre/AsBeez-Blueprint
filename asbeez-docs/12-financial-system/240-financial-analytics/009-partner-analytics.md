# Partner Analytics

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/009-partner-analytics.md

---

## Purpose

Partner analytics measures qualifying activity, attribution, commissions, bonuses, incentives, fees, tax, reserves, holds, settlement, payout, retention, and program performance.

## Rules

Registration, referral, genealogy, or participant count is not payable or revenue evidence. Analytics disclose program, source, attribution, funding, qualification, country/entity/currency, policy, and reconciliation status.

## Measures

Track qualified activity, attribution rate, commission/bonus/incentive, funding, reserve, hold, settlement, payout, reversal, retention, concentration, exceptions, and program variance.

Each metric states source activity, program version, currency, entity/country, confidence, and reconciliation status.
