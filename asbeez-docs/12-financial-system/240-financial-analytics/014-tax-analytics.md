# Tax Analytics

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/014-tax-analytics.md

---

## Purpose

Tax analytics measures assessments, taxable basis, rates, exemptions, registrations, withholding, filings, remittances, payable/recoverable balances, adjustments, and exceptions.

## Rules

Tax analytics remains separate from revenue, fees, vendor/partner payable, rewards, and cash. Metrics identify jurisdiction, authority, entity, country, currency, period, rule/provider version, filing/remittance status, reconciliation, and evidence.

## Measures

Track taxable basis, assessed/collected/withheld/payable/recoverable/remitted, exemption, registration, filing timeliness, amendment, exception, and tax variance by authority and period.

Tax metrics retain assessment evidence, rule/provider version, jurisdiction, currency, entity, filing status, and reconciliation certification.
