# Cash Flow Analytics

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/005-cash-flow-analytics.md

---

## Purpose

Cash-flow analytics explains confirmed and projected inflows/outflows, liquidity, timing, currency, reserves, refunds, chargebacks, taxes, payouts, settlements, provider balances, and treasury exposure.

## Rules

Confirmed cash, restricted/reserved cash, provider funds in transit, expected cash, forecast cash, wallet balances, and payables are separate measures. Analytics use treasury/bank/provider/reconciliation evidence and disclose freshness, assumptions, currency, entity, country, and scenario.

## Measures

Track operating/investing/financing inflows and outflows, liquidity gap, reserve coverage, payout/refund/tax timing, provider settlement, FX, forecast variance, and scenario sensitivity.
