# Wallet Analytics

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/006-wallet-analytics.md

---

## Purpose

Wallet analytics measures monetary wallet balances, pending/held/reserved/restricted values, movements, transfers, refunds, redemptions, payouts, exceptions, and program-only RP/ABC/AHC quantities.

## Rules

Monetary wallet analytics reconcile to wallet subledger and GL controls. RP, ABC, and AHC quantities are reported separately from cash, available balance, payable, income, or payout. Reports disclose wallet type, unit/currency, owner class, country, entity, freshness, and reconciliation status.

## Measures

Track credits, debits, holds, reservations, restricted value, available value, refunds, transfers, redemptions, payouts, aging, exceptions, and monetary versus program-only quantities.
