# Profitability Analytics

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/004-profitability-analytics.md

---

## Purpose

Profitability analytics evaluates recognized revenue, direct costs, provider/payment fees, vendor/partner allocations, reward costs, refunds, chargebacks, tax effects, reserves, operating expense, and contribution by product, vendor, partner, country, channel, and program.

## Rules

Profitability views state cost/revenue basis, allocation method, period, currency, entity, country, assumptions, and reconciliation status. GMV, forecast margin, vendor sales, partner commission, reserve, reward quantity, and cash are not profit without approved accounting basis.

## Measures

Contribution, gross margin, net margin, cost-to-serve, acquisition cost, vendor/partner economics, reward cost, refund/chargeback cost, reserve coverage, and variance are reported with allocation drivers and sensitivity limits.
