# Chargeback Analytics

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/240-financial-analytics/013-chargeback-analytics.md

---

## Purpose

Chargeback analytics measures cases, exposure, evidence, deadlines, reserves, fees, vendor/partner/platform liability, outcomes, recoveries, loss, and reconciliation.

## Rules

Case count is not realized loss; reserve is not final liability; vendor exposure is not automatically vendor fault. Analytics label provider, reason, amount/currency, age, entity/country, reserve basis, liability decision, outcome, and source freshness.

## Measures

Track case volume, rate, amount, exposure, reserve, fee, age, evidence deadline, representment, arbitration, win/loss, recovery, realized loss, vendor/partner/platform allocation, and reconciliation status.
