# Country Financial Report

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/230-financial-reporting/014-country-financial-report.md

---

## Purpose

The Country Financial Report presents local financial position, performance, cash, tax, reserves, vendors, partners, rewards, payouts, FX, and regulatory measures for a country/entity.

## Rules

Local currency, legal entity, tax authority, bank/provider, chart/report mapping, policy, period, and statutory evidence are retained. Global consolidation does not erase local detail or present a converted total as local source currency.

## Certification

Country reports retain local trial balance, statements, tax, cash, reserve, vendor/partner, reward, payout, FX, reconciliation, filing, and approval references.
