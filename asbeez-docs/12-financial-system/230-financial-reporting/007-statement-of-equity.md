# Statement Of Equity

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/230-financial-reporting/007-statement-of-equity.md

---

## Purpose

The statement of equity reports contributed capital, retained results, current-period result, distributions, and other approved equity movements for a legal entity.

## Rules

Member participation, ABC records, wallets, rewards, vendor/partner payables, reserves, and funds are not equity without explicit legal and accounting classification. Closing entries, capital contributions, distributions, and restatements reference approved corporate/accounting evidence and period.

## Certification

The report states entity, period, currency, opening equity, movements, closing equity, closing-entry references, approvals, restatements, and reconciliation status.
