# General Ledger Report

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/230-financial-reporting/008-general-ledger-report.md

---

## Purpose

The General Ledger Report presents posted journal entries, account balances, trial balance, adjustments, reversals, periods, dimensions, and audit references.

## Required Filters

Entity, country, currency, period, account/chart version, journal type, source context, dimensions, posting status, and reconciliation/certification state.

## Rules

Reports derive from immutable posted entries and identify draft/approved/posted/reversed states. Ledger reports do not silently include projections, wallet snapshots, provider balances, reward quantities, or budget values as posted accounting.

## Certification

The report states query scope, period status, account/chart version, dimensions, source snapshot, adjustments, reconciliation status, reviewer, approver, and export integrity.
