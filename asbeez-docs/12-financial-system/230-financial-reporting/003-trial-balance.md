# Trial Balance

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/230-financial-reporting/003-trial-balance.md

---

## Purpose

The trial balance reports posted debit and credit totals by account, entity, country, currency, period, chart version, and dimensions.

## Required Checks

- total debits equal total credits;
- opening plus posted activity equals closing;
- subledger control accounts agree;
- clearing, suspense, intercompany, tax, reserves, wallets, vendor/partner, reward, payment, and treasury accounts are reviewed; and
- adjustments and closing entries are included with approval.

## Rules

Trial balance is generated from immutable posted entries and reconciled control totals. A balanced trial balance does not prove complete source attribution, tax correctness, reward lineage, vendor/partner allocation, or cash reconciliation.
