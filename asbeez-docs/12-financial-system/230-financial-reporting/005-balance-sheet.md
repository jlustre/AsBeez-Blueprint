# Balance Sheet

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/230-financial-reporting/005-balance-sheet.md

---

## Purpose

The balance sheet reports assets, liabilities, equity, and approved classifications at a period end for a legal entity and reporting scope.

## AsBeez Components

Cash/provider receivables, customer/vendor/partner receivables/payables, tax, reward liabilities, wallets, reserves, deferred revenue, settlement clearing, FX, fixed/intangible assets, and equity are separately classified and reconciled.

## Rules

Member/vendor/partner displayed balances are not assets or liabilities without approved accounting treatment. Restricted/reserved cash is separately identified. Report includes currency, entity, country, period, chart version, reconciliation status, and certification.
