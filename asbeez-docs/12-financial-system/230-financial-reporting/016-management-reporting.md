# Management Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/230-financial-reporting/016-management-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Management reporting supports decisions about marketplace growth, vendors, partners, rewards, country expansion, liquidity, reserves, costs, revenue, and risk.

## Report Families

- GMV, customers, vendors, partners, orders, conversion, retention, and acquisition;
- recognized revenue, fees, costs, margins, vendor/partner obligations, rewards, tax, reserves, and cash;
- budget/forecast/actual, variance, scenario, liquidity, FX, payout, refund, chargeback, and treasury; and
- risk, compliance, reconciliation, operational performance, and exception dashboards.

## Rules

Management reports may include estimates and operational metrics but label source, freshness, assumptions, confidence, currency, entity, country, and reconciliation status. GMV is not revenue; reward quantities are not cash; forecasts are not guarantees.
