# Report AI Capabilities

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/230-financial-reporting/022-report-ai-capabilities.md

---

## Purpose

AI may assist reporting with narrative explanation, anomaly detection, variance analysis, forecast interpretation, reconciliation summaries, and report discovery while source data, accounting policy, and certification controls remain authoritative.

## Permitted Assistance

- explain report changes and variances;
- identify anomalies, missing data, stale snapshots, and exceptions;
- summarize reconciliations, close status, tax, treasury, reserves, rewards, vendors, partners, and FX;
- suggest report dimensions and scenario comparisons; and
- answer evidence-linked questions with provenance.

## Prohibited Actions

AI must not invent financial facts, alter source/ledger/report values, certify reports, submit regulatory filings, hide exceptions, or present estimates as actual revenue, cash, profit, payable, or income.

## Governance

Outputs include model/version, data scope, source snapshot, confidence, reason, provenance, policy context, limitations, and human approval where material. Sensitive financial and personal data is minimized and access-controlled.
