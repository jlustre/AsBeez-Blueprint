# Income Statement

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/230-financial-reporting/004-income-statement.md

---

## Purpose

The income statement reports recognized AsBeez revenue, costs, expenses, gains/losses, and result for a defined period and reporting scope.

## AsBeez Presentation

Separate marketplace/platform revenue, subscriptions, services, advertising, fees, payment/provider costs, rewards, refunds, chargebacks, operations, tax effects, FX gains/losses, and other approved categories. GMV, vendor/partner proceeds, tax collected, customer payments, RP/ABC/AHC, and reserves are not automatically revenue or expense.

## Rules

Revenue follows performance-obligation and principal/agent policy. Report includes entity, country, currency, period, chart/policy version, reconciled source, estimates, and exceptions. Amendments reference prior certified reports.
