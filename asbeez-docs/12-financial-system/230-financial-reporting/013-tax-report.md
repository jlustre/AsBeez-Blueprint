# Tax Report

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/230-financial-reporting/013-tax-report.md

---

## Purpose

The Tax Report presents assessments, tax lines, exemptions, registrations, withholding, payable/recoverable amounts, filings, remittances, amendments, and exceptions by authority and period.

## Rules

Tax is not AsBeez revenue, vendor/partner payable, reward value, or fee. Report includes jurisdiction, authority, entity, country, currency, basis, rate, policy version, filing/remittance status, reconciliation, and evidence.

## Certification

The report retains assessment population, exemption/registration evidence, invoice and withholding lines, filing/remittance references, amendments, reviewer, approver, and reconciliation status.
