# Vendor Settlement Report

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/230-financial-reporting/011-vendor-settlement-report.md

---

## Purpose

The Vendor Settlement Report presents vendor gross sales, entitlement, fees, tax, partner allocation, reserves, holds, refunds, chargebacks, adjustments, approved settlement, payout, and outstanding payable.

## Rules

Vendor sales and entitlement are not automatically AsBeez revenue. Report distinguishes estimated, calculated, held, approved, payable, paid, reversed, disputed, and exception values by vendor, order, country, entity, currency, and policy.

## Certification

The report retains settlement batch, vendor subledger, payout, tax, reserve/hold, refund/chargeback, reconciliation, period, and approval references.
