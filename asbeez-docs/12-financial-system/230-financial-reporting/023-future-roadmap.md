# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/230-financial-reporting/023-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve report definitions, dimensions, periods, entities, countries, currencies, policies, certification, and access;
- implement financial statements, GL/subledger, wallet, reward, vendor/partner, tax, country, treasury, reserve, and reconciliation reports;
- implement management/regulatory reports, snapshots, scheduling, exports, API, events, security, and audit; and
- connect close, budgets, forecasts, actuals, exceptions, and source evidence.

## Medium Term

- automate reconciled dataset refresh, certification, report delivery, and amendment workflows;
- support localized statutory and multi-entity consolidation reporting;
- improve operational dashboards and variance/forecast explanations; and
- add explainable AI assistance with human approval.

## Long Term

- support regional reporting services and high-volume certified snapshots;
- provide governed regulator, auditor, vendor, partner, and operational integrations;
- strengthen continuous reporting and anomaly detection; and
- preserve portable report/source/certification evidence across jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No report may be certified, exported as authoritative, submitted to a regulator, or presented as actual financial performance without approved definition, source, reconciliation, period, policy, access, certification, and audit controls.
