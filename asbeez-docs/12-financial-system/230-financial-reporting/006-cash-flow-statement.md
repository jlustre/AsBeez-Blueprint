# Cash Flow Statement

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/230-financial-reporting/006-cash-flow-statement.md

---

## Purpose

The cash flow statement reports cash and cash-equivalent movements by operating, investing, and financing activity for a defined entity, country, currency, and period.

## AsBeez Components

Customer/provider collections, vendor/partner payouts, refunds, chargebacks, taxes, reserves, payment/provider fees, treasury transfers, FX, capital spending, financing, and restricted cash are separately identified.

## Rules

Wallet display balances, reward quantities, vendor/partner payables, reserves, and provider balances are not cash without approved custody and reconciliation. Cash flow uses reconciled bank/provider evidence and discloses restricted, in-transit, forecast, and FX effects.
