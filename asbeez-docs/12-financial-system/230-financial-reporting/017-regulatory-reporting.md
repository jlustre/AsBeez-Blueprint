# Regulatory Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/230-financial-reporting/017-regulatory-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Regulatory reporting produces jurisdiction-specific financial, tax, payment, consumer, AML/KYC, sanctions, privacy, reserve, payout, and corporate reports from approved evidence.

## Rules

Each report has authority, form/schema, entity, country, period, currency, population, policy/rule version, preparation, review, approval, submission, amendment, retention, and evidence. Regulatory output is never generated from unapproved estimates without clear designation and legal approval.

## Controls

Access, export, filing, correction, legal hold, retention, and submission evidence are audited. Local reports remain traceable to source/ledger/reconciliation records and global reports.
