## Purpose

Financial Reporting transforms reconciled ledger, subledger, operational, treasury, tax, budget, and forecast data into certified, statutory, regulatory, management, and operational reports.

## AsBeez Boundary

Certified financial reports derive from immutable posted ledger records and reconciled source data. Management and forecast reports may include estimates, GMV, scenarios, pending obligations, or provisional values, but must label them clearly and never present them as actual revenue, cash, profit, payable, or guaranteed income.

## Principles

- every report has definition, scope, period, entity, country, currency, dimensions, source, policy/version, and certification status;
- GMV, customer collections, recognized revenue, vendor/partner obligations, tax, rewards, reserves, cash, and profit are distinct;
- reports are reproducible and preserve chart, account, policy, and rate versions;
- unresolved reconciliation exceptions and estimates are disclosed; and
- exports, schedules, access, amendments, and approvals are audited.
