# Consolidated Financial Report

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/230-financial-reporting/015-consolidated-financial-report.md

---

## Purpose

The Consolidated Financial Report combines approved entity/country results into group reporting using controlled mappings, eliminations, FX translation, and policy versions.

## Rules

Consolidation retains local statements, currencies, tax, intercompany, reserve, vendor/partner, reward, and treasury evidence. Intercompany balances and transfers are eliminated only with approved mappings. Consolidated GMV, revenue, cash, liabilities, and profit remain distinct metrics.

## Certification

Consolidated reports disclose entities included/excluded, currency/rates, translation date, eliminations, period, chart version, reconciliation status, approvals, and limitations.
