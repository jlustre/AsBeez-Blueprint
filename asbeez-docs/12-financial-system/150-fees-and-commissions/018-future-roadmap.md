# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/150-fees-and-commissions/018-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve fee/commission types, payers, beneficiaries, countries, entities, currencies, plans, funding, tax, and recognition policies;
- implement calculation, allocation, invoicing, collection, recognition, settlement, and payout mappings;
- connect orders, payments, subscriptions, vendors, partners, referrals, rewards, tax, reserves, wallets, and GL;
- implement reconciliation, reporting, APIs, events, security, and audit; and
- establish disclosure, refund, chargeback, and dispute adjustments.

## Medium Term

- support localized fee and commission plans;
- automate provider, vendor, partner, and referral reconciliation;
- improve pricing, cost, margin, liability, and payout forecasting; and
- add explainable AI assistance with human approval.

## Long Term

- support regional fee services and high-volume calculation;
- provide governed vendor/partner integrations;
- improve scenario modeling for marketplace and reward-funded programs; and
- preserve portable calculation and allocation evidence across jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No fee or commission feature may present an assessed charge as revenue, an unqualified commission as payable, a reward as money, or a vendor/partner obligation as AsBeez revenue without approved source, plan, funding, tax, accounting, disclosure, reconciliation, and audit rules.
