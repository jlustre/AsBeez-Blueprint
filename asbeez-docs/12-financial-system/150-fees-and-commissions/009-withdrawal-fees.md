# Withdrawal Fees

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/150-fees-and-commissions/009-withdrawal-fees.md

---

## Purpose

Withdrawal fees are provider, bank, network, or platform charges applied to an approved payout or withdrawal under disclosed policy.

## Required Data

Payout/request, payer, beneficiary, method/provider, amount, currency, rate/fixed fee, tax, country/entity, effective policy, minimum/maximum, and accounting mapping.

## Rules

Withdrawal fees cannot make RP, ABC, AHC, unclassified reward, or estimated value payable. Fees are distinct from commission, vendor/partner obligation, reserve, tax, and payout principal. The net amount and fee are disclosed and reconciled to provider evidence.

## Reporting States

Track estimated, disclosed, assessed, approved, collected, waived, refunded, disputed, and outstanding withdrawal fees by payout, method, provider, country, entity, currency, and policy version.
