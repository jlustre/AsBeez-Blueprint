# Vendor Commissions

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/150-fees-and-commissions/012-vendor-commissions.md

---

## Purpose

Vendor commissions are approved platform or channel allocations calculated from vendor order/service activity under contract or program policy.

## Rules

Vendor commission may be a platform fee charged to the vendor or an obligation owed to another beneficiary; these meanings must not be conflated. The calculation identifies gross basis, tax, vendor entitlement, partner allocation, reserve, reward effect, country/entity, and recognition/settlement condition.

## Corrections

Refunds, chargebacks, cancellations, disputes, and fee-plan changes create linked adjustments. Vendor commissions do not automatically create RP, ABC, AHC, or payout effects.
