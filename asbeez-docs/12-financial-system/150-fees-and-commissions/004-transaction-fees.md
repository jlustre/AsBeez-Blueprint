# Transaction Fees

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/150-fees-and-commissions/004-transaction-fees.md

---

## Purpose

Transaction fees are charges assessed on an approved marketplace order, payment, vendor transaction, or service transaction under a published fee plan.

## Required Data

Source order/payment/service, payer, basis, rate/fixed amount, minimum/maximum, currency, tax, vendor/partner allocation, country/entity, effective plan, refund/chargeback behavior, and recognition condition.

## Rules

Transaction fee assessment is distinct from collection and revenue recognition. The fee cannot reduce tax, vendor entitlement, partner commission, reserve, or reward effect invisibly. Refunds and chargebacks apply the approved fee policy and create linked corrections.

## Reporting States

Report assessed, approved, invoiced, collected, recognized, refunded, disputed, waived, and outstanding transaction fees separately by order, vendor, partner, country, entity, currency, and policy version.
