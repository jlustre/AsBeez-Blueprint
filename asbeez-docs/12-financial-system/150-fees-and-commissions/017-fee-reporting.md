# Fee Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/150-fees-and-commissions/017-fee-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Fee reporting separates assessed, invoiced, collected, recognized, payable, paid, held, refunded, disputed, and reversed fees and commissions by source, payer, beneficiary, plan, country, entity, currency, and period.

## Report Families

- transaction, subscription, listing, service, payment, withdrawal, and FX fees;
- platform revenue and deferred/unearned fee amounts;
- vendor, partner, and referral commissions;
- tax, reserves, refunds, chargebacks, discounts, and adjustments; and
- reconciliation, exception, margin, cost, forecast, and certified financial views.

## Rules

Reports state basis, plan/policy version, source, currency, entity, country, recognition status, and reconciliation status. Fees are not called revenue before recognition; commissions are not called paid income before settlement/payout; GMV, customer payment, and reward quantities remain separate.
