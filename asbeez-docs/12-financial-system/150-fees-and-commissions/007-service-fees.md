# Service Fees

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/150-fees-and-commissions/007-service-fees.md

---

## Purpose

Service fees are charges for approved professional, support, integration, technology, fulfillment, or other delivered services.

## Required Data

Service/contract, customer/vendor/partner, deliverable/milestone, basis, rate/fixed amount, currency, tax, country/entity, invoice/payment, delivery evidence, recognition schedule, and refund/credit policy.

## Rules

Service fees are distinct from order consideration, vendor entitlement, partner commission, tax, payment fee, reserve, reward, and payout. Assessment or invoice issue does not recognize revenue until the service obligation is satisfied.

## Reporting States

Track assessed, approved, invoiced, collected, deferred, recognized, refunded, credited, disputed, and outstanding service fees by contract, service, payer, country, entity, currency, and policy version.
