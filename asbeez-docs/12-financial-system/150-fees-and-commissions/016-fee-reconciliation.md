# Fee Reconciliation

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/150-fees-and-commissions/016-fee-reconciliation.md

---

## Purpose

Fee reconciliation proves that fee and commission assessments, invoices, collections, revenue, vendor/partner obligations, payment/withdrawal/FX costs, tax, reserves, refunds, chargebacks, and General Ledger control accounts agree.

## Required Checks

- source order/payment/invoice/service/settlement agrees to basis;
- calculation uses the correct effective plan and policy;
- payer, beneficiary, currency, tax, country, entity, and dimensions agree;
- assessment, invoice, collection, recognition, payable, and payout states agree;
- refunds, chargebacks, cancellations, disputes, and adjustments propagate; and
- partner/vendor/referral commissions are qualified and funded.

## Exceptions

Duplicate assessment, stale plan, unexplained residual, incorrect beneficiary, tax mismatch, fee/revenue mismatch, unqualified commission, provider difference, or missing downstream adjustment is an owned exception even when totals balance.
