# Partner Commissions

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/150-fees-and-commissions/011-partner-commissions.md

---

## Purpose

Partner commissions are approved obligations for qualifying partner-referred commerce, channel activity, integration services, or revenue-sharing agreements.

## Rules

Registration, recruitment, genealogy, or referral count alone cannot create a commission. The program must define qualifying source, attribution, funding, cap, refund/chargeback treatment, tax, country, compliance, and payout conditions. Partner commission is not automatically AsBeez revenue.

## Required Detail

Partner/program, source order/service, attribution, basis, rate/fixed amount, currency, fee/tax/reserve/hold, approval, settlement, payout, and reversal references.
