# Subscription Fees

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/150-fees-and-commissions/005-subscription-fees.md

---

## Purpose

Subscription fees are recurring or scheduled charges for approved member, vendor, enterprise, technology, or service access.

## Required Data

Subscription/contract, customer/vendor, plan/version, service period, price, billing interval, currency, tax, discount, cancellation/proration, country/entity, invoice/payment, and recognition schedule.

## Rules

Price or invoice issuance does not automatically equal earned fee revenue. Failed payment, pause, cancellation, upgrade, downgrade, refund, and credit create explicit schedule or adjustment events. Subscription fees do not create RP, ABC, AHC, commission, or payout effects unless a separate approved policy applies.

## Reporting States

Track scheduled, assessed, invoiced, collected, deferred, recognized, credited, refunded, disputed, cancelled, and outstanding subscription fees by plan, service period, customer/vendor, country, entity, currency, and policy version.
