# Foreign Exchange Fees

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/150-fees-and-commissions/010-foreign-exchange-fees.md

---

## Purpose

Foreign exchange fees are approved spreads, conversion charges, or provider costs applied when value moves between currencies.

## Required Data

Source/target currency, source/target amount, rate, rate source/timestamp, spread/fee, rounding, provider, payer, transaction, country/entity, policy version, and accounting treatment.

## Rules

FX fee is distinct from realized/unrealized gain/loss, payout fee, payment fee, platform revenue, tax, vendor/partner amount, and reward value. Historical conversions remain reproducible and are not recalculated using current rates.

## Reporting States

Track quoted, approved, applied, settled, reversed, disputed, and outstanding FX fees with source/target amounts, rate, spread, provider, timestamp, country, entity, and policy version.
