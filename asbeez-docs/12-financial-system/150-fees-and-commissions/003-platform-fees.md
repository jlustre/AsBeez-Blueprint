# Platform Fees

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/150-fees-and-commissions/003-platform-fees.md

---

## Purpose

Platform fees are amounts AsBeez charges for approved marketplace, vendor, partner, technology, listing, advertising, payment, or support services.

## Required Data

Fee ID, service/source, payer, basis, rate/fixed amount, currency, tax, country/entity, plan/version, performance obligation, invoice/payment, refund/chargeback, and recognition mapping.

## Rules

Platform fees are distinct from customer payment, vendor entitlement, partner/referral commission, tax, reserve, payout fee, and reward effect. A fee becomes revenue only when the related service is earned. Fees must be disclosed and calculated server-side.

## States and Reporting

Track assessed, approved, invoiced, collected, deferred, recognized, refunded, disputed, waived, and outstanding amounts separately by fee plan, payer, service, country, entity, currency, and policy version.
