## Purpose

Fees and Commissions define how AsBeez charges for approved services and calculates obligations owed to vendors, partners, and referrers.

## AsBeez Boundary

Fees are charges for a service or cost. Commissions are obligations created by qualifying commerce, service delivery, or approved program rules. Neither is automatically revenue, payable, cash, or reward value until source, policy, tax, performance, funding, and settlement conditions are satisfied.

## Principles

- fee/commission basis, payer/beneficiary, rate, cap, currency, tax, and effective version are explicit;
- customer payment, vendor entitlement, platform revenue, partner commission, referral commission, tax, reserves, and rewards remain separate;
- calculations are server-side, reproducible, disclosed, and idempotent;
- refunds, chargebacks, cancellations, disputes, and policy changes create linked adjustments;
- allocation and recognition are separate from assessment; and
- reports distinguish assessed, invoiced, collected, recognized, payable, paid, held, reversed, and disputed amounts.
