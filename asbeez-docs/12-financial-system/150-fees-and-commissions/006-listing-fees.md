# Listing Fees

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/150-fees-and-commissions/006-listing-fees.md

---

## Purpose

Listing fees are disclosed charges for publishing, maintaining, or promoting a vendor/product/service listing under an approved plan.

## Required Data

Vendor/listing, service period, placement, plan/version, amount/rate, currency, tax, invoice/payment, delivery/performance evidence, refund/credit rule, and recognition mapping.

## Rules

Listing fee billing is distinct from transaction sales, vendor entitlement, advertising revenue, partner commission, tax, and rewards. A listing fee is recognized as revenue only as the listing service is delivered under policy.

## Reporting States

Track listing fee plan, period, assessed, invoiced, collected, recognized, deferred, refunded, credited, disputed, and outstanding amounts by vendor, listing, country, entity, currency, and policy version.
