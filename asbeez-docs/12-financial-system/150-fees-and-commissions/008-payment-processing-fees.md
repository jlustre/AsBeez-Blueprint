# Payment Processing Fees

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/150-fees-and-commissions/008-payment-processing-fees.md

---

## Purpose

Payment processing fees are provider, network, bank, or payment-method costs associated with authorization, capture, refund, dispute, or settlement.

## Required Data

Provider, payment/attempt/capture/refund/dispute, fee type, gross/net basis, rate/amount, currency, country/entity, payer/funding owner, settlement evidence, tax, and accounting mapping.

## Rules

Provider fee is distinct from customer charge, platform fee, vendor entitlement, partner commission, tax, reserve, reward, and chargeback principal. Recovery or pass-through requires approved terms and disclosure. Provider evidence is reconciled before final recognition.

## Reporting States

Track estimated, assessed, provider-confirmed, collected, allocated, recognized, refunded, disputed, recovered, and outstanding processing fees by provider, payment, country, entity, currency, and policy version.
