# Invoice Localization

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/090-invoicing/015-invoice-localization.md

---

## Purpose

Invoice localization adapts issued content and calculation presentation to country, legal entity, language, currency, tax, numbering, date, address, and delivery requirements without changing financial meaning silently.

## Localized Elements

- legal issuer and tax registration;
- recipient and place-of-supply fields;
- invoice number and document type;
- currency, precision, exchange-rate disclosure;
- tax labels, rates, exemptions, and authority;
- language, dates, addresses, payment terms, and delivery; and
- statutory fields, signatures, QR/e-invoice data, or archival format.

## Rules

Localization is selected from effective-dated country/entity policy. A translated display is not a new accounting amount. Any local tax or numbering requirement is preserved with source evidence and mapped to global reporting.
