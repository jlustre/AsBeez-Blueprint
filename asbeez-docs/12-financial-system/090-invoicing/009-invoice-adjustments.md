# Invoice Adjustments

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/090-invoicing/009-invoice-adjustments.md

---

## Purpose

Invoice adjustments correct or update an amount due for an approved reason without editing an issued invoice.

## Adjustment Types

- price or quantity correction;
- tax correction;
- approved fee or discount correction;
- currency or rounding correction;
- service-period or billing correction; and
- refund, return, dispute, or settlement correction.

## Required Evidence

Original invoice/line, reason, source order/contract, amount and currency, tax impact, party, policy version, preparer, approver, effective/posting date, and credit/debit note or GL reference.

## Rules

Adjustments cannot create an unapproved reward, vendor, partner, revenue, or payout effect. They preserve original invoice and payment allocation history and reconcile to customer subledger, tax, payment, settlement, and GL records.
