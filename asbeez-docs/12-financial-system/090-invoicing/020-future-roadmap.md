# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/090-invoicing/020-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve launch entities, countries, invoice types, numbering, currencies, and tax policies;
- implement invoice draft, issue, delivery, payment allocation, credit/debit note, and compliance lifecycles;
- connect orders, payments, customer/vendor/partner subledgers, tax, settlement, rewards, and GL mappings;
- establish immutable documents, correction, retention, reconciliation, and audit evidence; and
- implement secure APIs, events, localization, and delivery workflows.

## Medium Term

- support recurring billing, regional e-invoicing, and localized statutory formats;
- automate payment matching, tax validation, overdue detection, and note propagation;
- improve vendor/partner and marketplace invoice statements; and
- add explainable AI assistance with human approval.

## Long Term

- support multi-entity, multi-currency invoice services;
- provide governed partner invoicing integrations;
- strengthen revenue, tax, collection, and liquidity forecasting; and
- preserve portable invoice evidence across jurisdictions and providers.

## Roadmap Gate

No invoice feature may represent RP, ABC, AHC, reward, commission, vendor entitlement, payment, revenue, or payout as another category without approved source, policy, tax, legal, accounting, disclosure, and reconciliation rules.
