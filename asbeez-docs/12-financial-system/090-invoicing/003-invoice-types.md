# Invoice Types

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/090-invoicing/003-invoice-types.md

---

## Purpose

Invoice type determines the commercial and legal context, template, numbering, tax, delivery, and payment terms.

## Types

- customer marketplace invoice;
- vendor or partner service invoice;
- member or subscription invoice;
- platform/API/service invoice;
- advertising or campaign invoice;
- credit or debit note; and
- pro forma or draft estimate, which is not an accounting invoice until issued.

## Rules

Type is explicit and linked to issuer, recipient, contract/order, entity, country, currency, tax policy, and revenue/receivable treatment. A pro forma document cannot be used to prove payment, revenue, settlement, or reward qualification.

## Operational Differences

Customer marketplace invoices reference order and fulfillment state. Vendor and partner invoices reference approved services or settlement agreements. Subscription invoices reference billing schedules and service periods. Credit and debit notes reference an issued invoice and use controlled numbering, tax, delivery, and General Ledger treatment.
