# Debit Notes

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/090-invoicing/011-debit-notes.md

---

## Purpose

A debit note increases an amount due for an approved additional charge, correction, service, tax, or contractual adjustment.

## Required Data

Debit note ID/number, original invoice/contract/order, affected lines, reason, amount, currency, tax, recipient, issuer, approval, issue date, and delivery evidence.

## Rules

Debit notes are not used to conceal an unapproved price change, vendor/partner obligation, reward effect, or late accounting correction. The recipient and payment status are explicit, and the note reconciles to customer/vendor subledger, tax, receivable, and GL records.

## Accounting and Allocation

The note identifies the added line, source contract/order/service, tax assessment, payment terms, and accounting mapping. It may increase a receivable or approved payable only after review and issuance; it does not capture payment, recognize reward activity, or create a payout by itself.
