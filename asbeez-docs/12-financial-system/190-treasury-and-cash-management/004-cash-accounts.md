# Cash Accounts

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/190-treasury-and-cash-management/004-cash-accounts.md

---

## Purpose

Cash Accounts are internal treasury views of cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, clearing, provider balances, or funds in transit mapped to legal entities, currencies, and General Ledger accounts.

## Categories

- unrestricted operating cash;
- restricted/reserved cash;
- payment provider funds in transit;
- payout/refund clearing;
- tax and designated fund cash; and
- intercompany or treasury transfer clearing.

## Rules

Cash category, ownership, availability, restriction, currency, entity, source evidence, and GL mapping are explicit. A wallet, reserve, vendor payable, reward quantity, or provider display balance is not cash merely because it appears in a platform interface.
