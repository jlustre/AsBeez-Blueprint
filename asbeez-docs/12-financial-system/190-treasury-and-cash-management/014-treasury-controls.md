# Treasury Controls

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/190-treasury-and-cash-management/014-treasury-controls.md

---

## Purpose

Treasury controls protect cash custody, transfers, liquidity, reserves, bank/provider integrations, country operations, FX, and sensitive financial authority.

## Controls

- dual approval and separation of duties;
- bank account and beneficiary verification;
- transfer limits, velocity, purpose, and idempotency;
- sanctions, source-of-funds, country, entity, and tax checks;
- restricted/reserved cash controls;
- provider/bank credential and webhook security;
- reconciliation, exception aging, and period-close review; and
- emergency lock, recovery, and audit procedures.

## Rules

No individual or service should initiate, approve, execute, and reconcile the same material movement. Manual overrides require reason, evidence, expiry, second approval where material, and post-event review.
