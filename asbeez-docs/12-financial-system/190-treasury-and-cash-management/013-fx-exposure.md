# FX Exposure

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/190-treasury-and-cash-management/013-fx-exposure.md

---

## Purpose

FX exposure measures the currency risk created by open cash, receivable, payable, reserve, payout, tax, settlement, provider, and forecast positions.

## Required Detail

Currency, entity, country, source balance/obligation, amount, maturity, expected settlement currency/date, rate, valuation basis, counterparty, hedge/limit, and policy version.

## Rules

Exposure reports distinguish transaction, settlement, functional, base, and reporting currencies; realized versus unrealized gain/loss; and confirmed versus forecast positions. Exposure does not alter source amounts or authorize a hedge/transfer.
