# Cash Positioning

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/190-treasury-and-cash-management/005-cash-positioning.md

---

## Purpose

Cash positioning reports available, restricted, reserved, pending, in-transit, expected, and projected cash by entity, country, currency, account, provider, and purpose.

## Position Components

```text
Bank/provider confirmed balance
- restricted/reserved cash
- pending/outbound transfers
+ expected confirmed inflows
- approved near-term obligations
= usable/projected position
```

## Rules

Position reports distinguish confirmed from forecast values and identify source freshness. Vendor/partner payouts, refunds, taxes, chargebacks, reward liabilities, reserves, payroll, provider settlements, and FX are included only through approved source schedules.
