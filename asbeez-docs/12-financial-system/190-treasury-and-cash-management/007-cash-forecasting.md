# Cash Forecasting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/190-treasury-and-cash-management/007-cash-forecasting.md

---

## Purpose

Cash forecasting estimates future cash inflows, outflows, timing, currencies, and liquidity gaps for AsBeez entities and countries.

## Forecast Inputs

Provider settlements, customer collections, invoices, vendor/partner settlements and payouts, refunds, chargebacks, taxes/remittances, reserves, payroll/operating costs, subscriptions, advertising/services, FX, bank timing, and scenario assumptions.

## Rules

Forecasts distinguish confirmed, scheduled, expected, estimated, restricted, and stressed values. Each forecast carries source period, confidence, currency, entity, assumptions, policy/model version, and freshness. Forecast output does not authorize transfers or change obligations.

## Scenarios

Base, downside, upside, provider delay, chargeback surge, refund surge, FX movement, payout acceleration, tax due-date, and country corridor scenarios are reviewed by Treasury/Finance.
