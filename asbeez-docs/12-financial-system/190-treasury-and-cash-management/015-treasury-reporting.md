# Treasury Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/190-treasury-and-cash-management/015-treasury-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Treasury reporting separates confirmed cash, restricted/reserved funds, provider balances, in-transit amounts, liquidity forecasts, obligations, transfers, FX, and reconciliation status.

## Report Families

- bank/provider cash by account, entity, country, currency, and purpose;
- cash position, liquidity minimum, reserve coverage, commitments, and available funds;
- expected collections, settlements, payouts, refunds, taxes, chargebacks, and operating outflows;
- transfers, concentration, intercompany, FX exposure, gains/losses, and fees; and
- reconciliation, exceptions, controls, forecasts, and certified treasury reports.

## Rules

Every report labels confirmed versus forecast, source freshness, currency, entity, restrictions, policy/model version, and reconciliation status. Provider or wallet displayed balances are not called available company cash without custody and reconciliation evidence.
