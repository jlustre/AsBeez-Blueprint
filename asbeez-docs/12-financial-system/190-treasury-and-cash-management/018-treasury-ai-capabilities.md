# Treasury AI Capabilities

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/190-treasury-and-cash-management/018-treasury-ai-capabilities.md

---

## Purpose

AI may assist Treasury with cash forecasting, reconciliation matching, liquidity scenarios, FX exposure, anomaly detection, provider comparison, and exception prioritization while deterministic controls remain authoritative.

## Permitted Assistance

- forecast cash and liquidity under scenarios;
- identify unusual bank/provider movement or concentration;
- propose reconciliation matches and transfer review priority;
- analyze FX exposure, rate anomalies, and settlement timing; and
- explain position, forecast, or exception evidence.

## Prohibited Actions

AI must not directly execute/approve transfers, release reserves, alter cash positions, choose accounting rates, bypass controls, classify provider balance as company cash, or fund payouts/rewards without authorized workflows.

## Governance

Recommendations include model/version, evidence scope, confidence, reason, provenance, policy context, human decision, and audit trail. Bank, provider, identity, treasury, and financial data is minimized and access-controlled.
