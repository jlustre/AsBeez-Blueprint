# Country Treasury

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/190-treasury-and-cash-management/012-country-treasury.md

---

## Purpose

Country Treasury manages local bank accounts, currencies, payment providers, liquidity, reserves, taxes, payout corridors, capital controls, and regulatory restrictions.

## Required Data

Country/jurisdiction, legal entity, local currency, bank/provider, tax authority, restricted funds, reserve, payout/payment methods, liquidity minimum, FX corridor, signatories, and local policy.

## Rules

Country operations cannot move, concentrate, convert, or remit funds without local entity, tax, compliance, banking, FX, and approval requirements. Local cash views consolidate to global treasury without erasing statutory detail.

## Reporting

Track local cash position, restricted/reserved funds, liquidity minimum, provider/bank settlements, tax remittances, payouts, refunds, FX exposure, transfers, exceptions, and reconciliation status by country, entity, currency, and policy version.
