## Purpose

Treasury and Cash Management governs bank/provider cash, custody, liquidity, cash positioning, transfers, forecasting, concentration, country operations, FX exposure, reconciliation, controls, and reporting.

## AsBeez Boundary

Treasury manages organizational cash and liquidity. It does not own customer, member, vendor, partner, reward, wallet, settlement, tax, or payout eligibility; those domains provide approved obligations and movements for treasury execution and reconciliation.

## Principles

- every cash account, provider balance, currency, entity, country, and restriction is explicit;
- cash received is distinct from revenue, payable, wallet balance, reserve, and reward value;
- transfers require approved purpose, source/destination, currency, authority, and idempotency;
- bank/provider evidence is reconciled to subledgers and General Ledger;
- liquidity forecasts include refunds, chargebacks, payouts, taxes, reserves, settlement timing, and FX; and
- treasury AI recommends but does not execute material movement without controls.
