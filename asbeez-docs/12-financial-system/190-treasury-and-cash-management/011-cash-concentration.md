# Cash Concentration

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/190-treasury-and-cash-management/011-cash-concentration.md

---

## Purpose

Cash concentration moves or positions cash across approved accounts, entities, countries, or currencies to improve liquidity while preserving ownership, restrictions, tax, intercompany, and audit treatment.

## Rules

Concentration requires approved source/destination, purpose, currency/FX, entity, restricted-fund treatment, transfer authority, limits, and reconciliation. Customer, vendor, partner, reward, charity, promotional, tax, and reserve funds cannot be concentrated as unrestricted company cash.

## Controls

Monitor bank/provider concentration, counterparty exposure, country restrictions, currency mismatch, transfer timing, and minimum balances. Intercompany and cross-border movements require documented accounting and tax treatment.
