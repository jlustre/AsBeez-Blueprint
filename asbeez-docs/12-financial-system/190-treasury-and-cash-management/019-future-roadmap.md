# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/190-treasury-and-cash-management/019-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve entities, countries, banks, providers, currencies, restrictions, reserves, and liquidity policies;
- implement account, cash position, forecasting, transfer, reconciliation, concentration, country, and FX exposure workflows;
- connect payments, payouts, refunds, taxes, reserves, vendor/partner settlement, rewards, and GL;
- implement controls, API, events, security, reporting, audit, and exception operations; and
- establish liquidity thresholds, contingency funding, and recovery procedures.

## Medium Term

- automate bank/provider ingestion, reconciliation, transfer monitoring, and forecast updates;
- support multi-entity treasury and localized country restrictions;
- improve FX, liquidity, reserve, payout, refund, tax, and chargeback forecasting; and
- add explainable AI assistance with human approval.

## Long Term

- support regional treasury services and high-volume cash operations;
- provide governed bank/provider and partner integrations;
- strengthen scenario planning and resilience controls; and
- preserve portable cash, transfer, and reconciliation evidence across jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No treasury feature may move, concentrate, release, classify, forecast, or report cash without approved account ownership, purpose, currency, entity, country, restrictions, authority, reconciliation, and audit rules.
