# Liquidity Management

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/190-treasury-and-cash-management/006-liquidity-management.md

---

## Purpose

Liquidity management ensures AsBeez can meet approved customer refunds, vendor/partner payouts, taxes, payment provider settlements, chargebacks, reserves, operating costs, and cross-border obligations when due.

## Inputs

Confirmed cash, restricted/reserved balances, settlement schedules, payout queues, refund/chargeback exposure, tax due dates, provider delays, subscription/revenue inflows, FX, country/entity restrictions, and stress assumptions.

## Controls

Minimum liquidity, reserve coverage, funding triggers, payment priority, escalation, cash concentration, currency limits, and emergency access are approved and effective-dated. Liquidity action cannot bypass payout, tax, compliance, reserve, or accounting controls.
