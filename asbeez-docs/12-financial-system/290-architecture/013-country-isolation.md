# Country Isolation

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/290-architecture/013-country-isolation.md

---

## Purpose

Country isolation protects local legal, tax, currency, data, banking, provider, payout, reserve, reward, and reporting boundaries while enabling approved global consolidation.

## Rules

Country/entity scope is attached to data, accounts, policies, providers, access, reports, and operations. Cross-country movement requires approved entity, tax, FX, intercompany, sanctions, privacy, and reconciliation treatment. Consolidation never erases local statutory history.

## Evidence

Country operations retain local authority, entity, currency, provider, registration, policy, access, transfer, report, reconciliation, and approval references for every cross-boundary operation.
