# Expired Or Forfeited Rewards

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/060-reward-finance/012-expired-or-forfeited-rewards.md

---

## Purpose

Expiry or forfeiture changes a reward’s program status under a disclosed, effective-dated rule. It does not erase the original RP, ABC, matrix, AHC, liability, or expense history.

## Required Conditions

The system records reward type, source event, member, country, policy/version, notice, eligibility period, reason, dispute window, affected monetary liability, and destination treatment if any.

## Rules

- expiry cannot be retroactively applied to a period or country where the rule was not effective;
- forfeiture requires a defined policy breach or disqualifying event and review path;
- non-monetary quantities and monetary liabilities are adjusted separately;
- funds cannot become revenue merely because a reward expired; and
- refund, chargeback, fraud, and legal restrictions may suspend rather than forfeit value.

## Accounting Effect

Any liability release, reserve change, expense reversal, statutory transfer, or company-held allocation requires an approved journal mapping, evidence, and reconciliation.
