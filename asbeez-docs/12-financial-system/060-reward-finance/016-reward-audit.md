# Reward Audit

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/060-reward-finance/016-reward-audit.md

---

## Purpose

Reward audit provides evidence that reward events and any monetary consequences were generated, classified, measured, adjusted, reconciled, and reported under approved rules.

## Audit Trail

Retain order/payment/source event, RP/ABC/matrix/AHC IDs, member/vendor/partner references, country/entity/currency, program and policy versions, qualification evidence, account mappings, liability/expense/reserve calculations, approvals, wallet/payout effects, reversals, notices, and reconciliation outcomes.

## Audit Questions

- Was the source a genuine qualifying marketplace event?
- Was the participant and country eligible at the event time?
- Were RP, ABC, matrix, and AHC events generated exactly once?
- Was any monetary effect legally and financially classified before posting?
- Was funding, liability ceiling, reserve, refund, and chargeback treatment applied?
- Can every report and payout be traced to immutable source and ledger records?

## Controls

Posted financial records and reward history are append-only. Access is role-scoped, exports are logged, sensitive member data is minimized, and AI recommendations retain provenance without authorizing postings.
