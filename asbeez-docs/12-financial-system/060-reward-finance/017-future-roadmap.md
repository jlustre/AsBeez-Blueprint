# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/060-reward-finance/017-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve RP, ABC, AHC, wallet, payout, and funding classifications by launch country;
- connect order, payment, refund, vendor, partner, tax, reserve, wallet, and GL events;
- implement reward liability, expense, reserve, adjustment, and reconciliation controls;
- publish member and financial reports with clear units and statuses; and
- establish legal, Finance, Compliance, and audit approval gates.

## Medium Term

- improve reward-liability forecasting and utilization measurement;
- automate refund, chargeback, expiry, forfeiture, and fraud propagation;
- support multi-entity, multi-currency, and country-specific reward policies;
- provide replayable event lineage and certified reporting; and
- add explainable anomaly detection with human approval.

## Long Term

- support regional reward-finance services and high-volume event processing;
- model approved redemption and payout programs without weakening controls;
- provide scenario planning for marketplace-funded reward programs; and
- expose governed APIs for approved partners and auditors.

## Roadmap Gate

No new reward program may launch a financial effect without qualifying commerce, approved terms, jurisdictional classification, funding, liability ceiling, account mapping, reserve treatment, reversal rules, reconciliation, disclosure, and audit evidence.
