# Unclaimed Rewards

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/060-reward-finance/011-unclaimed-rewards.md

---

## Purpose

Unclaimed rewards are approved reward obligations or benefits that remain available under program rules but have not been redeemed, claimed, or paid within the applicable process.

## Classification

The system distinguishes unavailable program quantities, recognized monetary liabilities, pending redemption, failed payout, dormant balance, and legally escheatable property. These categories cannot be collapsed into one “unclaimed” balance.

## Rules

The member receives clear notice, claim conditions, expiry rules, and any country-specific consequences. Unclaimed status does not automatically create revenue or permit the company to use the funds. Transfers to company-held, charity, reserve, or statutory accounts require approved policy and evidence.

## Reporting

Report unclaimed quantity and monetary liability separately by reward type, member, country, program, age, and policy version. Reconciliation includes notices, redemption attempts, payout failures, and resulting liability movements.
