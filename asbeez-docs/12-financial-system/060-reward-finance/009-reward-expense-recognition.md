# Reward Expense Recognition

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/060-reward-finance/009-reward-expense-recognition.md

---

## Purpose

Reward expense recognition records the approved cost of reward, referral, compensation, promotion, redemption, or loyalty activity when the applicable accounting policy determines that an expense has occurred.

## Recognition Inputs

Qualifying source, reward event, program and policy version, performance/benefit condition, expected redemption or payout, funding source, country, currency, liability measurement, vendor/partner/member allocation, and reversal/expiry assumptions.

## Rules

Reward expense is not created merely by RP issuance, ABC creation, matrix placement, or AHC display. The policy must identify the economic benefit, obligation, measurement basis, and period. Expense and liability recognition are separate decisions and may occur at different times.

## Reversals and Estimates

Refunds, chargebacks, fraud, expiry, forfeiture, redemption, and updated utilization estimates create controlled adjustments or reversals. Historical entries remain immutable and estimates are versioned by period.
