# Reward Liabilities

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/060-reward-finance/008-reward-liabilities.md

---

## Purpose

A reward liability is an approved present or probable monetary obligation arising from a classified reward event. RP, ABC, and AHC quantities are not liabilities merely because they exist in a program ledger.

## Recognition Requirements

Recognition requires:

- valid qualifying marketplace or approved program source;
- jurisdictional legal and accounting classification;
- enforceable or expected obligation assessment;
- measurable amount and currency;
- approved funding source and liability ceiling;
- policy version, release/redemption conditions, and expiry treatment; and
- reversal, refund, chargeback, fraud, and dispute rules.

## Liability States

```text
Proposed -> Recognized -> Pending Release -> Released/Settled
Recognized -> Held -> Reversed | Expired | Forfeited
```

## Measurement

Report gross recognized, released, redeemed, reversed, expired, forfeited, held, and outstanding amounts separately from RP/AHC quantities. The liability maps to GL control accounts by country, entity, currency, program, and policy version.
