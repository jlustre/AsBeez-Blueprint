# ABC Creation Accounting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/060-reward-finance/005-abc-creation-accounting.md

---

## Purpose

ABC creation accounting records the financial evaluation of an AsBeez Business Cell created from qualifying RP. ABC creation itself is a platform participation event, not an automatic monetary transaction.

## Source Requirements

RP consumption event, member, ABC ID, country, eligibility rule/version, source order history, fraud/compliance status, creation time, and any approved financial classification.

## Default Treatment

The default result is a non-monetary ABC record with no journal entry, wallet credit, income, company ownership, or guaranteed earning. A monetary consequence may be recognized only under an approved country and program policy with explicit funding, liability, expense, redemption, or payout treatment.

## Reversal

If the qualifying RP is reversed because of refund, chargeback, fraud, or policy breach, the ABC is not silently deleted. The Rewards/Matrix domains determine participation status; Reward Finance evaluates any recognized financial effect for hold, reversal, expiry, or compensation.
