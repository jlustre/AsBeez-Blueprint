# Referral Reward Accounting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/060-reward-finance/007-referral-reward-accounting.md

---

## Purpose

Referral reward accounting records the financial evaluation of approved referral programs tied to qualifying AsBeez commerce or other explicitly approved activity.

## Qualification Boundary

Registration, invitation, genealogy, or recruitment alone does not create a financial obligation. A referral reward requires a qualifying source transaction, attribution evidence, program/rule version, participant eligibility, country availability, fraud review, and any required customer return period.

## Required Detail

Referral event, referring member/partner, referred customer/member where permitted, order and vendor, qualification status, reward unit, monetary classification, amount, currency, funding source, cap, hold, reversal conditions, approval, and payout/redemption state.

## Corrections

Refunds, chargebacks, cancellations, duplicate attribution, or fraud create explicit reversal, hold, or adjustment events. The original referral history remains immutable and any financial obligation reconciles to commission and settlement subledgers.
