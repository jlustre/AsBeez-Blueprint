# Reward Reserves

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/060-reward-finance/010-reward-reserves.md

---

## Purpose

Reward reserves protect against expected or uncertain reward-related obligations, refunds, chargebacks, fraud, redemption, payout, or program exposure. A reserve is not itself a reward, wallet balance, or revenue account.

## Required Detail

Reserve ID, reward/program source, country, entity, currency, exposure population, measurement method, rate/assumption, funding account, liability relationship, owner, approval, release condition, review date, and utilization history.

## Rules

Reserve calculations use documented populations and policy versions. Reserve funding must be identifiable from approved marketplace revenue, compensation allocation, company funds, or another lawful source. Release or utilization creates a new event and cannot be used for an unrelated program.

## Monitoring

Report reserve coverage, expected exposure, recognized liability, released amount, utilization, aged items, and variance by program, country, entity, currency, and policy version.
