# AHC Accounting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/060-reward-finance/004-ahc-accounting.md

---

## Purpose

AHC accounting evaluates the financial consequences of AsBeez Hive Credit events generated by approved Beehive Matrix rules. The AHC quantity remains a program record unless an approved policy creates a monetary effect.

## Required Source

AHC event, member, ABC, matrix node/cycle, source descendant or qualifying activity, level/rule version, country, legal entity, program status, funding source, and financial classification status.

## Possible Outcomes

- no monetary effect;
- pending classification or compliance review;
- measured reward liability;
- reward or compensation expense;
- approved redemption obligation;
- monetary wallet credit; or
- reversal, expiry, forfeiture, or rejection.

## Rules

AHC cannot be purchased directly, treated as cash, or paid out merely because it appears in a member account. Any monetary recognition requires jurisdictional approval, funding, liability ceiling, account mapping, reserve treatment, and explicit payout/redemption conditions.
