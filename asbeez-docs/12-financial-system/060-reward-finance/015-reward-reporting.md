# Reward Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/060-reward-finance/015-reward-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Reward reporting separates program activity from financial reporting so members, operations, Finance, Legal, and leadership do not confuse quantities, eligibility, obligations, and realized monetary outcomes.

## Report Families

- RP earned, pending, consumed, reversed, expired, and outstanding;
- ABC created, active, placed, completed, and historically recorded;
- matrix cycles, placements, distributions, and AHC quantities;
- approved reward liabilities, expense, reserves, redemptions, wallet credits, and payouts;
- referral/commission qualification and settlement;
- refund, chargeback, fraud, expiry, forfeiture, and adjustment impact; and
- country, entity, currency, program, vendor, partner, and member views.

## Rules

Every report labels unit, currency, status, period, source, policy version, and whether the value is program-only, estimated, recognized, payable, paid, or reconciled. “Earnings,” “income,” “cash,” and “profit” are not used for program quantities or estimates without approved legal and accounting basis.

## Certification

Financial reward reports derive from reconciled subledgers and GL projections. Management dashboards may be provisional but disclose freshness and exceptions; certified reports retain query scope, source versions, and approval evidence.
