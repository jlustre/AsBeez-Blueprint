# Vendor Adjustments

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/130-refunds-returns-and-cancellations/011-vendor-adjustments.md

---

## Purpose

Vendor adjustments update vendor earnings, fees, reserves, holds, settlement, or payout treatment after a refund, return, cancellation, chargeback, dispute, or correction.

## Required Detail

Original order/line/earning/settlement, vendor, adjustment reason, gross/tax/fee/reserve/hold amounts, refund/chargeback, partner and reward effects, currency, country/entity, policy, approval, and subledger/GL reference.

## Rules

The adjustment does not delete vendor earnings or settlement history. A vendor payable may be reduced, held, restored, or corrected only through an approved event. It cannot silently create AsBeez revenue, partner liability, or reward treatment.
