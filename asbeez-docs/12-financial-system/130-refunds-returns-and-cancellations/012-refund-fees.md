# Refund Fees

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/130-refunds-returns-and-cancellations/012-refund-fees.md

---

## Purpose

Refund fees, restocking charges, provider costs, or cancellation charges are amounts applied only when disclosed, contractually and legally permitted, and calculated under approved policy.

## Required Detail

Fee ID, payer, source order/line, reason, amount/rate, currency, tax, country/entity, funding owner, refund eligibility, vendor/partner impact, revenue/expense mapping, and policy version.

## Rules

Refund fees cannot be hidden in the refund amount, used to bypass consumer rights, or treated as automatic AsBeez revenue. Provider costs, vendor charges, and platform revenue are distinct. Fees are separately disclosed and reconciled to refund, invoice, settlement, tax, and GL records.
