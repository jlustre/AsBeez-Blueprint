# Full Refunds

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/130-refunds-returns-and-cancellations/004-full-refunds.md

---

## Purpose

A full refund returns or credits the complete eligible customer consideration for an order, invoice, or defined transaction scope after approved eligibility.

## Required Effects

Original order/payment/invoice, refund amount, tax, fees, payment method, credit note, provider execution, customer credit, vendor/partner adjustment, reserve/hold, reward reversal, revenue correction, and reconciliation references.

## Rules

Full refund means full eligible customer amount, not necessarily reversal of every unrelated fee or reward effect. Each downstream component follows its policy. A full refund cannot exceed refundable captured value and does not delete the original financial events.
