# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/130-refunds-returns-and-cancellations/018-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve refund, return, cancellation, tax, payment, wallet, vendor, partner, reward, and country policies;
- implement request, eligibility, approval, full/partial execution, returns, cancellations, notes, and reconciliation;
- connect payments, invoices, wallets, rewards, vendor/partner settlement, reserves, and GL mappings;
- implement fraud controls, evidence, API, events, security, and audit; and
- establish customer, vendor, partner, and operations support workflows.

## Medium Term

- automate provider/wallet/credit-note reconciliation and downstream adjustments;
- improve refund, return, chargeback, reward, reserve, and vendor/partner forecasting;
- support localized consumer and tax rules; and
- add explainable AI assistance with human review and appeals.

## Long Term

- support regional refund services and high-volume event processing;
- provide governed partner/vendor integrations;
- improve abuse, liability, liquidity, and customer-experience forecasting; and
- preserve portable refund evidence across jurisdictions and providers.

## Roadmap Gate

No refund feature may erase history, deny lawful rights through opaque automation, or create customer credit, vendor/partner adjustment, reward reversal, revenue, tax, wallet, or GL effects without approved source, policy, evidence, reconciliation, and audit rules.
