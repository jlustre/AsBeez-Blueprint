# Reward Reversals

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/130-refunds-returns-and-cancellations/010-reward-reversals.md

---

## Purpose

Reward reversals evaluate how a refund, return, cancellation, chargeback, fraud finding, or invalid qualification affects RP, ABC, matrix, AHC, reward liability, expense, reserve, wallet, and payout records.

## Rules

- original RP, ABC, matrix, AHC, and financial events are never deleted;
- RP may be reversed, held, adjusted, or requalified under Rewards policy;
- ABC/matrix history remains intact while participation status is evaluated by owning domains;
- AHC quantity and any monetary liability are adjusted separately;
- wallet or payout effects require distinct approved events; and
- refunds cannot be used to silently erase reward history or create a new benefit.

## Evidence

Reversal references refund/return/cancellation, order/line/payment, member, vendor/partner, reward event, country, program/rule version, financial classification, account mapping, and resulting adjustment.
