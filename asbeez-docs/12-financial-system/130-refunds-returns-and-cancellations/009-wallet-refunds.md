# Wallet Refunds

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/130-refunds-returns-and-cancellations/009-wallet-refunds.md

---

## Purpose

Wallet refunds credit an approved monetary member/customer wallet when policy permits an alternative to returning funds through the original payment method.

## Preconditions

Refund eligible and approved, wallet type supports monetary credit, owner/country/currency match, provider/original-method policy permits wallet route, compliance passes, and idempotency is established.

## Rules

Wallet credit is not proof of cash refund or payout and must reference the original order/payment/invoice and refund decision. Reward display wallets cannot receive monetary refund value. Credit, availability, statement, vendor/partner, tax, revenue, and GL effects are reconciled separately.
