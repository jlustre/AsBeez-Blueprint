# Refund Fraud Controls

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/130-refunds-returns-and-cancellations/014-refund-fraud-controls.md

---

## Purpose

Refund fraud controls identify abuse, duplicate refund, account takeover, friendly fraud, return abuse, payment fraud, collusion, and reward manipulation while preserving lawful customer and vendor rights.

## Signals

Velocity, repeated reasons, device/account patterns, payment and destination mismatch, serial returns, refund-to-wallet anomalies, chargeback history, vendor/customer/partner relationships, reward reversals, shipping evidence, and policy/country risk.

## Rules

Risk signals recommend review, hold, limit, or escalation; they do not independently deny a lawful refund or alter financial records. Decisions record evidence, model/rule version, confidence/reason, reviewer, customer communication, and appeal path.

## Controls

Refund and reward abuse monitoring is separated from payment authentication and vendor dispute review. False positives, bias, privacy, data retention, and regulatory requirements are monitored. A held refund does not become a forfeiture or revenue entry.
