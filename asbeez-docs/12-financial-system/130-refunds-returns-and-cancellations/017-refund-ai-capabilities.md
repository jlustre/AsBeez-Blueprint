# Refund AI Capabilities

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/130-refunds-returns-and-cancellations/017-refund-ai-capabilities.md

---

## Purpose

AI may assist refunds with eligibility explanation, anomaly detection, fraud review, reconciliation matching, support routing, and return-risk forecasting while deterministic policy and human rights controls remain authoritative.

## Permitted Assistance

- explain refund eligibility and missing evidence;
- identify duplicate, unusual, or coordinated refund patterns;
- propose reconciliation matches and exception priority;
- forecast refund, reserve, chargeback, and vendor/partner exposure; and
- recommend review routing without deciding lawful entitlement alone.

## Prohibited Actions

AI must not directly approve/deny a refund, alter amount, route money, issue credit notes, reverse rewards, adjust vendor/partner balances, bypass consumer rights, or create revenue/GL effects.

## Governance

Recommendations include model/version, evidence scope, confidence, reason, provenance, policy context, reviewer decision, and appeal/communication record. Payment, identity, vendor, reward, and customer data is minimized and access-controlled.
