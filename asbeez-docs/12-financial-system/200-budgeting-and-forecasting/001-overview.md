## Purpose

Budgeting and Forecasting provides structured plans and forward-looking estimates for AsBeez revenue, costs, liquidity, reserves, rewards, vendor/partner obligations, country operations, capital investment, and strategic decisions.

## AsBeez Boundary

Budgets and forecasts are planning views, not General Ledger truth, invoices, payables, wallet balances, payout approvals, or cash transfers. They must distinguish GMV, recognized revenue, customer collections, vendor/partner obligations, commissions, taxes, rewards, reserves, expenses, and cash.

## Principles

- every plan has scope, owner, period, entity, country, currency, dimensions, assumptions, and version;
- actuals derive from reconciled ledger/subledger/reporting data;
- forecasts identify confirmed, scheduled, expected, estimated, restricted, and stressed values;
- scenarios do not alter accounting history or authorize transactions; and
- revisions preserve prior versions, approvals, reasons, and variance explanations.
