# Expense Forecasting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/200-budgeting-and-forecasting/009-expense-forecasting.md

---

## Purpose

Expense forecasting estimates future operating, capital, payment, provider, payout, tax, reward, refund, chargeback, vendor/partner, reserve, compliance, and country costs.

## Drivers

Volume, headcount, contracts, provider rates, infrastructure, marketing, support, fraud, refunds, chargebacks, rewards, reserves, FX, tax, vendor/partner programs, country rollout, and approved commitments.

## Rules

Forecast expense is distinct from budget, commitment, accrual, cash payment, and posted GL expense. Estimates include assumptions, confidence, timing, currency, entity, country, policy/model version, and owner. AI or scenario estimates do not authorize procurement or payment.
