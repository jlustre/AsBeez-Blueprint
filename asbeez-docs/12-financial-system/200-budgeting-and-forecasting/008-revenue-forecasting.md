# Revenue Forecasting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/200-budgeting-and-forecasting/008-revenue-forecasting.md

---

## Purpose

Revenue forecasting estimates future AsBeez recognized revenue and related drivers by stream, entity, country, currency, product, channel, vendor, partner, and period.

## Drivers

Orders, GMV, conversion, customer activity, platform fees, subscriptions, services, advertising, provider/payment costs, refunds, chargebacks, vendor/partner obligations, tax, recognition schedules, and country expansion.

## Rules

Forecasts distinguish GMV, customer collections, vendor/partner proceeds, platform fees, deferred/accrued revenue, recognized revenue, and reward effects. Forecasts carry assumptions, confidence, source, policy/model version, currency, entity, and freshness; they do not alter revenue recognition or authorize spending.
