# Scenario Planning

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/200-budgeting-and-forecasting/011-scenario-planning.md

---

## Purpose

Scenario planning models alternate assumptions and outcomes without mutating actuals, approved budgets, or posted accounting records.

## Scenarios

Base, downside, upside, new-country launch, vendor/partner growth, reward-liability change, refund/chargeback surge, provider delay, FX movement, payout acceleration, tax change, liquidity stress, and infrastructure investment.

## Required Data

Scenario ID, owner, assumptions, drivers, affected entities/countries/currencies, horizon, model version, confidence, outputs, comparison baseline, approval, and decision notes.

## Rules

Scenarios distinguish sensitivity from forecast and do not represent commitments. Outputs cannot be presented as actual revenue, cash, earnings, liability, or guaranteed result.
