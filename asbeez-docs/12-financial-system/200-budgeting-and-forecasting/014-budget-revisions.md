# Budget Revisions

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/200-budgeting-and-forecasting/014-budget-revisions.md

---

## Purpose

Budget revisions update an approved plan or forecast while preserving prior versions, approvals, assumptions, allocations, and variance history.

## Required Data

Original version, revised version, reason, trigger, affected lines/dimensions, amount/currency changes, effective period, source evidence, impact, owner, approver, and publication time.

## Rules

Revisions do not rewrite actuals, posted accounting, invoices, payables, wallets, reserves, or cash. A revision may change future planning or forecast values only after approval. Emergency revisions are time-limited, reasoned, and reviewed.

## Publication Controls

The revised version records prior/current totals, affected lines and dimensions, variance impact, forecast/cash impact, approval, effective period, publication time, and rollback or superseded status.
