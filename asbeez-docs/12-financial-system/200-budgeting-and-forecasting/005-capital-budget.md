# Capital Budget

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/200-budgeting-and-forecasting/005-capital-budget.md

---

## Purpose

The Capital Budget plans approved investments in technology, infrastructure, equipment, software, data, security, facilities, or acquisitions that may create assets or long-term capability.

## Required Data

Project, owner, business case, amount, currency, entity, country, timing, funding source, capitalization policy, expected benefit, risk, approval, procurement, and actual/commitment status.

## Rules

Capital budget approval does not capitalize an asset or authorize cash transfer by itself. Accounting follows asset capitalization, impairment, depreciation/amortization, procurement, tax, and period policies. Projects must track approved, committed, spent, forecast, variance, and benefits separately.
