# Cash Flow Forecasting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/200-budgeting-and-forecasting/010-cash-flow-forecasting.md

---

## Purpose

Cash-flow forecasting estimates timing and currency of cash inflows/outflows and liquidity gaps using treasury, order, payment, settlement, payout, tax, reserve, refund, chargeback, revenue, and expense schedules.

## Components

- customer and provider collections;
- vendor/partner settlements and payouts;
- refunds, chargebacks, and reserves;
- taxes and remittances;
- operating/capital spending;
- subscriptions, fees, advertising, and services; and
- FX, transfers, bank timing, and country restrictions.

## Rules

Forecast separates confirmed, scheduled, expected, estimated, restricted, and stressed cash. It preserves currency/entity/country and assumptions, reconciles actuals to bank/provider/GL evidence, and never authorizes a transfer or changes a payable.
