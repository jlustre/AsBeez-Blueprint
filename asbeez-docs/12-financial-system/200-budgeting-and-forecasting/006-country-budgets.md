# Country Budgets

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/200-budgeting-and-forecasting/006-country-budgets.md

---

## Purpose

Country budgets plan revenue, costs, liquidity, taxes, reserves, payouts, vendors, partners, rewards, and investments for a country/entity under local currency and policy.

## Rules

Each country budget retains local currency, entity, tax, banking, FX, regulatory, reserve, payout, and reporting assumptions and maps to global reporting. Consolidation does not erase local statutory detail. Country budget approval cannot enable a country feature without Legal, Tax, Compliance, Finance, and operational launch approval.

## Monitoring

Track approved plan, actuals, commitments, forecast, FX impact, tax/reserve exposure, liquidity, vendor/partner obligations, reward programs, payout capacity, and variance by country, entity, currency, and policy version.
