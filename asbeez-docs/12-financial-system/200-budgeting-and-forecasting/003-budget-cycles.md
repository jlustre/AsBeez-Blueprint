# Budget Cycles

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/200-budgeting-and-forecasting/003-budget-cycles.md

---

## Purpose

Budget cycles define the calendar, stages, owners, assumptions, submission, review, approval, publication, monitoring, and revision of AsBeez plans.

## Stages

```text
Design -> Submission -> Review -> Challenge -> Approval -> Published -> Monitored -> Closed
```

## Rules

Cycle scope includes entity, country, currency, department, product, channel, program, and reporting calendar. Published budgets are versioned and immutable; revisions require reason, impact, effective period, and approval. Cycle approval does not authorize individual spend, payout, transfer, or reward activity.

## Inputs and Monitoring

Cycle inputs include reconciled actuals, prior budget, revenue/expense drivers, cash/liquidity, reserves, tax, vendor/partner obligations, rewards, FX, country plans, and strategic assumptions. Monitoring records submissions, approvals, revisions, variance, owner, and publication status.
