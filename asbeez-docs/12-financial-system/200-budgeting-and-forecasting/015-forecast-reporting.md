# Forecast Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/200-budgeting-and-forecasting/015-forecast-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Forecast reporting presents approved budgets, current forecasts, scenarios, actuals, commitments, variances, assumptions, and confidence by entity, country, currency, department, product, channel, program, and period.

## Report Families

- GMV, recognized revenue, collections, vendor/partner obligations, commissions, rewards, and tax;
- operating/capital expense, commitments, reserves, refunds, chargebacks, and payouts;
- cash flow, liquidity, FX, treasury, provider settlement, and country plans;
- budget-versus-actual, forecast-versus-actual, scenario, variance, and risk views; and
- certified planning packages and management dashboards.

## Rules

Every report labels actual/forecast/scenario, source, freshness, assumptions, confidence, model/version, currency, entity, country, and reconciliation status. Forecasts are not called actual revenue, cash, profit, payable, or guaranteed outcome.
