# Budget AI Capabilities

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/200-budgeting-and-forecasting/018-budget-ai-capabilities.md

---

## Purpose

AI may assist budgeting with driver analysis, scenario generation, anomaly detection, forecast explanation, variance prioritization, and liquidity/revenue/expense forecasting while approved models and human governance remain authoritative.

## Permitted Assistance

- identify drivers and unusual variance;
- suggest scenarios, assumptions, or forecast ranges;
- explain budget/actual/forecast differences;
- prioritize review and reporting exceptions; and
- forecast revenue, expenses, cash, reserves, rewards, taxes, and FX under disclosed assumptions.

## Prohibited Actions

AI must not publish a budget, approve material revisions, alter actuals, post accounting, authorize spending/payouts/transfers, release reserves, or present forecast as guaranteed revenue, cash, profit, or income.

## Governance

Recommendations include model/version, data scope, assumptions, confidence, reason, provenance, policy context, human decision, and revision history. Financial, personnel, vendor, partner, customer, and strategic data is minimized and access-controlled.
