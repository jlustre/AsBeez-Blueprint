# Budget Approvals

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/200-budgeting-and-forecasting/013-budget-approvals.md

---

## Purpose

Budget approvals authorize a plan, allocation, scenario, revision, or forecast for planning and governance purposes.

## Controls

Approval records scope, amount, currency, entity, country, department/project, assumptions, funding, policy, reviewer, approver, time, decision, and conditions. Material or cross-entity plans require Finance leadership and relevant Tax, Treasury, Legal, or business approval.

## Rules

Budget approval does not authorize individual payment, payout, transfer, purchase, reserve release, commission, reward, or accounting posting. Separation of duties applies, and rejected/expired approvals remain auditable.

## Approval States

```text
Draft -> Submitted -> Under Review -> Approved -> Published
Submitted -> Rejected | Returned | Expired
```

Approval conditions, exceptions, delegated authority, and post-publication monitoring are retained with the plan version.
