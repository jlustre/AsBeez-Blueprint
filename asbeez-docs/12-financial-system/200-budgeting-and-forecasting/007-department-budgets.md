# Department Budgets

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/200-budgeting-and-forecasting/007-department-budgets.md

---

## Purpose

Department budgets assign approved operating or capital resources to accountable teams, functions, projects, products, or programs.

## Rules

Each line has owner, cost center/project, account, entity, country, currency, period, funding source, and approval. Department budgets do not override procurement, payment, payout, reserve, tax, security, or period-close controls. Shared costs use a documented allocation driver and preserve source totals.

## Monitoring

Report budget, commitments, actuals, forecast, remaining, variance, transfers, and revisions by department, cost center, project, account, country, entity, and period.

Material variances require owner, explanation, corrective action, due date, approval, and link to source actuals or commitments.
