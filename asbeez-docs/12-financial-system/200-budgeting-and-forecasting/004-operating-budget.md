# Operating Budget

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/200-budgeting-and-forecasting/004-operating-budget.md

---

## Purpose

The Operating Budget plans recurring and controllable costs and operating income for AsBeez entities, countries, departments, products, channels, and programs.

## Scope

People, support, compliance, technology, cloud, payment/provider fees, marketing, vendor/partner acquisition, rewards/promotions, refunds/chargebacks, tax operations, treasury, and administration are separately classified.

## Rules

Budget lines map to Chart of Accounts and dimensions but do not post the GL. Actuals compare to reconciled entries, while commitments and forecasts remain distinct. Reward, vendor, partner, reserve, and charity spending require their own approved funding and policy.
