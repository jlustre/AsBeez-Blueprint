# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/200-budgeting-and-forecasting/019-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve planning calendars, entities, countries, currencies, dimensions, revenue/cost drivers, and governance;
- implement operating/capital/country/department budgets and versioned cycles;
- implement revenue, expense, cash, liquidity, reserve, reward, vendor/partner, tax, and FX forecasts;
- implement scenarios, variance, approvals, revisions, reporting, API, events, security, and audit; and
- connect actuals to reconciled ledger/subledger/revenue/treasury reporting.

## Medium Term

- automate driver updates, actual refresh, variance classification, and forecast reconciliation;
- support multi-entity planning and country launch scenarios;
- improve liquidity, revenue, expense, reward-liability, reserve, FX, and payout forecasting; and
- add explainable AI assistance with human approval.

## Long Term

- support regional planning services and high-volume scenario modeling;
- provide governed business-unit and partner planning integrations;
- strengthen resilience, capital allocation, and stress testing; and
- preserve portable assumptions, approvals, and forecast evidence across periods.

## Roadmap Gate

No budget or forecast feature may present a plan as actual, authorize a financial movement, alter posted history, or classify RP/ABC/AHC, vendor/partner, tax, reserve, or cash values without approved scope, assumptions, policy, authority, reconciliation, and audit rules.
