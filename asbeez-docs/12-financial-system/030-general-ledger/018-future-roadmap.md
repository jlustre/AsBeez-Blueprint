# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/030-general-ledger/018-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- publish the initial account register and journal templates;
- implement posting validation, idempotency, outbox publication, and trial balance;
- connect payments, billing, settlements, tax, rewards, and reconciliation;
- define close checklists and adjustment approval; and
- monitor clearing, suspense, projection lag, and unmatched balances.

## Medium Term

- support multiple legal entities and localized statutory reporting;
- automate subledger control totals and provider reconciliation;
- add certified report snapshots and chart-version reporting;
- improve partitioning, archival, and recovery operations; and
- provide controlled finance administrator workflows.

## Long Term

- support regional ledger services and high-volume event ingestion;
- provide explainable anomaly detection and reconciliation assistance;
- strengthen intercompany consolidation and multi-book accounting; and
- preserve portable audit evidence across infrastructure changes.

## Roadmap Gate

No performance, automation, or AI improvement may weaken immutability, double-entry balancing, auditability, period controls, or source traceability.
