# Ledger Retention

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/030-general-ledger/017-ledger-retention.md

---

## Purpose

Ledger retention preserves posted entries, source references, approvals, audit evidence, reconciliation records, and report snapshots for the longest applicable accounting, tax, legal, regulatory, dispute, and contractual period.

## Retention Rules

- Retention schedules are jurisdiction- and record-type-aware.
- A legal hold suspends deletion or destruction for affected evidence.
- Archived data remains integrity-protected, discoverable, and access-controlled.
- Personal data is minimized, masked, or redacted only when doing so does not violate financial evidence requirements.
- Destruction requires approved authorization, a schedule reference, scope, date, and audit record.

## Recovery and Verification

Backups and archives are encrypted, access-controlled, geographically appropriate, and tested for restoration. Integrity verification confirms that journal totals, source references, and audit chains remain usable after migration or restore.
