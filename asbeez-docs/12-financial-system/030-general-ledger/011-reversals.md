# Reversals

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/030-general-ledger/011-reversals.md

---

## Purpose

A reversal creates a new entry that negates a prior posted entry, usually because the original accounting effect was valid for a period but should no longer remain in the current state.

## Appropriate Uses

- automatic reversal of a period-end accrual;
- cancellation of a temporary estimate;
- reversal of a provisional recognition when the source event is voided; or
- correction where the original entry is fully and demonstrably negated.

## Rules

The reversal references the original entry, preserves its account and dimension scope unless an approved policy says otherwise, records reason and effective date, and is itself balanced and immutable. A reversal is not a deletion and does not erase the original from reports or audit history.

## Timing

Reversals may post in the same period, a future period, or an approved adjustment period. The selected timing must be explicit and must not conceal a closed-period error.
