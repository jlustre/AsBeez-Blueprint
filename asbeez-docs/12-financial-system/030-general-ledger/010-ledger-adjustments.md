# Ledger Adjustments

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/030-general-ledger/010-ledger-adjustments.md

---

## Purpose

An adjustment is a controlled financial entry that corrects classification, timing, valuation, accrual, or other accounting treatment without changing posted history.

## Adjustment Types

- accrual and deferral;
- reclassification;
- currency revaluation;
- tax correction;
- estimate or allowance update;
- period-end close adjustment; and
- approved error correction.

## Required Evidence

Every adjustment includes reason code, affected source or prior entry, preparer, approver, accounting policy, effective and posting dates, accounts, dimensions, calculation, supporting evidence, and reversal or settlement plan where applicable.

## Controls

Adjustments to closed periods require authorized restatement or adjustment-period treatment. Manual journal access is restricted and monitored. Adjustments cannot be used to bypass payment, settlement, tax, or reward workflows.
