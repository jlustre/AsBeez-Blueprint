# Risk AI Capabilities

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/250-fraud-risk-and-controls/022-risk-ai-capabilities.md

---

## Purpose

AI may assist fraud and risk operations with anomaly detection, scoring, case prioritization, evidence retrieval, graph analysis, loss forecasting, and control explanation while human, legal, compliance, and deterministic controls remain authoritative.

## Permitted Assistance

- identify patterns and related subjects;
- suggest risk score or review priority;
- retrieve and summarize evidence;
- forecast exposure, loss, recovery, and control drift; and
- recommend, but not execute, holds, reviews, or remediation.

## Prohibited Actions

AI must not independently label fraud, deny lawful rights, fabricate evidence, set final score thresholds, release/freeze funds, alter accounting, close cases, or expose sensitive data.

## Governance

Outputs include model/version, data scope, features/signals, confidence, reason, provenance, fairness/drift metrics, human decision, appeal, and audit trail. Financial, identity, behavioral, vendor, partner, and customer data is minimized and access-controlled.
