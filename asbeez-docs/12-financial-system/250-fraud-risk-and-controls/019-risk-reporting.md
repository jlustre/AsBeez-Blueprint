# Risk Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/250-fraud-risk-and-controls/019-risk-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Risk reporting presents fraud signals, cases, holds, freezes, losses, recoveries, reserves, disputes, refunds, payouts, rewards, vendor/partner exposure, tax/compliance events, control performance, and residual risk.

## Report Families

- alerts, scores, thresholds, false positives, review, appeals, and SLA;
- payment, wallet, payout, refund, chargeback, reward, vendor, partner, tax, reserve, and treasury risk;
- realized loss, expected exposure, reserve coverage, recovery, prevention, and control effectiveness; and
- country/entity/provider/model/rule/drift and regulatory reporting.

## Rules

Reports label signal versus confirmed outcome, estimate versus realized loss, currency, entity, country, policy/model version, source, confidence, privacy classification, and reconciliation status.
