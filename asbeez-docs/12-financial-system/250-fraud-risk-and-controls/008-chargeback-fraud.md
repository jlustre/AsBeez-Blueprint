# Chargeback Fraud

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/250-fraud-risk-and-controls/008-chargeback-fraud.md

---

## Purpose

Chargeback fraud controls identify friendly fraud, collusion, fabricated disputes, repeated abuse, provider manipulation, and evidence tampering while preserving due process.

## Rules

Signals support evidence collection, reserve/hold, representment, case review, or escalation. They do not prove liability or authorize loss. Case, evidence, provider outcome, vendor/partner/platform decision, reward, reserve, and GL effects remain separate and reconciled.

## Measures

Track case rate, amount, reason, age, evidence completeness, representment outcome, reserve coverage, win/loss, recovery, realized loss, false positives, and vendor/partner/platform allocation.
