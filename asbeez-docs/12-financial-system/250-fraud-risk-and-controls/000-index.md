## Design Authority

Fraud, Risk, and Controls owns risk signals, scoring, monitoring, holds, cases, evidence, control decisions, losses, recoveries, and risk reporting. Payments, Refunds, Disputes, Wallets, Payouts, Rewards, Vendor/Partner Finance, Tax, Reserves, Treasury, Reconciliation, and the General Ledger provide or consume controlled facts.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Approve risk domains, countries, entities, data, retention, models, rules, thresholds, controls, and appeals.
2. Implement transaction/payment/wallet/payout/refund/dispute/reward/vendor/partner/account/tax/treasury controls.
3. Implement monitoring, scoring, holds, cases, evidence, loss/recovery, reconciliation, reporting, API, events, and audit.
4. Add governed AI, model risk, fairness, drift, and regional risk integrations.
