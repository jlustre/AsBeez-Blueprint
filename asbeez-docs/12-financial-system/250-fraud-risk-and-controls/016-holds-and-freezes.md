# Holds And Freezes

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/250-fraud-risk-and-controls/016-holds-and-freezes.md

---

## Purpose

Holds and freezes restrict selected financial or account operations while risk, fraud, dispute, compliance, security, or operational review occurs.

## Required Data

Action ID, subject, scope, amount/unit/currency, reason, signal/evidence, rule/model version, owner, start/review/expiry, affected operations, communication, appeal, release, and outcome.

## Rules

A hold/freeze does not change ownership, balance, liability, or historical records and is not proof of fraud. It is proportionate, time-bound, reviewable, and released only by authorized evidence-based decision. Emergency action receives post-event review.

## Monitoring

Track action scope, amount/unit, duration, review SLA, release rate, escalation, appeal, customer/participant impact, false positives, and unresolved aging by country/entity and risk type.
