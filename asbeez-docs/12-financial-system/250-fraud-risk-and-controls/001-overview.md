## Purpose

Fraud, Risk, and Controls protect AsBeez customers, members, vendors, partners, funds, rewards, payments, wallets, payouts, refunds, and financial records through monitoring, prevention, investigation, and governed intervention.

## AsBeez Boundary

A risk signal is not proof of fraud, liability, policy breach, or customer fault. Controls may recommend authentication, hold, review, freeze, rejection, or escalation, but decisions require evidence, policy, authority, proportionality, privacy, and appeal handling.

## Principles

- protect legitimate customer/vendor/partner/member activity and lawful refunds;
- separate fraud signals from accounting, tax, payout, and liability decisions;
- preserve immutable financial and operational history;
- use explainable reason codes, confidence, evidence, model/rule version, and human review;
- apply country, entity, privacy, sanctions, KYC/KYB, and retention rules; and
- reconcile losses, recoveries, holds, reserves, rewards, payouts, and adjustments.
