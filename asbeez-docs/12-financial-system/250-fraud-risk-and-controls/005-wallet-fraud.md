# Wallet Fraud

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/250-fraud-risk-and-controls/005-wallet-fraud.md

---

## Purpose

Wallet fraud controls protect monetary wallets, reward display quantities, transfers, refunds, redemptions, holds, and payout destinations from takeover, manipulation, replay, collusion, and unauthorized use.

## Rules

Monitor login/device, destination changes, velocity, balance movements, reward conversion, refund-to-wallet, transfer relationships, and account recovery. Holds/freezes preserve balances and history; they do not prove fraud or confiscate value. RP/ABC/AHC quantities remain separate from monetary wallet analysis.

## Response Evidence

Wallet responses record signal, scope, score/confidence, rule/model version, hold/freeze action, owner, review deadline, customer communication, appeal, release, and resolution without exposing sensitive detection logic.
