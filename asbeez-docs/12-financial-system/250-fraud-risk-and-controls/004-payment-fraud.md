# Payment Fraud

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/250-fraud-risk-and-controls/004-payment-fraud.md

---

## Purpose

Payment fraud controls detect unauthorized payment use, stolen credentials, synthetic identity, card testing, transaction laundering, provider abuse, and suspicious funding behavior.

## Rules

Signals may trigger authentication, hold, decline, provider review, dispute evidence, or case creation. They do not prove fraud or authorize chargeback loss. Payment state, customer rights, refunds, vendor/partner effects, rewards, and GL remain separate workflows.

## Evidence

Retain attempt, method/provider reference, authentication, device/session, amount/currency, country, order, account, rule/model, reason, decision, reviewer, and appeal/outcome with privacy controls.
