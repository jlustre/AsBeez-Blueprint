# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/250-fraud-risk-and-controls/023-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve risk domains, countries, entities, data, retention, models, rules, thresholds, controls, and appeals;
- implement transaction, payment, wallet, payout, refund, chargeback, reward, vendor, partner, account, tax, and treasury risk;
- implement monitoring, scoring, holds, cases, evidence, loss/recovery, reporting, API, events, security, and audit; and
- connect risk decisions to reconciliation, reserves, payouts, refunds, rewards, and close without bypassing ownership.

## Medium Term

- automate evidence, case routing, control testing, model drift, and loss/recovery analytics;
- support country-specific risk and regulatory reporting;
- improve graph, velocity, anomaly, and liquidity-risk analysis; and
- add explainable AI assistance with human approval.

## Long Term

- support regional risk services and high-volume real-time controls;
- provide governed provider, vendor, partner, and regulator integrations;
- strengthen fairness, privacy, resilience, and scenario testing; and
- preserve portable decision and evidence history across jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No risk feature may deny rights, assign liability, freeze funds, alter accounting, reverse rewards, or close a case without evidence, policy, proportionality, authority, appeal, reconciliation, and audit controls.
