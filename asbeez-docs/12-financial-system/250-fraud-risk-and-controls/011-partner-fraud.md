# Partner Fraud

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/250-fraud-risk-and-controls/011-partner-fraud.md

---

## Purpose

Partner fraud controls identify fabricated attribution, self-referral, collusion, recruitment-only activity, duplicate participants, false service delivery, commission/bonus/incentive manipulation, and payout abuse.

## Rules

Registration, genealogy, or referral count is not proof of qualifying value. Controls may hold or review obligations, but liability and payable decisions require evidence, program policy, funding, approval, and appeal.

## Measures

Track attribution integrity, duplicate participants, qualifying activity, commission/bonus/incentive holds, payout velocity, confirmed abuse, recovery, false positives, and partner appeal outcomes.
