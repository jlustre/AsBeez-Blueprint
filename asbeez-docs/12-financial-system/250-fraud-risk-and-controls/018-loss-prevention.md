# Loss Prevention

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/250-fraud-risk-and-controls/018-loss-prevention.md

---

## Purpose

Loss prevention reduces expected fraud, dispute, refund, payout, reward, vendor/partner, tax, and operational loss while preserving lawful customer and participant outcomes.

## Controls

Authentication, limits, holds, reserves, verification, evidence, provider controls, reconciliation, education, recovery, insurance, and operational remediation are measured by prevention, false positives, customer impact, recovery, and residual loss.

## Rules

Loss prevention cannot hide loss through reserve, classification, fee, refund denial, reward reversal, or vendor deduction. Realized loss, expected exposure, reserve, recovery, and customer/vendor/partner liability remain separate and reconciled.
