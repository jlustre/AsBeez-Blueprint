# Risk Scoring

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/250-fraud-risk-and-controls/014-risk-scoring.md

---

## Purpose

Risk scoring prioritizes transaction, account, payment, wallet, payout, refund, dispute, reward, vendor, partner, tax, and treasury review.

## Score Contract

Score ID/version, subject/event, signals, weights/model, confidence, threshold, action recommendation, country/entity, time, evidence, explanation, reviewer, and outcome.

## Rules

Scores are decision support, not proof of fraud, liability, or denial. Threshold changes are governed and tested for fairness, false positives, drift, privacy, and country impact. Material actions require authorized human or deterministic policy review.

## Monitoring

Track score distribution, calibration, precision/recall where appropriate, drift, threshold performance, override, appeal, false-positive, loss, recovery, and fairness outcomes by country/entity and subject class.
