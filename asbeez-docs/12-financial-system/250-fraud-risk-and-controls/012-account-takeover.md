# Account Takeover

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/250-fraud-risk-and-controls/012-account-takeover.md

---

## Purpose

Account takeover controls protect customer, member, vendor, partner, wallet, payment, reward, and payout accounts from unauthorized access or recovery.

## Signals and Controls

Login/device/session change, credential reset, MFA failure, destination change, unusual location/velocity, support recovery, payment/refund/payout/reward activity, and relationship anomalies may trigger step-up, lock, hold, review, or recovery.

## Rules

Controls preserve access and financial history, minimize sensitive detection disclosure, and require identity/ownership evidence for release. A security signal does not prove fraud or authorize reversal without separate case and financial workflows.
