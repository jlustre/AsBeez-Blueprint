# Refund Fraud

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/250-fraud-risk-and-controls/007-refund-fraud.md

---

## Purpose

Refund fraud controls identify duplicate refunds, account takeover, refund-to-wallet abuse, return abuse, friendly fraud, collusion, and manipulation of vendor, partner, reward, or payout effects.

## Rules

Signals recommend review, hold, limit, or escalation; they do not independently deny lawful refund rights. Original refund/order/payment/reward/vendor/partner history remains immutable, and each decision records evidence, rule/model version, reviewer, communication, and appeal.

## Measures

Track duplicate rate, refund velocity, amount, destination, reason, account/device relationship, false positives, review time, recovered loss, and customer/vendor impact.
