# Reward Abuse

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/250-fraud-risk-and-controls/009-reward-abuse.md

---

## Purpose

Reward abuse controls identify duplicate accounts, self-referrals, fabricated commerce, refund cycling, matrix manipulation, AHC exploitation, bonus abuse, and attempts to convert non-monetary program units into unauthorized money.

## Rules

Signals may hold, review, reverse, or disqualify events under approved Rewards and legal policy. RP, ABC, matrix, and AHC history is never deleted. Monetary liability, wallet, payout, vendor/partner, and accounting effects are evaluated separately.

## Measures

Track duplicate/self-referral patterns, qualifying-order integrity, RP reversal, ABC/matrix anomalies, AHC exposure, bonus abuse, holds, appeals, confirmed abuse, false positives, and financial impact.
