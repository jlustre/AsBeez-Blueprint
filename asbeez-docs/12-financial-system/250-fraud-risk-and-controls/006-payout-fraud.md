# Payout Fraud

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/250-fraud-risk-and-controls/006-payout-fraud.md

---

## Purpose

Payout fraud controls protect withdrawal requests, destinations, vendor/partner settlements, member wallets, reward conversions, reserves, and provider transfers from unauthorized or manipulated payment.

## Rules

Monitor destination changes, cooling-off, velocity, beneficiary relationship, identity, country, tax, source payable, reserve/hold, device, provider, and return patterns. A payout hold is not proof of fraud or final liability; release requires evidence, revalidation, and authority.

## Response States

Allow, step-up, hold, review, reject, freeze, submit, return, and release decisions retain reason, evidence, rule/model version, owner, deadline, appeal, and outcome.
