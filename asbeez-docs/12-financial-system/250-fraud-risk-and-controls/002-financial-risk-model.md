# Financial Risk Model

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/250-fraud-risk-and-controls/002-financial-risk-model.md

---

## Purpose

The Financial Risk Model classifies threats, exposure, controls, decisions, losses, recoveries, and residual risk across the AsBeez ecosystem.

## Risk Domains

Transaction/payment fraud, account takeover, wallet/payout abuse, refund/chargeback abuse, reward abuse, vendor/partner collusion, tax/compliance risk, liquidity/FX risk, provider risk, data/security risk, and operational control failure.

## Risk Record

Risk ID, subject/event, type, amount/currency, entity/country, signal, evidence, likelihood, impact, score, rule/model version, control, decision, owner, review date, action, appeal, loss/recovery, and audit references.

## Rules

Risk score supports prioritization and control selection; it is not a final finding. High-impact actions require human/authorized review, reason, evidence, proportionality, and documented outcome.
