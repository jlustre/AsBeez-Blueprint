# Vendor Fraud

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/250-fraud-risk-and-controls/010-vendor-fraud.md

---

## Purpose

Vendor fraud controls identify false sales, collusion, return/refund abuse, counterfeit or misrepresented goods, fake fulfillment, payment manipulation, fee evasion, and settlement/payout abuse.

## Rules

Signals may place holds/reserves, require evidence, restrict listings/payouts, or escalate. They do not establish vendor fault without investigation and policy. Vendor earnings, settlement, tax, rewards, reserves, and customer rights remain separate workflows.

## Measures

Track order/refund/return/chargeback rates, fulfillment evidence, payout holds, reserve coverage, customer complaints, collusion signals, confirmed loss, recovery, and vendor appeal outcomes.
