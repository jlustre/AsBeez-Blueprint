# FX Gains And Losses

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/170-multi-currency-and-fx/011-fx-gains-and-losses.md

---

## Purpose

FX gains and losses record the difference created by currency movement between recognition, settlement, payment, conversion, or revaluation of a monetary balance.

## Types

- realized gain/loss on settlement or conversion;
- unrealized gain/loss from period-end revaluation; and
- rounding or provider difference under approved policy.

## Rules

Gain/loss is distinct from FX fee, platform revenue, vendor/partner entitlement, tax, reserve, reward, or customer charge. Each entry references original amount/currency, carrying amount, settlement amount/rate, valuation rate, source, period, entity, and policy. Historical source amounts are never changed.
