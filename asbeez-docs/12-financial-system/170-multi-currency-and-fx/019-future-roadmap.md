# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/170-multi-currency-and-fx/019-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve currencies, entities, countries, providers, base/functional/reporting contexts, and FX policies;
- implement rate sourcing, locks, conversion, fees, rounding, settlement, and revaluation;
- connect payments, invoices, wallets, vendor/partner settlement, payouts, tax, rewards, and GL mappings;
- implement reconciliation, risk limits, API, events, security, audit, and reporting; and
- establish provider fallback, exception, and liquidity operations.

## Medium Term

- support localized corridors and multi-entity treasury;
- automate provider comparison, rate validation, reconciliation, and revaluation;
- improve FX exposure, gain/loss, liquidity, and settlement forecasting; and
- add explainable AI assistance with human approval.

## Long Term

- support regional FX services and high-volume conversion;
- provide governed provider, bank, vendor, partner, and payout integrations;
- strengthen scenario planning and risk controls; and
- preserve portable conversion and valuation evidence across jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No FX feature may change source currency, apply a rate, create a fee, recognize gain/loss, settle a cross-border obligation, or post accounting without approved currency, rate, source, timestamp, rounding, tax, compliance, reconciliation, and audit rules.
