# FX AI Capabilities

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/170-multi-currency-and-fx/018-fx-ai-capabilities.md

---

## Purpose

AI may assist FX operations with rate anomaly detection, provider comparison, reconciliation, risk forecasting, liquidity planning, and explanation while deterministic rate, conversion, accounting, tax, and treasury controls remain authoritative.

## Permitted Assistance

- identify stale, outlier, conflicting, or manipulated rates;
- suggest provider/routing or rate-lock review;
- propose reconciliation matches and exception priority;
- forecast exposure, gains/losses, liquidity, and settlement timing; and
- explain conversion and revaluation evidence.

## Prohibited Actions

AI must not independently choose an accounting rate, alter historical conversion, approve a material lock, execute transfer, hide gain/loss, bypass risk/tax/compliance, or create FX fee/GL effects.

## Governance

Recommendations include model/version, evidence scope, confidence, reason, provenance, policy context, human decision, and audit trail. Financial, treasury, provider, and customer data is minimized and access-controlled.
