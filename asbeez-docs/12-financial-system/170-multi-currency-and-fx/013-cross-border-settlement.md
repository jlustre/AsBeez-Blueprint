# Cross Border Settlement

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/170-multi-currency-and-fx/013-cross-border-settlement.md

---

## Purpose

Cross-border settlement manages orders, vendor/partner obligations, payouts, taxes, fees, reserves, compliance, and FX when parties, entities, providers, or beneficiaries are in different countries or currencies.

## Required Detail

Source/settlement currencies, rate/lock, fee/spread, parties, legal entities, countries, tax jurisdictions, sanctions/compliance, payment/provider, settlement window, reserves, payable, and reconciliation references.

## Rules

Cross-border settlement does not change original transaction facts or hide tax, vendor, partner, reward, or payout obligations. Each conversion, withholding, fee, gain/loss, and provider movement is explicit. Unsupported corridors, stale rates, missing registrations, or failed compliance block or hold settlement.
