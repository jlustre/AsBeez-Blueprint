# Supported Currencies

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/170-multi-currency-and-fx/003-supported-currencies.md

---

## Purpose

Supported currencies define the monetary units AsBeez may display, price, invoice, collect, settle, store in wallets, report, or use for accounting.

## Required Data

ISO code, name, exponent, minor unit, status, country/market availability, entity support, payment/provider support, tax/reporting rules, effective dates, and conversion restrictions.

## Rules

Currency activation requires Finance, Tax, Compliance, Payments, and operational approval. Unsupported, suspended, obsolete, or restricted currencies block new transactions while preserving historical records and conversions.

## AsBeez Use

Marketplace pricing, invoice currency, customer payment, vendor/partner settlement, wallet currency, payout currency, and reporting currency may differ only under explicit policy and recorded conversion.
