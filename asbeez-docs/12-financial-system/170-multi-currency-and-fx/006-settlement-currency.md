# Settlement Currency

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/170-multi-currency-and-fx/006-settlement-currency.md

---

## Purpose

Settlement currency is the currency in which an approved vendor, partner, provider, wallet, treasury, or payout obligation is settled.

## Rules

Settlement currency may differ from transaction currency only under disclosed policy and approved conversion. The settlement record retains original amount/currency, target amount/currency, rate, spread, fee, source, timestamp, rounding, beneficiary, country, entity, and tax context.

## AsBeez Applications

Vendor/partner settlements, provider settlements, payout methods, member wallets, reserves, refunds, and cross-border orders may each have distinct currency rules. Settlement currency does not change revenue, tax, reward, or payable classification.
