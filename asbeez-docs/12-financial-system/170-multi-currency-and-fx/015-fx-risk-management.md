# FX Risk Management

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/170-multi-currency-and-fx/015-fx-risk-management.md

---

## Purpose

FX risk management controls exposure from currency volatility, rate uncertainty, provider spreads, settlement timing, corridor restrictions, liquidity, and revaluation.

## Risk Areas

- open receivable/payable and wallet exposure;
- vendor/partner settlement and payout currency mismatch;
- tax and reserve currency exposure;
- rate lock expiry and provider concentration;
- cross-border liquidity and timing; and
- unsupported, stale, or manipulated rates.

## Controls

Limits, approval thresholds, rate freshness, provider diversification, hedge/treasury policy, scenario analysis, exception alerts, and period-end revaluation are effective-dated and auditable. Risk controls cannot rewrite transaction currency or hide realized loss.
