# Chargeback Settings

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/390-configuration/012-chargeback-settings.md

---

## Purpose

Chargeback settings define provider reason/stage mapping, evidence deadlines, response routing, liability, reserves, fees, recovery, escalation, retention, and reconciliation policy.

## Rules

Settings cannot fabricate evidence, suppress deadlines, predetermine liability outside approved policy, release reserves, or alter posted accounting. Provider state remains evidence until approved mapping. Country, entity, currency, legal, dispute, and retention rules are explicit.

## Change Control

Changes require dispute/legal/risk/accounting review, deadline and reserve impact, provider contract tests, effective date, approval, monitoring, rollback, and audit evidence.
