# Period Close Settings

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/390-configuration/017-period-close-settings.md

---

## Purpose

Period-close settings define calendars, cutoff, posting windows, close tasks, accrual/deferral policy, adjustment types, reconciliation requirements, approval thresholds, lock/reopen authority, and certification.

## Rules

Settings are entity/country/calendar scoped and effective-dated. They cannot allow ordinary posting to locked periods, suppress unresolved critical differences, or certify without trial balance, reconciliations, evidence, and segregation of duties. Reopen policy requires reason, authority, impact, correction, and re-close.

## Change Control

Changes require accounting, tax, treasury, reporting, operations, and audit impact review; representative close testing; effective date; approval; communication; and historical policy preservation.
