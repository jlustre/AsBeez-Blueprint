# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/390-configuration/023-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- establish typed configuration, ownership, scope, policy/version, approval, testing, deployment, monitoring, rollback, expiry, and audit standards;
- implement global/country/currency/rounding, ledger, wallet, payment, payout, invoice, refund, dispute, fee, tax, reserve, reconciliation, close, reporting, risk, AI, and feature settings; and
- connect configuration to APIs, events, administration, operations, observability, testing, and recovery.

## Medium Term

- add configuration impact analysis, dependency graphs, staged/canary rollout, country/entity certification, automated drift detection, and policy simulation;
- improve provider routing, risk/AI governance, report/metric versioning, and safe operational flagging; and
- provide self-service proposals with approval and separation of duties.

## Long Term

- support portable policy/configuration packages across independently deployed contexts and regions;
- provide governed predictive impact analysis; and
- preserve reproducible historical financial meaning through technology, provider, policy, and regional change.

## Roadmap Gate

No configuration change may rewrite historical meaning, bypass authorization, weaken ledger/reconciliation/tax/risk/security/audit controls, expose sensitive data, or create an unapproved financial effect.
