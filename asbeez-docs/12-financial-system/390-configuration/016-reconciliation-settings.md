# Reconciliation Settings

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/390-configuration/016-reconciliation-settings.md

---

## Purpose

Reconciliation settings define source scopes, matching keys, tolerances, timing windows, currency/FX treatment, materiality, schedules, ownership, exception routing, approval, and certification.

## Rules

Settings must identify ledger/subledger/provider/bank/wallet/reward/vendor/partner/tax/reserve/country scope, source version, control totals, duplicate/missing logic, and acceptable timing/rounding. They cannot hide differences, auto-edit balances, or certify without reviewer and unresolved-risk treatment.

## Change Control

Changes require source-owner/accounting review, historical comparability assessment, sample/backfill tests, entity/country/currency impact, effective date, approval, monitoring, and re-reconciliation plan.
