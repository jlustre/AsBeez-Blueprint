# Reporting Settings

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/390-configuration/018-reporting-settings.md

---

## Purpose

Reporting settings define report definitions, datasets, account mappings, dimensions, periods, entity/country/currency/FX/tax basis, filters, schedules, freshness, certification, exports, and retention.

## Rules

Settings distinguish posted actuals, estimates, projections, forecasts, budgets, provider evidence, and management views. They cannot alter source data, conceal exceptions, or present uncertified/estimated data as certified actuals. Mapping and metric changes are versioned and preserve historical lineage.

## Change Control

Report, metric, mapping, filter, FX, tax, or certification changes require source/accounting review, sample output comparison, security/privacy scope, approval, effective date, delivery review, and reconciliation impact.
