# Tax Settings

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/390-configuration/014-tax-settings.md

---

## Purpose

Tax settings define jurisdiction, registration, nexus, tax type/code/rate, classification, exemption, withholding, rounding, filing, remittance, provider, and effective policy.

## Rules

Settings are country/entity/jurisdiction scoped, versioned, effective-dated, and linked to address/classification and provider evidence. Tax collected/withheld is not revenue. Historical calculations remain reproducible; changes use new versions or approved notes/adjustments.

## Change Control

Tax owners approve changes with legal registration, filing, invoice/refund, accounting, country, provider, rounding, report, reconciliation, and retention impact. Test taxable/non-taxable, exemption, withholding, boundary, FX, refund, and late-data cases.
