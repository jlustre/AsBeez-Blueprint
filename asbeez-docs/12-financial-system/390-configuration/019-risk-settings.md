# Risk Settings

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/390-configuration/019-risk-settings.md

---

## Purpose

Risk settings define transaction, payment, wallet, payout, refund, chargeback, reward, vendor, partner, account, velocity, scoring, monitoring, hold, case, and loss-prevention policies.

## Rules

Settings identify signal/rule/model version, threshold, scope, country/entity, currency, action, expiry, appeal, human authority, and audit. AI/provider output is advisory unless a documented deterministic control applies. Risk settings cannot silently freeze/release funds, deny lawful rights, alter accounting, or close cases without authorized workflow.

## Change Control

Changes require risk/compliance/legal/accounting review, false-positive/fairness/drift analysis, replay/simulation tests, approval, staged rollout, monitoring, appeal readiness, rollback, and effective-date preservation.
