## Purpose

Financial Periods and Close govern the calendars, posting windows, cut-off, accruals, deferrals, adjustments, trial balance, reconciliations, approvals, and reporting required to produce reliable AsBeez financial results.

## AsBeez Boundary

Period close does not rewrite operational history or force unresolved payment, vendor, partner, reward, tax, reserve, payout, or treasury differences into a clean balance. It classifies, accrues, adjusts, reconciles, escalates, and documents exceptions.

## Principles

- each period has entity, country, currency, calendar, status, owner, and close policy;
- ordinary postings are blocked after close except approved adjustment/restatement workflows;
- actuals derive from immutable ledger and reconciled subledgers;
- AsBeez-specific close includes orders, payments, invoices, vendor/partner settlement, rewards, reserves, payouts, tax, FX, treasury, and provider reconciliation; and
- close reports disclose estimates, exceptions, late entries, and unresolved differences.
