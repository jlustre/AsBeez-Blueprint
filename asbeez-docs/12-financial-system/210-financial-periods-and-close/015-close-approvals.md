# Close Approvals

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/210-financial-periods-and-close/015-close-approvals.md

---

## Purpose

Close approvals authorize period completion after evidence, reconciliations, trial balance, adjustments, checklists, and exceptions have been reviewed.

## Approval Evidence

Period/entity/country/currency scope, close checklist, trial balance, reconciliations, material variances, accruals/deferrals, tax, reserves, FX, treasury, vendor/partner, reward, payout, and exception decisions.

## Rules

Preparer, reviewer, approver, and period-reopen authority are separated according to materiality. Approval does not erase exceptions or authorize future payment; it locks the period and publishes the approved close state. Reopening requires new authority and audit evidence.
