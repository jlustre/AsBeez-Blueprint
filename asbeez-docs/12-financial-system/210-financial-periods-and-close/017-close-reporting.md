# Close Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/210-financial-periods-and-close/017-close-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Close reporting publishes approved financial statements, management reports, reconciliations, variance explanations, tax/treasury schedules, and exception disclosures for a closed period.

## Report Families

- trial balance, balance sheet, income statement, cash flow, and equity;
- GMV, recognized revenue, vendor/partner obligations, fees, commissions, rewards, tax, reserves, and payouts;
- bank/provider cash, liquidity, FX, refunds, chargebacks, and treasury;
- budget/forecast/actual and variance; and
- reconciliation, close checklist, adjustment, audit, and exception reports.

## Rules

Reports state period, entity, country, currency, chart version, policy, source, approval, estimates, exceptions, and certification status. Closed reports are immutable; amendments and restatements reference original versions.
