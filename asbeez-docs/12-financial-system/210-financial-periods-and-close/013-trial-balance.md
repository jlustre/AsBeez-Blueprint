# Trial Balance

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/210-financial-periods-and-close/013-trial-balance.md

---

## Purpose

The trial balance lists posted debit and credit totals by account, entity, country, currency, period, chart version, and reporting dimensions to prove ledger balance before close.

## Required Checks

- total debits equal total credits;
- account and period balances agree to posted journal lines;
- subledger control totals agree;
- clearing, suspense, intercompany, tax, reserve, wallet, vendor/partner, reward, payment, and treasury accounts are reviewed; and
- adjustments and closing entries are approved and included.

## Rules

Trial balance is generated from immutable posted entries. A balanced trial balance does not prove complete source attribution or correct classification; reconciliation and exception review remain mandatory.
