# Period Opening

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/210-financial-periods-and-close/004-period-opening.md

---

## Purpose

Period opening activates an approved accounting period after calendar, account, entity, currency, policy, opening balance, and operational readiness checks.

## Opening Checks

- prior period is closed or approved for carry-forward;
- opening balances and retained results are verified;
- chart of accounts and required dimensions are active;
- payment, provider, wallet, vendor/partner, reward, tax, reserve, treasury, and FX integrations are available;
- close deadlines, owners, and checklist are published; and
- period status and permissions are auditable.

## Rules

Opening a period does not create entries, approve budgets, release reserves, or authorize payouts. Failed readiness checks keep the period planned or restricted.
