# Adjusting Entries

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/210-financial-periods-and-close/011-adjusting-entries.md

---

## Purpose

Adjusting entries correct timing, classification, accrual, deferral, tax, FX, reserve, revenue, liability, or other accounting treatment without editing posted history.

## Required Evidence

Reason, source/original entry, affected account/dimensions, amount/currency, period, policy, calculation, preparer, approver, effective/posting dates, reversal plan, and downstream reconciliation impact.

## Rules

Adjustments to closed periods require approved authority or restatement treatment. Adjustments cannot bypass source workflow, create unqualified reward/vendor/partner/payable effects, or use suspense to hide an unresolved issue.

## Close Evidence

Every adjustment appears in the close register with status, materiality, account/dimension impact, source reconciliation, reviewer, approver, posting period, reversal/settlement plan, and report impact.
