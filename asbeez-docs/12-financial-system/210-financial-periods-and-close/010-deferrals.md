# Deferrals

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/210-financial-periods-and-close/010-deferrals.md

---

## Purpose

Deferrals move recognized or paid amounts to the periods in which the related service, benefit, cost, or obligation is delivered or incurred.

## AsBeez Examples

Subscriptions, prepaid services, listing/service fees, advertising campaigns, insurance, vendor/partner programs, reserves, and other period-based obligations.

## Rules

Each schedule identifies source, performance/cost period, amount/currency, entity, country, release method, policy, owner, and remaining balance. Deferral is not a refund, reserve, payable, or cash movement; release creates controlled entries and reconciliation.

## Close Treatment

Deferral schedules are reviewed at each close, with released, remaining, cancelled, refunded, impaired, and exception balances reported. Schedule changes require reason, effective period, approval, and link to the source contract/order/invoice.
