## Design Authority

Financial Periods and Close owns calendars, period states, cut-off, close workflows, adjustments, approvals, and reporting certification. General Ledger, Tax, Treasury, Revenue, Payments, Payouts, Vendor/Partner Finance, Rewards, Reserves, Budgets, and Reconciliation provide authoritative facts and evidence.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Approve calendars, entities, countries, currencies, period states, owners, and materiality.
2. Implement period opening/locking, cut-off, monthly/quarterly/year-end close, accruals, deferrals, and adjustments.
3. Connect subledgers, tax, treasury, FX, reserves, rewards, vendor/partner, revenue, budgets, and GL.
4. Implement trial balance, checklists, approvals, reconciliation, reporting, API, events, audit, and governed AI.
