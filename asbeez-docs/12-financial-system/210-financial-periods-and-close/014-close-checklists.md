# Close Checklists

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/210-financial-periods-and-close/014-close-checklists.md

---

## Purpose

Close checklists provide the controlled tasks, owners, evidence, dependencies, and sign-offs required to close an AsBeez period.

## Checklist Areas

- calendar, cut-off, period lock, and late-entry review;
- payments, provider, bank, wallet, customer, invoice, refund, dispute, and payout reconciliation;
- vendor/partner settlement, commissions, fees, tax, withholding, reserves, rewards, and funds;
- accruals, deferrals, FX, treasury, intercompany, clearing, suspense, and trial balance;
- budget/forecast variance and reporting; and
- approvals, audit evidence, retention, and archive.

## Rules

Each item has owner, due date, status, evidence, dependency, exception, reviewer, and approval. A checklist cannot be marked complete while material exceptions are undocumented or unowned.
