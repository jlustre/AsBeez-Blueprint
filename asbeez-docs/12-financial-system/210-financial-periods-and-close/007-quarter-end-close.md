# Quarter End Close

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/210-financial-periods-and-close/007-quarter-end-close.md

---

## Purpose

Quarter-end close consolidates monthly results, reviews forecasts and variances, validates tax/reserve/FX/liquidity positions, and prepares management, statutory, and regulatory reporting.

## Additional Checks

- quarter-to-date revenue, GMV, vendor/partner obligations, rewards, fees, and expenses;
- reserve coverage and liability assumptions;
- country/entity consolidation and intercompany balances;
- FX revaluation and exposure;
- tax filings, remittances, and withholding;
- treasury liquidity, provider, bank, payout, and refund evidence; and
- budget/forecast revision and approval.

## Rules

Quarter close cannot replace monthly source reconciliation or hide unresolved exceptions. Restatements, late entries, and policy changes are versioned and approved.
