# Close Reconciliation

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/210-financial-periods-and-close/016-close-reconciliation.md

---

## Purpose

Close reconciliation confirms that General Ledger, subledgers, operational financial records, external providers, tax, treasury, and period reports agree before close.

## Required Scopes

- payments, provider settlements, bank, refunds, chargebacks, payouts;
- customer invoices and receivables;
- vendor/partner earnings, commissions, settlements, and payables;
- RP/ABC/AHC events and approved reward liabilities;
- wallets, reserves, compensation/charity/promotional funds;
- tax assessments, withholding, filings, remittances;
- FX conversions, revaluation, gains/losses; and
- clearing, suspense, intercompany, budgets, forecasts, and variances.

## Outcomes

Matched, timing difference, estimate, missing source, duplicate, classification mismatch, provider difference, unsupported adjustment, or approved exception. Every outcome records population, amount, currency, owner, age, evidence, reviewer, and resolution.
