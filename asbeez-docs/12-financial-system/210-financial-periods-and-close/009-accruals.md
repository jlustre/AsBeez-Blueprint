# Accruals

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/210-financial-periods-and-close/009-accruals.md

---

## Purpose

Accruals recognize earned or incurred activity in the correct period when invoice, payment, settlement, or final evidence arrives later.

## AsBeez Examples

Vendor/partner services, provider fees, payment fees, reward expense/liability, refunds/chargebacks, taxes, advertising delivery, subscriptions, cloud/operations, and treasury costs.

## Required Data

Source, basis, amount/currency, entity, country, period, estimate, policy, owner, approval, reversal/settlement plan, and subsequent evidence.

## Rules

Accruals are estimates, not cash, payable confirmation, or revenue by themselves. They are reviewed, reversed or settled through explicit entries, and reconciled to source subledgers, invoices, settlements, tax, reserves, and GL.
