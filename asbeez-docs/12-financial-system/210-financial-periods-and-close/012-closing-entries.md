# Closing Entries

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/210-financial-periods-and-close/012-closing-entries.md

---

## Purpose

Closing entries transfer approved period results and reset temporary reporting accounts according to entity, accounting, tax, and reporting policy.

## Preconditions

Trial balance balanced, subledgers reconciled, tax/reserve/FX/treasury reviewed, adjustments approved, exceptions documented, reports approved, and period authorized for close.

## Rules

Closing entries are balanced, immutable, linked to period and policy, and do not delete revenue/expense history. They cannot close unresolved material differences or alter vendor, partner, reward, tax, reserve, wallet, or cash source records.

## Close Evidence

The closing-entry package includes trial balance before/after, entry IDs, accounts, entity/country/currency, approval, retained-result treatment, report impact, exception disposition, and archive reference.
