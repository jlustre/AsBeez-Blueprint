# Month End Close

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/210-financial-periods-and-close/006-month-end-close.md

---

## Purpose

Month-end close produces a controlled monthly financial position and identifies unresolved source, accounting, tax, treasury, and operational differences.

## Close Sequence

1. freeze/cut off source activity and late-entry policy;
2. complete payments, provider, invoice, customer, vendor, partner, wallet, reward, reserve, tax, payout, and treasury reconciliations;
3. record accruals, deferrals, FX revaluation, adjustments, and approved corrections;
4. run trial balance and control totals;
5. review variance, exceptions, estimates, and close checklist;
6. obtain approval and lock period; and
7. publish certified reports and carry-forward balances.

## Rules

Close cannot hide unresolved differences or convert forecasts into actuals. Late activity uses approved adjustment periods or next-period treatment with disclosure.
