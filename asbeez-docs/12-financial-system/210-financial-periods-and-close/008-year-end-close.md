# Year End Close

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/210-financial-periods-and-close/008-year-end-close.md

---

## Purpose

Year-end close produces approved annual financial statements, statutory returns, tax filings, retained-result treatment, budget baseline, and archived audit evidence.

## Additional Checks

- all periods, entities, countries, currencies, and intercompany balances;
- revenue recognition, deferred/accrued/unearned balances;
- vendor/partner payables and payout completeness;
- reward liabilities, reserves, compensation/charity/promotional funds;
- tax assessments, withholding, filings, remittances, and legal holds;
- FX revaluation, treasury, provider/bank, refunds, chargebacks, and disputes; and
- closing entries, retained results, reports, approvals, and archive.

## Rules

Year-end close does not delete or rewrite history. Restatement, amendment, or post-close correction follows approved authority and retains prior statements and evidence.
