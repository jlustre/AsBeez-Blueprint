# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/210-financial-periods-and-close/018-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve calendars, periods, entities, countries, currencies, close owners, and materiality policies;
- implement opening, cut-off, locking, monthly/quarterly/year-end close, accrual, deferral, adjustment, and closing entries;
- connect payments, invoices, vendor/partner, rewards, reserves, tax, treasury, FX, budgets, and GL;
- implement trial balance, reconciliations, checklists, approvals, reporting, API, events, and audit; and
- establish exception, late-entry, reopening, and restatement operations.

## Medium Term

- automate source cut-off, close tasks, reconciliation, accrual schedules, and variance analysis;
- improve multi-entity, country, currency, tax, reserve, reward, and treasury close reporting;
- add evidence completeness and close-risk monitoring; and
- provide explainable AI assistance with human approval.

## Long Term

- support regional close services and high-volume period processing;
- provide governed statutory/reporting integrations;
- strengthen continuous close and scenario planning; and
- preserve portable close evidence across systems and jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No close feature may hide unresolved differences, alter posted history, bypass period locks, or publish certified reports without evidence, reconciliation, approval, authority, and audit rules.
