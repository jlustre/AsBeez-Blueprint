# Cross Country Reconciliation

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/220-reconciliation/012-cross-country-reconciliation.md

---

## Purpose

Cross-country reconciliation proves that local country/entity records consolidate correctly without erasing local currency, tax, regulatory, reserve, payout, provider, or policy evidence.

## Required Checks

- local bank/provider cash and settlement;
- local orders, invoices, payments, refunds, taxes, payouts, vendors, partners, rewards, reserves, and GL;
- intercompany transfers and FX conversion;
- country registration, filing, remittance, and reporting; and
- global mapping, consolidation, and currency translation.

## Rules

Country totals may be consolidated only through approved mappings and rates. A consolidated total does not replace local reconciliation or prove cross-border tax/intercompany correctness.
