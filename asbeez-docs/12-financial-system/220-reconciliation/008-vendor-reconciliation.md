# Vendor Reconciliation

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/220-reconciliation/008-vendor-reconciliation.md

---

## Purpose

Vendor reconciliation matches orders, earnings, fees, tax, reserves, holds, refunds, chargebacks, settlements, payouts, statements, and vendor payable control accounts.

## Rules

Vendor population, order/line, fulfillment/refund status, gross/net, currency, country/entity, policy, settlement, payout, and provider evidence must agree. Missing vendor attribution, duplicate earnings, premature payout, or unexplained payable is an exception even when totals balance.

## Evidence

Retain source population, control totals, matched/unmatched lines, settlement and payout references, reviewer, approval, exception age, and resolution action.
