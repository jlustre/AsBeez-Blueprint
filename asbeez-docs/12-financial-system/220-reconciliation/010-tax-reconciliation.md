# Tax Reconciliation

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/220-reconciliation/010-tax-reconciliation.md

---

## Purpose

Tax reconciliation matches assessments, invoice tax lines, payments, settlements, withholding, filings, remittances, tax subledger, and GL payable/recoverable accounts.

## Rules

Jurisdiction, authority, basis, rate, exemption, registration, currency, period, filing, remittance, refunds, notes, chargebacks, and withholding must agree. Tax collected/withheld remains separate from revenue, vendor/partner payable, reward, and fee.

## Evidence

Retain assessment population, tax lines, exemption/registration evidence, filing and remittance references, control totals, reviewer, approval, exception age, and amendment history.

Tax reports must distinguish assessed, collected, withheld, payable, recoverable, filed, remitted, overdue, adjusted, and disputed amounts.
