# Reserve Reconciliation

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/220-reconciliation/011-reserve-reconciliation.md

---

## Purpose

Reserve reconciliation matches reserve/fund balances to exposure populations, funding, commitments, holds, releases, utilization, returns, treasury/provider evidence, subledgers, and GL accounts.

## Rules

Purpose, owner, beneficiary/exposure, country/entity/currency, source, assumptions, balance, release/utilization, and policy must agree. A reserve total cannot be accepted while attribution, funding, or exposure evidence is missing.

## Evidence

Retain exposure population, funding entries, commitments, holds, releases, utilization, returns, treasury/provider evidence, reviewer, approval, exception age, and resolution.

Reserve reports must distinguish target, funded, committed, held, available, utilized, released, returned, expired, and outstanding amounts.
