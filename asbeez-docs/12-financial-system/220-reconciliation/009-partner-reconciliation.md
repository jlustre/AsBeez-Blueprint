# Partner Reconciliation

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/220-reconciliation/009-partner-reconciliation.md

---

## Purpose

Partner reconciliation matches qualifying order/service activity, attribution, commissions, bonuses, incentives, holds, reserves, tax, settlements, payouts, and partner payable control accounts.

## Rules

Registration, referral, genealogy, or participant count is not payable evidence. Reconciliation requires source activity, program/version, attribution, funding, qualification, country/entity/currency, settlement, payout, and adjustment links.

## Evidence

Retain activity population, attribution proof, program rule, control totals, matched/unmatched records, settlement/payout references, reviewer, approval, exception age, and resolution.

Partner reports must distinguish estimated, approved, payable, paid, held, reversed, and disputed outcomes.
