# Reconciliation AI Capabilities

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/220-reconciliation/020-reconciliation-ai-capabilities.md

---

## Purpose

AI may assist reconciliation with matching suggestions, anomaly detection, exception prioritization, root-cause analysis, and forecasted discrepancy risk while deterministic rules and human approval remain authoritative.

## Permitted Assistance

- propose matches with evidence and confidence;
- identify duplicate, missing, stale, currency, attribution, or timing patterns;
- prioritize aged or material exceptions;
- suggest resolution paths and supporting records; and
- explain control-total and variance differences.

## Prohibited Actions

AI must not confirm a high-risk match, close an exception, edit source/ledger records, suppress a discrepancy, approve a write-off, alter tolerance, or fabricate evidence.

## Governance

Recommendations include model/version, input scope, evidence, confidence, reason, provenance, policy context, human decision, and audit trail. Financial, customer, vendor, partner, tax, reward, and provider data is minimized and access-controlled.
