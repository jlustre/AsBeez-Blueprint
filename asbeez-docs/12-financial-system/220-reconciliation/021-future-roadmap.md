# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/220-reconciliation/021-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve reconciliation scopes, sources, domains, frequencies, rules, tolerances, owners, and materiality;
- implement GL/subledger, bank/provider, wallet, reward, vendor/partner, tax, reserve, country, FX, and close reconciliation;
- implement exception, discrepancy, approval, reporting, API, events, security, and audit workflows; and
- connect actuals, settlements, payouts, rewards, reserves, budgets, forecasts, and treasury.

## Medium Term

- automate evidence import, matching, exception aging, resolution, and close certification;
- improve cross-country, multi-currency, provider, reward, vendor/partner, tax, and reserve reconciliation; and
- add explainable AI assistance with human approval.

## Long Term

- support continuous reconciliation and regional high-volume processing;
- provide governed source/provider integrations;
- strengthen control-total, anomaly, and root-cause forecasting; and
- preserve portable evidence across systems and jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No reconciliation feature may suppress, auto-close, rewrite, or certify financial differences without source evidence, scope, rule, owner, approval, resolution, and audit controls.
