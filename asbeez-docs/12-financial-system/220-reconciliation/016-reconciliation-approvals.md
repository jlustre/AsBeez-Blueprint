# Reconciliation Approvals

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/220-reconciliation/016-reconciliation-approvals.md

---

## Purpose

Reconciliation approvals certify completed sessions, accepted timing differences, tolerances, exceptions, write-offs, corrections, and close impact.

## Approval Evidence

Scope, source/target, population, control totals, unmatched amount/count, currency, entity/country, rules/tolerance, exception list, resolution, owner, reviewer, approver, date, and report/period impact.

## Rules

Preparer, resolver, reviewer, and approver are separated according to materiality and risk. Approval does not alter source or ledger facts; it authorizes the documented disposition and close/reporting use. Reopened reconciliation requires new evidence and approval.
