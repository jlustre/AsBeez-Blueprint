# Reconciliation Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/220-reconciliation/017-reconciliation-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Reconciliation reporting presents matched populations, control totals, timing differences, exceptions, aging, resolutions, approvals, and certification across AsBeez financial domains.

## Report Families

- GL/subledger and source control totals;
- bank/provider/payment/payout reconciliation;
- wallet, reward, vendor, partner, tax, reserve, country, FX, and treasury reconciliation;
- close, budget/forecast, variance, and exception reporting; and
- aging, severity, root cause, resolution, write-off, and audit reports.

## Rules

Reports distinguish matched, timing, estimated, exception, approved, unresolved, and certified values. A reconciliation report states source period, currency, entity, country, rules/version, tolerance, freshness, reviewer, approver, and limitations.
