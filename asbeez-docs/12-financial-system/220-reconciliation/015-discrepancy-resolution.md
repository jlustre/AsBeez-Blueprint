# Discrepancy Resolution

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/220-reconciliation/015-discrepancy-resolution.md

---

## Purpose

Discrepancy resolution investigates, corrects, accepts, escalates, or writes off reconciliation differences while preserving source and posted history.

## Resolution Paths

- source-system correction;
- provider/bank clarification;
- timing-difference acceptance;
- compensating or adjusting entry;
- reserve/hold or liability correction;
- duplicate cancellation through a new event;
- approved write-off or exception acceptance; and
- fraud, tax, legal, or operational escalation.

## Rules

Resolution identifies root cause, affected records, amount/count/unit, currency, entity/country, policy, owner, approver, action, effective/posting period, and report impact. It cannot close an exception by deleting, editing, or silently netting records.
