# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/110-vendor-finance/019-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve vendor entities, countries, currencies, fee plans, tax, reserve, and payout policies;
- connect orders, payments, invoices, vendor subledger, settlement, refunds, chargebacks, rewards, and GL mappings;
- implement vendor account, earnings, holds, reserves, settlement, payout, statements, and reconciliation;
- implement destination verification, tax withholding, compliance, APIs, events, and audit; and
- publish vendor reports that separate sales, entitlement, revenue, tax, and paid amounts.

## Medium Term

- support localized vendor finance and multi-entity settlement;
- automate provider/bank reconciliation, reserve release, refund, and chargeback propagation;
- improve vendor forecasting and exception operations; and
- add explainable AI assistance with human approval.

## Long Term

- support regional vendor finance services and high-volume settlement;
- provide governed vendor and partner integrations;
- improve liquidity, reserve, and payout forecasting; and
- preserve portable vendor financial evidence across jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No vendor feature may present sales, earnings, platform fees, reward value, payable, payout, cash, or AsBeez revenue as another category without approved source, policy, tax, accounting, disclosure, reconciliation, and audit rules.
