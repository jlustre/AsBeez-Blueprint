## Purpose

Vendor Finance records the financial relationship between AsBeez and marketplace vendors: sales proceeds, platform fees, taxes, reserves, holds, refunds, chargebacks, settlements, payouts, statements, and reconciliation.

## AsBeez Boundary

Vendor sales and entitlement are not automatically AsBeez revenue. Orders provide commercial facts, Settlement calculates the approved amount owed, Vendor Finance maintains the vendor payable detail, Payouts execute transfers, and the General Ledger records posted monetary effects.

## Principles

- vendor entitlement is line- and order-traceable;
- platform fees, tax, partner allocation, reserves, refunds, chargebacks, and rewards are separate components;
- an order is not payable merely because it was created or paid;
- holds and reserves remain attributable and have release conditions;
- vendor statements derive from reconciled subledger and settlement data; and
- corrections preserve original history through adjustments, reversals, and notes.
