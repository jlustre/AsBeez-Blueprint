# Vendor Financial Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/110-vendor-finance/015-vendor-financial-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Vendor financial reporting separates vendor marketplace performance and entitlement from AsBeez revenue, tax, cash, payout, reward, and partner measures.

## Report Families

- GMV and order sales by vendor, product, category, country, and period;
- gross vendor earnings and net entitlement;
- platform commissions and fees;
- tax collected, withheld, payable, and remitted;
- reserves, holds, refunds, chargebacks, adjustments, and disputes;
- approved settlement, payout, provider fee, and outstanding payable; and
- reconciliation, forecast, exception, and statement certification.

## Rules

Every report labels gross/net basis, currency, entity, country, period, policy version, source, and reconciliation status. Vendor sales are not called AsBeez revenue; pending earnings are not called paid income; reward quantities are not included as cash or payable without approved classification.
