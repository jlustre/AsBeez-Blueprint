# Vendor Refunds

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/110-vendor-finance/010-vendor-refunds.md

---

## Purpose

Vendor refunds record the vendor-side financial consequences of customer refunds, returns, cancellations, credits, and related order corrections.

## Required Detail

Refund/order/line, vendor, original earning and settlement, customer payment/invoice, gross and tax amount, platform fee, partner allocation, reserve/hold, reward effect, approved adjustment, currency, and GL/subledger references.

## Rules

A refund does not delete the original order, earning, settlement, payout, or reward history. The vendor entitlement, platform fee, partner obligation, tax, reserve, and reward consequences are evaluated separately. Refund treatment follows contract, fulfillment, return, and country policy.
