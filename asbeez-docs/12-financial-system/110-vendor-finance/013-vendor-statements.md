# Vendor Statements

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/110-vendor-finance/013-vendor-statements.md

---

## Purpose

Vendor statements provide an ordered, reconciled view of vendor sales, earnings, fees, tax, reserves, holds, refunds, chargebacks, settlement, payouts, and remaining payable.

## Statement Contents

Vendor/entity/country/currency, period, opening payable, gross order lines, discounts, tax, platform fees, partner allocations, reserves, holds, refunds, chargebacks, adjustments, approved settlement, payouts, fees, closing payable, available payout, exceptions, and source references.

## Rules

Statements distinguish gross sales, vendor entitlement, AsBeez revenue, tax, and paid amounts. Pending, held, reserved, disputed, or unreconciled value is visible but not presented as available payout. Issued statement lines are retained; corrections appear as new lines.

## Certification

Certified statements derive from reconciled vendor subledger, settlement, payout, tax, and GL projections. Provisional statements disclose freshness, estimates, and exceptions.
