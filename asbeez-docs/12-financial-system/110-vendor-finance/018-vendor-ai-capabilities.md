# Vendor AI Capabilities

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/110-vendor-finance/018-vendor-ai-capabilities.md

---

## Purpose

AI may assist vendor finance with reconciliation, anomaly detection, fee explanation, settlement forecasting, support routing, and statement explanation while deterministic financial, tax, payout, and compliance rules remain authoritative.

## Permitted Assistance

- identify unusual earnings, refunds, chargebacks, fees, reserves, or payout patterns;
- propose reconciliation matches and exception priority;
- forecast settlement, payout, reserve, and liquidity needs;
- explain statement or settlement differences; and
- recommend, but not approve, operational review actions.

## Prohibited Actions

AI must not directly alter vendor earnings, fees, reserves, holds, tax, settlement, payout, account status, or reward treatment; approve a payout; bypass compliance; or present GMV/vendor sales as AsBeez revenue.

## Governance

Recommendations include model/version, evidence scope, confidence, reason, provenance, policy context, and human decision for material action. Vendor tax, bank, identity, and financial data is minimized and access-controlled.
