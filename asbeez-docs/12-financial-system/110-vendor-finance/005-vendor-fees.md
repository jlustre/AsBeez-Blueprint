# Vendor Fees

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/110-vendor-finance/005-vendor-fees.md

---

## Purpose

Vendor fees are disclosed charges to vendors for approved onboarding, subscription, listing, transaction, payment, advertising, technology, or support services.

## Required Data

Fee ID, type, vendor, source service/order, basis, rate/amount, currency, tax, country/entity, effective policy, funding owner, invoice/settlement treatment, and refund/credit behavior.

## Rules

Fees must be disclosed before the relevant obligation and calculated server-side. A vendor fee is not a hidden reduction of tax, partner commission, reward liability, or vendor gross sales. Revenue recognition follows the associated service, while deductions from payable follow settlement policy.
