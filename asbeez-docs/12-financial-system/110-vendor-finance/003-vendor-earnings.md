# Vendor Earnings

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/110-vendor-finance/003-vendor-earnings.md

---

## Purpose

Vendor earnings record gross and net vendor entitlement generated by qualifying marketplace orders and services before and after fees, tax, reserves, refunds, chargebacks, holds, and payout.

## Earnings Components

Gross line consideration, discounts, tax, platform commission/fee, partner allocation, reserve, hold, refund/chargeback adjustment, payout fee, and net payable are separately identified.

## Qualification

An earning becomes payable only after order/payment/fulfillment and vendor policy conditions pass. The precise timing varies by product, service, delivery, return window, fraud, dispute, and country rule.

## Rules

Vendor earnings are not AsBeez revenue and are not a reward balance. They reference order/line, vendor, country/entity, currency, settlement window, policy version, and subledger/GL control account. Reversals and adjustments preserve the original earning event.
