# Platform Commission

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/110-vendor-finance/004-platform-commission.md

---

## Purpose

Platform commission is an approved amount or rate charged for AsBeez marketplace, technology, listing, advertising, payment, or other vendor service.

## Required Data

Commission ID, vendor, order/line/service, basis, rate or fixed amount, currency, tax, country/entity, plan/version, effective period, recognition condition, refund/chargeback behavior, and settlement mapping.

## Rules

Commission is distinct from vendor gross sales, tax, partner commission, reserves, reward effects, and payout fees. It becomes AsBeez revenue only when the related platform performance obligation is recognized. Vendor-facing display must distinguish assessed, invoiced, collected, recognized, and deducted amounts.
