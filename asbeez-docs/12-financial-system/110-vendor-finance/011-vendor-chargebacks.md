# Vendor Chargebacks

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/110-vendor-finance/011-vendor-chargebacks.md

---

## Purpose

Vendor chargebacks record the vendor-side exposure and adjustments created by a payment dispute, issuer reversal, fraud claim, or provider chargeback.

## Required Detail

Chargeback ID, payment/provider/order, vendor, disputed amount, fee, currency, reason, evidence deadline, reserve/hold, earning/settlement/payout impact, representment result, and GL/subledger references.

## Rules

Chargeback exposure may hold or reduce vendor payable according to approved contract and policy; it is not automatically vendor fault or AsBeez revenue. Original payment, earning, settlement, reward, and payout records remain immutable. Win/loss outcomes create explicit adjustments and reconciliation evidence.
