# Wallet Compliance

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/050-member-wallets/016-wallet-compliance.md

---

## Purpose

Wallet compliance determines whether a wallet may be created, credited, debited, transferred, converted, redeemed, or paid out under the member, country, program, and legal-entity rules.

## Controls

- identity and member status verification;
- KYC/KYB and beneficial-owner checks where required;
- sanctions and restricted-party screening;
- source-of-funds and transaction monitoring;
- country availability and tax status;
- reward, payout, and conversion classification;
- fraud, chargeback, and dispute review; and
- record retention and consent requirements.

## AsBeez Reward Boundary

RP, ABC, and AHC display or ownership does not itself establish a monetary wallet, income, or payout right. A monetary credit or withdrawal requires approved jurisdictional classification, funding, terms, limits, tax handling, and payout authorization.

## Failure Behavior

Missing or stale compliance evidence blocks the operation or places the wallet under review. The system records the decision, policy version, reason, reviewer/system, and next action without exposing sensitive screening data to unauthorized users.
