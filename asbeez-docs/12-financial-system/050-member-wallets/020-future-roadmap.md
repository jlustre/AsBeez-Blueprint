# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/050-member-wallets/020-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- define launch wallet types, countries, currencies, and legal classifications;
- implement wallet creation, typed balances, movements, holds, reservations, and locks;
- connect marketplace refunds, vendor/partner settlement, reward conversions, and payouts;
- reconcile wallets to subledgers and GL control accounts; and
- implement security, compliance, limits, statements, and audit evidence.

## Medium Term

- add country-specific wallet and payout policies;
- support approved redemption and reward conversion workflows;
- improve provider reconciliation, dispute handling, and reserve release;
- provide member, vendor, and operations statements; and
- introduce explainable anomaly detection and support assistance.

## Long Term

- support multiple legal entities and regional wallet services;
- provide scalable event replay and balance rebuild;
- improve liquidity and reward-liability forecasting; and
- expose governed wallet capabilities to approved partner integrations.

## Roadmap Gate

No wallet feature may display or process RP, ABC, AHC, reward, commission, or settlement value as cash or guaranteed income without approved legal classification, funding, accounting, compliance, and payout rules.
