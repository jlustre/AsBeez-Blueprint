# Wallet AI Capabilities

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/050-member-wallets/019-wallet-ai-capabilities.md

---

## Purpose

AI may assist Member Wallet operations with explanation, detection, forecasting, and support while deterministic wallet, accounting, compliance, and authorization rules remain authoritative.

## Permitted Assistance

- explain balance components, holds, pending status, and statement movements;
- identify unusual credit, debit, transfer, payout, or login patterns;
- recommend reconciliation cases or support routing;
- forecast liquidity or expected wallet workload; and
- suggest, but not approve, reserve, limit, or compliance review actions.

## Prohibited Actions

AI must not directly create, alter, delete, unlock, convert, debit, credit, transfer, or pay out wallet value; bypass KYC/KYB, sanctions, limits, holds, or approval; classify RP/ABC/AHC as money; or invent a source event.

## Governance

Every recommendation includes model/version, input scope, confidence, reason, provenance, policy context, and human decision where material. Sensitive wallet and screening data is access-controlled, minimized, and excluded from unauthorized training or exposure.
