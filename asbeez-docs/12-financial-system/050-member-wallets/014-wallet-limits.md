# Wallet Limits

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/050-member-wallets/014-wallet-limits.md

---

## Purpose

Wallet limits control exposure and permitted activity by wallet type, country, member status, risk tier, currency, operation, and time period.

## Limit Types

- minimum and maximum wallet balance;
- daily, monthly, and lifetime credit/debit/transfer/payout limits;
- transaction and velocity limits;
- reserve or hold thresholds;
- reward conversion or redemption limits; and
- country or program-specific limits.

## Rules

Limits are versioned, effective-dated, explainable, and evaluated before reservation or final movement. A limit cannot convert RP, ABC, or AHC into monetary eligibility unless the reward policy explicitly permits it. Failed limit checks do not partially debit a wallet.
