# Representment

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/140-chargebacks-and-disputes/005-representment.md

---

## Purpose

Representment submits an approved evidence response to a provider or issuer within the case deadline.

## Process

1. validate case, reason, amount, deadline, and eligible evidence;
2. assemble and review evidence package;
3. obtain required operations, legal, or vendor approval;
4. submit through secured provider channel;
5. record provider receipt and expected decision; and
6. update reserve/hold and downstream workflows only from mapped outcomes.

## Rules

Representment does not guarantee a win and does not erase the chargeback exposure. Evidence and submission are immutable versions. Vendor or partner input may be required, but liability is not assigned solely because a vendor supplied the product.
