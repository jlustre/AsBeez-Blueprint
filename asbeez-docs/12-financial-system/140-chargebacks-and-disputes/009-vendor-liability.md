# Vendor Liability

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/140-chargebacks-and-disputes/009-vendor-liability.md

---

## Purpose

Vendor liability determines whether and how a chargeback exposure affects vendor earnings, payable, reserve, hold, settlement, or payout under contract, evidence, and applicable law.

## Rules

Vendor supply or order association does not automatically establish vendor fault. Liability considers product/service responsibility, fulfillment, evidence, agreement, platform role, fraud, customer rights, provider rules, and final outcome. Pending cases may hold or reserve vendor entitlement without final deduction.

## Outcomes

No vendor effect, temporary hold, reserve allocation, vendor adjustment, shared liability, platform liability, recovered amount, or disputed exception. Each outcome references case, order, vendor, settlement, evidence, policy, and reconciliation.
