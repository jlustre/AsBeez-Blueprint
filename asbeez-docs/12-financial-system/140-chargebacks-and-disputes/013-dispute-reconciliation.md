# Dispute Reconciliation

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/140-chargebacks-and-disputes/013-dispute-reconciliation.md

---

## Purpose

Dispute reconciliation proves that provider cases, payments, orders, invoices, refunds, evidence, reserves, holds, vendor/partner liability, platform accounting, fees, rewards, payouts, and General Ledger effects agree.

## Required Checks

- one provider case maps to one source payment/order scope;
- disputed amount, fee, currency, and status match provider evidence;
- evidence, representment, pre-arbitration, and arbitration deadlines/outcomes are recorded;
- reserve/hold and liability decisions agree to settlement and vendor/partner records;
- refund, reward, tax, revenue, wallet, and payout effects are linked where applicable; and
- wins, losses, returns, recoveries, and adjustments reconcile to GL.

## Outcomes

Matched, timing difference, duplicate, missing source, provider mismatch, evidence exception, liability mismatch, reserve variance, accounting mismatch, or approved correction. Every exception has owner, age, evidence, severity, and resolution.
