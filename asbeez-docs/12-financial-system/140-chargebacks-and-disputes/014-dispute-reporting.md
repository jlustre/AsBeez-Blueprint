# Dispute Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/140-chargebacks-and-disputes/014-dispute-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Dispute reporting separates case volume, exposure, reserves, fees, liability, outcomes, recoveries, vendor/partner effects, customer refunds, reward effects, and realized financial loss.

## Report Families

- cases by provider, reason, country, entity, payment method, vendor, partner, and period;
- open exposure, reserve, hold, fee, expected loss, realized loss, recovery, and aging;
- representment, pre-arbitration, arbitration, win/loss, and deadline performance;
- customer, vendor, partner, platform, tax, reward, refund, settlement, and payout effects; and
- reconciliation, exception, fraud, evidence, and compliance reporting.

## Rules

Reports label case status, source, currency, policy/version, liability decision, reserve basis, accounting status, and reconciliation status. A dispute count is not a loss; a reserve is not a realized chargeback; vendor exposure is not automatically vendor fault.
