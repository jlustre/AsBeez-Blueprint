# Chargeback Accounting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/140-chargebacks-and-disputes/008-chargeback-accounting.md

---

## Purpose

Chargeback accounting records the monetary effect of a dispute notification, provider fee, temporary exposure, representment, arbitration, win/loss, return, or correction.

## Possible Effects

- payment/provider clearing or receivable;
- chargeback loss or expense;
- vendor/partner payable hold or adjustment;
- platform liability or expense;
- reserve placement or utilization;
- tax, refund, revenue, reward, wallet, or payout adjustment; and
- recovered funds or reversal after a win.

## Rules

Recognition follows evidence, provider state, liability policy, entity, country, currency, and accounting period. A received dispute may create exposure or reserve without final loss. Every entry references case, payment, order, source, policy, and reconciliation IDs.
