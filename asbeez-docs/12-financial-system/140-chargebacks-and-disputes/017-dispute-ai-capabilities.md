# Dispute AI Capabilities

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/140-chargebacks-and-disputes/017-dispute-ai-capabilities.md

---

## Purpose

AI may assist dispute operations with case classification, deadline prediction, evidence retrieval, anomaly detection, reconciliation, and exposure forecasting while provider, legal, compliance, and human decision controls remain authoritative.

## Permitted Assistance

- classify reason and evidence requirements;
- identify missing or inconsistent evidence;
- prioritize deadlines and exception cases;
- propose reconciliation matches and exposure forecasts; and
- recommend review routing or representment preparation.

## Prohibited Actions

AI must not decide legal/vendor/platform liability alone, fabricate evidence, submit a case, mark win/loss, release reserves, alter accounting, deny customer rights, or expose sensitive data.

## Governance

Recommendations include model/version, evidence scope, confidence, reason, provenance, policy context, human decision, and appeal/review record. Payment, identity, vendor, customer, and dispute data is minimized and access-controlled.
