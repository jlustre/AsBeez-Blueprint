# Dispute Evidence

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/140-chargebacks-and-disputes/004-dispute-evidence.md

---

## Purpose

Dispute evidence supports a fact-based response to a payment or transaction challenge and must be relevant, authorized, complete, time-bound, and traceable to source systems.

## Evidence Types

- payment authorization/capture and authentication;
- customer consent, order, invoice, terms, and delivery;
- product/service fulfillment, return, cancellation, and communication;
- refund attempts, credits, wallet actions, and provider status;
- vendor/partner attribution, settlement, and policy records; and
- fraud, identity, device, and operational evidence where lawful.

## Rules

Evidence packages retain source ID, type, hash/integrity, captured time, provider deadline, access classification, redaction, and submission version. Evidence cannot be fabricated, altered after submission, or exposed beyond need-to-know access.
