## Purpose

Chargebacks and Disputes govern external payment challenges, evidence, representment, pre-arbitration, arbitration, liability, reserves, fees, accounting, reconciliation, and downstream customer/vendor/partner/reward effects.

## AsBeez Boundary

A dispute is a claim or provider state, not proof of fault. Payments own provider state, Disputes own case and evidence workflow, Settlement owns obligations, Vendor/Partner Finance owns participant effects, Reward Finance owns reward effects, and the General Ledger records approved monetary consequences.

## Principles

- original payment, order, invoice, refund, earning, settlement, reward, and payout history remains immutable;
- dispute reason, amount, currency, deadline, evidence, owner, and provider reference are explicit;
- reserves and holds protect exposure without assuming final liability;
- win/loss/arbitration outcomes create new adjustments and reconciliation evidence;
- customer, vendor, partner, platform, tax, reward, and revenue impacts are separate; and
- fraud and AI signals support review but do not replace evidence or due process.
