# Platform Liability

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/140-chargebacks-and-disputes/010-platform-liability.md

---

## Purpose

Platform liability determines chargeback exposure retained by AsBeez under marketplace role, contract, payment arrangement, consumer law, provider rules, fraud controls, or approved risk policy.

## Rules

Platform liability is not assumed merely because AsBeez processed a payment or operates the marketplace. The decision considers principal/agent role, terms, tax, provider contract, vendor evidence, customer rights, insurance/reserves, and dispute outcome.

## Accounting

Recognized platform exposure may create reserve, loss, expense, clearing, recovery, or payable effects. Pending exposure remains distinct from realized loss. Recoveries after a win or vendor/insurer contribution are new events and reconcile to the original case.
