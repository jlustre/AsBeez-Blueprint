# Arbitration

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/140-chargebacks-and-disputes/007-arbitration.md

---

## Purpose

Arbitration is the formal provider/network decision stage after representment or pre-arbitration when a dispute remains unresolved.

## Required Data

Case, prior evidence/submissions, arbitration fee, amount/currency, deadlines, decision authority, provider outcome, liability decision, reserve/hold, vendor/partner/platform adjustment, and reconciliation references.

## Rules

Arbitration outcomes are mapped to won, lost, withdrawn, invalid, or other provider states through versioned policy. The outcome may adjust exposure, reserve, liability, revenue, vendor/partner payable, reward, or payout status, but never edits the original payment or order history.
