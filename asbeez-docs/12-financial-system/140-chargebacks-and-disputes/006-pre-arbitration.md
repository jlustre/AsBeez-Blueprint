# Pre Arbitration

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/140-chargebacks-and-disputes/006-pre-arbitration.md

---

## Purpose

Pre-arbitration is a provider or network escalation stage where a representment is challenged before formal arbitration.

## Required Data

Case, prior submission, new reason, provider deadline, additional amount/fee, evidence request, decision authority, reserve/hold, and response outcome.

## Rules

Pre-arbitration acceptance, rejection, or response does not itself determine vendor or platform fault. Decisions follow evidence, contract, provider rules, and approved liability policy. Additional fees and exposure are separately recorded and reconciled.

## Decision Controls

The case records whether AsBeez accepts, contests, negotiates, or escalates the claim, who approved the decision, what evidence supports it, the response deadline, and the expected financial exposure. A decision may adjust a hold or reserve but cannot edit the original payment or settlement records.
