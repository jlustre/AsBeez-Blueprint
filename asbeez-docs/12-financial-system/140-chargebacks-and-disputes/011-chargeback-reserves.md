# Chargeback Reserves

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/140-chargebacks-and-disputes/011-chargeback-reserves.md

---

## Purpose

Chargeback reserves restrict or designate value against expected dispute exposure before final liability or provider resolution.

## Required Data

Reserve ID, case/population, vendor/partner/platform owner, basis, rate/amount, currency, country/entity, probability/assumption, start/review/release condition, funding account, owner, approval, and utilization.

## Rules

Reserve is not a chargeback loss, vendor fault, revenue, fee, or free cash. It remains linked to exposure and may be released, utilized, increased, or reclassified only through controlled events. Reserve calculations are effective-dated and reviewed against case outcomes.
