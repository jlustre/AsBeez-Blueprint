# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/140-chargebacks-and-disputes/018-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve provider, country, entity, dispute, liability, reserve, tax, vendor, partner, reward, and accounting policies;
- implement case, evidence, lifecycle, representment, pre-arbitration, arbitration, and deadline controls;
- connect payments, orders, invoices, refunds, vendor/partner finance, reserves, rewards, payouts, and GL mappings;
- implement reconciliation, reporting, API, events, security, audit, and support workflows; and
- establish fair fraud controls and appeal/review processes.

## Medium Term

- automate provider evidence and deadline management;
- improve exposure, reserve, liability, recovery, and liquidity forecasting;
- support localized consumer, network, tax, and provider rules; and
- add explainable AI assistance with human decision controls.

## Long Term

- support regional dispute services and high-volume case processing;
- provide governed provider, vendor, partner, and insurer integrations;
- improve network, fraud, and customer-experience forecasting; and
- preserve portable evidence across providers and jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No dispute feature may assign fault, deny rights, release reserves, create loss, adjust vendor/partner liability, reverse rewards, or post accounting effects without evidence, authority, policy, reconciliation, and audit rules.
