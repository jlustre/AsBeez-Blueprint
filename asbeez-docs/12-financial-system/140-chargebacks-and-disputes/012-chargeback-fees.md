# Chargeback Fees

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/140-chargebacks-and-disputes/012-chargeback-fees.md

---

## Purpose

Chargeback fees are provider, network, bank, arbitration, or operational costs associated with a dispute case.

## Required Data

Fee ID, case/provider, payer or funding owner, amount/rate, currency, country/entity, effective policy, tax, vendor/partner/platform treatment, and accounting mapping.

## Rules

Fees are distinct from disputed principal, vendor liability, platform loss, reserve, tax, commission, and revenue. Fee recovery from a vendor or partner requires contract and liability approval. Fees are reconciled to provider evidence and never hidden in a customer refund or reward adjustment.

## Recovery and Reporting

Report assessed, paid, recovered, waived, disputed, and outstanding fees by case, provider, country, entity, currency, payer, and policy version. A recovered fee is a new approved allocation and does not change the original provider charge or dispute history.
