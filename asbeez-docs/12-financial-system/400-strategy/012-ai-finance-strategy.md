# AI Finance Strategy

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/400-strategy/012-ai-finance-strategy.md

---

## Purpose

AI finance strategy uses governed models and assistants to explain, forecast, classify, detect, prioritize, retrieve, summarize, and recommend across financial operations.

## Priority Uses

Reconciliation matching, anomaly/fraud prioritization, cash/revenue/expense/liquidity forecasting, close/audit/compliance assistance, report drafting, natural-language read-only query, evidence retrieval, support explanations, and configuration impact analysis.

## Non-Negotiable Boundary

AI cannot directly post, approve, pay, alter balances, release/freeze funds, certify, close, make final fraud/compliance/legal decisions, or bypass authentication, authorization, risk, tax, reconciliation, audit, privacy, country, or recovery controls. Human/deterministic authority remains accountable.

## Governance

Scale only with use-case owner, risk tier, data minimization, model/prompt/version, evaluation, fairness/drift, security/privacy, human approval, audit, monitoring, appeal, incident, rollback, and retirement controls.
