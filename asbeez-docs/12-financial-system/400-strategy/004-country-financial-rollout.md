# Country Financial Rollout

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/400-strategy/004-country-financial-rollout.md

---

## Purpose

Country rollout establishes local financial capability, entity, policy, provider, data, operational, and control readiness before production launch.

## Readiness Checklist

Legal entity/registration; tax/nexus/filing; banking/payment/payout; supported currency/FX; Chart of Accounts and calendar; wallet/reward treatment; vendor/partner settlement; reserves/liquidity; KYC/KYB/AML/sanctions; privacy/residency/retention; fraud/risk; APIs/events/integrations; reconciliation/close/reporting; support/runbooks; security/audit; SLO/incident/DR; and trained owners.

## Launch Gates

Complete sandbox and controlled-pilot tests, opening balances and mappings, provider evidence/reconciliation, forecast/liquidity coverage, approval matrices, incident/recovery exercises, and go/no-go authority. Rollback or pause must be defined before launch; local history remains isolated and auditable.
