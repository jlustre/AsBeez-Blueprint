# Cost Optimization

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/400-strategy/014-cost-optimization.md

---

## Purpose

Cost optimization reduces per-transaction, provider, infrastructure, operational, treasury, tax, support, and reporting cost without weakening financial correctness, resilience, compliance, security, or stakeholder trust.

## Levers

Provider routing and negotiation, payment/payout fee reduction, batching where safe, queue and storage efficiency, projection/report workload management, automation of evidence/triage, staffing and runbook improvement, reconciliation prevention, reserve/liquidity efficiency, and waste/duplicate reduction.

## Guardrails

Do not reduce controls, audit evidence, retention, reconciliation, support quality, fraud/compliance coverage, recovery, privacy, or country readiness for cost. Evaluate total cost, failure/chargeback/loss exposure, latency, capacity, provider concentration, and customer/vendor/partner impact before change.
