# Tax Strategy

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/400-strategy/008-tax-strategy.md

---

## Purpose

Tax strategy establishes sustainable jurisdiction, nexus, registration, classification, calculation, collection, withholding, filing, remittance, audit, and cross-border practices.

## Strategic Requirements

Maintain country/entity registrations, tax-policy ownership, product/service classification, customer/vendor status, address evidence, exemption, provider coverage, invoice/refund treatment, marketplace obligations, withholding, reporting, retention, and dispute readiness.

## Rules

Tax collected or withheld is not revenue. Tax decisions are jurisdiction/version/effective-date specific and reproducible. Expansion, pricing, provider, product, reward, vendor, partner, and legal-entity changes require tax impact review, testing, reconciliation, and approved implementation.
