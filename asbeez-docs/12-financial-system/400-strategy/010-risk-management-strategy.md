# Risk Management Strategy

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/400-strategy/010-risk-management-strategy.md

---

## Purpose

Risk strategy protects AsBeez, customers, members, vendors, partners, providers, and liquidity from transaction fraud, account takeover, payout abuse, refund/chargeback loss, reward abuse, operational error, compliance exposure, provider failure, and concentration risk.

## Strategic Model

Combine deterministic rules, provider evidence, models/AI, human review, reserves, holds, limits, monitoring, cases, appeals, loss measurement, insurance/compensation policy, and continuous control testing. Segment risk by product, country, entity, currency, actor, provider, amount, velocity, and lifecycle.

## Rules

Risk decisions are explainable, proportionate, privacy-aware, appealable, and versioned. AI/provider signals are advisory unless documented deterministic policy applies. Risk controls cannot silently alter accounting, deny lawful rights, release/freeze funds without authority, or close cases without evidence.
