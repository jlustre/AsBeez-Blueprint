# Compliance Strategy

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/400-strategy/009-compliance-strategy.md

---

## Purpose

Compliance strategy enables lawful, trusted financial growth through accounting, tax, AML/KYC/KYB, sanctions, source-of-funds, privacy, licensing, consumer, data-residency, audit, and country controls.

## Strategic Requirements

Use risk-based controls, local legal/entity ownership, policy versioning, screening/monitoring, approval and separation of duties, evidence/retention, reporting, appeals, training, provider oversight, independent testing, and incident response.

## Expansion Gate

No country, provider, product, reward, wallet, payout, payment, or AI capability launches without documented legal/compliance assessment, registrations, data/privacy basis, control design, operational owner, testing, escalation, audit trail, and recovery plan. Uncertainty becomes hold/review/exception rather than silent approval.
