## Design Authority

Financial Strategy owns strategic priorities, sequencing, investment gates, expansion readiness, provider portfolio, operating model, liquidity, tax/compliance, risk, automation, AI, data, cost, scalability, and roadmap decisions. Domain, country, accounting, legal, compliance, risk, and treasury owners retain their respective authorities.

## Strategic Authority

Growth is conditional on immutable financial truth, reconciled sources, legal/entity/country readiness, liquidity, tax/compliance, security/privacy, risk controls, operational capacity, support, observability, testing, and recovery. Efficiency or automation cannot weaken these conditions; AI remains advisory/approval-gated.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Establish system strategy, operating model, architecture/data/API/event foundations, governance, security, compliance, testing, observability, and administration.
2. Launch country/entity and payment/banking/provider capabilities through documented readiness gates.
3. Strengthen treasury, tax, risk, reconciliation, close, reporting, support, operations, and recovery.
4. Scale automation, AI, data/analytics, cost optimization, and independent/regional contexts under control gates.
5. Reassess strategy using financial correctness, liquidity, loss, cost, reliability, compliance, stakeholder, and roadmap evidence.
