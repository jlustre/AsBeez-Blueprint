# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/400-strategy/016-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- establish authoritative ledger/data/API/event foundations, operating model, administration, configuration, security, compliance, testing, observability, and integration controls;
- launch bounded country/provider capabilities through readiness gates for tax, banking, liquidity, risk, reconciliation, close, support, and recovery; and
- measure reliability, financial correctness, loss, cost, liquidity, close quality, provider health, and stakeholder outcomes.

## Medium Term

- expand countries/entities and provider portfolio with local compliance, currency, tax, banking, operations, and data-residency readiness;
- improve automation, governed AI, data/analytics, forecasting, reconciliation, treasury, reporting, scalability, and cost efficiency; and
- extract or regionalize bounded contexts only when contracts, controls, observability, and operating maturity are proven.

## Long Term

- provide resilient global financial infrastructure supporting marketplace, wallets, rewards, vendors, partners, and regional services;
- operate predictive risk, liquidity, capacity, and control intelligence under human/deterministic authority; and
- preserve portable, auditable, privacy-safe financial semantics across technology, provider, policy, and geographic change.

## Strategic Gates

No growth, automation, AI, provider, architecture, or cost decision proceeds with unresolved material ledger/reconciliation imbalance, inadequate liquidity, missing tax/compliance/legal readiness, unsafe security/privacy, untested recovery, or weakened stakeholder protections.
