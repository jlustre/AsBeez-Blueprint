# Treasury Strategy

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/400-strategy/007-treasury-strategy.md

---

## Purpose

Treasury strategy ensures sufficient, appropriately located, correctly currency-matched liquidity for payments, payouts, refunds, settlements, reserves, tax, vendors, partners, operations, and growth.

## Strategic Priorities

Cash visibility, liquidity forecasting, reserve adequacy, bank/provider diversification, concentration control, legal-entity/country positioning, FX exposure, transfer efficiency, yield/return policy, contingency funding, and reconciled cash evidence.

## Controls

Treasury decisions use approved forecasts and current reconciled sources. Transfers require source/destination, entity/country/currency, sanctions, limits, dual approval, idempotency, and audit. Reserves, liabilities, pending/provider funds, and reward units are not free cash. Strategy is reviewed against stress scenarios and recovery plans.
