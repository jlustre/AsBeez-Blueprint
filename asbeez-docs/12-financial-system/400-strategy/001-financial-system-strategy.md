## Purpose

The AsBeez Financial System strategy creates trusted financial infrastructure for marketplace commerce, wallets, rewards, vendors, partners, payments, tax, treasury, reporting, and global growth.

## Strategic Pillars

1. Preserve immutable, balanced, auditable financial truth.
2. Convert commerce events into policy-controlled revenue, liability, expense, tax, reserve, settlement, and cash effects.
3. Scale through modular contexts, canonical contracts, resilient providers, country/entity readiness, and observable operations.
4. Protect members, customers, vendors, partners, and AsBeez through security, compliance, risk, liquidity, and recovery controls.
5. Use automation and AI for assistance and efficiency while retaining human/deterministic authority.

## Success Measures

Accuracy, reconciliation completion, close quality, payment/payout reliability, liquidity coverage, provider resilience, tax/compliance readiness, loss and fraud control, cost per transaction, forecast quality, audit findings, recovery performance, and stakeholder trust.
