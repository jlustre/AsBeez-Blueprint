# Financial Operating Model

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/400-strategy/002-financial-operating-model.md

---

## Purpose

The financial operating model assigns decision rights, ownership, service levels, controls, processes, and evidence across finance, accounting, treasury, tax, risk, compliance, operations, support, engineering, and audit.

## Model

Central standards govern chart, ledger, policy, security, audit, data, architecture, and control totals; domain and country teams execute approved local workflows; shared services provide payments, payouts, reconciliation, reporting, and support; independent audit/control functions provide assurance.

## Rules

Use clear RACI, separation of duties, dual control, country/entity scope, runbooks, queues, handoffs, SLOs, escalation, incident response, reconciliation, close, and continuous improvement. Scale staffing and authority with volume, materiality, risk, legal obligations, and operational complexity.
