# Data And Analytics Strategy

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/400-strategy/013-data-and-analytics-strategy.md

---

## Purpose

Data and analytics strategy turns governed financial facts into trusted reporting, forecasting, risk, compliance, operational, product, and management insight.

## Strategic Requirements

Use immutable ledger/source facts, controlled subledgers, versioned dimensions, certified snapshots, data lineage, entity/country/currency/period scope, metric definitions, FX/tax basis, freshness, privacy, retention, access, quality, reconciliation, and reproducible transformations.

## Rules

Analytics distinguish GMV, payments, recognized revenue, liabilities, expenses, cash, reserves, rewards, estimates, forecasts, and provider evidence. Warehouses and BI are read-only; stale, incomplete, uncertified, or estimated data is labeled. Insights do not authorize financial actions.
