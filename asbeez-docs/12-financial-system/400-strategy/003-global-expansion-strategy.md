# Global Expansion Strategy

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/400-strategy/003-global-expansion-strategy.md

---

## Purpose

Global expansion extends AsBeez commerce and financial capability across countries and legal entities without compromising local compliance, liquidity, data residency, tax, provider, accounting, or operational control.

## Expansion Gates

Confirm legal entity/registration, banking/payment/payout coverage, currency/FX, tax/nexus, KYC/KYB/AML/sanctions, privacy/residency, Chart of Accounts, accounting calendar, reserve/liquidity, provider contracts, support/operations, risk, reporting, reconciliation, audit, incident, and DR readiness.

## Sequencing

Start with a bounded pilot and controlled volume; validate local operations and reconciliation; expand capabilities and providers; then increase volume after close, tax, risk, liquidity, and recovery evidence meet thresholds. Consolidation never erases local statutory history.
