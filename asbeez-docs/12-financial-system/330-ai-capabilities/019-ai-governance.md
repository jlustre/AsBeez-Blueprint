# AI Governance

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/330-ai-capabilities/019-ai-governance.md

---

## Purpose

AI governance controls the lifecycle, risk, accountability, transparency, validation, deployment, monitoring, and retirement of financial AI capabilities.

## Governance Requirements

Maintain use case owner, business purpose, risk tier, data sources, model/prompt/rule version, training/evaluation scope, limitations, decision boundary, human owner, approval, deployment date, jurisdictions, vendors, security/privacy review, and retirement plan.

## Model Controls

Test accuracy, calibration, explainability, fairness, robustness, drift, leakage, adversarial behavior, privacy, reproducibility, latency, cost, and worst-case outcomes. Monitor production performance, overrides, appeals, incidents, data shift, and unauthorized use. Material changes require revalidation.

## Non-Negotiable Boundary

AI may explain, forecast, detect, prioritize, retrieve, classify, and recommend. It may not directly post, approve, pay, alter balances, release/freeze funds, certify, close, set final legal/compliance outcomes, or bypass controls. Human and deterministic authorities remain accountable.
