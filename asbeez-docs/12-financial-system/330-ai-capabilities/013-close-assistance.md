# Close Assistance

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/330-ai-capabilities/013-close-assistance.md

---

## Purpose

AI assists period close by summarizing checklist progress, identifying missing evidence, prioritizing reconciliations, suggesting accrual/deferral candidates, detecting unusual balances, and drafting status reports.

## Output

Return period/entity/country, task, source evidence, materiality, dependency, exception, confidence, recommended owner, suggested action, and close-readiness limitation. Accrual and deferral suggestions include assumptions and must be reviewed under accounting policy.

## Controls

AI cannot post adjustments, open/lock/reopen/close periods, approve reconciliations, certify close, waive exceptions, or change accounting policy. Authorized finance personnel perform and approve actions with separation of duties and audit evidence.
