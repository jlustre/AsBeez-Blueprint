# Human Approval

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/330-ai-capabilities/018-human-approval.md

---

## Purpose

Human approval converts an AI recommendation into an authorized business decision only after the responsible person validates evidence, policy, scope, and consequences.

## Approval Record

Retain recommendation ID, model/version, input/source scope, confidence, rationale, alternatives, materiality, policy version, actor, role, authentication strength, decision, comments, timestamp, correlation, conflicts, and resulting command/effect.

## Required Review

Mandatory review applies to journal/account mappings, material classifications, reconciliation resolutions, fraud/compliance outcomes, fund holds/releases, payouts, refunds, reserves, tax, close, report certification, and any action with legal, customer, vendor, partner, country, or financial liability impact.

## Rules

Approval is not a rubber stamp: reviewers may accept, reject, modify through a controlled command, request evidence, or escalate. Separation of duties, thresholds, dual control, appeal, and re-review apply. AI cannot approve its own recommendation or impersonate a human.
