# Fraud Detection

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/330-ai-capabilities/007-fraud-detection.md

---

## Purpose

AI assists fraud operations through pattern detection, graph analysis, risk scoring suggestions, case prioritization, evidence retrieval, and loss/exposure forecasting.

## Output

Return risk signal, candidate score/range, reasons, related entities/transactions, model/rule version, features used, confidence, data scope, fairness/drift indicators, and recommended human review path.

## Prohibited Decisions

AI must not independently label fraud, deny lawful rights, set final thresholds, freeze/release funds, deny payout/refund, alter accounting, close cases, fabricate evidence, or expose sensitive data. Deterministic controls and authorized human/legal/compliance decisions remain authoritative.
