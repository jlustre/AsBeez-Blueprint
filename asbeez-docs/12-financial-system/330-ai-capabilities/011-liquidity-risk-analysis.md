# Liquidity Risk Analysis

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/330-ai-capabilities/011-liquidity-risk-analysis.md

---

## Purpose

AI analyzes liquidity exposure across cash, bank/provider settlement, wallets, reserves, payout obligations, refunds, chargebacks, vendor/partner settlements, tax, FX, countries, and legal entities.

## Output

Return exposure, horizon, stress scenario, available versus restricted cash, reserve constraints, concentration, currency/FX risk, dependency assumptions, confidence, trigger, and recommended human review. Show source freshness and unresolved reconciliation exceptions.

## Controls

AI cannot move money, release reserves, change limits, approve treasury transfers, or declare solvency. Treasury and risk owners validate source data, legal/entity restrictions, stress assumptions, and escalation thresholds. Alerts and recommendations are audited.
