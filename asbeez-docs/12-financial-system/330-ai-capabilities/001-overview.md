# Overview

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/330-ai-capabilities/001-overview.md

---

## Purpose

AI capabilities assist AsBeez financial operations with explanation, classification, retrieval, forecasting, anomaly detection, prioritization, reconciliation support, and recommendations while deterministic rules, authorized humans, and the General Ledger remain authoritative.

## Permitted Uses

AI may summarize records, identify patterns, forecast scenarios, recommend account/category matches, prioritize exceptions, draft reports, explain variances, retrieve evidence, and suggest next actions with confidence, provenance, limitations, and human review.

## Prohibited Uses

AI must not independently post, approve, pay, release funds, alter balances, change account mappings, certify reports, close periods/cases, make final fraud/compliance decisions, override policy, or bypass authentication, authorization, risk, tax, reconciliation, audit, or country controls.

## Common Contract

Every output identifies model/version, prompt or rule version where applicable, input scope, data time, source references, confidence/uncertainty, decision owner, human disposition, policy constraints, and audit correlation. Sensitive financial, identity, behavioral, vendor, partner, and customer data is minimized and access-controlled.
