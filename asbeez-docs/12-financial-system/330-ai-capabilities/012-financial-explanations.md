# Financial Explanations

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/330-ai-capabilities/012-financial-explanations.md

---

## Purpose

AI explains financial balances, transactions, variances, fees, tax, rewards, vendor/partner earnings, reserves, refunds, disputes, reports, and reconciliation status in role-appropriate language.

## Explanation Contract

Explanations cite source records, posted versus projected status, period, entity/country/currency, calculation or policy version, freshness, assumptions, confidence, and unresolved exceptions. They distinguish fact, inference, estimate, recommendation, and unknown state.

## Controls

Explanations cannot invent missing evidence, expose unauthorized data, alter records, certify a result, or substitute for accounting, tax, legal, audit, or compliance judgment. Users can inspect source lineage and report a correction; material corrections are retained.
