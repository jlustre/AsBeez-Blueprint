# Cash Flow Forecasting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/330-ai-capabilities/008-cash-flow-forecasting.md

---

## Purpose

AI forecasts cash inflows, outflows, timing, balances, and liquidity scenarios using approved historical, current, budget, settlement, payout, treasury, and provider data.

## Output

Return horizon, base/scenario assumptions, source periods, entity/country/currency, confidence interval, sensitivity drivers, seasonality, known obligations, forecast freshness, and variance history. Distinguish posted actuals, commitments, estimates, and provider-pending amounts.

## Controls

Forecasts do not authorize transfers, payouts, reserve releases, borrowing, investment, or accounting entries. Treasury and finance owners approve assumptions and actions. Material changes, sparse data, currency changes, country constraints, and model drift trigger review.
