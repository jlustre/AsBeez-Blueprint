# Revenue Forecasting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/330-ai-capabilities/009-revenue-forecasting.md

---

## Purpose

AI forecasts revenue and related drivers across marketplace orders, subscriptions, services, advertising, fees, commissions, countries, currencies, vendors, partners, and channels.

## Output

Return recognized-revenue basis, forecast horizon, source periods, GMV versus net/platform revenue distinction, deferred/accrued/unearned treatment, assumptions, scenarios, confidence, FX basis, tax exclusion/inclusion, and variance drivers.

## Controls

Forecasts never recognize revenue, alter contract/account mappings, approve budgets, or replace accounting policy. Finance validates revenue-recognition rules, source data, materiality, country/entity scope, and model drift before using a forecast for planning or reporting.
