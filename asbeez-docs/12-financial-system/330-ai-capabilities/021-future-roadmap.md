# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/330-ai-capabilities/021-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- implement governed explanations, classification, reconciliation assistance, anomaly/fraud prioritization, forecasting, report drafting, and read-only natural-language query;
- establish model/prompt/rule inventory, human approval, audit lineage, security controls, privacy minimization, evaluation, drift monitoring, and incident response; and
- connect recommendations to normal authorized APIs without direct financial side effects.

## Medium Term

- add country/entity-aware models, scenario analysis, evidence graphs, continuous control monitoring, model risk dashboards, and contract/evaluation automation;
- improve fairness, explainability, calibration, uncertainty, appeals, and human workflow ergonomics; and
- provide governed AI assistance for vendors, partners, treasury, tax, close, audit, and reporting.

## Long Term

- support specialized financial models and regional data boundaries with portable governance;
- use agentic orchestration only inside bounded read-only or approval-gated workflows; and
- preserve human accountability, deterministic controls, immutable ledger authority, and auditability through every capability expansion.

## Roadmap Gate

No AI capability may directly post, approve, pay, alter balances, release/freeze funds, certify, close, make final legal/compliance decisions, or bypass authentication, authorization, risk, tax, reconciliation, audit, privacy, country, or recovery controls.
