# Compliance Monitoring

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/330-ai-capabilities/015-compliance-monitoring.md

---

## Purpose

AI assists compliance monitoring by identifying patterns, prioritizing alerts, summarizing evidence, detecting policy drift, and recommending review paths across KYC/KYB, AML, sanctions, source of funds, tax, country, payout, wallet, payment, and vendor/partner activity.

## Output

Return alert type, scope, signals, affected subject/transactions, policy/version, jurisdiction, confidence, source evidence, urgency, false-positive context, and recommended human/legal/compliance action.

## Controls

AI cannot make final suspicious-activity, sanctions, KYC/KYB, account closure, fund freeze/release, payout denial, reporting, or legal decisions. Deterministic rules and authorized compliance officers remain authoritative. Sensitive evidence is minimized, access-controlled, retained, and appeal/review capable.
