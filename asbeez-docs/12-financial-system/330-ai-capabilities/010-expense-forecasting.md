# Expense Forecasting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/330-ai-capabilities/010-expense-forecasting.md

---

## Purpose

AI forecasts operating, payment/provider, reward, commission, tax, vendor/partner, treasury, technology, country, and capital expenses from approved actuals, commitments, budgets, and drivers.

## Output

Return expense classification, recognized/accrued/committed basis, horizon, cost center/entity/country, assumptions, seasonality, confidence, scenarios, materiality, variance, and source lineage. Identify one-time, recurring, contractual, discretionary, and policy-sensitive components.

## Controls

Forecasts do not create commitments, approve spend, capitalize assets, recognize expenses, change budgets, or post journals. Finance owners review classifications, policy, contract evidence, allocation, tax, and model drift before action.
