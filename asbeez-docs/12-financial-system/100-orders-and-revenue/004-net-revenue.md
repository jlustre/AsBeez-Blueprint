# Net Revenue

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/100-orders-and-revenue/004-net-revenue.md

---

## Purpose

Net revenue is recognized AsBeez revenue after approved contra-revenue items and presentation rules, not simply GMV less an arbitrary percentage.

## Components

Net revenue may be derived from recognized platform fees, subscriptions, advertising, technology services, and other approved obligations, less applicable discounts, refunds, credits, and contra-revenue items. Taxes collected for authorities, vendor collections, partner obligations, and reward quantities are excluded unless policy expressly requires another presentation.

## Rules

Each report defines gross basis, deductions, principal/agent treatment, recognition period, entity, country, currency, and policy version. Cash received, invoice issued, GMV, and net revenue remain separately reported.

## Corrections

Refunds, chargebacks, returns, cancellations, and revenue reclassification use linked contra entries or adjustments. Historical recognized revenue is never silently overwritten.
