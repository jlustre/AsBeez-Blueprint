## Purpose

Orders and Revenue define the financial meaning of AsBeez marketplace, vendor, partner, subscription, service, and advertising activity.

## AsBeez Boundary

An order is a commercial source. GMV is a marketplace activity measure. Customer payment, vendor entitlement, partner commission, tax, reserves, reward effects, and AsBeez revenue are separate values. Revenue is recognized only when the applicable performance obligation and accounting policy are satisfied.

## Principles

- server-calculated order totals and immutable source snapshots;
- explicit principal/agent, vendor, partner, tax, reward, and settlement treatment;
- no revenue recognition from order creation, invoice issuance, payment authorization, or reward issuance alone;
- refunds, chargebacks, returns, cancellations, and discounts propagate through controlled adjustments;
- revenue is classified by entity, country, currency, product/service, channel, and policy version; and
- GMV and operational metrics are never presented as company revenue or profit.
