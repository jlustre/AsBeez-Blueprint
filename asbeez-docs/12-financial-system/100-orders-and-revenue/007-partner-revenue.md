# Partner Revenue

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/100-orders-and-revenue/007-partner-revenue.md

---

## Purpose

Partner revenue and partner-related allocations describe amounts arising from approved partner services, integrations, referrals, or revenue-sharing agreements. Partner commission payable is not automatically AsBeez revenue.

## Required Detail

Partner, agreement/program version, source order/service, attribution, gross basis, fee or share, tax, reserve, adjustment, country/entity, currency, performance obligation, recognition period, settlement, and payout status.

## Rules

Registration, referral, or integration alone does not create revenue or payable. Partner obligations require qualifying commerce or approved service evidence. Revenue presentation follows contract and principal/agent policy; partner collections, commission, tax, and platform revenue remain separately reported.
