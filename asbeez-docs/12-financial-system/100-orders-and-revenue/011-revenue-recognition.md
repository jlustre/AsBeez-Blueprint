# Revenue Recognition

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/100-orders-and-revenue/011-revenue-recognition.md

---

## Purpose

Revenue recognition determines when an approved AsBeez performance obligation has been satisfied and an amount may move from deferred/accrued/unearned treatment to recognized revenue.

## Recognition Inputs

Contract/order/invoice, performance obligation, principal/agent conclusion, delivery or acceptance evidence, price allocation, tax, discounts, refunds, entity, country, currency, service period, policy version, and accounting period.

## Rules

- order creation, invoice issue, payment authorization, payment capture, GMV, vendor sale, partner referral, RP, ABC, or AHC does not automatically recognize revenue;
- revenue is recognized only for AsBeez’s earned performance obligation;
- taxes and amounts held for vendors/partners/authorities are excluded under approved presentation;
- estimates and variable consideration are constrained and versioned; and
- refunds, disputes, returns, cancellations, and chargebacks create linked reversals or contra-revenue.
