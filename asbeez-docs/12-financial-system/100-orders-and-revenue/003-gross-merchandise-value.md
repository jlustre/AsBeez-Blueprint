# Gross Merchandise Value

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/100-orders-and-revenue/003-gross-merchandise-value.md

---

## Purpose

Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) measures the gross value of marketplace merchandise or services ordered during a defined period before selected deductions. It is an operating metric, not automatically AsBeez revenue.

## Measurement

GMV definition must state whether it includes or excludes tax, shipping, discounts, cancellations, refunds, returns, chargebacks, failed payments, gift credits, and offline orders. The chosen version is effective-dated and reportable.

## AsBeez Use

GMV helps measure marketplace scale, vendor activity, category performance, and reward-source commerce. It includes customer order value regardless of whether AsBeez acts as principal or agent, while revenue reporting applies the approved accounting presentation.

## Controls

GMV is derived from order lines and status events, reconciled to order and payment populations, and restated through versioned metric logic. It must not be used in member or investor material as profit, income, cash, or company revenue without clear qualification.
