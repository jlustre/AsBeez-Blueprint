# Platform Fees

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/100-orders-and-revenue/005-platform-fees.md

---

## Purpose

Platform fees are amounts AsBeez charges for approved marketplace, vendor, partner, payment, listing, technology, or other platform services.

## Required Data

Fee ID, source order/contract/service, payer, basis, rate or fixed amount, currency, tax, country/entity, effective policy, principal/agent treatment, recognition condition, and refund/chargeback behavior.

## Rules

Platform fees are distinct from customer payment, vendor entitlement, partner commission, tax, reserve, and reward financial effects. A fee is not revenue until the related performance obligation is recognized. Fees cannot be introduced through client-supplied payment or reward fields.

## Reporting

Report assessed, invoiced, collected, recognized, deferred, refunded, disputed, and outstanding fees by vendor, partner, country, entity, currency, product, channel, and policy version.
