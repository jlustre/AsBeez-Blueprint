# Vendor Revenue

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/100-orders-and-revenue/006-vendor-revenue.md

---

## Purpose

Vendor revenue describes the vendor’s sales proceeds and entitlement generated through AsBeez marketplace activity. It is not automatically AsBeez revenue.

## Boundary

Vendor gross sales, vendor payable, AsBeez platform fee, tax, partner commission, reserve, refund, and reward effect are separate components. Principal/agent and contractual presentation determine whether any vendor-related amount appears in AsBeez revenue.

## Required Detail

Vendor, order/line, product/service, gross consideration, discounts, tax, platform fee, partner allocation, reserve, refund/chargeback, net entitlement, settlement period, currency, country/entity, and recognition policy.

## Rules

Vendor entitlement is calculated by Settlement and reconciles to vendor subledger and payable accounts. It is not paid merely because an order exists, and a vendor sale does not itself create RP, ABC, or AHC financial treatment.
