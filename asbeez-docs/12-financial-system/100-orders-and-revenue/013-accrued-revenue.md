# Accrued Revenue

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/100-orders-and-revenue/013-accrued-revenue.md

---

## Purpose

Accrued revenue records an earned AsBeez performance obligation for which invoicing or collection has not yet occurred, when recognition criteria and evidence are satisfied.

## Required Evidence

Contract/order/service, delivered milestone or usage, amount calculation, customer, entity, country, currency, tax, billing timing, recognition policy, period, and subsequent invoice/settlement plan.

## Rules

Accruals are estimates subject to review and reversal or conversion when invoiced. They cannot be created from GMV, order placement, unpaid vendor sales, RP/ABC/AHC events, or unverified service claims. Uncollectible or disputed amounts are separately assessed.
