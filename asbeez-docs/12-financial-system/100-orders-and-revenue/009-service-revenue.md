# Service Revenue

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/100-orders-and-revenue/009-service-revenue.md

---

## Purpose

Service revenue arises from completed or continuously delivered AsBeez platform, technology, professional, integration, support, or other approved services.

## Required Detail

Contract/order, service description, customer, provider/partner, deliverable or milestone, acceptance evidence, price, tax, currency, entity, period, invoice/payment, and recognition policy.

## Rules

Revenue recognition follows the performance obligation and evidence of delivery, not merely invoice or payment status. Failed, disputed, refunded, or incomplete services create controlled deferral, reversal, credit, or adjustment. Service revenue is distinct from vendor collections, partner commission, tax, and reward effects.
