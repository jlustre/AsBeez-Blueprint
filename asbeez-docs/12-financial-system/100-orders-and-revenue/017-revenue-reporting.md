# Revenue Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/100-orders-and-revenue/017-revenue-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Revenue reporting separates marketplace activity, customer consideration, vendor/partner obligations, AsBeez revenue, tax, costs, rewards, settlements, cash, and profitability.

## Report Families

- GMV by order, vendor, category, country, channel, and period;
- gross and net recognized revenue by stream and performance obligation;
- platform fees, subscriptions, services, advertising, and partner-related revenue;
- vendor and partner gross sales, entitlements, commissions, and payouts;
- tax, refunds, chargebacks, discounts, reserves, and reward financial effects;
- deferred, accrued, unearned, invoiced, collected, and outstanding amounts; and
- reconciliation, exceptions, forecast, and certified financial statements.

## Rules

Every report states definition, basis, period, entity, country, currency, principal/agent treatment, policy version, source, and reconciliation status. GMV is not called revenue; revenue is not called cash, earnings, or profit without approved context.

## Certification

Certified reports derive from reconciled order, invoice, payment, settlement, subledger, and GL records. Management reports may be provisional but disclose freshness, estimates, and exceptions.
