# Unearned Revenue

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/100-orders-and-revenue/014-unearned-revenue.md

---

## Purpose

Unearned revenue is consideration received or billed for goods or services that AsBeez has not yet delivered or recognized as revenue. It is generally a liability until the obligation is satisfied or the amount is refunded/adjusted.

## Distinction

Unearned revenue is a financial accounting state. It is not the same as vendor payable, customer wallet balance, reward liability, tax payable, or provider clearing, even when the source cash is shared in an order flow.

## Rules

Each balance references contract/order/invoice, performance obligation, service period, customer, entity, country, currency, tax, schedule, and policy. Release, refund, cancellation, credit, or write-off is a new controlled event and remains reconcilable.
