# Subscription Revenue

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/100-orders-and-revenue/008-subscription-revenue.md

---

## Purpose

Subscription revenue is recognized from approved member, vendor, enterprise, technology, or service subscriptions as the contracted service is delivered.

## Required Detail

Subscription/contract, customer, service period, plan/version, price, discounts, tax, currency, entity, invoice/payment, performance obligation, cancellation/proration, and recognition schedule.

## Rules

Invoice issuance or cash receipt may create receivable or deferred/unearned revenue, not immediate earned revenue. Pauses, cancellations, upgrades, downgrades, refunds, and credits create explicit schedule adjustments. Subscription status does not create RP, ABC, AHC, vendor, partner, or payout effects unless separate rules apply.
