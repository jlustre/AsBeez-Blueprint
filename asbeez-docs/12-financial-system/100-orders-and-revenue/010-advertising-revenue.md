# Advertising Revenue

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/100-orders-and-revenue/010-advertising-revenue.md

---

## Purpose

Advertising revenue arises from approved sponsored placement, campaigns, impressions, clicks, leads, or other advertising services delivered to vendors, partners, or advertisers.

## Required Detail

Campaign/contract, advertiser, placement, target, service metric, rate, budget, delivery evidence, tax, currency, entity, invoice/payment, credit/refund, and recognition schedule.

## Rules

Campaign budget or invoice issuance is not earned revenue until the contracted advertising service is delivered. Invalid traffic, cancelled campaigns, under-delivery, credits, refunds, and disputes create explicit adjustments. Vendor-funded promotions and reward-funded promotions are not advertising revenue unless an approved service was delivered.
