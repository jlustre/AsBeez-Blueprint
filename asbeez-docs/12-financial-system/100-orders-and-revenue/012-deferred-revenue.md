# Deferred Revenue

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/100-orders-and-revenue/012-deferred-revenue.md

---

## Purpose

Deferred revenue records consideration received or invoiced before AsBeez has satisfied the related performance obligation.

## Sources

Subscriptions, prepaid services, implementation fees, advertising campaigns, platform access, or other contracts with future delivery.

## Required Schedule

Contract/invoice, customer, obligation, amount, currency, service period, start/end, recognition method, entity, country, tax treatment, remaining balance, adjustment, and policy version.

## Rules

Release to revenue follows delivery or service evidence and period close. Cancellation, refund, credit, impairment, or contract change updates the schedule through controlled events. Deferred revenue is not a vendor payable, reward liability, or cash balance merely because it is unrecognized.
