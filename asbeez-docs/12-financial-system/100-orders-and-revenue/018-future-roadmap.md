# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/100-orders-and-revenue/018-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve launch entities, countries, currencies, revenue streams, principal/agent treatment, and policies;
- connect orders, invoices, payments, tax, vendor/partner settlements, rewards, reserves, and GL mappings;
- implement GMV, net revenue, platform fee, subscription, service, advertising, and recognition schedules;
- implement refunds, chargebacks, discounts, notes, deferred/accrued/unearned corrections; and
- establish reconciliation, reporting, audit, and period-close controls.

## Medium Term

- support multi-entity and localized statutory revenue reporting;
- automate order allocation, revenue schedules, payment matching, and settlement reconciliation;
- improve vendor/partner statements and margin analysis;
- add recurring billing, campaign measurement, and tax evidence; and
- provide explainable forecasting and anomaly detection.

## Long Term

- support regional revenue and consolidation services;
- provide governed partner and advertising integrations;
- improve revenue, GMV, reward-liability, and liquidity forecasting; and
- preserve portable source-to-report evidence across jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No metric or revenue feature may present GMV, customer payment, vendor proceeds, partner commission, reward value, tax, cash, or recognized revenue as another category without approved definition, source, policy, reconciliation, and disclosure.
