# Partner Reconciliation

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/120-partner-finance/013-partner-reconciliation.md

---

## Purpose

Partner reconciliation proves that qualifying source activity, attribution, commissions, bonuses, incentives, fees, tax, reserves, holds, settlements, payouts, statements, and General Ledger control accounts agree.

## Required Checks

- partner activity maps to genuine order/service/campaign evidence;
- attribution and program version are valid;
- commission/bonus/incentive calculations agree to approved rules and funding;
- fees, tax, reserves, holds, refunds, and chargebacks agree;
- settlement and payout agree to provider/bank evidence; and
- country/entity/currency/policy dimensions are consistent.

## Exceptions

Registration-only earning, duplicate attribution, missing order/service, unexplained residual, premature payout, stale policy, provider mismatch, or reward/partner misclassification is an owned exception even when totals balance.

## Evidence

Reconciliation stores populations, monetary totals, source periods, program/policy versions, account mappings, reviewer, approval, age, unmatched items, and resolution references.
