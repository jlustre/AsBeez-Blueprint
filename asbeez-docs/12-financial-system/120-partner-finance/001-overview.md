## Purpose

Partner Finance records the financial relationship between AsBeez and approved strategic, referral, integration, service, or channel partners: qualifying activity, commissions, bonuses, incentives, fees, reserves, holds, settlement, payouts, tax, statements, and reconciliation.

## AsBeez Boundary

Partner registration, referral, genealogy, or participation does not itself create revenue or a payable. A partner obligation requires qualifying commerce or approved service evidence, attribution, program rules, compliance, funding, and settlement approval.

## Principles

- partner activity, vendor sales, customer payment, platform revenue, tax, and reward effects are separate;
- commission, bonus, and incentive types have distinct qualification and funding rules;
- refunds, chargebacks, fraud, and attribution disputes propagate through explicit adjustments;
- holds and reserves remain attributable and have release conditions;
- payout executes approved settlement; and
- partner reporting distinguishes estimated, approved, payable, paid, and reversed values.
