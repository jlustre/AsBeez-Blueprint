# Partner Commissions

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/120-partner-finance/004-partner-commissions.md

---

## Purpose

Partner commissions are obligations calculated under an approved referral, channel, integration, or revenue-sharing program.

## Required Data

Commission ID, partner/program/agreement, source order/service, attribution, qualification evidence, basis, rate/fixed amount, tax, reserve, hold, country/entity, currency, cap, approval, settlement, payout, and reversal.

## Rules

Registration, recruitment, or referral count alone cannot create a commission. The program must identify genuine qualifying commerce or approved service delivery, funding source, liability ceiling, customer/refund period, and chargeback/fraud treatment. Commission is distinct from platform revenue and vendor entitlement.
