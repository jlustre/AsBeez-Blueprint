# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/120-partner-finance/018-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve partner types, countries, entities, currencies, programs, funding, tax, reserve, and payout rules;
- connect partner activity, orders/services, attribution, commissions, bonuses, incentives, settlement, payouts, and GL mappings;
- implement partner account, earnings, holds, reserves, adjustments, statements, and reconciliation;
- implement destination verification, compliance, tax, APIs, events, security, and audit; and
- publish reports that separate activity, payable, paid, tax, and AsBeez revenue.

## Medium Term

- support localized partner finance and multi-entity settlement;
- automate attribution, provider/bank reconciliation, refund, chargeback, and reserve propagation;
- improve program funding, liability, and payout forecasting; and
- add explainable AI assistance with human approval.

## Long Term

- support regional partner finance services and high-volume program processing;
- provide governed partner integrations and self-service statements;
- strengthen program-risk and liquidity forecasting; and
- preserve portable partner evidence across jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No partner feature may present registration, referral, genealogy, bonus, incentive, commission, activity, payable, payout, cash, or AsBeez revenue as another category without approved qualification, funding, policy, tax, accounting, disclosure, reconciliation, and audit rules.
