# Partner Financial Reporting

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/120-partner-finance/014-partner-financial-reporting.md

---

## Purpose

Partner financial reporting separates partner activity, attributed commerce, approved service value, commissions, bonuses, incentives, fees, tax, reserves, settlements, payouts, and AsBeez revenue.

## Report Families

- attributed orders, GMV, service activity, and campaign performance;
- qualified, calculated, approved, payable, paid, held, reversed, and disputed earnings;
- commissions, bonuses, incentives, fees, tax, reserves, and adjustments;
- settlement, payout, provider fee, outstanding payable, and exceptions; and
- country, entity, currency, program, partner type, source, and policy views.

## Rules

Every report labels source definition, period, currency, entity, country, program version, qualification status, reconciliation status, and whether the amount is estimated or paid. Partner activity is not called AsBeez revenue, cash, profit, or guaranteed income without approved context.

## Certification

Certified reports derive from reconciled partner/commission subledgers, settlement, payout, tax, order/service, and GL records. Provisional reports disclose freshness, estimates, and exceptions.
