# Partner Incentives

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/120-partner-finance/006-partner-incentives.md

---

## Purpose

Partner incentives are approved commercial or service-performance programs intended to influence partner behavior or delivery while remaining funded, measurable, and compliant.

## Required Data

Incentive ID, objective, partner population, qualifying activity, period, measurement, reward type, amount/rate, funding source, cap, country/entity, policy, approval, tax, and reversal rules.

## Rules

Incentives cannot reward mere recruitment or participant addition without qualifying commerce or approved service value. They cannot guarantee income, appreciation, passive return, or payout. The incentive may be non-monetary, a commission, bonus, expense, or approved payable; classification is explicit.

## Controls

Monitor budget, qualification, fraud, refund, chargeback, attribution, and country restrictions. Stop or hold the program when funding, liability ceiling, compliance, or reconciliation controls fail.
