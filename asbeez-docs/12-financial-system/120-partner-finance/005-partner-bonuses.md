# Partner Bonuses

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/120-partner-finance/005-partner-bonuses.md

---

## Purpose

Partner bonuses are additional approved benefits tied to a defined program milestone, qualifying commerce, service performance, campaign, or other lawful activity.

## Required Data

Bonus ID, partner/program, milestone or source activity, eligibility period, calculation, cap, funding owner, currency/unit, country/entity, tax, approval, hold, reversal, and payout/redemption condition.

## Rules

A bonus is not created by rank, registration, recruitment, or self-reported achievement alone. The program must define evidence, funding, maximum liability, customer/refund impact, country availability, and disclosure. Non-monetary bonus units remain separate from monetary payable.
