# Partner Reserves

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/120-partner-finance/007-partner-reserves.md

---

## Purpose

Partner reserves restrict a documented amount of partner entitlement to protect against refunds, chargebacks, fraud, attribution disputes, service failure, tax, or other approved exposure.

## Required Data

Reserve ID, partner/program, source population, exposure, basis, rate/amount, currency, country/entity, start/review/release conditions, owner, approval, utilization, and settlement/GL mapping.

## Rules

Reserve is not revenue, commission, forfeiture, or free cash. It remains attributable to the partner and exposure population. Release or utilization is a new event and cannot be applied to another partner, program, country, or activity.

## Monitoring

Report reserved, released, utilized, expired, and outstanding amounts by partner, program, exposure, country, entity, currency, age, and policy version.
