# Partner Statements

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/120-partner-finance/012-partner-statements.md

---

## Purpose

Partner statements provide an ordered, reconciled view of source activity, earnings, commissions, bonuses, incentives, fees, tax, reserves, holds, adjustments, settlement, payouts, and remaining payable.

## Contents

Partner/program/agreement, period, source activity, gross basis, commission/bonus/incentive, fees, tax, reserves, holds, adjustments, approved settlement, payout, available payable, exceptions, currency, country/entity, and source references.

## Rules

Statements distinguish estimated, qualified, approved, payable, paid, held, reversed, and disputed values. Registration, referral count, reward quantity, GMV, and vendor sales are not presented as partner cash or guaranteed income.

## Certification

Certified statements derive from reconciled partner/commission subledger, settlement, payout, tax, and GL projections. Provisional statements disclose freshness, estimates, and exceptions.
