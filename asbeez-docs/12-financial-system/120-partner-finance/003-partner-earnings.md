# Partner Earnings

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/120-partner-finance/003-partner-earnings.md

---

## Purpose

Partner earnings record amounts calculated for an approved partner program before and after fees, tax, reserves, holds, refunds, chargebacks, and settlement.

## Qualification

An earning requires a qualifying order, service, integration, campaign, or other approved activity; attribution evidence; program/agreement version; partner eligibility; country/compliance status; and funding rule.

## Required Detail

Partner, program/agreement, source order/service/activity, attribution, gross basis, rate/fixed amount, tax, fee, reserve, hold, adjustment, currency, country/entity, settlement period, approval, and payout references.

## Rules

Partner earnings are not automatically AsBeez revenue and are not created from registration, invitation, genealogy, or recruitment alone. Reversals preserve the original earning and link to refund, chargeback, fraud, attribution, or policy evidence.
