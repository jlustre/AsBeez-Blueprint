# Operating Model

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/370-operations/002-operating-model.md

---

## Purpose

The operating model assigns accountability, decision rights, service ownership, controls, coverage, escalation, and evidence requirements for financial operations.

## Roles

Finance operations manages queues and workflows; accounting owns policy, chart, ledger, close, and reporting; treasury owns cash/liquidity; tax owns tax decisions; risk/compliance owns holds and reviews; engineering owns platform reliability; support manages customer/vendor/partner communication; audit provides independent assurance.

## Rules

Use least privilege, separation of duties, dual control for material/high-risk actions, country/entity scope, documented runbooks, shift handoff, queue aging, SLOs, incident process, and reconciliation. Operators may recommend or initiate approved workflows but cannot bypass authority or alter immutable facts.
