# Tax Operations

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/370-operations/011-tax-operations.md

---

## Purpose

Tax operations manage registrations, jurisdiction, calculation, collection, withholding, exemptions, filings, remittance, notices, provider evidence, adjustments, and reconciliation.

## Procedures

Review transactions and nexus/registration, validate classifications/address, calculate and approve tax, reconcile tax payable/receivable/withholding, prepare filings/remittance, track deadlines, resolve provider/jurisdiction exceptions, and retain evidence by country/entity/period.

## Rules

Tax collected or withheld is not revenue. Operators cannot overwrite historical calculations, bypass registration or exemption controls, or approve their own material adjustment. Corrections use notes/adjustments and linked ledger/tax evidence.
