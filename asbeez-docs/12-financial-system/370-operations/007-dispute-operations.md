# Dispute Operations

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/370-operations/007-dispute-operations.md

---

## Purpose

Dispute operations manage chargeback intake, deadlines, evidence, representment, arbitration, liability, reserves, recovery, loss, communications, and accounting reconciliation.

## Procedures

Monitor provider cases and response deadlines, validate payment/order/evidence, request authorized evidence, track liability and reserve, submit representment, record outcome, notify affected parties, and reconcile fees/reversals/recovery/loss.

## Rules

Provider case status is evidence until approved mapping. Operators cannot fabricate evidence, suppress a deadline, change liability without authority, release reserves, or alter posted records. Case history, evidence hashes, decisions, approvals, and financial effects remain auditable.
