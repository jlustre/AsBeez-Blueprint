# Period Close Operations

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/370-operations/009-period-close-operations.md

---

## Purpose

Period-close operations execute calendar, cutoff, accrual, deferral, adjustment, reconciliation, trial-balance, reporting, approval, lock, reopen, and certification controls.

## Procedure

Confirm scope and calendar; freeze/cut off inputs; review queues and late data; complete ledger/subledger/provider/tax/treasury reconciliations; record approved accruals/deferrals/adjustments; validate trial balance and reports; complete checklist and approvals; lock/close; publish exceptions and evidence.

## Rules

Do not force unresolved payment, payout, vendor, partner, reward, tax, reserve, FX, treasury, or provider differences into a clean result. Reopening a locked/closed period requires authority, reason, impact assessment, and auditable correction.
