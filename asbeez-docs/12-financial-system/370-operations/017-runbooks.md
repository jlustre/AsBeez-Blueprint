# Runbooks

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/370-operations/017-runbooks.md

---

## Purpose

Runbooks provide approved, versioned, executable guidance for recurring operations, exception response, incident containment, reconciliation, close, continuity, and recovery.

## Runbook Contract

Each runbook defines purpose, trigger, scope, prerequisites, authority, roles, inputs, checks, safe steps, stop conditions, commands/queries, expected evidence, rollback/compensation, reconciliation, escalation, communication, completion criteria, owner, version, review date, and test history.

## Rules

Runbooks never instruct direct edits to posted journals, balances, provider evidence, audit records, or immutable events. Destructive, material, payout, reserve, period, security, tax, compliance, and country/entity actions require explicit approval and separation of duties. Every execution records operator, time, correlation, output, exception, and review.
