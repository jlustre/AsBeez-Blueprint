# Financial Support

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/370-operations/012-financial-support.md

---

## Purpose

Financial support handles customer, member, vendor, partner, and operator questions about payments, wallets, invoices, refunds, payouts, rewards, statements, disputes, tax, and reconciliation without exposing sensitive data or bypassing controls.

## Procedure

Authenticate and scope the requester; classify issue; inspect authorized projections and source references; explain pending/unknown/provider-evidence state; use approved commands or escalate to finance/risk/compliance/treasury; communicate expected timing; record correlation, evidence, action, approval, and resolution.

## Rules

Support cannot edit balances, mark payment/payout/refund complete, release holds, alter tax/revenue/accounting, disclose another party’s data, or bypass identity, risk, approval, or dispute processes. Suspected fraud, account takeover, privacy, legal, and security issues follow controlled escalation.
