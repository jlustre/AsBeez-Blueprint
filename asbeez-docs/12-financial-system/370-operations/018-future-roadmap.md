# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/370-operations/018-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- establish accountable operating model, daily controls, queues, runbooks, handoffs, escalation, support, exception, incident, continuity, and recovery standards;
- operationalize payment, payout, refund, dispute, reconciliation, close, treasury, tax, and provider workflows; and
- connect operations to observability, testing, audit, security, compliance, and financial control totals.

## Medium Term

- add workflow automation for safe triage, evidence collection, reconciliation, queue prioritization, and runbook execution with approval gates;
- improve regional/country operations, provider failover, staffing/capacity planning, SLOs, recovery exercises, and post-incident remediation; and
- measure operational quality, aging, cost, customer/vendor/partner impact, and control effectiveness.

## Long Term

- support independently operated financial contexts with consistent controls and portable runbooks;
- provide predictive operational risk and capacity analysis; and
- preserve human accountability, ledger authority, reconciliation, auditability, privacy, and recovery through automation.

## Roadmap Gate

No operating change may bypass authorization, separation of duties, immutable accounting, reconciliation, tax/compliance, privacy, country/entity, security, audit, or recovery controls.
