# Daily Financial Operations

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/370-operations/003-daily-financial-operations.md

---

## Purpose

Daily financial operations provide repeatable checks and actions for the prior-day and current-day financial state across all entities, countries, currencies, providers, and periods.

## Daily Control Cycle

Review system health, payment/payout/refund/dispute queues, wallet and reserve movements, provider callbacks/settlements, ledger posting, control totals, reconciliation exceptions, tax/FX issues, liquidity, fraud/compliance holds, support cases, and alert/incident status. Confirm handoff, ownership, aging, and unresolved risk.

## Rules

Use approved reports and runbooks; compare projections/subledgers to ledger; investigate unknown and duplicate states; record approvals/actions; communicate material customer/vendor/partner impact; and escalate control failures. Daily operations never force balances or suppress exceptions to make a report appear clean.
