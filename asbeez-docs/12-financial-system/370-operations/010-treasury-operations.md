# Treasury Operations

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/370-operations/010-treasury-operations.md

---

## Purpose

Treasury operations manage bank/cash accounts, cash positioning, liquidity, transfers, funding, reserves, concentration, settlement, FX exposure, country/entity restrictions, and forecasting.

## Procedures

Review available/restricted cash, expected payment/payout/refund/tax/vendor/partner obligations, provider/bank settlement, reserve requirements, currency exposure, concentration, forecast variance, reconciliations, and transfer queues. Execute approved transfers with dual control and evidence.

## Rules

Do not treat reserves, liabilities, pending provider funds, or reward units as free cash. Transfers require entity/country/currency, sanctions, limits, source/destination, approval, idempotency, and reconciliation. Treasury forecasts and dashboards do not authorize movement.
