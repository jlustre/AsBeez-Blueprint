# Incident Management

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/370-operations/014-incident-management.md

---

## Purpose

Operations incident management coordinates detection, triage, containment, communication, recovery, financial impact assessment, reconciliation, evidence, and remediation for service, provider, security, compliance, data, and accounting incidents.

## Procedure

Declare severity and scope; assign incident and financial owners; preserve logs/traces/events/provider evidence; contain safely; communicate; isolate or pause affected workflows; assess customer/vendor/partner/country/entity/currency/period impact; recover; reconcile; complete security/compliance/audit review; document root cause and corrective action.

## Rules

Do not edit posted records, blindly retry unknown provider effects, suppress exceptions, release holds, or declare success without authoritative evidence and reconciliation. Material incidents require approvals, decision log, timeline, notifications, customer/support handling, and post-incident testing.
