# Reconciliation Operations

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/370-operations/008-reconciliation-operations.md

---

## Purpose

Reconciliation operations compare ledger, subledger, bank, provider, wallet, reward, vendor, partner, tax, reserve, and country sources and resolve differences through controlled workflows.

## Procedures

Schedule and run scope; capture source snapshots; validate completeness, period, entity, country, currency, keys, timing, tolerance, and control totals; classify matches/differences; assign owners; propose and approve resolution; certify or reopen with evidence.

## Rules

Never hide differences, edit balances, or certify without defined scope and reviewer evidence. Provider, bank, and operational status are evidence until matched. Adjustments are authorized, idempotent, linked to source and ledger, and followed by post-adjustment reconciliation.
