# Payout Fees

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/080-payouts-and-withdrawals/010-payout-fees.md

---

## Purpose

Payout fees are provider, bank, network, platform, or conversion costs charged to an approved payout according to disclosed policy.

## Required Detail

Fee type, payer, amount or rate, currency, provider, effective period, tax treatment, minimum/maximum, source, and account mapping.

## Rules

Fees are shown before approval where applicable and cannot convert a non-monetary reward into a payable amount. Fees are distinct from vendor/partner commission, marketplace revenue, tax, reserve, and reward expense. Provider fees reconcile to provider settlement evidence.

## Corrections

Incorrect fees are corrected through adjustment or compensating entries referencing the payout and original fee; they are not silently overwritten.
