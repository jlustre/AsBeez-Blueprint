# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/080-payouts-and-withdrawals/021-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve payoutable sources, countries, entities, currencies, providers, and legal classifications;
- implement withdrawal request, validation, approval, hold, processing, retry, failure, and reversal lifecycles;
- connect wallets, vendor/partner settlement, reward conversion, payment/provider subledgers, and GL mappings;
- implement destination verification, compliance, limits, fees, reconciliation, and audit; and
- publish secure APIs, events, statements, and operations workflows.

## Medium Term

- support regional payout methods and provider routing;
- automate provider/bank reconciliation and returned-fund handling;
- improve reserve, tax, fraud, and liquidity forecasting;
- add controlled batch processing and multi-entity settlement; and
- provide explainable anomaly detection with human approval.

## Long Term

- support regional payout services and high-volume event processing;
- expose governed partner payout capabilities;
- support approved reward redemption/payout programs; and
- provide portable audit evidence across providers and jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No payout feature may send RP, ABC, AHC, commission, wallet, or settlement value as money without approved source classification, funding, destination, country, compliance, tax, reserve, accounting, disclosure, reconciliation, and reversal rules.
