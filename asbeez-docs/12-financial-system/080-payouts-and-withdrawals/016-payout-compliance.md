# Payout Compliance

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/080-payouts-and-withdrawals/016-payout-compliance.md

---

## Purpose

Payout compliance determines whether an approved monetary obligation may be sent to a recipient under country, entity, tax, identity, sanctions, source-of-funds, program, and provider rules.

## Controls

- member/vendor/partner identity and beneficiary verification;
- KYC/KYB and beneficial ownership where required;
- sanctions and restricted-party screening;
- source-of-funds and transaction monitoring;
- tax residency, withholding, reporting, and documentation;
- reward/commission classification and income disclosure;
- fraud, refund, chargeback, reserve, and dispute review; and
- country and provider availability.

## AsBeez Reward Boundary

RP, ABC, AHC, matrix placement, or referral display does not establish a payout entitlement. A payout requires an approved monetary conversion or settlement obligation, disclosed terms, funding, liability/accounting treatment, and jurisdictional approval.

## Failure Behavior

Missing, stale, failed, or restricted compliance evidence blocks, holds, or rejects payout. Screening details are minimized and access-controlled while decision reason, policy version, reviewer/system, and next action remain auditable.
