# Minimum And Maximum Payouts

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/080-payouts-and-withdrawals/009-minimum-and-maximum-payouts.md

---

## Purpose

Minimum and maximum payout rules control operating cost, risk, liquidity, tax, fraud exposure, and country/program limits.

## Limit Inputs

Wallet/payout type, source program, member/vendor/partner status, country, currency, risk tier, compliance status, daily/monthly/lifetime totals, reserve, fees, tax, and provider capability.

## Rules

Limits are effective-dated and disclosed before request. A minimum does not make a reward payable; a maximum does not authorize a payout beyond available approved value. Failed checks do not debit or consume eligibility.

## Exceptions

Overrides require authorized role, reason, evidence, expiry, second approval where material, and post-event review. Country or program restrictions cannot be overridden by ordinary operations.
