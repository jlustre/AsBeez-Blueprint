# Revenue Recognition Policy

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/004-revenue-recognition-policy.md

---

## Purpose

Revenue recognition policy determines when AsBeez has satisfied performance obligations and may recognize revenue from marketplace fees, subscriptions, services, advertising, technology, and other approved streams.

## Rules

Order creation, invoice issue, payment authorization/capture, GMV, vendor sale, partner referral, RP, ABC, and AHC do not automatically recognize revenue. Policy defines principal/agent treatment, variable consideration, refunds, chargebacks, credits, deferrals, accruals, country/entity/currency, evidence, and reversal.

## Governance

Accounting, Finance, Legal/Tax, and relevant business owners approve policy and examples. Every recognized amount references policy version, performance evidence, period, entity, country, currency, and GL mapping.
