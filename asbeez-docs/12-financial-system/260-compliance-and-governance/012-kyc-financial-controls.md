# KYC Financial Controls

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/012-kyc-financial-controls.md

---

## Purpose

KYC/KYB controls verify identity, legal existence, ownership, control, residency, tax status, risk, and beneficiary eligibility before permitted financial activity.

## Required Checks

Identity/business documents, beneficial ownership, tax registration, address/residency, sanctions/PEP/adverse media where required, source of funds, destination ownership, country restrictions, review expiry, and evidence integrity.

## Rules

KYC/KYB status is scoped to person/entity, country, activity, and effective date. Missing, stale, failed, or restricted status blocks or holds relevant payment, wallet, payout, settlement, or account operations. Sensitive data is minimized and access-controlled.
