# Tax Policy

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/010-tax-policy.md

---

## Purpose

Tax policy defines jurisdiction, registration, place of supply, taxable basis, rates, exemptions, withholding, invoicing, filing, remittance, reporting, corrections, and evidence.

## Rules

Tax collected/withheld is not AsBeez revenue or an arbitrary fee. Tax is separate from pricing, vendor/partner obligation, reward, reserve, payout, and revenue recognition. Refunds, returns, chargebacks, discounts, notes, and withholding create controlled tax adjustments.

## Governance

Tax and Legal own jurisdictional interpretation; Finance, Product, Payments, Invoicing, Vendor/Partner, and Operations implement approved versions. Provider output is evidence/input, not a substitute for approved policy.
