# Reserve Policy

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/007-reserve-policy.md

---

## Purpose

Reserve policy defines when AsBeez creates, funds, measures, holds, releases, utilizes, returns, and reports reserves for refunds, chargebacks, vendors, partners, rewards, liquidity, tax, country, and operating exposure.

## Rules

Every reserve has purpose, exposure, owner, beneficiary, source, currency, entity, country, assumptions, target, funding, release/use condition, review cadence, and account mapping. Reserve is not revenue, free cash, realized loss, payable, or reward value by default.

## Governance

Finance/Treasury, Legal/Tax, Risk, and relevant business owners approve material reserve methodology. Changes are effective-dated, tested, reconciled, and disclosed.
