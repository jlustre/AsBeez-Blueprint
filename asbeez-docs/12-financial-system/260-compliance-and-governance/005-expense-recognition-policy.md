# Expense Recognition Policy

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/005-expense-recognition-policy.md

---

## Purpose

Expense recognition policy determines when resources consumed for AsBeez operations, payments, rewards, refunds, chargebacks, commissions, reserves, tax, technology, and services become expense.

## Rules

Cash payment, budget approval, invoice receipt, reserve funding, or reward quantity does not automatically equal expense. Policy defines period, accrual/deferral, capitalization, allocation, evidence, country/entity/currency, reversal, and account mapping.

## Controls

Expenses require owner, source, approval, budget/commitment context, tax treatment, classification, and close-period evidence. Material estimates are reviewed and versioned.
