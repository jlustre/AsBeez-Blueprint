# Financial Governance

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/002-financial-governance.md

---

## Purpose

Financial governance assigns decision rights, accountability, oversight, escalation, policy ownership, risk acceptance, control testing, and change authority across AsBeez Finance.

## Governance Bodies

Finance/Accounting, Treasury, Tax, Legal/Compliance, Risk/Fraud, Product, Engineering, Operations, Internal Audit, executive leadership, and country operators have defined roles and delegated limits.

## Required Governance

- chart and accounting policy;
- revenue, expense, tax, reward, reserve, payout, and refund classification;
- country/entity launch and change approval;
- material transaction, adjustment, write-off, exception, and risk acceptance;
- control testing, close certification, audit response, and incident escalation; and
- policy/version and evidence retention.

## Rules

No team may unilaterally change a policy owned by another control domain or bypass a legal, tax, accounting, sanctions, KYC, or audit gate.
