# Asset Capitalization Policy

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/006-asset-capitalization-policy.md

---

## Purpose

Asset capitalization policy determines when technology, software, equipment, facilities, data, or acquisition expenditure creates an asset instead of current-period expense.

## Required Evidence

Project/asset, legal entity, country, amount/currency, ownership/control, useful life, service date, capitalization threshold, components, impairment, depreciation/amortization, approval, and close/reporting impact.

## Rules

Budget approval, vendor invoice, cash payment, or forecast does not automatically capitalize an asset. Reward, wallet, reserve, vendor/partner, and fund balances are not company assets without approved accounting classification.
