# Audit Requirements

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/018-audit-requirements.md

---

## Purpose

Audit requirements ensure an independent reviewer can trace financial decisions, records, controls, approvals, calculations, exceptions, and reports from source activity through ledger and outcome.

## Required Evidence

Orders, payments, invoices, refunds, disputes, wallets, rewards, vendors, partners, tax, reserves, treasury, FX, budgets, reconciliations, policies, approvals, model versions, access, exports, and corrections.

## Rules

Posted records and evidence are immutable or append-only. Audit access is least-privilege and logged. Findings have owner, severity, remediation, due date, retest, closure approval, and retained evidence. AI recommendations retain provenance and human decisions.
