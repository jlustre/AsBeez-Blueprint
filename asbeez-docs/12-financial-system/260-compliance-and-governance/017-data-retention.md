# Data Retention

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/017-data-retention.md

---

## Purpose

Data retention preserves financial, tax, payment, payout, reward, wallet, fraud, KYC/AML, sanctions, policy, approval, reconciliation, and audit evidence for the longest applicable legal, regulatory, accounting, dispute, and contractual period.

## Rules

Retention schedules are record-type, country, entity, privacy, and legal-hold aware. Archived data is integrity-protected, access-controlled, discoverable, and restorable. Personal data is minimized or redacted only when financial/evidentiary obligations permit. Destruction requires approval, scope, schedule, date, and audit record.

## Lifecycle

```text
Active -> Archived -> Legal Hold | Eligible for Destruction -> Destroyed
```

Retention decisions record record type, jurisdiction, schedule, legal hold, owner, authorization, destruction evidence, and integrity verification.
