# Approval Matrices

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/016-approval-matrices.md

---

## Purpose

Approval matrices define who may request, review, approve, execute, override, reopen, or certify financial actions by amount, risk, country, entity, currency, program, and operation.

## Operations

Adjustments, write-offs, payouts, transfers, refunds, reserves, tax filings, reward liability, vendor/partner settlement, risk releases, reconciliation exceptions, period close, and report certification.

## Rules

Thresholds and roles are versioned and effective-dated. Approval cannot be self-approved where separation of duties applies. Delegation, expiry, quorum, emergency override, evidence, and post-event review are explicit.

## Evidence

Every approval records request, scope, amount/currency, entity/country, operation, policy/version, risk, approvers, delegation/quorum, decision, conditions, timestamp, and downstream action.
