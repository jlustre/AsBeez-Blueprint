# Refund Policy

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/008-refund-policy.md

---

## Purpose

Refund policy defines eligibility, timing, line scope, method, fees, tax, return, cancellation, customer rights, vendor/partner adjustment, reward reversal, wallet credit, fraud review, and reconciliation.

## Rules

Refunds preserve original order, payment, invoice, vendor, partner, reward, reserve, payout, and ledger history. Fraud signals may trigger review but do not independently deny lawful rights. Full/partial refunds cannot exceed eligible source value, and each downstream effect is separately controlled.

## Governance

Consumer/legal, Tax, Finance, Payments, Operations, Vendor/Partner, Rewards, and Risk owners approve country/product variations and material changes.
