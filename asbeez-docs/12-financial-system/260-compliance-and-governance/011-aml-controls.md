# AML Controls

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/011-aml-controls.md

---

## Purpose

AML controls identify, prevent, monitor, investigate, escalate, and report money-laundering and terrorist-financing risk across payments, wallets, payouts, transfers, vendors, partners, rewards, and treasury.

## Controls

Customer/member/vendor/partner risk, transaction monitoring, velocity, source of funds, sanctions, KYC/KYB, beneficial ownership, suspicious activity escalation, record retention, training, case management, and regulatory reporting.

## Rules

Alerts are risk signals, not proof. Decisions are evidence-based, proportionate, confidential, documented, and subject to authorized review. AML controls cannot edit financial records or expose investigation data to unauthorized parties.
