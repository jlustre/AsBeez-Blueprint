# Payout Policy

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/009-payout-policy.md

---

## Purpose

Payout policy defines payoutable sources, withdrawal eligibility, destination, limits, fees, reserves, holds, tax, compliance, approval, provider execution, failure, return, and reversal.

## Rules

Settlement determines what is owed; Wallets determine what is available; Payouts execute approved transfers. RP, ABC, AHC, unclassified rewards, estimated earnings, or registration/referral activity are not payoutable without approved monetary classification and funding.

## Controls

Identity/KYC/KYB, sanctions, source of funds, tax, destination ownership, country, currency, fraud, reserve, limit, dual approval, idempotency, reconciliation, and appeal controls are mandatory.
