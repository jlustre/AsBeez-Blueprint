## Purpose

Compliance and Governance defines the authority, policies, controls, approvals, evidence, retention, and country requirements that govern AsBeez financial activity.

## AsBeez Boundary

Policy approval authorizes behavior and control; it does not create revenue, payable, wallet value, reward liability, tax, payout, or ledger entries. Financial systems must apply effective-dated policies to marketplace, payment, reward, vendor, partner, tax, treasury, reserve, and reporting workflows.

## Principles

- policy owner, authority, scope, effective dates, version, exceptions, and evidence are explicit;
- legal, tax, accounting, privacy, AML/KYC, sanctions, consumer, and country requirements are release gates;
- separation of duties protects initiation, approval, posting, settlement, payout, reconciliation, and reporting;
- controls are proportionate, explainable, reviewable, and appealable; and
- policy changes preserve historical decisions and source versions.
