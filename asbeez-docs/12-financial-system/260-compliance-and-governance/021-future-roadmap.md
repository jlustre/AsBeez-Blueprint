# Future Roadmap

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/021-future-roadmap.md

---

## Near Term

- approve governance bodies, policy owners, countries, entities, authorities, controls, retention, and approval matrices;
- implement accounting/revenue/expense/asset, reserve/refund/payout/tax, AML/KYC/sanctions/source-of-funds policies;
- implement separation of duties, country gates, policy versions, audit evidence, and legal holds; and
- connect fraud/risk, reconciliation, close, reporting, treasury, rewards, vendors, partners, and payouts.

## Medium Term

- automate policy impact analysis, control testing, evidence collection, retention, and audit response;
- strengthen country compliance and regulatory reporting;
- improve model/rule governance, drift, fairness, and exception oversight; and
- add explainable AI assistance with human approval.

## Long Term

- support regional compliance services and multi-entity governance;
- provide governed auditor, regulator, provider, and partner integrations;
- strengthen continuous controls and policy simulation; and
- preserve portable policy, approval, and evidence history across jurisdictions.

## Roadmap Gate

No financial feature may launch or change without policy owner, authority, effective version, country/entity scope, evidence, approval, controls, retention, audit, and exception treatment.
