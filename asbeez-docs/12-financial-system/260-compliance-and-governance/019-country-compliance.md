# Country Compliance

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/019-country-compliance.md

---

## Purpose

Country compliance records the legal, tax, accounting, consumer, payment, AML/KYC, sanctions, privacy, retention, payout, reward, reserve, and reporting requirements for each AsBeez country/entity scope.

## Country Launch Gate

Before activation, approve legal entity, registrations, tax types, payment providers, payout corridors, currencies, product/service restrictions, reward classification, reserve/funding, KYC/AML, sanctions, data residency/retention, terms, disclosures, and operational ownership.

## Rules

Country settings are versioned and effective-dated. Local statutory detail is retained alongside global mappings. A country cannot be enabled merely because a technical provider supports it.
