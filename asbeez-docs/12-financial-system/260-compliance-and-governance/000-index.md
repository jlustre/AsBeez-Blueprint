## Design Authority

Compliance and Governance owns financial policy authority, approvals, control design, evidence, retention, country gates, and audit readiness. Domain teams implement approved policies; Legal, Tax, Finance, Risk, Treasury, Operations, and Internal Audit retain their respective decision rights.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Establish governance bodies, owners, authorities, country/entity scope, approval matrices, separation of duties, and policy versioning.
2. Approve accounting, revenue, expense, asset, reserve, refund, payout, tax, AML/KYC, sanctions, and source-of-funds policies.
3. Implement retention, audit, control testing, country compliance, evidence, legal hold, and exception workflows.
4. Connect all financial domains and add governed automation/AI only after control certification.
