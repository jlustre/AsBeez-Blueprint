# Source Of Funds

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/014-source-of-funds.md

---

## Purpose

Source-of-funds controls determine whether the origin of money used for payment, wallet credit, transfer, reserve, payout, or investment is understood and permissible.

## Required Evidence

Originating account/provider, payer/beneficiary, transaction history, order/service, business purpose, country/entity, ownership, amount/currency, supporting documents, review, risk decision, and retention.

## Rules

Source-of-funds review is risk-based and does not itself prove fraud or tax treatment. Missing, inconsistent, restricted, or high-risk evidence blocks or holds the operation and creates a confidential case. RP, ABC, and AHC are not source of money merely because they appear in a program record.
