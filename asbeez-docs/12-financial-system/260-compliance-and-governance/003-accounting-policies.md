# Accounting Policies

> **Document:** 12-financial-system/260-compliance-and-governance/003-accounting-policies.md

---

## Purpose

Accounting policies define recognition, measurement, classification, presentation, correction, consolidation, currency, period, and evidence rules used by AsBeez financial domains.

## Required Policy Scope

Revenue, expenses, assets, liabilities, rewards, commissions, reserves, tax, FX, wallets, vendor/partner settlement, refunds, chargebacks, payouts, equity, periods, and reporting.

## Rules

Every policy has owner, authority, version, effective date, jurisdiction/entity scope, examples, account mappings, exceptions, implementation impact, approval, and review date. Policies cannot retroactively change posted records without approved restatement treatment.
