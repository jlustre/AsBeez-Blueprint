# Distribution Rules

> **Document:** 11-beehive-matrix/040-distribution-engine/003-distribution-rules.md

---

# Overview

The **Distribution Rules** define the complete set of business policies governing how **AsBeez Hive Credits (AHC)** are distributed throughout the Beehive Matrix whenever a new **AsBeez Business Cell (ABC)** is created.

These rules ensure that every distribution is:

- fair
- deterministic
- transparent
- replayable
- auditable
- immutable

The Distribution Rules form the financial governance layer of the Distribution Engine and guarantee that identical genealogy structures always produce identical financial outcomes.

---

# Purpose

The Distribution Rules exist to:

- standardize reward allocation
- define recipient eligibility
- prevent inconsistent calculations
- preserve financial integrity
- enforce country isolation
- support replay
- simplify auditing
- ensure long-term scalability

---

# Business Philosophy

The Beehive Matrix rewards **participation, contribution, and sustainable growth** rather than randomness or manual intervention.

Every Business Cell contributes value to its qualified ancestors according to published business rules that remain consistent for every member.

No individual, administrator, or automated process may alter these rules retroactively.

---

# Guiding Principles

## Deterministic

The same genealogy always produces the same distribution.

---

## Immutable

Completed distributions are permanent.

Corrections are made through compensating transactions.

---

## Transparent

Every allocation is explainable.

---

## Replayable

Historical distributions can always be reconstructed.

---

## Event Driven

Distributions occur only after valid business events.

---

## Country Isolated

Rewards never cross country boundaries.

---

## Auditable

Every allocation leaves a permanent financial record.

---

# Rule 1 — Distribution Trigger

Distribution begins only after:

- Reward Point threshold reached
- Business Cell successfully created
- Matrix placement completed
- Genealogy finalized
- Validation passed

No manual distribution is permitted.

---

# Rule 2 — Distribution Source

Each distribution has exactly one source.

```text
Business Cell

↓

One Distribution Event
```

The source Business Cell remains permanently associated with every resulting allocation.

---

# Rule 3 — Distribution Recipients

Recipients are determined exclusively from the immutable genealogy.

Eligible recipients are ancestor Business Cells.

Descendants never receive rewards generated by their ancestors.

---

# Rule 4 — Maximum Distribution Levels

The default maximum reward depth is:

```text
12 Levels
```

Country-specific configurations may define different limits.

Historical distributions preserve the configuration active at the time of processing.

---

# Rule 5 — Default Level Unlock

Every newly qualified member initially unlocks:

```text
Levels 1–9
```

Additional levels require referral qualification.

---

# Rule 6 — Referral Unlock Rules

Default unlocking:

| Qualified Referrals | Reward Levels |
|--------------------:|--------------:|
| 0 | 9 |
| 3 | 10 |
| 6 | 11 |
| 9 | 12 |

Referral counts are cumulative.

---

# Rule 7 — Qualified Recipient

A recipient Business Cell must satisfy all of the following:

- active
- not suspended
- valid ownership
- correct country
- genealogy integrity
- unlocked earning level
- reward eligibility

Failure of any validation causes the recipient to be skipped according to system policy.

---

# Rule 8 — Country Isolation

A Business Cell distributes rewards only within its own country matrix.

Example:

```text
USA

↓

USA Ancestors

✓
```

```text
USA

↓

Canada Ancestor

✗
```

No cross-country distributions are permitted.

---

# Rule 9 — One Allocation Per Recipient

Each qualified ancestor Business Cell may receive only one allocation from a single Business Cell generation event.

Duplicate allocations are prohibited.

---

# Rule 10 — Allocation Amount

The default allocation is:

```text
10 AHC
```

per qualified level.

Country-specific configurations may override this amount.

Historical transactions retain their original values.

---

# Rule 11 — Distribution Order

Recipients are processed sequentially.

```text
Level 1

↓

Level 2

↓

...

↓

Level 12
```

Processing order guarantees deterministic replay.

---

# Rule 12 — Skip Policy

Recipients may be skipped because of:

- inactive Business Cell
- suspended account
- locked earning level
- invalid genealogy
- country mismatch
- ownership validation failure

Skip events are permanently recorded.

---

# Rule 13 — Reserved Distribution

If no qualified recipient exists for a level, the system follows the configured reserve policy.

Possible policies include:

- company reserve
- promotional reserve
- charity reserve
- future distribution pool

The selected policy is country configurable.

---

# Rule 14 — Immutable Ledger

Every allocation creates exactly one immutable ledger record.

Ledger entries cannot be edited or deleted.

---

# Rule 15 — Wallet Synchronization

Wallet balances are updated only after successful ledger creation.

```text
Ledger

↓

Wallet Event

↓

Wallet Update
```

Wallets are derived from ledger transactions.

---

# Rule 16 — Financial Conservation

Every generated Hive Credit must be accounted for.

```text
Generated AHC

=

Allocated AHC

+

Reserved AHC

+

Company AHC
```

No credits may disappear.

No credits may be created outside approved business rules.

---

# Rule 17 — Duplicate Prevention

A distribution event may only execute once.

Protection mechanisms include:

- idempotency keys
- event versioning
- unique ledger constraints
- replay validation

---

# Rule 18 — Replay

Replay reconstructs historical distributions from immutable events.

Replay never creates duplicate rewards.

Replay never modifies historical records.

---

# Rule 19 — Historical Integrity

Historical distributions always preserve:

- allocation amount
- country configuration
- earning rules
- genealogy state
- qualification status
- event ordering

Future configuration changes never alter completed transactions.

---

# Rule 20 — Auditability

Every allocation must record:

- source Business Cell
- recipient Business Cell
- member
- genealogy level
- country
- amount
- timestamp
- event identifier
- processing version

---

# Rule 21 — Configuration Versioning

Each distribution references the reward configuration version active during execution.

This enables accurate replay even after future business rule changes.

---

# Rule 22 — Event Publication

Every successful distribution publishes domain events.

Representative events:

- DistributionStarted
- RecipientQualified
- RecipientSkipped
- AHCAllocated
- LedgerCreated
- WalletUpdated
- DistributionCompleted

---

# Rule 23 — Error Recovery

If processing fails:

```text
Rollback

↓

Retry

↓

Dead Letter Queue

↓

Administrator Review
```

Partial distributions are not permitted.

---

# Rule 24 — AI Observability

Distribution events become training data for:

- fraud detection
- anomaly detection
- trend forecasting
- operational intelligence
- executive analytics

AI never modifies completed allocations.

---

# Rule 25 — Compliance

The Distribution Engine enforces:

- role-based authorization
- immutable audit history
- country isolation
- financial traceability
- regulatory compliance
- event retention policies

# Distribution Decision Matrix

| Condition | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| Business Cell created | Continue |
| Placement successful | Continue |
| Country validated | Continue |
| Recipient qualified | Allocate AHC |
| Recipient not qualified | Skip recipient |
| Duplicate allocation detected | Reject |
| Ledger persistence failed | Rollback |
| Wallet update delayed | Retry asynchronously |

# Summary

The Distribution Rules establish the immutable governance framework for all AsBeez Hive Credit allocations within the Beehive Matrix. They define when distributions occur, who qualifies to receive rewards, how allocations are calculated, how financial integrity is preserved, and how every transaction is recorded, replayed, and audited. By combining deterministic processing, immutable ledgers, Event Sourcing, CQRS, and strict country isolation, these rules ensure that every Hive Credit generated by a Business Cell is distributed consistently, transparently, and with complete accountability across the entire AsBeez ecosystem.
