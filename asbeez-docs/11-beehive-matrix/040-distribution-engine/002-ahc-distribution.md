# Overview

The **Distribution Engine** is the component of the AsBeez Beehive Matrix responsible for converting newly created **AsBeez Business Cells (ABCs)** into financial rewards by distributing **AsBeez Hive Credits (AHC)** to qualified ancestor Business Cells.

It represents the financial execution layer of the Beehive Matrix.

Whenever a Business Cell is successfully generated and permanently placed into the genealogy, the Distribution Engine performs a deterministic calculation to determine:

- who receives rewards
- how much each recipient receives
- whether recipients qualify
- which ledger entries are created
- which wallet balances are updated

Every distribution is:

- deterministic
- immutable
- replayable
- auditable
- traceable
- event-driven

The Distribution Engine guarantees that every Hive Credit generated by the platform is completely accounted for throughout its lifecycle.

# Purpose

The Distribution Engine exists to:

- distribute Hive Credits
- identify qualified recipients
- validate earning eligibility
- create immutable financial ledgers
- update wallets
- publish financial events
- support reporting
- provide complete auditability

---

# Business Philosophy

Every Business Cell represents newly created value within the AsBeez ecosystem.

Instead of concentrating that value in a single participant, the Beehive Matrix distributes it across multiple qualified ancestors according to transparent business rules.

The Distribution Engine guarantees that:

- identical inputs always produce identical outputs
- every reward is fully traceable
- every allocation is permanently recorded
- no reward is duplicated
- no reward disappears

This deterministic approach creates trust throughout the ecosystem.

# Core Concepts

## Distribution

A distribution is the allocation of Hive Credits from one Business Cell to one or more qualified ancestor Business Cells.

---

## Distribution Source

The Business Cell that generated the distribution.

Example:

```text
ABC-2500

↓

Creates Distribution
```

---

## Distribution Recipient

A qualified ancestor Business Cell that receives Hive Credits.

Recipients are identified from the immutable genealogy.

---

## Distribution Level

The genealogy distance between the source Business Cell and the receiving ancestor.

Example:

```text
Source

↓

Parent

Level 1

↓

Grandparent

Level 2
```

---

## Qualified Recipient

A recipient qualifies only if all business rules are satisfied.

Qualification may include:

- active Business Cell
- unlocked earning level
- valid ownership
- country match
- genealogy integrity

---

## Distribution Event

Each recipient allocation becomes an independent immutable distribution event.

---

# Guiding Principles

## Deterministic

The same genealogy always produces identical distributions.

---

## Immutable

Completed distributions can never be modified.

Corrections are made through compensating events.

---

## Replayable

Entire financial history can be reconstructed by replaying events.

---

## Transparent

Every distribution can be traced back to its originating Business Cell.

---

## Event Driven

Distribution begins only after receiving appropriate domain events.

---

## Country Isolated

Distributions never cross country boundaries.

---

## Eventually Consistent

Wallet balances update asynchronously while preserving financial consistency.

# Summary

The Distribution Engine is the financial execution service of the AsBeez Beehive Matrix. It transforms newly generated Business Cells into deterministic Hive Credit distributions by identifying qualified ancestors, validating eligibility, calculating allocations, creating immutable ledger records, updating wallet balances, and publishing domain events. Built upon Event Sourcing, CQRS, replayability, and country isolation, the Distribution Engine ensures every Hive Credit generated within the ecosystem is allocated fairly, transparently, and with complete financial accountability, forming the foundation of trust for the entire AsBeez reward infrastructure.
