# Overview

The **Distribution Engine** is responsible for calculating, validating, allocating, recording, and distributing **AsBeez Hive Credits (AHC)** generated whenever a new **AsBeez Business Cell (ABC)** is created.

It serves as the financial execution layer of the Beehive Matrix by ensuring that every qualified ancestor receives the correct reward according to the immutable genealogy and the configured business rules.

The Distribution Engine operates independently from:

- Matrix placement
- Reward Point (RP) accumulation
- Business Cell generation
- Wallet management

while integrating seamlessly with each of them through an event-driven architecture.

Every distribution is:

- deterministic
- replayable
- auditable
- immutable
- traceable
- country-aware

# Business Philosophy

The creation of every Business Cell represents the creation of new value within the ecosystem.

Rather than concentrating rewards in a single participant, the Beehive Matrix distributes that value across multiple qualified ancestors according to transparent and deterministic business rules.

The Distribution Engine ensures that this value distribution remains:

- fair
- predictable
- automated
- verifiable
- consistent
- globally scalable

# Core Business Concepts

## Distribution

The process of allocating Hive Credits generated by one Business Cell to qualified ancestor Business Cells.

---

## Distribution Source

The Business Cell that generates the reward.

Example:

```text
ABC-9200

↓

Creates

↓

Distribution
```

---

## Distribution Recipient

A qualified ancestor Business Cell that receives Hive Credits.

---

## Qualified Ancestor

A Business Cell eligible to receive rewards based on:

- genealogy
- earning level
- qualification rules
- Business Cell status

---

## Distribution Event

A single allocation from one source Business Cell to one qualified ancestor.

---

# Guiding Principles

## Deterministic

The same genealogy always produces identical distributions.

---

## Immutable

Completed distributions are never edited.

---

## Replayable

Every distribution can be reconstructed from event history.

---

## Event Driven

Distribution begins after successful Business Cell placement.

---

## Country Isolated

Distributions never cross country matrices.

---

## Auditable

Every allocation is permanently recorded.

---

## Eventually Consistent

Wallet balances are updated asynchronously using domain events.

# Best Practices

- Keep distribution logic deterministic.
- Never modify completed distributions.
- Generate immutable financial ledgers.
- Publish domain events after successful allocations.
- Separate calculation from wallet posting.
- Maintain replay compatibility.
- Validate recipient eligibility before allocation.
- Preserve country isolation.
- Optimize projections independently from write models.
- Ensure every Hive Credit is fully traceable from source to recipient.

# Summary

The Distribution Engine is the financial execution layer of the AsBeez Beehive Matrix. It transforms newly created Business Cells into deterministic, immutable Hive Credit allocations by traversing qualified genealogy relationships, validating earning rules, calculating distributions, generating financial ledgers, and updating recipient wallets through an event-driven architecture. Built upon CQRS, Event Sourcing, replayability, and country isolation, the Distribution Engine ensures every Hive Credit generated within the ecosystem is distributed fairly, transparently, and with complete auditability while providing a scalable foundation for future reward models and AI-powered financial intelligence.
