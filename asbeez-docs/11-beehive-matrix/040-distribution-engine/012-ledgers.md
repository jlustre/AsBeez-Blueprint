# Overview

The **Ledger** is the authoritative financial record of every **AsBeez Hive Credit (AHC)** transaction generated by the Distribution Engine.

It serves as the **single source of truth** for all financial activities related to reward generation, distribution, adjustments, settlements, reconciliations, and financial reporting.

Unlike wallet balances, which represent a member's current usable balance, the Ledger represents the complete immutable financial history of the platform.

Every financial movement is permanently recorded.

Nothing is ever deleted.

Nothing is overwritten.

Nothing disappears.

---

# Purpose

The Ledger exists to:

- record every financial transaction
- preserve financial integrity
- support immutable accounting
- provide complete auditability
- enable deterministic replay
- facilitate reconciliation
- support financial reporting
- provide regulatory evidence

---

# Business Philosophy

Money should always leave footprints.

Every Hive Credit generated by the platform must leave an immutable record that can be traced from its origin until its final settlement.

If a financial transaction cannot be explained through the Ledger, it should not exist.

# Append-Only Model

Ledger entries are never:

- edited
- deleted
- overwritten

Corrections create new entries.

Example:

```text
Original

100 AHC
```

Correction:

```text
Adjustment

+20 AHC
```

Historical entries remain unchanged.

# Summary

The Ledger is the financial backbone of the AsBeez Distribution Engine, serving as the immutable, append-only, and authoritative record of every Hive Credit transaction throughout the ecosystem. By recording all distributions, adjustments, settlements, liabilities, Company Holding allocations, and financial events, the Ledger guarantees complete transparency, deterministic replay, enterprise-grade auditability, and regulatory readiness. As the single source of truth from which wallets, reports, analytics, and reconciliations are derived, it provides the scalable accounting foundation required for a global, country-aware, event-driven reward platform.
