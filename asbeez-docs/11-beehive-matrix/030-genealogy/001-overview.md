# Overview

The **Genealogy Engine** is the permanent relationship management system of the AsBeez Beehive Matrix. It defines, records, and preserves the complete hierarchical relationships among every **AsBeez Business Cell (ABC)** throughout its lifetime.

Where the **Matrix Engine** determines *where* a Business Cell is placed, the **Genealogy Engine** permanently records *how every Business Cell is connected*.

These relationships become the foundation for:

- reward distribution
- hierarchy traversal
- matrix visualization
- reporting
- ownership tracking
- AI analytics
- fraud detection
- compliance auditing
- historical reconstruction

Every Business Cell belongs to exactly one genealogy within a country-specific Beehive Matrix.

Once established, genealogy relationships are immutable.

# Relationship Hierarchy

Every Business Cell participates in a hierarchy.

Example:

```text
Root

├── ABC-001
│   ├── ABC-010
│   │   ├── ABC-100
│   │   └── ABC-101
│   └── ABC-011
│
└── ABC-002
```

This hierarchy remains permanent.

---

# Genealogy vs Matrix

Although closely related, they are different concepts.

| Matrix Engine | Genealogy Engine |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Determines placement | Preserves relationships |
| Finds available nodes | Stores lineage |
| Executes placement algorithms | Traverses hierarchies |
| Optimizes capacity | Enables reporting |
| Handles spillover | Maintains ancestry |

Together they form the structural foundation of the Beehive Matrix.

# Permanent Lineage

Once a Business Cell receives a parent:

```text
Parent

↓

Business Cell

↓

Relationship Locked
```

The relationship never changes.

---

# Immutability

The following never change:

- parent
- ancestry
- descendants
- generation
- genealogy position

Only projections and reports may be rebuilt.

---

# Country Isolation

Every genealogy belongs to one country.

Example:

```text
USA Matrix

↓

USA Genealogy
```

and

```text
Canada Matrix

↓

Canada Genealogy
```

Relationships never cross country boundaries.

---

# Unlimited Growth

The genealogy supports unlimited expansion.

Example:

```text
Generation 1

↓

Generation 2

↓

Generation 3

↓

...

↓

Generation N
```

There is no architectural limitation on genealogy depth.

---

# Deterministic Traversal

Given the same genealogy:

Every traversal always returns identical results.

Example:

```text
ABC-501

↓

Ancestors

↓

Always Same Result
```

This guarantees reproducible reporting.

# Summary

The Genealogy Engine is the permanent relationship management foundation of the AsBeez Beehive Matrix. It preserves the immutable parent, child, ancestor, descendant, sibling, and lineage relationships of every Business Cell while enabling reward distribution, reporting, AI analytics, auditing, and historical reconstruction. Designed around event-driven architecture, deterministic traversal, and country-specific isolation, the Genealogy Engine ensures that every Business Cell remains permanently connected within a scalable, transparent, and fully auditable digital family tree that serves as one of the core structural pillars of the AsBeez ecosystem.
