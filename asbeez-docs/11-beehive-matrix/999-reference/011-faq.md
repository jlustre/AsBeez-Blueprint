# General Questions

---

## 1. What is the AsBeez Beehive Matrix?

The Beehive Matrix is the proprietary reward distribution engine that powers the AsBeez ecosystem.

It transforms accumulated **Reward Points (RP)** into **AsBeez Business Cells (ABC)** and distributes **AsBeez Hive Credits (AHC)** to qualified Business Cells within a country-specific 3×12 genealogy structure.

It is **not** a traditional MLM matrix, binary plan, or forced recruiting compensation system. It is a marketplace-driven reward infrastructure that is activated through platform activity.

---

## 2. What is a Business Cell (ABC)?

A Business Cell represents a permanent earning unit within the Beehive Matrix.

Each Business Cell:

- belongs to one member
- belongs to one country
- occupies one permanent matrix position
- earns independently
- has its own lifecycle
- has its own reward history
- is immutable after creation

A member may own an unlimited number of Business Cells.

---

## 3. What are Reward Points (RP)?

Reward Points are the accumulation unit generated from qualifying activities throughout the AsBeez ecosystem.

Examples include:

- marketplace purchases
- subscriptions
- platform-defined promotional campaigns
- future qualifying activities approved by platform governance

Reward Points are not cash and cannot be withdrawn directly.

---

## 4. What are AsBeez Hive Credits (AHC)?

AsBeez Hive Credits are the internal reward credits distributed by the Beehive Matrix after Business Cell creation.

Default conversion:

```text
10 AHC = $1.00 USD equivalent
```

The conversion value is configurable by platform policy and may differ by country if officially approved.

---

## 5. Are Reward Points transferable?

No.

Reward Points belong exclusively to the earning member.

The only exception is a legally recognized beneficiary process upon the member's death, subject to platform policy and local laws.

---

## 6. Do Reward Points expire?

No.

Reward Points remain in the member's account until they are consumed to create one or more Business Cells.

---

## 7. Can Reward Points be manually edited?

Not directly.

Corrections require an auditable adjustment process that creates new ledger entries and supporting events rather than modifying historical records.

---

# Business Cell Questions

---

## 8. How is a Business Cell created?

When accumulated Reward Points reach the country-specific threshold, the Reward Engine automatically creates one or more Business Cells.

Example:

```text
Threshold = 120 RP

Balance = 360 RP

Result

3 Business Cells Created
```

---

## 9. Can multiple Business Cells be created at once?

Yes.

The system continuously evaluates the available Reward Point balance.

If enough points exist, multiple Business Cells are generated in a single transaction.

---

## 10. Can a Business Cell be deleted?

No.

Business Cells are permanent.

Historical integrity must always be preserved.

---

## 11. Can a Business Cell change owners?

No.

Ownership is permanent.

Transfers are not supported.

---

## 12. Can a Business Cell move to another country?

No.

A Business Cell permanently belongs to the country in which it was created.

Future Business Cells may belong to a new country after an approved residency transfer.

---

## 13. Can a Business Cell earn forever?

No.

A Business Cell stops earning once it reaches the configured completion criteria for its matrix lifecycle.

Historical records remain permanently available.

---

# Country Questions

---

## 14. Why are matrices separated by country?

Country isolation supports:

- regulatory compliance
- taxation
- currency management
- localized configurations
- performance
- scalability
- governance

Each country maintains an independent Beehive Matrix.

---

## 15. Can members refer people in other countries?

Yes.

Cross-country referrals are allowed.

Referral relationships remain intact regardless of country.

Reward distribution always occurs within each member's active country matrix.

---

## 16. Can members change countries?

Yes.

A verified residency transfer may be approved by administrators.

Effects:

- future Business Cells use the new country
- historical Business Cells remain in their original country
- historical rewards remain unchanged

---

## 17. Can countries have different Reward Point thresholds?

Yes.

Example:

| Country | Threshold |
|----------|-----------|
| United States | 120 RP |
| Canada | 120 RP |
| Philippines | 60 RP |
| Singapore | 120 RP |

Thresholds are configurable and must follow platform governance rules.

---

# Matrix Questions

---

## 18. What is a 3×12 matrix?

Each Business Cell supports:

- 3 positions per level
- 12 levels deep

Maximum theoretical positions:

```text
797,160
```

This structure defines reward eligibility and distribution boundaries.

---

## 19. Does every Business Cell have its own matrix?

Yes.

Every Business Cell is an independent earning entity with its own genealogy and earning lifecycle.

---

## 20. Can matrix positions change?

No.

Matrix placement is permanent after assignment.

---

## 21. What happens if an ancestor position does not exist?

The reward allocation follows the official platform policy for unoccupied positions.

Depending on configuration, the value may:

- remain with the company reserve account
- fund promotional pools
- support charitable initiatives
- follow future governance rules

The allocation decision is configuration-driven and fully auditable.

---

# Referral Questions

---

## 22. How many earning levels are unlocked initially?

By default:

```text
9 Levels
```

---

## 23. How are additional levels unlocked?

Qualified referrals increase earning depth.

Default progression:

| Qualified Referrals | Levels |
|---------------------|--------|
| 0–2 | 9 |
| 3–5 | 10 |
| 6–8 | 11 |
| 9+ | 12 |

Platform governance may revise these thresholds.

---

## 24. What is a qualified referral?

A referral becomes qualified after generating at least one Business Cell.

Registration alone does not qualify the referral.

---

# Wallet Questions

---

## 25. Can members withdraw Reward Points?

No.

Reward Points cannot be withdrawn.

Only eligible wallet balances created through reward distributions may be withdrawn according to platform policy.

---

## 26. Are wallet transactions editable?

No.

Wallet history is supported by immutable ledger entries.

Corrections are handled using compensating transactions.

# Financial Questions

---

## 27. Why is the Ledger append-only?

An append-only ledger guarantees:

- complete audit history
- replay capability
- financial integrity
- regulatory compliance
- fraud resistance

Historical financial records are never overwritten.

---

## 28. What happens if an error is discovered?

The platform creates compensating transactions.

Original entries remain intact.

# Developer Questions

---

## 41. Where should business rules be implemented?

Business rules belong in the Domain Layer.

They should never be duplicated across controllers, APIs, user interfaces, or reports.

---

## 42. Why should events be published after committing a transaction?

Publishing before a successful commit may produce inconsistent system behavior.

The correct order is:

```text
Validate

↓

Execute Business Logic

↓

Persist Aggregate

↓

Commit Transaction

↓

Publish Event
```

---

## 43. Why are read models considered disposable?

Read models can always be rebuilt from the immutable Event Store.

Only transactional data and events represent the authoritative source of truth.

---

# Operations Questions

---

## 44. How is disaster recovery performed?

Recovery process:

1. Restore infrastructure.
2. Restore the Event Store.
3. Replay events.
4. Rebuild projections.
5. Validate financial consistency.
6. Resume platform operations.

---

## 45. Why are backups regularly verified?

A backup is only valuable if it can be successfully restored.

Routine verification confirms backup integrity and recovery readiness.
