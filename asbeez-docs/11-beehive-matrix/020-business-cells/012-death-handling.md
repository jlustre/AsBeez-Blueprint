> **Document:** 11-beehive-matrix/020-business-cells/012-death-handling.md

# Overview

The **Business Cell Death Handling Policy** defines how the AsBeez platform manages the Business Cells, rewards, ownership rights, account status, and operational continuity when a member passes away.

Since an **AsBeez Business Cell (ABC)** is a **permanent digital business asset**, it does not cease to exist when its owner dies. Instead, the platform follows a controlled succession process that preserves historical integrity while ensuring that future rights and benefits are transferred according to the member's beneficiary designation or applicable law.

Death handling is:

- legally compliant
- configuration-driven
- country-aware
- deterministic
- event-driven
- fully auditable

---

# Purpose

This policy exists to:

- preserve Business Cell permanence
- protect surviving beneficiaries
- comply with inheritance laws
- prevent fraudulent claims
- ensure uninterrupted historical records
- standardize succession workflows
- safeguard financial integrity
- maintain platform stability

# Business Philosophy

A Business Cell is earned through legitimate marketplace participation.

Death does not erase that achievement.

Therefore:

> **Death changes operational ownership—it never erases historical ownership or the existence of the Business Cell.**

The Business Cell remains permanently embedded within the Beehive Matrix.

# Future Reward Handling

Platform configuration determines whether future rewards are:

- temporarily held in escrow
- accumulated until estate completion
- distributed immediately after verification
- paid to an approved trust
- paid to successor owner

## Option B

Future rewards flow into successor wallet.

---

Future rewards remain in escrow until legal authorization.

# Pending Transactions

Transactions already completed before death remain valid.

Examples include:

# Summary

The Business Cell Death Handling Policy establishes a comprehensive, legally compliant framework for managing AsBeez Business Cells following the death of a member. Rather than affecting the permanent existence of the Business Cell, the policy preserves immutable ownership history, genealogy, placement, financial records, and audit trails while providing a structured succession process for transferring future operational rights to verified beneficiaries or legally authorized successors. Through deterministic workflows, robust identity verification, jurisdiction-aware compliance, and append-only event recording, the platform ensures that every Business Cell continues to serve as a permanent digital business asset while protecting both member families and the long-term integrity of the AsBeez ecosystem.
