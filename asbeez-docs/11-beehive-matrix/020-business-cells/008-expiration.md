# Business Cell Expiration Policy

> **Document:** 11-beehive-matrix/020-business-cells/008-expiration.md

# Overview

The **Business Cell Expiration Policy** defines the rules governing the lifespan of an **AsBeez Business Cell (ABC)**.

One of the fundamental principles of the AsBeez ecosystem is that a Business Cell represents a **permanent business asset** earned through legitimate marketplace activity.

Accordingly, **Business Cells do not expire**.

Once created, a Business Cell remains part of the Beehive Matrix forever, preserving its genealogy, historical records, financial integrity, and contribution to the ecosystem.

This policy distinguishes AsBeez from many traditional compensation systems where positions may expire because of inactivity, subscription lapses, or annual renewal requirements.

---

# Purpose

This policy exists to:

- define Business Cell longevity
- preserve financial integrity
- establish immutable ownership
- protect member investments
- simplify compensation
- eliminate arbitrary expiration
- improve long-term confidence
- maintain historical accuracy

# Difference from Membership

Membership and Business Cells are separate concepts.

| Membership | Business Cell |
|------------|---------------|
| May become inactive | Never expires |
| Can be suspended | Permanent |
| May require compliance | Historical asset |
| Represents a person | Represents an earning asset |

Membership status does not erase Business Cells.

Business Cells remain historical assets.

If a member dies:

Business Cells remain permanently recorded.

# Summary

The Business Cell Expiration Policy establishes that every AsBeez Business Cell is a permanent digital business asset that never expires, regardless of member inactivity, country changes, administrative status, or the passage of time. Once earned through legitimate Reward Point accumulation, a Business Cell retains its immutable ownership, genealogy, financial history, and audit records indefinitely. By prohibiting deletion and expiration while supporting archival for operational efficiency, the policy safeguards long-term member confidence, regulatory compliance, financial integrity, and the enduring value of every Business Cell within the AsBeez Beehive Matrix.
