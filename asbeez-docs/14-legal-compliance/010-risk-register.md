## Purpose

The legal-compliance risk register records legal, regulatory, contractual, consumer, privacy, tax, payments, rewards, direct-selling, country, security, AI, and operational risks with ownership and treatment.

## Required Fields

Risk ID, description, cause, affected product/process/country/entity, legal/regulatory basis, likelihood, impact, materiality, controls, owner, action, due date, status, residual risk, acceptance authority, evidence, review date, trigger, and incident/decision references.

## Priority Risks

Unapproved country launch; rewards classified as money/investment or income promise; recruitment-based compensation; payment/money-transmission exposure; tax/nexus failure; vendor/member consumer harm; privacy/security breach; sanctions/AML/KYC failure; provider concentration; liquidity/settlement failure; AI discrimination/leakage; and inaccurate financial disclosure.

## Treatment

Avoid, reduce, transfer, monitor, hold, escalate, or accept only with documented authority. A risk acceptance cannot override law, accounting truth, consumer rights, privacy, security, or mandatory controls. Reassess after incidents, product/provider/policy/country changes.
