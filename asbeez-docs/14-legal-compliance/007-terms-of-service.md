## Purpose

The Terms of Service define the contractual relationship among AsBeez, customers, members, vendors, partners, and users of the platform.

## Required Sections

Eligibility and account; marketplace role; products/services; orders and payment; wallet and reward units; fees/taxes; refunds/returns/cancellations; disputes; payouts/withdrawals; prohibited conduct; fraud/compliance holds; privacy; intellectual property; acceptable use; availability; changes; suspension/termination; complaints; governing law; jurisdiction; limitation/indemnity; and contact/notice.

## Financial Clarity

Terms must not describe RP, ABC, or AHC as money, currency, equity, investment, security, ownership, or guaranteed return without jurisdiction-specific legal approval. They must distinguish platform points/benefits from monetary wallets, disclose restrictions/expiry/reversal, and avoid income or appreciation promises.

## Change Control

Material changes require legal/compliance/product/finance review, version/effective date, user notice or consent where required, language/localization, retained acceptance evidence, and compatibility with country-specific terms. Terms do not override mandatory consumer, tax, privacy, or payment law.
