## Purpose

Privacy governs collection, use, access, disclosure, transfer, retention, deletion, correction, and protection of personal, financial, identity, behavioral, vendor, partner, and AI data.

## Requirements

Document purpose and legal basis; data inventory/classification; minimization; transparency/notice; consent where required; subject rights; children/vulnerable-person controls; processors/subprocessors; country transfer/residency; encryption/tokenization; access; retention/legal hold; deletion/anonymization; breach response; and independent review.

## Financial and AI Data

Payment credentials, tax/identity evidence, wallet and payout data, fraud/risk signals, prompts, retrieval sources, model inputs/outputs, and audit records require role, purpose, country, and retention controls. AI output does not expand a user's access and cannot make final legal/compliance decisions.

## Operational Rules

Sensitive data is masked in logs, events, reports, support, test data, prompts, and exports. Providers receive only approved fields under contract and security review. Privacy incidents trigger containment, evidence preservation, impact assessment, notification analysis, remediation, and audit.
