# Global Country Rules

## Country Assignment

Each Member belongs to one and only one country.

A Member's assigned country determines:

- Currency
- Tax Rules
- Reward Configuration
- Withdrawal Rules
- Legal Compliance
- Payment Providers
- Promotions
- Reporting

---

## Cross-Country Participation

Members may interact across country boundaries.

Examples include:

- Referrals
- Marketplace Purchases
- Vendor Transactions
- Reward Qualification

Cross-country participation does not change the Member's assigned country.

---

## Business Rules

| Rule ID | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| BR-CTR-001 | Every Member belongs to exactly one country. |
| BR-CTR-002 | Members may refer Members from any supported country. |
| BR-CTR-003 | Cross-country referrals are eligible for qualification. |
| BR-CTR-004 | A Member's country cannot change automatically through referrals or purchases. |
| BR-CTR-005 | Country-specific configurations are determined by the Member's assigned country. |
## Country Migration

Members may request to change their assigned country when they have permanently relocated to another supported country.

Country migration is subject to administrative review and approval.

A Member's country assignment will not be changed until all required verification has been successfully completed.

---

## Residency Verification

To request a country migration, a Member must provide proof of legal residency in the destination country.

Acceptable documents may include:

- Government-issued ID
- Permanent Resident Card
- Residency Permit
- Driver's License
- Utility Bill
- Bank Statement
- Government Correspondence
- Other administrator-approved documents

The list of accepted documents is configurable.

---

## Country Migration Effects

Once approved, the Member will be reassigned to the new country.

Future activities will use the new country's:

- Currency
- Tax Rules
- Reward Configuration
- Withdrawal Rules
- Payment Providers
- Regulatory Compliance

Historical transactions remain associated with the country in which they originally occurred.

---

## Business Rules

| Rule ID | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| BR-CTR-006 | A Member may request to change their assigned country only through the official Country Migration process. |
| BR-CTR-007 | Proof of legal residency is required before a country migration may be approved. |
| BR-CTR-008 | Country migration requires administrative approval. |
| BR-CTR-009 | Historical transactions retain their original country assignment. |
| BR-CTR-010 | Future transactions follow the rules of the newly assigned country. |
| BR-CTR-011 | A Member may belong to only one country at any given time. |

## Legal and Compliance Caveat

Country assignment and migration are platform rules that require jurisdiction-specific review. They do not determine legal residence, tax residence, licensing, citizenship, consumer rights, sanctions status, or regulatory obligations by themselves. Compliance, tax, privacy, and legal controls may require additional evidence or restrictions.
