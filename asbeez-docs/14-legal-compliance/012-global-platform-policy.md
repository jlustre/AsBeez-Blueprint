## Overview

The Member Country Migration Policy defines how the AsBeez platform handles Members who permanently relocate from one supported country to another.

The policy preserves ownership of all existing assets while ensuring that future activities comply with the business, financial, and regulatory rules of the Member's newly assigned country.

The objective is to protect Members, maintain accounting integrity, and preserve the consistency of the Beehive Matrix.

---

## Guiding Principles

The policy follows these principles:

- Membership belongs to the individual, not to a country.
- Existing assets remain the property of the Member.
- Historical records are immutable.
- Future activities follow the rules of the Member's new country.
- Country migration does not modify historical financial or reward transactions.

## Asset Ownership

The following assets remain with the Member after an approved country migration.

| Asset | Migration Result |
|--------|------------------|
| Membership Account | Retained |
| Reward Points (RP) | Retained |
| Existing ABCs | Retained |
| Existing AHC | Retained |
| Wallet Balances | Retained |
| Referral Relationships | Retained |
| Qualified Referrals | Retained |

Ownership is never transferred or reset due solely to country migration.

---

## Existing ABCs

ABCs created before migration remain permanently associated with their original matrix.

The following information never changes:

- ABC Number
- Creation Date
- Country of Origin
- Matrix of Origin
- Historical Transactions

The Member remains the owner of the ABC regardless of future country changes.

---

## Future ABCs

After migration, any newly created ABC participates in the matrix of the Member's newly assigned country.

Example:

```text
ABC #1001 → USA Matrix
ABC #1002 → USA Matrix

(Member migrates to Canada)

ABC #1003 → Canada Matrix
ABC #1004 → Canada Matrix
```

Existing ABCs are never moved between matrices.

---

## Reward Points (RP)

Previously earned RP remains with the Member.

Future RP is earned according to the rules of the Member's assigned country.

RP balances are not reset during migration.

---

## AsBeez Hive Credits (AHC)

Previously earned AHC remains available to the Member.

Future AHC generation follows the reward configuration applicable to the Member's assigned country.

Historical AHC transactions remain unchanged.

---

## Wallet Balances

Wallet balances remain with the Member.

Existing balances retain their original currency.

Future earnings may be credited using the currency of the Member's newly assigned country.

The platform may maintain multiple currency balances for a single Member.

---

## Historical Records

Country migration does not modify:

- Historical Orders
- Reward Transactions
- Ledger Entries
- Matrix History
- Wallet Transactions
- Tax Records
- Audit Logs

Historical records always retain the country, currency, and business rules that were effective when the transaction occurred.

---

## Future Transactions

After migration, future transactions use the new country's:

- Currency
- Tax Configuration
- Reward Configuration
- Withdrawal Rules
- Payment Providers
- Regulatory Policies

Only future activities are affected.

---

## Reporting

Reports distinguish between:

- Country of Origin
- Current Member Country
- Historical Transactions
- Future Transactions

This preserves complete financial and regulatory traceability.

---

## Business Rules

| Rule ID | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| BR-CMP-001 | Every Member belongs to exactly one country at any given time. |
| BR-CMP-002 | Country migration requires proof of legal residency. |
| BR-CMP-003 | Country migration requires administrative approval. |
| BR-CMP-004 | Existing RP, ABC, AHC, Wallet balances, and referrals remain the property of the Member after migration. |
| BR-CMP-005 | Existing ABCs remain permanently associated with the matrix in which they were originally created. |
| BR-CMP-006 | New ABCs created after migration participate in the matrix of the Member's newly assigned country. |
| BR-CMP-007 | Historical transactions are never recalculated following country migration. |
| BR-CMP-008 | Future transactions follow the business rules of the Member's newly assigned country. |
| BR-CMP-009 | Wallet balances retain their original currency. Future earnings may use the currency of the Member's assigned country. |
| BR-CMP-010 | Every country migration request and decision must be permanently recorded for auditing and compliance purposes. |
