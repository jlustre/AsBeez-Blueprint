# Loyalty and Rewards Compliance

> **Status:** Initial control framework  
> **Owner:** Legal and Compliance  
> **Release gate:** No rewards or matrix feature is launch-ready until this document is completed for each launch jurisdiction.

## Scope

This document governs Reward Points (RP), AsBeez Business Cells (ABC), AsBeez Hive Credits (AHC), matrix participation, wallets, and related promotions or payouts.

## Non-Negotiable Controls

- Rewards must be tied to genuine, documented marketplace activity.
- No participant may be required to recruit another person to earn a reward.
- No document, interface, campaign, or representative may promise income, profit, appreciation, passive returns, or guaranteed withdrawals.
- RP, ABC, and AHC must not be described as money, currency, equity, ownership of AsBeez, an investment, or a transferable financial instrument unless Legal approves that classification in writing.
- Reward eligibility, balances, reversals, expiry, and payout conditions must be disclosed before participation.
- Refunds, chargebacks, fraud findings, and policy breaches must reverse or suspend affected rewards through auditable ledger entries.
- Country availability and reward rules must be configurable and blocked where required approvals do not exist.

## Required Evidence Before Launch

For each jurisdiction, Legal must approve the classification of RP, ABC, AHC, and wallet balances; consumer, advertising, direct-selling, gaming, tax, payments, and money-transmission analysis; participant terms and disclosures; the funding source and reserve policy; and all reward-related marketing copy.

## Open Questions

- Which jurisdictions are in the first release?
- Can AHC ever be redeemed for cash, or only for platform benefits?
- What exact marketplace activity funds each reward and what is the maximum liability?
- Which events reverse qualification or placement after a refund or chargeback?

## Authority and Caveat

This is a release-control framework, not a jurisdiction-specific legal opinion. Legal must approve classification, disclosures, funding, marketing, payout, tax, consumer, payments, and direct-selling treatment for each launch country. Reward policy does not create money, revenue, ownership, or ledger value by itself.
