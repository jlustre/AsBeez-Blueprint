# Income Disclosure

> **Status:** Required disclosure template  
> **Release gate:** Legal must approve this document before any earnings or opportunity claim is published.

## Minimum Disclosure

AsBeez does not guarantee income, profit, rewards, qualification, placement, AHC distribution, wallet conversion, or withdrawal. Results vary based on qualifying marketplace activity, applicable rules, refunds, fraud controls, country restrictions, and the availability of an approved payout program.

RP, ABC, and AHC are platform-defined program units. They are not presented as cash, currency, company ownership, securities, investments, or guaranteed financial returns unless a jurisdiction-specific legal analysis expressly permits that description.

Participation is voluntary. Recruitment is not required to make a qualifying purchase or receive ordinary customer benefits. No participant should purchase products, pay fees, or incur debt for the purpose of pursuing an earning outcome.

## Required Publication Data

Before publication, Finance and Legal must provide the reporting period, eligible participant population, outcome distribution where legally required, material expenses and payout restrictions, methodology, source data, exclusions, and preparation date.

Testimonials and examples must identify whether they are typical, disclose material conditions, and avoid implying that the result is representative when it is not.

## Approval

Every disclosure requires dated approval from Legal, Compliance, Finance, and the business owner before use in product copy, advertising, onboarding, training, investor material, or representative communications.
