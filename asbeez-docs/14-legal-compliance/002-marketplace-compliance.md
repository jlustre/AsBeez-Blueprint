## Purpose

Marketplace compliance governs the relationship among customers, members, vendors, partners, AsBeez, payment providers, tax authorities, and service providers.

## Required Analysis

Define marketplace role and contracting parties; seller/vendor responsibility; customer terms; product/service restrictions; payments and money movement; refunds/disputes; consumer protection; tax collection/reporting; privacy; advertising; accessibility; country/entity licensing; complaint handling; records; and enforcement.

## Financial Boundaries

GMV, customer payment, vendor/partner entitlement, fees, commissions, tax, reserves, refunds, and AsBeez recognized revenue are separate concepts. Marketplace configuration or legal terms do not create revenue, liability, wallet value, or ledger entries without approved commercial facts and accounting policy.

## Launch Controls

Require vendor verification/agreement, prohibited-product review, pricing/disclosure review, payment/payout approval, tax registration, refund/dispute process, privacy basis, country readiness, support/runbooks, monitoring, reconciliation, audit evidence, and change review.
