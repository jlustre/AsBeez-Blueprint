# Index

## Purpose

This domain defines the legal, regulatory, contractual, privacy, consumer, tax, marketplace, rewards, direct-selling, country, security, AI, and risk controls required for AsBeez.

## Authority

Legal and Compliance documents are control frameworks and policy references, not universal legal advice. Jurisdiction-specific law, regulator guidance, signed agreements, approved legal opinions, and mandatory consumer/privacy/tax requirements take precedence. Financial policy and the General Ledger remain authoritative for accounting meaning and monetary state.

## Release Gate

No country, product, marketplace, reward, wallet, payment, payout, AI, provider, promotion, agreement, or financial disclosure is launch-ready without documented legal/compliance owner, jurisdiction, classification, contract/disclosure, tax/privacy/security analysis, operational controls, testing, monitoring, approval, and review date.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Establish legal authority, country scope, risk register, privacy, contracts, and policy versioning.
2. Complete marketplace, rewards, direct-selling, income-disclosure, payment, tax, and consumer analysis per launch jurisdiction.
3. Implement vendor/member agreements, country rules, operational controls, evidence, complaints, appeals, and incident response.
4. Test privacy, security, AML/KYC/KYB, sanctions, tax, refunds, payouts, rewards, disclosures, and data retention.
5. Review legal changes, incidents, providers, products, AI, and expansion before each material release.
