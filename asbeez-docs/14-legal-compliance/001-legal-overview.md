## Purpose

Legal and Compliance defines the legal, regulatory, consumer, privacy, tax, marketplace, rewards, direct-selling, country, contractual, and risk requirements that govern AsBeez.

## Authority

Legal requirements, regulator guidance, signed agreements, and jurisdiction-specific advice take precedence over platform assumptions. This documentation is an operating framework, not legal advice. Each launch, product, promotion, reward, payment, payout, AI capability, and country requires documented legal/compliance review.

## Core Principles

- describe AsBeez as a commerce and platform business unless a jurisdiction-specific analysis approves another classification;
- do not promise income, profit, passive returns, ownership, appreciation, guaranteed rewards, or guaranteed withdrawal;
- distinguish marketplace activity, rewards, money, tax, liabilities, fees, commissions, reserves, and revenue;
- use country/entity scope, effective-dated policy, evidence, consent, retention, privacy, security, and appeals; and
- treat uncertainty as hold, review, exception, or launch block rather than silent approval.

## Release Gate

No material feature launches without owner, jurisdiction, legal classification, consumer disclosures, contracts, tax/compliance analysis, control design, operational owner, testing, monitoring, incident response, and approval evidence.
