# Direct-Selling and Compensation Review

> **Status:** Initial review framework  
> **Release gate:** Required before any referral, matrix, representative, or income-related feature is enabled.

## Required Tests

- Rewards arise primarily from qualifying commerce, not recruitment or joining fees.
- A participant can receive the advertised customer benefit without recruiting anyone.
- The platform does not require inventory purchases, paid rank advancement, or recurring fees to preserve eligibility.
- Marketing does not imply guaranteed income, passive income, financial freedom, or a return on money paid in.
- Compensation is not calculated from the mere addition of participants.
- Representative activity has written terms, training limits, records, complaint handling, and prohibited-claims controls.
- Refunds and chargebacks reverse the underlying reward and downstream allocation.
- The plan has a documented maximum liability and an independently reviewable funding source.

## Evidence Package

Before approval, retain the plan rules, sample calculations, customer purchase data, compensation disclosures, representative scripts, jurisdiction analysis, and sign-off from Legal, Finance, Compliance, and Product.

## Decision

This review is incomplete until the launch jurisdictions, participant compensation model, reward funding model, and cash-out policy are explicitly approved. Product documentation must use pending legal classification where those decisions remain open.
