## Purpose

The Vendor Agreement defines onboarding, marketplace participation, products/services, fulfillment responsibilities, payments, fees, tax, settlements, reserves, payouts, refunds, disputes, compliance, privacy, security, data, intellectual property, support, and termination.

## Required Controls

Verify vendor identity/business, beneficial ownership, sanctions/AML/KYB where applicable, products and licenses, tax registrations, bank/payout destination, prohibited conduct, consumer obligations, service levels, complaints, records, insurance/indemnity, and country/entity scope.

## Financial Terms

Define gross order value, taxes, fees, commissions, refunds, chargebacks, reserves/holds, settlement timing, payout conditions, currency/FX, statements, adjustments, and reconciliation. Vendor entitlement is not automatically AsBeez revenue; provider or platform records are reconciled before final treatment.

## Enforcement

AsBeez may hold, suspend, investigate, restrict, or terminate under documented policy and applicable law, with notice, evidence, appeal/response process, data retention, and controlled financial settlement. Agreement changes are versioned and accepted.
