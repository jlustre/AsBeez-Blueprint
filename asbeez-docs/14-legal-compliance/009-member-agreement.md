## Purpose

The Member Agreement defines account eligibility, marketplace use, membership benefits, rewards, referrals, wallet boundaries, payments, withdrawals, privacy, acceptable conduct, disputes, compliance holds, and termination.

## Financial Clarity

Membership and customer benefits do not require recruitment. RP, ABC, and AHC are platform-defined units, not money, currency, equity, investment, securities, or guaranteed returns unless jurisdiction-specific legal approval says otherwise. Wallet balances, reward units, eligibility, expiry, reversals, conversion, and payout restrictions are separately disclosed.

## Member Protections

Provide clear pricing/fees, refund/return/cancellation rights, consent/notice, support/complaint paths, appeal/review for holds or decisions, privacy rights, accessibility, language, country rules, and lawful termination handling. Do not encourage debt, inventory loading, or purchases for speculative earning.

## Change Control

Material changes require legal/compliance/finance/product review, version/effective date, required notice or acceptance, retained evidence, and country-specific compatibility.
