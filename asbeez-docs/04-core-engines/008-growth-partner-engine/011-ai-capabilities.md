# Introduction

Artificial Intelligence enhances the Growth Partner Engine by helping Growth Partners discover suitable programs, prepare contribution records, organize evidence, understand performance, identify growth opportunities, and participate more effectively in the AsBeez ecosystem.

AI also supports administrators, reviewers, Program owners, compliance teams, and Growth Partner Success teams by improving classification, duplicate detection, attribution review, fraud analysis, performance interpretation, and operational decision support.

AI does not replace authoritative Growth Program rules, verified evidence, human accountability, or downstream Reward policies.

Within the Growth Partner Engine, AI operates as an assistant, analyst, coach, and controlled automation service.

---

# Purpose

The AI Capabilities component exists to:

- Simplify Growth Partner onboarding.
- Recommend appropriate Growth Partner Types.
- Match Growth Partners with suitable Growth Programs.
- Assist with Contribution preparation.
- Classify Contributions and evidence.
- Detect duplicates and suspicious claims.
- Support attribution review.
- Improve Contribution verification.
- Analyze Growth Partner Performance.
- Provide explainable coaching.
- Support Growth Program design and optimization.
- Enable governed AI Agents.
- Reduce administrative workload.
- Improve growth quality and integrity.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **AI should help Growth Partners create and document genuine value while preserving verification, fairness, privacy, Program governance, and accountable human judgment.**

# AI Philosophy

The Growth Partner Engine uses AI to assist with:

- Understanding
- Preparation
- Classification
- Matching
- Extraction
- Summarization
- Prediction
- Recommendation
- Anomaly detection

# AI Action Levels

AI capabilities should be classified by impact.

## Level 1 — Informational

AI explains or summarizes.

Examples:

- Explain Program eligibility.
- Summarize Contribution status.
- Compare Growth Partner Types.

Normally no approval is required.

---

## Level 2 — Drafting

AI prepares proposed content or a draft record.

Examples:

- Draft a Contribution.
- Draft an appeal.
- Prepare a Program rule.
- Draft a performance summary.

Human review is required before submission or publication.

---

## Level 3 — Low-Risk Automation

AI performs reversible, policy-approved actions.

Examples:

- Classify evidence.
- Apply metadata tags.
- Save a Contribution draft.
- Schedule reminders.
- Generate a checklist.
- Create an advisory alert.

Actions remain auditable.

---

## Level 4 — High-Impact Action

Examples:

- Submit a Contribution.
- Submit an appeal.
- Enroll in a Program.
- Change a Growth Partner profile.
- Publish public recognition content.

Explicit user or administrator authorization is required.

---

## Level 5 — Restricted Decision

Examples:

- Approve a Growth Partner.
- Assign a regulated Growth Partner Type.
- Activate a Growth Program.
- Approve a disputed Contribution.
- Resolve material attribution.
- Confirm high-value incentive eligibility.
- Suspend or terminate a Growth Partner.

AI may assist but must not independently execute these decisions.


# Business Rules

## GPE-AI-001

AI output is advisory unless an explicitly approved low-risk automation policy states otherwise.

---

## GPE-AI-002

AI must not independently approve, reject, suspend, terminate, or legally bind a Growth Partner.

---

## GPE-AI-003

AI must not activate or materially modify a Growth Program.

---

## GPE-AI-004

AI must not independently resolve material Contribution, duplicate, attribution, fraud, or appeal disputes.

---

## GPE-AI-005

AI must not issue RP, create ABCs, determine Hive placement, distribute AHC, allocate Platform Revenue, or perform Financial Settlement.

---

## GPE-AI-006

AI may not expose data beyond the invoking user's permissions.

---

## GPE-AI-007

Low-confidence classifications or evidence extraction must not update authoritative records automatically.

---

## GPE-AI-008

High-impact recommendations must be explainable and auditable.

---

## GPE-AI-009

AI systems must use versioned models, prompts, Agents, and evaluation criteria.

---

## GPE-AI-010

AI must not modify immutable Contributions, events, Performance Snapshots, attribution records, audit logs, Reward Ledgers, or financial records.

---

## GPE-AI-011

AI advisory events must remain clearly distinct from authoritative Growth Partner Events.

---

## GPE-AI-012

External content must be treated as untrusted and must not authorize actions.

---

## GPE-AI-013

AI Agents must use the same APIs, authorization, validation, and business rules as human users.

---

## GPE-AI-014

Regulated, legal, financial, privacy-sensitive, and materially disputed decisions require qualified human review.

---

## GPE-AI-015

AI recommendations must not use ABC ancestry, descendant position, Hive placement, or perceived network hierarchy as Growth Partner performance factors.

# Long-Term Vision

The Growth Partner Engine should evolve into an AI-assisted ecosystem growth environment that helps individuals and organizations contribute more effectively, transparently, and responsibly.

AI should help Growth Partners identify where they can create value, document that value accurately, understand the applicable Program rules, improve Contribution quality, and learn from measurable outcomes.

At the same time, AsBeez must preserve trust by ensuring that AI remains subordinate to verified evidence, versioned Program policies, authoritative engine records, privacy controls, and accountable human decisions.

---

# Closing Statement

Artificial Intelligence enables the Growth Partner Engine to reduce administrative friction, improve Contribution quality, identify risks, support Program design, explain performance, and guide Growth Partners toward meaningful opportunities.

Its role is not to replace verification, governance, or human accountability.

Its value comes from helping every participant understand the rules, prepare stronger Contributions, receive clearer guidance, and contribute genuine, measurable value to the AsBeez ecosystem.

---

# Growth Partner AI Principle

> **AI should make ecosystem growth more intelligent, not less accountable. It may guide, classify, summarize, predict, recommend, and automate approved low-risk work, but verified Contributions, Growth Program governance, attribution, incentive eligibility, Reward issuance, and financial outcomes must remain controlled by authoritative policies, secure systems, and accountable human judgment.**
