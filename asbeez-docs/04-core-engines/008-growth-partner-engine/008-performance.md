# Introduction

Growth Partner Performance defines how AsBeez measures the effectiveness, quality, consistency, integrity, and long-term impact of Growth Partners participating in approved Growth Programs.

Performance is based on verified Contributions rather than unverified activity, personal position, recruitment volume, or relationship hierarchy.

The Growth Partner Performance framework helps:

- Growth Partners understand their impact.
- Program managers evaluate Growth Program effectiveness.
- Administrators identify quality, risk, and support needs.
- The platform recognize meaningful ecosystem contribution.
- AI systems provide responsible coaching and recommendations.

Performance measurement does not independently create Reward Points, AsBeez Business Cells, AsBeez Hive Credits, or monetary settlement.

---

# Purpose

The Growth Partner Performance component exists to:

- Measure verified Growth Partner Contributions.
- Evaluate Contribution quality and outcomes.
- Track Growth Program participation.
- Maintain versioned performance scorecards.
- Support transparent recognition.
- Identify improvement opportunities.
- Detect declining activity or quality.
- Support Growth Partner coaching.
- Measure long-term ecosystem impact.
- Provide performance analytics.
- Support program governance.
- Preserve immutable performance history.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **Growth Partner Performance measures verified value created for the AsBeez ecosystem—not position, promises, or raw activity volume.**

---

# Performance Philosophy

A Growth Partner should not be considered successful merely because they:

- Submit many Contribution claims.
- Generate large numbers of invitations.
- Hold multiple Growth Partner Types.
- Enroll in many Growth Programs.
- Occupy a perceived leadership position.
- Have many social media followers.
- Introduce participants who do not complete qualifying outcomes.

Performance should reflect measurable and verified outcomes such as:

- Activated Members.
- Activated Platform Partners.
- Successful community initiatives.
- Completed educational activities.
- Sustainable strategic relationships.
- High-quality content.
- Strong compliance.
- Low reversal and dispute rates.
- Positive long-term outcomes.

# Scorecard Versioning

Performance Scorecards are versioned.

When the scoring method changes:

- Historical snapshots remain unchanged.
- Existing results preserve the original Scorecard Version.
- Future evaluations use the new version.
- Historical comparisons identify model changes.
- Scores should not be silently recalculated.

Performance Snapshots are immutable.

Changes to Scorecards affect future evaluations unless an approved governance process explicitly authorizes recalculation.

---

## PERF-007

Growth Partner Performance must not be based on ABC ancestry, descendant position, Hive placement, or network hierarchy.

---

## PERF-008

Performance results do not directly issue RP, create ABCs, distribute AHC, or initiate Financial Settlement.

---

## PERF-009

Performance levels and recognition must not imply guaranteed income or reward entitlement.

---

## PERF-010

Benchmarks must protect the confidentiality of other Growth Partners.

---

## PERF-011

Performance comparisons must account for materially different Programs, markets, Growth Partner Types, and evaluation periods.

---

## PERF-012

Serious compliance or integrity violations may override numerical performance according to policy.

---

## PERF-013

A Growth Partner must be able to view the factors contributing to their Performance Score where legally and operationally permitted.

---

## PERF-014

AI-generated Performance recommendations are advisory.

---

## PERF-015

Low performance alone must not automatically terminate Growth Partner status unless a separately approved lifecycle policy defines such a consequence.

---

## PERF-016

Historical Performance must preserve the Growth Partner Types and Program Versions active during the evaluation period.

---

## PERF-017

Performance appeals and administrative corrections must be auditable.

---

## PERF-018

Performance metrics must not encourage spam, low-quality introductions, coercive recruitment, or unsupported Contribution claims.

# Closing Statement

Growth Partner Performance transforms verified Contribution data into meaningful insight.

By measuring quality, outcomes, consistency, integrity, collaboration, and long-term impact—rather than hierarchy or activity volume—AsBeez can recognize genuine ecosystem growth while helping Growth Partners improve responsibly.

The framework supports transparency, fairness, coaching, recognition, governance, and continuous improvement without confusing performance with automatic Reward Asset or financial entitlement.

---

# Growth Partner Performance Principle

> **Performance reflects verified value created over time. Growth Partners should be measured by the quality, integrity, sustainability, and impact of their Contributions—not by hierarchy, raw recruitment volume, or unsupported activity. Every score must be explainable, versioned, auditable, and separate from automatic reward or financial entitlement.**
