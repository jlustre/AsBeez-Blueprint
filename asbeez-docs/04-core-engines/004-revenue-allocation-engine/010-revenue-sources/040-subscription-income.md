# Introduction

Subscription Revenue represents recurring revenue earned by AsBeez through optional subscription-based products and platform services.

Unlike Platform Participation Fees, Subscription Revenue is generated through recurring agreements for access to premium platform capabilities, value-added services, software features, analytics, training, or membership benefits.

Subscription Revenue is independent of Commercial Transactions and may exist alongside the Platform Participation Economy.

---

# Purpose

Subscription Revenue exists to:

- Diversify platform revenue.
- Provide premium platform services.
- Support recurring revenue.
- Offer advanced business tools.
- Deliver value-added capabilities.
- Reduce dependence on transaction-based revenue.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **Subscription Revenue is earned by providing continuous value through recurring platform services rather than through individual commercial transactions.**

# Business Rules

## SUB-001

Subscriptions must be governed by an active Subscription Agreement.

---

## SUB-002

Recurring billing schedules must be configurable.

---

## SUB-003

Subscription pricing must support multiple currencies.

---

## SUB-004

Subscription Revenue must be recorded independently from Platform Participation Fees.

---

## SUB-005

Subscription Revenue may use its own Revenue Allocation Policy.

---

## SUB-006

Subscription cancellation must preserve historical billing records.

---

## SUB-007

Subscription renewals must generate immutable billing events.

# Long-Term Vision

Subscription Revenue should become a complementary revenue stream that strengthens the financial sustainability of the AsBeez Platform while delivering ongoing value to Members and Platform Partners.

Recurring platform services should enhance the Participation Economy without replacing the core Platform Participation Fee model.

---

# Closing Statement

Subscription Revenue enables AsBeez to provide premium services through recurring value rather than one-time transactions.

By separating subscription-based services from transaction-based Platform Participation Fees, the platform gains a diversified and resilient revenue model capable of supporting long-term innovation and growth.

---

# Subscription Revenue Principle

> **Subscription Revenue is earned by continuously delivering value through recurring platform services, complementing the transaction-based Participation Economy while creating sustainable long-term revenue for the AsBeez Platform.**
