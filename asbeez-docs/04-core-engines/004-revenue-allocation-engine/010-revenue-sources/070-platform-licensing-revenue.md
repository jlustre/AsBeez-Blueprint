# Platform Licensing Revenue

## Purpose

This document defines **Platform Licensing Revenue** as one of the official revenue sources of the AsBeez ecosystem. It establishes the policies, business rules, accounting treatment, and allocation guidelines governing income generated through the licensing of the AsBeez platform, its technologies, intellectual property, and related services.

Platform Licensing Revenue allows AsBeez to generate recurring business income by enabling third parties to legally use portions of the AsBeez platform under approved licensing agreements while maintaining ownership of all intellectual property.

---

# Overview

While AsBeez is primarily designed as a global AI-native commerce ecosystem, certain organizations may require private or customized deployments of the platform for their own businesses, industries, governments, or member organizations.

Instead of rebuilding similar systems from scratch, qualified organizations may license portions of the AsBeez technology stack.

Examples include:

- Enterprise marketplace platforms
- Government procurement portals
- Corporate employee reward platforms
- Educational marketplace systems
- Association membership platforms
- Industry-specific commerce portals
- Franchise management systems
- White-label marketplace solutions
- AI-powered customer engagement platforms

These licensing arrangements generate Platform Licensing Revenue for the AsBeez ecosystem.

---

# Objectives

Platform Licensing Revenue exists to:

- Generate recurring high-margin revenue.
- Expand the AsBeez technology footprint.
- Increase platform adoption globally.
- Monetize proprietary software and AI technologies.
- Support enterprise digital transformation.
- Create additional funding for ecosystem growth.
- Diversify company revenue beyond marketplace transactions.

# Types of Platform Licensing

## Standard Software License

Organizations purchase the right to use selected AsBeez software modules.

Examples:

- Membership Engine
- Commerce Engine
- Rewards Engine
- CRM Engine
- Vendor Engine
- AI Engine

Ownership remains with AsBeez.

---

## Enterprise License

Large organizations receive customized deployments.

Typical customers include:

- National corporations
- Government agencies
- Universities
- Financial institutions
- Healthcare organizations

Enterprise licenses may include:

- Custom integrations
- Dedicated infrastructure
- Priority support
- SLA agreements
- Dedicated account management

---

## White-Label License

Approved partners may deploy AsBeez under their own branding.

Typical features:

- Custom logos
- Brand colors
- Domain names
- Email templates
- UI customization
- Local language support

Core intellectual property remains owned by AsBeez.

---

## Industry License

Industry-specific versions may be licensed for vertical markets.

Examples include:

- Healthcare
- Insurance
- Real Estate
- Hospitality
- Construction
- Education
- Agriculture
- Manufacturing
- Nonprofit organizations

---

## Government License

Government entities may license the platform for:

- Citizen engagement
- Public marketplaces

## API Licensing

Organizations may purchase licensed access to proprietary APIs.

- AI Recommendation API

- Identity API

AsBeez AI capabilities may be licensed independently.

Examples include:

- AI customer assistants

- AI analytics

Licensing may be based on:

- Per user
- Per organization
- Per API call
- Monthly subscription
- Annual subscription

# Licensing Agreement Requirements

Every licensing agreement should define:

- Licensed products
- License duration
- Geographic scope
- Number of users
- Permitted usage
- Prohibited activities
- Data ownership
- Intellectual property rights
- Renewal conditions
- Termination clauses
- Support commitments
- Security requirements
- Compliance obligations

No platform license shall transfer ownership of AsBeez intellectual property.

---

# Territory Licensing

Certain agreements may grant exclusive or non-exclusive rights within defined geographic territories.

Examples:

- Country
- State or Province
- Region
- Industry sector
- Enterprise group

Territory restrictions shall be explicitly documented in each agreement.

# Business Rules

- All licenses require an executed agreement before activation.
- Intellectual property ownership always remains with AsBeez.
- Unauthorized redistribution is prohibited.
- License violations may result in suspension or termination.
- Enterprise pricing may differ from standard pricing.
- White-label deployments remain subject to AsBeez licensing policies.
- Revenue allocations must be processed through the Revenue Allocation Engine.
- Every licensing transaction must be auditable.
- All renewals and expirations shall be tracked automatically.
