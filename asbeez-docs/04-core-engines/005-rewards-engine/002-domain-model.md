# Introduction

The Rewards Domain is responsible for measuring, creating, distributing, and preserving economic value throughout the AsBeez ecosystem.

Unlike traditional reward systems that merely award points, the AsBeez Rewards Engine models an economic ecosystem inspired by the natural behavior of honeybees.

Participants build **AsBeez Business Cells (ABCs)**.

Each Business Cell becomes an independent productive asset capable of generating **AsBeez Hive Credits (AHC)** throughout its lifetime.

The Rewards Engine serves as the authoritative source for all Reward Assets and their complete economic history.

---

# Purpose

The Rewards Domain exists to:

- Measure business activity.
- Create Business Cells.
- Generate Reward Assets.
- Manage Hive Credits.
- Maintain Reward Ledgers.
- Distribute Hive Credits.
- Calculate reward balances.
- Support Membership qualification.
- Support Financial settlement.
- Publish reward events.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **Business activity creates Business Cells. Business Cells create Hive Credits. Hive Credits create economic opportunity.**

# Core Domain Entities

## Reward Account

Represents the participant's complete reward portfolio.

Attributes include:

- Reward Account ID
- Identity ID
- Membership ID
- Country
- Status
- Created Date

---

## Business Cell (ABC)

The Business Cell is the fundamental economic unit of the AsBeez ecosystem.

A Business Cell is created after the participant satisfies the configured RP qualification threshold.

Every Business Cell owns:

- Hive Structure
- AHC Ledger
- Descendant Business Cells
- Ancestor Relationships
- Lifetime Production History

A Member may own many Business Cells.

Each Business Cell operates independently.

---

## Reward Points (RP)

Reward Points measure qualifying business activity.

RP serves as the input for Business Cell creation.

RP is:

- Accumulated
- Auditable
- Configuration-driven

RP is never financial value.

---

## AsBeez Hive Credit (AHC)

AHC represents the economic value generated by Business Cells.

Whenever a new Business Cell is created:

- Hive Credits are generated.
- Hive Credits are distributed to qualifying ancestor Business Cells.
- Each receiving Business Cell accumulates AHC independently.

AHC never expires.

---

## Business Hive

Every Business Cell owns its own Business Hive.

The Business Hive consists of:

- Descendant Business Cells
- Ancestor relationships
- Hive production
- Hive capacity
- Distribution history

The Business Hive is independent from the Member.

---

## Reward Ledger

Every reward transaction is permanently recorded.

Examples include:

- RP Earned
- RP Reversed
- Business Cell Created
- AHC Distributed
- AHC Converted
- Incentive Awarded
- Badge Granted

Reward Ledgers are immutable.

---

## Reward Balance

Balances are calculated.

Examples include:

- RP Balance
- ABC Count
- AHC Balance
- Recognition Count

Balances are never manually maintained.

---

## Incentive Program

Defines configurable programs that reward participation.

Examples:

- Launch Campaign
- Leadership Incentive
- Seasonal Promotion
- Country Campaign

---

## Recognition Program

Defines non-financial achievements.

Examples:

- Badges
- Awards
- Milestones
- Certifications

# Reward Assets

The Rewards Engine manages several Reward Assets.

| Reward Asset | Purpose |
|--------------|---------|

# Business Rules

## RE-001

Every Customer owns exactly one Reward Account.

---

## RE-002

A Member may own multiple Business Cells.

---

## RE-003

Every Business Cell owns its own Business Hive.

---

## RE-004

Every Business Cell maintains its own AHC Ledger.

---

## RE-005

Reward Points generate Business Cells.

---

## RE-006

Business Cells generate Hive Credits.

---

## RE-007

Hive Credits are distributed to qualifying ancestor Business Cells according to the platform's distribution policy.

---

## RE-008

AHC never expires.

---

## RE-009

Reward transactions are immutable.

Corrections create reversing transactions.

---

## RE-010

Reward balances are derived from Reward Ledgers.

---

## RE-011

Financial value is determined exclusively by the Financial Engine.

The Rewards Engine measures economic value.

# Design Principles

The Rewards Domain follows these architectural principles:

- Business Cell First
- Reward Assets First
- Event-Driven
- Ledger-Based
- Immutable History
- Configuration Over Customization
- AI-Native
- Global by Design

# Long-Term Vision

The Rewards Domain should evolve into a reusable economic platform capable of supporting multiple industries, Membership Programs, and future business models.

Every Business Cell should function as an independent economic asset that contributes to a larger ecosystem while maintaining complete transparency, traceability, and sustainability.

# Closing Statement

The Rewards Domain is the economic foundation of the AsBeez Platform.

By modeling Business Cells as productive assets and Hive Credits as measurable economic value, the Rewards Engine transforms participation into a transparent, scalable, and sustainable ecosystem inspired by the natural efficiency of a honeybee colony.

# Domain Principle

> **Business activity creates Business Cells. Business Cells generate Hive Credits. Hive Credits represent measurable economic value that flows throughout the hive according to transparent, configurable, and auditable business rules.**
