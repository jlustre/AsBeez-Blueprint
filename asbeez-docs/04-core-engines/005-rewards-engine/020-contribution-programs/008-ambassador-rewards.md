# Introduction

The Ambassador Rewards Program recognizes Members who serve as trusted representatives of the AsBeez brand by promoting the platform, supporting local communities, expanding strategic relationships, and helping grow the ecosystem.

Unlike Sponsor Rewards, which recognize Member sponsorship, Ambassador Rewards recognize broader contributions that increase awareness, credibility, adoption, and long-term growth of the AsBeez ecosystem.

Ambassadors become visible leaders who help build the future of AsBeez.

---

# Purpose

The Ambassador Rewards Program exists to:

- Expand brand awareness.
- Build trusted community leaders.
- Encourage ecosystem advocacy.
- Support regional growth.
- Recognize outstanding representation.
- Strengthen Member engagement.
- Develop future leaders.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **The greatest ambassadors do more than promote a platform—they represent its values, strengthen its community, and inspire others to contribute.**

---

# Ambassador Philosophy

An Ambassador represents the AsBeez ecosystem both online and offline.

Ambassadors are expected to:

- demonstrate integrity
- educate others
- support new Members
- promote company values
- represent the brand professionally
- help grow local and global communities

The Ambassador Rewards Program recognizes these long-term contributions.

```text

Recognition Updated

```

---

# Qualifying Contributions

Examples include:

- Representing AsBeez at public events
- Hosting educational seminars
- Organizing community meetups
- Speaking at conferences
- Producing educational content
- Building regional communities
- Assisting country launches
- Supporting strategic initiatives
- Participating in media interviews
- Promoting the AsBeez brand responsibly

The list of qualifying activities is fully configurable.

---

# Reward Assets

Ambassador contributions may be recognized through:

- Reward Points (RP)
- Recognition
- Ambassador Titles
- Special Badges
- Exclusive Certifications
- Invitation-only Events
- Leadership Opportunities

Awarded RP follows the standard economic lifecycle:

```text
RP

↓

ABC

↓

AHC
```

---

# Ambassador Levels

The program may support progressive levels.

Examples:

| Level | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Community Ambassador | Supports local communities. |
| Regional Ambassador | Represents a city, province, or state. |
| National Ambassador | Represents a country. |
| Global Ambassador | Represents AsBeez internationally. |

Advancement criteria are configurable.

---

# Qualification Criteria

Examples may include:

- Minimum Membership duration
- Good standing
- Active participation
- Community involvement
- Leadership record
- Completion of required training
- Acceptance of the Ambassador Code of Conduct

Qualification requirements are configuration-driven.

# Long-Term Vision

The Ambassador Rewards Program should cultivate a global network of trusted leaders who represent the values, mission, and vision of AsBeez.

As the platform expands internationally, Ambassadors will play a critical role in helping launch new regions, supporting local communities, sharing knowledge, and building lasting relationships that strengthen the Business Hive.

# Closing Statement

Ambassadors represent the human face of the AsBeez ecosystem.

By recognizing leadership, integrity, advocacy, and community building, the Ambassador Rewards Program encourages Members to become trusted representatives who inspire others and contribute to the long-term growth and reputation of AsBeez.

# Ambassador Principle

> **Ambassadors multiply trust. Through leadership, service, education, and advocacy, they strengthen the AsBeez ecosystem far beyond their own personal success, creating lasting value for Members, Vendors, Customers, and communities around the world.**
