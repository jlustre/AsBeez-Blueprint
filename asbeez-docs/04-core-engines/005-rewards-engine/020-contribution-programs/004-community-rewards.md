# Introduction

The Community Rewards Program recognizes Members who strengthen the AsBeez ecosystem through service, collaboration, mentorship, and community engagement.

Not every valuable contribution is commercial.

Members who help others, share knowledge, organize activities, and contribute to a positive culture play an essential role in the long-term success of the platform.

Community Rewards encourage a culture of generosity, collaboration, and shared success.

---

# Purpose

The Community Rewards Program exists to:

- Encourage collaboration.
- Recognize volunteerism.
- Promote mentorship.
- Reward positive participation.
- Build stronger communities.
- Increase Member engagement.
- Strengthen the AsBeez culture.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **A thriving community is built not only through commerce, but through people helping people succeed.**

---

# Community Philosophy

Community Rewards recognize contributions that improve the ecosystem without requiring direct commercial transactions.

The goal is to encourage Members to:

- share knowledge
- mentor others
- solve problems
- welcome newcomers
- organize activities
- contribute to a positive environment

These contributions strengthen the Business Hive even when they do not immediately generate Reward Points through commerce.

---


# Qualifying Contributions

Examples include:

- Mentoring new Members
- Answering community questions
- Helping Members complete onboarding
- Organizing local meetups
- Speaking at company events
- Hosting webinars
- Creating educational content
- Translating documentation
- Writing tutorials
- Reporting platform bugs
- Participating in beta testing
- Moderating community discussions
- Volunteering during company events
- Supporting charitable initiatives

The list should remain fully configurable.

---

# Reward Assets

Community contributions may be rewarded with:

- Reward Points (RP)
- Recognition Badges
- Community Titles
- Certificates
- Special Achievements
- Leaderboard Recognition

Rewards should be proportional to the contribution.

---

# Reward Examples

| Contribution | Suggested Reward |
|--------------|-----------------:|
| Answer accepted by community | 25 RP |
| Mentor a new Qualified Member | 100 RP |
| Host an official webinar | 200 RP |
| Organize a local event | 300 RP |
| Volunteer at company conference | Configurable |
| Submit verified bug report | Configurable |
| Translate official documentation | Configurable |

Actual values are defined through Reward Policies.

---

# Community Recognition

Examples include:

- Community Helper
- Mentor
- Educator
- Volunteer
- Ambassador
- Event Organizer
- Knowledge Contributor
- Community Champion

Recognition may accompany RP but remains independent.

---

# Business Rules

## COM-001

Community Rewards recognize verified contributions that strengthen the ecosystem.

---

## COM-002

Community Rewards are configuration-driven.

---

## COM-003

Community Rewards may include RP, Recognition, or both.

---

## COM-004

Awarded RP participates in the standard RP → ABC → AHC lifecycle.

---

## COM-005

Community contributions must be verifiable.

---

## COM-006

Administrators may approve, reject, or revoke rewards according to Reward Policies.

---

## COM-007

Community Reward transactions are immutable.

Corrections create reversing transactions.

---

## COM-008

Community contribution criteria may vary by country or community.

---

# Configuration

Administrators should be able to configure:

- Eligible contributions
- RP awards
- Recognition awards
- Approval workflow
- Country availability
- Effective dates
- Daily or monthly limits
- Verification requirements

---

# Verification

Community contributions may be verified through:

- Administrative approval
- AI-assisted review
- Peer voting
- Event attendance records
- Platform activity logs
- Community moderators

Verification methods are configurable.

---

# Analytics

The Community Rewards Program measures:

- Community engagement
- Volunteer participation
- Mentorship activity
- Educational contributions
- Event participation
- Community health
- Reward effectiveness

# Long-Term Vision

The Community Rewards Program should foster a culture where Members are motivated not only by personal success, but also by helping others succeed.

AsBeez should become known as a community where collaboration, generosity, education, and service are recognized alongside commercial achievement.

---

# Closing Statement

Strong ecosystems are built on relationships, trust, and collaboration.

The Community Rewards Program ensures that Members who invest their time, knowledge, and energy into helping others are recognized as essential contributors to the long-term health and growth of the AsBeez ecosystem.

---

# Community Principle

> **Communities flourish when people willingly help one another. Community Rewards recognize and celebrate those who strengthen the AsBeez ecosystem through service, mentorship, education, collaboration, and positive leadership.**
