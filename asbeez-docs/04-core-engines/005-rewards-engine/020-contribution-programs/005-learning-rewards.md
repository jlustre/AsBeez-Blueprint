# Introduction

The Learning Rewards Program encourages continuous education and professional development by recognizing Members who complete approved learning activities.

Knowledge is one of the most valuable contributions a Member can make to the AsBeez ecosystem.

By rewarding learning, the platform promotes better customer service, stronger leadership, improved compliance, and long-term business success.

Learning Rewards transform education into measurable contribution.

---

# Purpose

The Learning Rewards Program exists to:

- Encourage continuous learning.
- Increase product knowledge.
- Improve Member professionalism.
- Promote compliance.
- Develop leadership.
- Encourage platform adoption.
- Reward educational achievement.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **Knowledge creates confidence. Confidence creates value. Learning strengthens the entire AsBeez ecosystem.**

---

# Learning Philosophy

Members who invest in learning become better equipped to:

- serve customers
- mentor new Members
- build stronger businesses
- represent the company professionally
- contribute innovative ideas

The Learning Rewards Program recognizes these investments through configurable Reward Assets.

# Qualifying Learning Activities

Examples include:

- New Member Orientation
- Product Knowledge Courses
- Compliance Training
- Leadership Development
- Sales Skills Training
- Customer Service Training
- Vendor Training
- AI Platform Training
- Technical Certifications
- Business Development Courses
- Company Conferences
- Live Webinars
- Recorded Training
- Knowledge Assessments

Future learning activities should be configurable.

---

# Reward Assets

Learning achievements may award:

- Reward Points (RP)
- Recognition Badges
- Certificates
- Digital Credentials
- Learning Titles
- Achievement Milestones

Awarded RP follows the standard RP → ABC → AHC lifecycle.

---

# Sample Learning Rewards

| Learning Activity | Suggested Reward |
|-------------------|-----------------:|
| Complete Orientation | 50 RP |
| Product Certification | 100 RP |
| Leadership Certification | 200 RP |
| AI Training | 150 RP |
| Annual Compliance Training | Configurable |
| Complete Learning Path | Configurable |

Reward values are defined through Reward Policies.

---

# Learning Paths

Courses may be grouped into structured learning paths.

Examples:

- New Member Path
- Sales Professional Path
- Leadership Path
- Vendor Specialist Path
- AI Professional Path
- Executive Leadership Path

Completing an entire learning path may unlock additional Recognition or Rewards.

---

# Certifications

The platform may issue certifications upon successful completion.

Examples:

- Certified Product Advisor
- Certified Business Builder
- Certified Mentor
- Certified Vendor Specialist
- Certified AI Practitioner
- Certified Leadership Coach

Certifications become part of the Member's permanent profile.

---

# Business Rules

## LRN-001

Learning Rewards recognize verified educational achievements.

---

## LRN-002

Learning Rewards may award RP, Recognition, Certifications, or combinations thereof.

---

## LRN-003

Awarded RP participates in the standard RP → ABC → AHC lifecycle.

---

## LRN-004

Learning completion must be verifiable.

---

## LRN-005

Courses may require assessments before rewards are granted.

---

## LRN-006

Reward values are configuration-driven.

---

## LRN-007

Learning history is permanently recorded.

---

## LRN-008

Learning programs may vary by country, industry, role, or Membership level.

# Configuration

Administrators should be able to configure:

- Eligible courses
- RP values
- Passing scores
- Certification requirements
- Learning paths
- Country availability
- Effective dates
- Expiration rules (where applicable)

---

# Verification

Learning completion may be verified through:

- Online assessments
- Instructor approval
- Event attendance
- Platform activity
- Completion certificates
- Learning Management System (LMS) integration

Verification methods are configurable.

The Learning Rewards Program measures:

- Certification rates
- Learning engagement
- Knowledge growth

- Training effectiveness
- Reward utilization
