# Introduction

The Vendor Rewards Program recognizes Members who contribute to the growth and success of the AsBeez Vendor Ecosystem.

Vendors are strategic partners that expand the platform's products, services, and opportunities.

By introducing and helping qualified Vendors become successful, Members strengthen the Business Hive and create long-term value for the entire ecosystem.

Vendor Rewards recognize these contributions through configurable Reward Assets and Recognition Programs.

---

# Purpose

The Vendor Rewards Program exists to:

- Expand the Vendor ecosystem.
- Encourage quality Vendor referrals.
- Support Vendor onboarding.
- Promote Vendor success.
- Increase product and service diversity.
- Strengthen local and global marketplaces.
- Reward ecosystem expansion.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **A stronger Vendor ecosystem creates greater value for every Member, Customer, and Business Cell within AsBeez.**

---

# Vendor Philosophy

Vendors are more than suppliers.

They are ecosystem partners.

Every successful Vendor contributes to:

- Customer satisfaction
- Marketplace diversity
- Business growth
- Community development
- Long-term sustainability

Members who help attract and develop successful Vendors deserve recognition for their contribution.

```text
Vendor Opportunity Identified

↓

↓

Vendor Activated

Vendor Rewards Awarded

↓

Reward Ledger Updated

Recognition Updated
```

---

# Qualifying Contributions

Examples include:

- Referring a prospective Vendor
- Assisting Vendor onboarding
- Helping Vendors complete training
- Supporting Vendor launch
- Helping Vendors achieve activation milestones
- Recruiting strategic partners
- Expanding regional Vendor coverage

Future qualifying contributions should be configurable.

---

# Reward Assets

Vendor contributions may award:

- Reward Points (RP)
- Recognition
- Vendor Achievement Badges
- Certificates
- Campaign Rewards

Awarded RP follows the standard economic lifecycle:

```text
RP

↓

ABC

↓

AHC
```

---

# Vendor Milestones

The program should support milestone-based rewards.

Examples:

| Milestone | Suggested Reward |
|-----------|-----------------:|
| Vendor Referral Submitted | Configurable |
| Vendor Approved | 250 RP |
| Vendor Activated | 500 RP |
| Vendor Reaches Sales Milestone | Configurable |
| Vendor Anniversary | Recognition |

Reward values are defined through Reward Policies.

---

# Vendor Categories

Programs may distinguish between:

- Product Vendors
- Service Providers
- Professional Services
- Technology Partners
- Logistics Partners
- Educational Partners
- Strategic Partners

Each category may have different qualification and reward rules.

---

# Business Rules

## VEN-001

Vendor Rewards recognize verified contributions to Vendor ecosystem growth.

---

## VEN-002

Vendor Rewards are configuration-driven.

---

## VEN-003

Vendor Rewards may award RP, Recognition, or both.

---

## VEN-004

Awarded RP participates in the standard RP → ABC → AHC lifecycle.

---

## VEN-005

Vendor approval must occur before milestone-based rewards are granted unless a campaign specifies otherwise.

---

## VEN-006

Vendor Rewards are permanently recorded in immutable Reward Ledgers.

---

## VEN-007

Vendor programs may vary by country, industry, or Vendor category.

---

## VEN-008

Vendor Rewards may be funded through Campaigns or future Hive Growth Fund policies.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The Vendor Rewards Program should help create the world's largest collaborative marketplace by encouraging Members to continuously introduce high-quality Vendors that expand opportunities for Customers and strengthen the AsBeez ecosystem.

As the platform grows, Vendor Rewards should evolve to recognize not only Vendor acquisition but also long-term Vendor success, innovation, customer satisfaction, and strategic partnerships.

# Closing Statement

A thriving marketplace depends on exceptional Vendors.

The Vendor Rewards Program recognizes Members who help build that marketplace by introducing, supporting, and developing Vendor relationships that create lasting value for Customers, Members, and the Business Hive.

# Vendor Reward Principle
