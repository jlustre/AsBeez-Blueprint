# Overview

Contribution Programs define the various ways participants create measurable value within the AsBeez ecosystem.

Rather than rewarding only purchases or recruitment, the AsBeez platform recognizes multiple forms of meaningful contribution that strengthen the ecosystem.

Each Contribution Program specifies:

- what contribution is being recognized
- the eligibility requirements
- the applicable business rules
- the Reward Assets that may be awarded
- any recognition or qualifications that may result

Contribution Programs do not define the Reward Assets themselves.

Instead, they define **how Reward Assets are earned.**

# Relationship to Reward Assets

Contribution Programs generate Reward Assets.

The relationship is illustrated below.

```text
Contribution

↓

Contribution Program

↓

Reward Policy

↓

Reward Asset

↓

Reward Ledger

↓

Business Cell

↓

Hive Credit

↓

Financial Settlement (when applicable)
```

Contribution Programs determine **why** a reward is granted.

Reward Assets determine **what** is granted.

# Contribution Philosophy

The AsBeez ecosystem is built upon the principle that value can be created in many different ways.

Examples include:

- purchasing products or services
- introducing new participants
- helping Members succeed
- registering Vendors
- participating in company campaigns
- completing education
- contributing to the community
- creating innovative ideas
- supporting ecosystem growth

Each contribution strengthens the Business Hive and may be recognized through one or more Reward Assets.

---

# Design Principles

Contribution Programs follow these principles:

- Contribution First
- Configuration Over Customization
- Event-Driven
- Country Configurable
- Transparent
- Auditable
- AI-Assisted
- Extensible

New Contribution Programs should be introduced through configuration whenever possible rather than requiring platform redesign.

---

# Standard Contribution Flow

Every Contribution Program follows the same lifecycle.

```text
Participant Contribution

↓

Eligibility Validation

↓

Program Rules Evaluated

↓

Reward Assets Awarded

↓

Reward Ledger Updated

↓

Business Events Published

↓

Notifications Sent

↓

Analytics Updated
```

This consistent lifecycle ensures predictable platform behavior regardless of the type of contribution.

# Current Contribution Programs

## Sponsor Rewards

Rewards Members who successfully introduce and develop new Members within the ecosystem.

Typical Reward Assets:

- Reward Points (RP)

---

## Company Campaigns

Temporary or recurring programs created by the company to encourage specific behaviors that support ecosystem growth.

Examples include:

- Vendor Registration Campaigns
- Product Launch Campaigns
- Country Expansion Campaigns
- Seasonal Promotions
- Leadership Challenges

Typical Reward Assets:

- Reward Points (RP)
- Recognition

---

## Recognition Programs

Non-financial programs that acknowledge achievement, leadership, milestones, certifications, and outstanding contributions.

Typical Reward Assets:

- Badges
- Certificates
- Achievements
- Leaderboards

# AI Within Contribution Programs

Artificial Intelligence may assist by:

- recommending campaigns
- personalizing challenges
- predicting engagement
- identifying high-value contributors
- detecting abuse
- optimizing reward policies

AI provides recommendations only.

Business rules remain under administrative control.

# Closing Statement

Contribution Programs transform participation into measurable value.

By recognizing many different forms of contribution through a unified and configurable framework, the AsBeez platform encourages Members to build a stronger ecosystem while maintaining a consistent economic model centered on Reward Assets, Business Cells, and Hive Credits.

---

# Contribution Principle

> **The strength of the AsBeez ecosystem is determined not by a single activity, but by the collective contributions of its participants. Contribution Programs ensure that every meaningful action—whether commercial, educational, social, or strategic—can be recognized through a transparent, configurable, and sustainable rewards framework.**
