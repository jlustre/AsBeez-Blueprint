# Introduction

The AsBeez Hive Credit (AHC) Engine governs the creation, distribution, accumulation, and lifecycle of Hive Credits throughout the AsBeez ecosystem.

AHC represents the economic value generated whenever a new AsBeez Business Cell (ABC) is created.

Unlike Reward Points, which measure qualifying activity, or Business Cells, which represent productive assets, Hive Credits represent the value produced by those assets.

AHC is the "honey" produced by the Business Hive.

---

# Purpose

The AHC Engine exists to:

- Generate Hive Credits
- Distribute Hive Credits
- Track Hive Credit accumulation
- Maintain AHC ledgers
- Support financial settlement
- Preserve complete audit history
- Publish Hive Credit events

---

# Guiding Principle

> **Business Cells produce Hive Credits. Hive Credits represent the economic value flowing throughout the AsBeez Hive.**

---

# What is AHC?

AsBeez Hive Credit (AHC) is the standardized economic credit generated by the creation of Business Cells.

AHC is:

- an economic credit
- measurable
- auditable
- permanent
- configuration-driven
- convertible through the Financial Engine

AHC is NOT:

- cash
- currency
- commission
- wallet balance
- cryptocurrency

AHC represents earned economic value.

The AHC model is inspired by honey production inside a bee colony.

| Honeybee Colony | AsBeez |
|-----------------|---------|
| Bee builds wax cell | Member creates Business Cell |
| Cell stores honey | Business Cell accumulates Hive Credits |
| Honey feeds the hive | Hive Credits create economic opportunity |
| Hive grows stronger | Business ecosystem grows stronger |

AHC is the digital equivalent of honey.

---

# How AHC is Generated

Whenever a new Business Cell is created:

1. The country-configured ABC Threshold determines the amount of AHC generated.
2. The newly created Business Cell produces that amount of AHC.
3. The generated AHC is distributed upward through the qualifying ancestor Business Cells.
4. Each eligible ancestor Business Cell accumulates its allocated Hive Credits.
5. The distribution is recorded in immutable AHC Ledgers.

Example:

```text
ABC Threshold = 120 RP

↓

New Business Cell Created

↓

120 AHC Generated

↓

Distributed to qualifying ancestor Business Cells

↓

Each ancestor accumulates AHC
```

---

# Country Configuration

The amount of AHC generated by each new Business Cell is equal to the country's configured ABC Threshold.

Examples:

| Country | ABC Threshold | AHC Generated |
|---------|--------------:|--------------:|
| United States | 120 RP | 120 AHC |
| Canada | 60 RP | 60 AHC |
| Philippines | 36 RP | 36 AHC |

This ensures the economic model adapts to each country's qualification requirements.

---

# AHC Distribution

AHC is distributed to ancestor Business Cells.

Distribution follows the Business Hive.

Rules:

- Distribution begins with the newly created Business Cell.
- Only qualifying ancestor Business Cells participate.
- Distribution is limited to twelve ancestral levels.
- Distribution percentages are defined by the platform's Reward Policies.
- Descendant Business Cells remain independently owned.

The AHC Engine performs the distribution.

---

# Business Hive

Every Business Cell establishes its own Business Hive.

Each Business Hive may contain up to:

- 3 direct descendants
- 12 generations
- 66,430 maximum descendant Business Cells

The Business Hive determines the maximum potential AHC production.

---

# Hive Capacity

Each Business Hive has a finite maximum production capacity.

Formula:

```text
Maximum AHC Capacity

=

Maximum Descendant Business Cells

×

Country ABC Threshold
```

Where:

Maximum Descendant Business Cells = 66,430

Examples:

| ABC Threshold | Maximum AHC |
|--------------:|------------:|
| 120 RP | 7,971,600 AHC |
| 60 RP | 3,985,800 AHC |
| 36 RP | 2,391,480 AHC |

This represents the theoretical maximum production of a fully populated Business Hive.

---

# AHC Ledger

Every Hive Credit transaction is permanently recorded.

Examples:

| Date | Event | AHC |
|------|-------|----:|
| Jan 10 | Hive Credit Received | +10 |
| Jan 15 | Hive Credit Received | +25 |
| Jan 20 | Conversion Requested | -300 |

The ledger is immutable.

Corrections create new transactions.

Historical records are never modified.

---

# AHC Balance

Current AHC is derived from the AHC Ledger.

```text
AHC Earned

-

AHC Converted

+

Administrative Adjustments

=

Current AHC Balance
```

Balances are calculated.

They are never edited directly.

---

# Financial Conversion

AHC itself has no monetary value.

A Member may request that eligible Hive Credits be converted into financial value.

The conversion process is performed exclusively by the Financial Engine.

The Rewards Engine:

- tracks AHC
- validates eligibility
- publishes conversion events

The Financial Engine:

- determines exchange rates
- performs settlement
- records accounting entries
- issues payments

This separation preserves a clean architecture.

# Long-Term Vision

The AHC Engine provides the economic foundation of the AsBeez ecosystem.

As Business Cells expand their Business Hives, Hive Credits flow throughout the ecosystem, rewarding long-term contribution rather than short-term activity.

This creates a sustainable economy where value is continuously generated, transparently distributed, permanently recorded, and independently settled.

---

# Closing Statement

AsBeez Hive Credits are the digital "honey" of the AsBeez ecosystem.

They represent the economic value produced by Business Cells and distributed throughout the Business Hive according to transparent, configurable, and auditable business rules.

By separating Hive Credit generation from financial settlement, the AsBeez Platform establishes a scalable, sustainable, and globally adaptable economic model inspired by the remarkable efficiency of a honeybee colony.
