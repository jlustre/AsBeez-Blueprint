# Overview

The Reward Assets component defines the digital assets that power the AsBeez Participation Economy.

Reward Assets represent the units used to recognize, track, distribute, and eventually settle participation within the platform.

Each Reward Asset has a distinct purpose, lifecycle, and governance model.

Together, they provide a transparent and financially sustainable framework for rewarding Members based on real commercial activity.

---

# Purpose

The Reward Assets component exists to:

- Define platform reward assets.
- Standardize participation units.
- Support reward generation.
- Support reward distribution.
- Enable financial settlement.
- Maintain complete traceability.
- Preserve immutable reward history.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **Reward Assets represent participation—not currency. Every Reward Asset is backed by real platform value and exists to measure, distribute, or settle participation within the AsBeez ecosystem.**

# Reward Asset Lifecycle

```text
Qualified Platform Revenue

↓

Compensation Fund

↓

Reward Points (RP)

↓

AsBeez Business Cells (ABC)

↓

AsBeez Hive Credits (AHC)

↓

Financial Settlement
```

Each Reward Asset serves a different purpose within the lifecycle.

---

# Core Reward Assets

## Reward Points (RP)

Reward Points are the initial participation units generated by the Rewards Engine.

Characteristics:

- Financially backed by the Compensation Fund.
- Configurable conversion ratio.
- Immutable once issued.
- Used to create Business Cells.
- Not directly redeemable.

---

## AsBeez Business Cells (ABC)

Business Cells represent immutable participation units created from Reward Points.

Characteristics:

- Represent participation lineage.
- Preserve ancestry relationships.
- Support reward distribution.
- Cannot be modified after creation.
- Form the structural foundation of the Hive.

---

## AsBeez Hive Credits (AHC)

Hive Credits are the distributable reward units flowing through the ancestry network.

Characteristics:

- Generated from Business Cells.
- Distributed according to Reward Policies.
- Recorded in the AHC Ledger.
- Eligible for Financial Settlement according to platform policies.

---

## Future Reward Assets

Future versions of the platform may introduce additional Reward Assets.

Examples include:

- Recognition Badges
- Achievement Tokens
- Community Credits
- Innovation Credits
- Sustainability Credits
- Educational Credits

These assets should integrate into the existing Rewards Engine without changing its core architecture.

---

# Reward Asset Characteristics

Every Reward Asset should define:

- Asset ID
- Asset Type
- Asset Name
- Asset Code
- Creation Rules
- Ownership
- Status
- Effective Date
- Version

Additional metadata may be included where appropriate.

---

# Asset Principles

All Reward Assets should be:

- Immutable
- Traceable
- Auditable
- Configurable
- Policy-driven
- Financially governed
- Versioned where applicable


Each stage builds upon the previous one while preserving complete traceability.

# Long-Term Vision

The Reward Assets component should become a universal participation asset framework capable of supporting future reward models without changing the underlying Rewards Engine.

New Reward Assets should be introduced through governance and configuration while preserving compatibility with the existing Participation Economy.

---

# Reward Asset Principle

> **Reward Assets are the digital representation of participation within the AsBeez ecosystem. They are not money—they are governed, traceable, and financially backed assets that transform real platform value into measurable participation and ultimately into transparent financial settlement.**
