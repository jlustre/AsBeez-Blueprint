# Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a foundational capability of the AsBeez Platform.

Within the Commerce Engine, AI enhances the experience of Members, Platform Partners, and Administrators by providing intelligent recommendations, automation, forecasting, optimization, and decision support.

AI assists users throughout the commercial lifecycle while preserving the platform's principles of transparency, traceability, and human oversight.

---

# Purpose

The AI Capabilities component exists to:

- Improve shopping experiences.
- Assist Platform Partners.
- Increase commercial efficiency.
- Optimize catalog quality.
- Enhance search and discovery.
- Detect anomalies and fraud.
- Improve forecasting.
- Automate repetitive tasks.
- Generate business insights.
- Support future autonomous commerce.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **AI assists decision-making. Business policies remain authoritative. Human oversight is preserved for significant commercial actions.**

---

# AI Philosophy

Artificial Intelligence exists to enhance—not replace—human decision making.

AI should:

- Recommend
- Predict
- Analyze
- Classify
- Optimize
- Automate routine work

AI should never silently alter commercial records or bypass platform policies.

Every AI recommendation should remain explainable, auditable, and configurable.

# AI Safety

The Commerce Engine follows responsible AI principles.

AI should be:

- Transparent
- Explainable
- Auditable
- Configurable
- Secure
- Privacy-aware
- Human-supervised

AI decisions affecting commerce should be reviewable when appropriate.

---

# Human Approval

The following actions should require explicit human approval unless platform policies state otherwise:

- Publishing Catalog Offerings
- Major price changes
- Removing commercial listings
- Suspending Platform Partners
- Accepting negotiated quotes
- High-value commercial transactions
- Regulatory compliance actions

---

# AI Learning

AI models may continuously improve using:

- Commerce Events
- Catalog quality
- Search behavior
- Purchase behavior
- Anonymous analytics
- Customer feedback
- Platform performance metrics

Training data must comply with privacy and regulatory requirements.

# Privacy

AI must respect:

- Member privacy
- Platform Partner confidentiality
- Country-specific regulations
- Data retention policies
- Consent requirements

Personally identifiable information should only be processed according to applicable privacy policies.
