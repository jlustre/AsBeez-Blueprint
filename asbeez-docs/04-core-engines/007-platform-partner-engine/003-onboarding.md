# Introduction

Platform Partner Onboarding defines the process through which an organization applies, verifies its identity, accepts platform terms, configures its participation, and becomes eligible to offer Products or Services within the AsBeez ecosystem.

Onboarding is more than account registration.

It establishes the legal, operational, commercial, and compliance foundation required for a trusted relationship between AsBeez and the Platform Partner.

A Platform Partner must complete all required onboarding stages before it may participate in qualified commercial activity.

---

# Purpose

The Platform Partner Onboarding process exists to:

- Register participating organizations.
- Verify business identity.
- Confirm authorized representatives.
- Classify provider roles.
- Collect required legal and compliance documents.
- Establish a Platform Participation Agreement (PPA).
- Configure Platform Participation Fee (PPF) terms.
- Configure locations, branches, Products, and Services.
- Prepare the organization for commercial participation.
- Maintain a complete onboarding audit trail.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **Platform participation begins with trust. Every Platform Partner must establish a verified business identity, authorized representation, and approved participation terms before entering the AsBeez ecosystem.**

# Onboarding Participants

## Applicant Organization

The business or organization seeking Platform Partner status.

---

## Authorized Representative

An individual legally authorized to submit information, accept agreements, and act on behalf of the organization.

Examples include:

- Owner
- Officer
- Director
- Authorized Manager
- Legal Representative

---

## Platform Partner Administrator

An authorized representative who manages the organization after activation.

---

## Verification Team

Reviews business identity, legal registration, ownership, and supporting documents.

---

## Compliance Team

Reviews licensing, regulatory, insurance, risk, and jurisdiction-specific requirements.

---

## Platform Administrator

Provides final approval, rejection, suspension, or exception authorization.

# Stage 1 — Organization Registration

The applicant creates an organization application.

Required information may include:

- Public Business Name
- Legal Business Name
- Business Type
- Industry
- Country
- Primary Address
- Primary Contact
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Website
- Referral or Invitation Source

The application receives a unique Partner Application ID.

---

# Stage 2 — Business Profile

The organization creates its public and operational profile.

Information may include:

- Business Description
- Logo
- Cover Image
- Categories
- Products or Services Summary
- Business Hours
- Service Areas
- Languages
- Social Media
- Customer Support Details

Public profile information is separate from confidential legal information.

---

# Stage 3 — Legal Entity Information

The applicant provides authoritative legal information.

Examples include:

- Legal Name
- Registration Number
- Tax Identification Number
- Incorporation or Registration Date
- Business Structure
- Registered Address
- Jurisdiction
- Beneficial Ownership Information, where required

Legal information must remain protected and accessible only to authorized roles.

---

# Stage 4 — Authorized Representative Verification

The platform verifies that the applicant is permitted to act for the organization.

Verification may include:

- Identity Verification
- Role or Title
- Ownership Evidence
- Authorization Letter
- Corporate Resolution
- Contact Verification
- Electronic Signature

An organization may have multiple authorized representatives.

---

# Stage 5 — Provider Type Classification

The applicant selects one or more provider roles.

Supported roles include:

- Platform Product Provider
- Platform Service Provider
- Hybrid Provider
- Marketplace Partner
- Strategic Partner
- Community Partner
- Technology Partner
- Other Approved Partner Type

A Platform Partner may hold multiple roles.

---

# Stage 6 — Business Verification

Business verification confirms that the organization is legitimate and operational.

Verification may include:

- Business Registry Validation
- Tax Registration Validation
- Address Verification
- Domain or Website Verification
- Phone Verification
- Ownership Verification
- Sanctions Screening
- Adverse Media Review
- Risk Assessment

Verification requirements may vary by country, industry, Partner Type, and risk level.

---

# Stage 7 — Compliance Documentation

The platform collects and reviews required compliance records.

Examples include:

- Business License
- Professional License
- Insurance Certificate
- Regulatory Registration
- Health Permit
- Safety Certification
- Privacy Documents
- Industry Accreditation
- Background Verification
- Tax Forms

Requirements should be configuration-driven by:

- Country
- State or Province
- Industry
- Provider Type
- Product or Service Category

---

# Stage 8 — Platform Participation Agreement

The Platform Partner reviews and accepts the active Platform Participation Agreement.

The PPA may define:

- Participation Rights
- Platform Services
- Platform Participation Fee
- Qualified Transaction Value Rules
- Payment Terms
- Reporting Obligations
- Compliance Responsibilities
- Data Protection Requirements
- Renewal Terms
- Suspension and Termination
- Dispute Resolution

The accepted agreement version must be preserved permanently.

---

# Stage 9 — Platform Participation Fee Configuration

The applicable PPF configuration is selected or established according to the PPA.

Configuration may include:

- Percentage Fee
- Fixed Fee
- Hybrid Fee
- Revenue Category
- Effective Date
- Country
- Currency
- Excluded Charges
- Settlement Schedule
- Reporting Method

The Platform Partner Engine stores the relationship to the applicable configuration but does not calculate the fee for completed transactions.

---

# Stage 10 — Billing and Payment Setup

The Platform Partner configures how amounts owed to AsBeez will be collected or settled.

Options may include:

- Automatic Split Payment
- Payment Processor Settlement
- Direct Debit
- ACH
- Bank Transfer
- Invoice Billing
- Credit Card
- Manual Remittance
- Other Approved Method

Billing setup may require financial verification.

Sensitive banking and payment credentials should be managed by the appropriate Financial Engine or external payment provider.

---

# Stage 11 — Organization Structure

The Platform Partner may configure:

- Headquarters
- Branches
- Locations
- Departments
- Representatives
- Partner Users
- Roles
- Approval Workflows

Each branch or location may have independent:

- Contact Information
- Business Hours
- Offerings
- Service Areas
- Compliance Records
- Reporting Settings

---

# Stage 12 — Products and Services

The Platform Partner may begin configuring its Catalog.

Possible activities include:

- Create Products
- Create Services
- Define Pricing
- Configure Availability
- Upload Media
- Define Locations
- Configure Fulfillment
- Configure Booking Rules
- Submit Offerings for Approval

Catalog publishing remains governed by Commerce Engine policies.

---

# Stage 13 — QR Identity

The Platform Partner may receive one or more QR identities.

QR identities may identify:

- Organization
- Branch
- Location
- Checkout Station
- Representative
- Campaign
- Commercial Transaction

The QR identity allows Members to identify themselves or associate a transaction with a Platform Partner.

A QR scan does not by itself create Qualified Platform Revenue or rewards.

---

# Stage 14 — Orientation and Training

The Partner may be required to complete onboarding education.

Training may include:

- Platform Overview
- Partner Portal
- Member Identification
- QR Usage
- PPA and PPF Responsibilities
- Transaction Reporting
- Catalog Management
- Compliance Obligations
- Support Procedures
- Dispute Management
- Data Privacy
- Fraud Prevention

Completion may be required before activation.

---

# Stage 15 — Final Review

Before activation, the platform validates that all required onboarding tasks are complete.

Final review may confirm:

- Business verification passed
- Compliance documents approved
- Authorized representative verified
- PPA accepted
- PPF configured
- Billing method active
- Required training completed
- Provider roles approved
- Required Catalog or operational data completed

Incomplete applications return to the appropriate onboarding stage.

---

# Stage 16 — Activation

After final approval, the Platform Partner status becomes:

```text
Active
```

Activation may enable:

- Partner Portal Access
- Catalog Publishing
- Commercial Transactions
- Lead Acceptance
- Booking Programs
- Member QR Recognition
- Platform Reporting
- Participation Processing

Activation publishes a Platform Partner activation event.

- Insurance

Used for large organizations with:

- Multiple branches
- Multiple legal entities
- Custom integration
- Dedicated agreements
- Advanced governance

## Invitation-Only Onboarding

Used for strategic, pilot, or private partner programs.

---

## Expedited Onboarding

May be used for previously verified or low-risk organizations subject to platform policy.

# Business Rules

## ONB-001

Every onboarding application must reference one applicant organization.

---

## ONB-002

Every application must have at least one verified Authorized Representative before agreement acceptance.

---

## ONB-003

An organization must pass required business verification before activation.

---

## ONB-004

Commercial participation requires an accepted and active Platform Participation Agreement.

---

## ONB-005

The applicable Platform Participation Fee configuration must be established before qualified commercial processing begins.

---

## ONB-006

Regulated offerings may not be published until required licenses and compliance records are approved.

---

## ONB-007

Rejected applications remain available for audit and may not be physically deleted.

---

## ONB-008

Expired documents may trigger re-verification, restrictions, suspension, or renewal workflows.

---

## ONB-009

A Partner may not activate itself unless explicitly permitted by platform policy.

---

## ONB-010

All onboarding approvals, rejections, exceptions, and document decisions must be audited.

---

## ONB-011

Acceptance of a PPA must preserve the agreement version, timestamp, signatory, and evidence of consent.

---

## ONB-012

A single legal entity should not create duplicate Platform Partner records unless explicitly approved for a valid organizational reason.

# Duplicate Detection

The onboarding process should detect potential duplicate organizations using:

- Legal Name
- Registration Number
- Tax Identification
- Address
- Website Domain
- Phone Number
- Authorized Representative
- Beneficial Owner

Potential duplicates should be routed for review rather than automatically merged.

---

# Application Expiration

Incomplete applications may expire after a configurable period.

Before expiration, the platform may send reminders.

Expired applications may be:

- Resumed
- Reopened
- Re-submitted
- Archived

depending on onboarding policy.

# Security and Privacy

Onboarding data may contain sensitive organizational and personal information.

The platform should enforce:

- Role-Based Access Control
- Encryption
- Document Access Restrictions
- Audit Logging
- Data Retention Policies
- Consent Management
- Privacy Compliance
- Secure File Storage
- Least-Privilege Access

Public business profile data must remain separated from restricted legal and financial data.

# Long-Term Vision

Platform Partner Onboarding should evolve into a configurable, risk-based, AI-assisted onboarding framework capable of supporting organizations across countries, industries, and regulatory environments.

New Partner Types should reuse the same onboarding foundation while extending requirements through policies, checklists, document rules, and approval workflows.

The platform should reduce unnecessary friction while maintaining strong trust, legal integrity, compliance, and complete traceability.

---

# Closing Statement

Platform Partner Onboarding transforms an applicant organization into a trusted participant in the AsBeez ecosystem.

By combining business verification, authorized representation, compliance review, transparent participation terms, operational configuration, and education, the onboarding process establishes the foundation required for sustainable and responsible commercial participation.

---

# Platform Partner Onboarding Principle

> **Joining the platform is not merely creating an account. It is establishing a verified, governed, and trusted business relationship. Every Platform Partner must demonstrate legitimate identity, authorized representation, operational readiness, and acceptance of transparent participation terms before becoming active in the AsBeez ecosystem.**
