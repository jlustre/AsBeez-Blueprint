# Introduction

The Platform Partner Engine manages the complete lifecycle of organizations participating in the AsBeez Participation Economy.

Platform Partners are trusted organizations that provide products, services, expertise, bookings, business opportunities, or other forms of value to Members through the AsBeez Platform.

Unlike traditional vendor management systems, the Platform Partner Engine is designed to support a broad ecosystem of organizations across multiple industries while maintaining consistent participation policies, governance, and commercial standards.

Every commercial transaction, lead, booking, or service offered through the platform originates from a Platform Partner.

---

# Purpose

The Platform Partner Engine exists to:

- Register Platform Partners.
- Verify organizational identity.
- Manage organization profiles.
- Configure Platform Participation Agreements (PPA).
- Configure Platform Participation Fees (PPF).
- Publish products and services.
- Support commercial participation.
- Maintain compliance.
- Enable trusted business relationships.
- Support long-term platform growth.

---

# Vision

To create the world's most trusted ecosystem of Platform Partners where organizations of every size can participate in a transparent, AI-assisted, participation-based economy.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **Platform Partners are trusted organizations that create value for Members. The Platform Partner Engine provides the governance, identity, and participation framework that enables organizations to contribute sustainably to the AsBeez ecosystem.**

---

# What is a Platform Partner?

A Platform Partner is any approved organization that participates in the AsBeez Platform by providing products, services, expertise, or commercial opportunities to Members.

Platform Partners operate under a Platform Participation Agreement (PPA) and participate according to platform governance, commercial policies, and applicable regulations.

Platform Partners are organizations rather than individual Members.

Individuals working within an organization may be represented as Users, Employees, Managers, or Representatives associated with the Platform Partner.

# Business Identity

Every Platform Partner maintains a verified business identity including:

- Legal business information
- Business registration
- Tax information
- Business addresses
- Contact information
- Banking information
- Licensing information
- Verification status
- Participation status

Business identity forms the foundation for trust across the platform.

---

# Platform Participation Agreement (PPA)

Every Platform Partner participates under a Platform Participation Agreement.

The agreement defines:

- Participation terms
- Platform Participation Fee (PPF)
- Revenue calculation rules
- Payment schedules
- Settlement rules
- Responsibilities
- Compliance obligations
- Renewal terms

No commercial activity should generate Platform Participation Fees without an active PPA.

---

# Platform Participation Fee (PPF)

Platform Partners agree to contribute a Platform Participation Fee based on qualified commercial activity.

The Platform Participation Fee:

- Is governed by the Platform Participation Agreement.
- Supports the Participation Economy.
- Funds Platform Operations.
- Funds the Compensation Fund.
- Is processed through the Revenue Allocation Engine.
