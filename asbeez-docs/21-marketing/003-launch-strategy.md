# Launch Strategy

## Purpose

Launch strategy introduces approved AsBeez marketplace capabilities in controlled markets and channels with product, legal, financial, operational, security, privacy, and support readiness.

## Launch Gates

Confirm product scope, country/entity, vendor supply, customer journey, payment/refund/payout, tax, privacy/terms, reward classification, disclosures, risk/fraud, support/runbooks, reconciliation/close, reporting, monitoring, incident, DR, accessibility, localization, analytics consent, and budget.

## Phases

Pilot with bounded audience and provider volume; monitor customer/vendor quality, payments, refunds, disputes, support, complaints, compliance, cost, liquidity, and reconciliation; address findings; then expand channels/countries only after go/no-go authority approves evidence.

## Campaign Controls

Every campaign has owner, audience, claim substantiation, terms, disclosure, consent basis, country/channel restrictions, budget, dates, frequency limits, complaint route, measurement, incident stop condition, and archive. Pause campaigns for misleading claims, legal change, privacy issue, material complaint, fraud, or control failure.
