# Member Acquisition

## Purpose

Member acquisition invites customers into voluntary loyalty and community participation with clear benefits, conditions, privacy choices, and country availability.

## Approved Message

Explain membership benefits, qualifying marketplace activity, referrals as optional participation, RP/ABC/AHC as platform-defined units, eligibility, expiry, reversal, country restrictions, and support. Membership does not guarantee income, rewards, placement, ownership, appreciation, conversion, or withdrawal.

## Controls

Do not require recruitment, inventory purchases, fees, debt, or speculative purchases for ordinary customer benefits. Obtain consent for marketing, provide disclosures and terms before enrollment, protect minors/vulnerable users, and honor unsubscribe/access/privacy rights.

## Metrics

Consent, acquisition source, activation, first qualifying activity, repeat purchase, member retention, support/complaint rate, reward understanding, reversal/expiry, acquisition cost, country performance, and disclosure completion. Do not report reward units as cash or income.
