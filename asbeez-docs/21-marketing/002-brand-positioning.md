# Brand Positioning

## Purpose

AsBeez is positioned as a community-powered digital commerce ecosystem built on genuine marketplace activity, vendor growth, customer value, and transparent participation.

## Positioning Pillars

- trusted multi-vendor marketplace;
- practical tools and visibility for vendors;
- clear customer experience and support;
- voluntary loyalty participation tied to qualifying platform activity; and
- scalable technology with local country, tax, payment, privacy, and compliance readiness.

## Approved Language Direction

Use commerce, participation, loyalty, marketplace value, vendor growth, customer choice, and transparent platform rules. Explain that results, rewards, availability, eligibility, and payouts depend on applicable rules, activity, country, refunds, fraud controls, and approved programs.

## Prohibited Positioning

Do not position AsBeez as an investment, wealth-building system, passive-income opportunity, guaranteed income stream, security, ownership program, currency, or guaranteed-return product. Do not imply recruitment is required or that ordinary purchases guarantee financial outcomes.

## Governance

Brand claims require evidence, audience/country review, legal/compliance approval, accessibility, privacy, localization, and versioned asset retention.
