# Customer Acquisition

## Purpose

Customer acquisition brings qualified customers to approved marketplace products/services through transparent, useful, accessible, and privacy-respecting campaigns.

## Channels and Experience

Use search, content, partners, referrals, email, social, advertising, offers, and community channels with consent, frequency controls, attribution, age/country restrictions, accessible creative, clear pricing, vendor identity, taxes/fees, terms, delivery, returns, and support.

## Claims and Measurement

Claims must be accurate and substantiated; discounts show material conditions; testimonials disclose typicality; reward/customer benefits do not imply financial returns. Measure qualified traffic, conversion, repeat purchase, customer satisfaction, refund/complaint/fraud rate, acquisition cost, retention, consent, accessibility, and country/channel performance.

## Controls

Do not use sensitive data without approved basis, expose identity/payment/risk information, target prohibited audiences, or report GMV/payment as recognized revenue. Campaigns have approval, stop conditions, archive, privacy review, and legal/compliance escalation.
