# Content Strategy

## Purpose

Content educates customers, members, vendors, partners, and operators about products, commerce, platform use, loyalty, support, policies, and community participation.

## Content Pillars

Marketplace discovery and education; vendor enablement; customer trust and support; member/loyalty explanation; country/local content; financial-status literacy; privacy/security; accessibility; and transparent product/feature updates.

## Editorial Controls

Each asset has owner, audience, country/language, source/evidence, version, review date, accessibility check, privacy/consent basis, legal/compliance status, financial claim classification, and correction/archive process. Content uses clear terms for pending, estimated, provider-reported, failed, reversed, held, and unknown states.

## Prohibited Content

No unsupported earnings, income, investment, ownership, appreciation, guaranteed reward/placement/withdrawal, recruitment requirement, fabricated testimonial, hidden material condition, or misleading financial comparison. AI-generated content requires source review, disclosure where appropriate, human approval, and prompt/output evidence.

## Measurement

Measure comprehension, accessibility, engagement, qualified conversion, support reduction, complaint/correction rate, disclosure completion, retention, country/channel performance, and trust—not only reach or revenue.
