# Marketing Overview

## Purpose

AsBeez marketing connects customers, members, vendors, partners, and communities with approved marketplace products, services, tools, and participation benefits.

## Marketing Principles

Lead with genuine commerce and clear customer/vendor value; make claims accurate, substantiated, accessible, localized, and understandable; obtain consent for direct marketing; protect personal data; disclose material terms; and measure outcomes without misleading attribution.

## Audiences

Customers seek useful products and trustworthy transactions; vendors seek qualified demand and growth tools; members seek participation and loyalty benefits; partners seek approved collaboration; operators need compliant campaigns and measurable support.

## Measurement

Track reach, consent, engagement, conversion, repeat purchase, vendor quality, customer satisfaction, acquisition cost, retention, refund/complaint rate, accessibility, country/channel performance, and campaign attribution. Marketing metrics do not equal revenue or prove reward liability.

## Governance

Campaigns require owner, audience, country, channel, budget, claims/evidence, data basis, terms, disclosures, approval, start/end, monitoring, complaints, incident path, and archive. Financial or earnings-related claims require Legal, Compliance, Finance, and business-owner approval.
