# Marketing

## Purpose

This domain defines AsBeez brand, launch, vendor/member/customer acquisition, content, channel, measurement, and marketing-governance requirements.

## Marketing Authority

Marketing communicates approved products, marketplace benefits, vendor value, customer benefits, and transparent loyalty participation. Legal/compliance, product, finance, privacy, country, and brand authorities approve claims and campaigns before publication.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

Do not promise income, profit, passive returns, appreciation, investment, ownership, guaranteed rewards, guaranteed placement, guaranteed withdrawal, or financial security. Do not require recruitment or purchases for speculative earning. Marketing must distinguish customer commerce, vendor earnings, fees, tax, rewards, wallets, and recognized revenue; respect consent, privacy, accessibility, country restrictions, and disclosure approval.
