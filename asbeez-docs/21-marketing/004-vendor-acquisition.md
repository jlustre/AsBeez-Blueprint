# Vendor Acquisition

## Purpose

Vendor acquisition attracts qualified businesses whose products/services, operations, identity, tax, customer support, and fulfillment meet AsBeez marketplace requirements.

## Journey

Interest -> qualification -> application -> identity/business verification -> product/prohibited-category review -> agreement -> onboarding/training -> catalog approval -> pilot activity -> performance/support review.

## Value Proposition

Communicate storefront tools, marketplace demand, analytics, promotions, customer relationships, operational support, and transparent fees/settlement. Do not promise sales, earnings, payout timing, or guaranteed growth.

## Controls

Marketing and sales may not bypass KYB, sanctions, tax, product, privacy, country, agreement, fee, reserve, payout, or support requirements. Vendor claims and testimonials are substantiated, localized, consented, and archived. Vendor data is minimized and access-controlled.

## Metrics

Qualified applications, approval rate, onboarding completion, time to first approved listing/order, product quality, customer satisfaction, refund/dispute rate, retention, support burden, acquisition cost, and country/provider risk.
