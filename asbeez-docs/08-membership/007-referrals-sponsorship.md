# Referrals & Sponsorship

## Introduction

The **Referrals & Sponsorship** module governs how relationships are established, maintained, and rewarded within the AsBeez ecosystem. It defines how Customers and Members introduce new participants to the platform, how sponsor relationships are permanently recorded, and how referral-related incentives are managed in a transparent, ethical, and sustainable manner.

Unlike traditional MLM systems where recruitment is the primary focus, AsBeez is a **Marketplace-First** platform. Referrals exist to encourage genuine product and service recommendations, not aggressive recruitment. Customers should join because they see value in the Marketplace, while Membership and Rewards naturally follow through continued participation.

This module ensures that sponsorship relationships remain historically accurate, referral incentives are fairly distributed, and the integrity of the ecosystem is preserved.

---

# Objectives

The Referrals & Sponsorship module aims to:

- Encourage organic platform growth.
- Reward ethical recommendations.
- Maintain permanent sponsor relationships.
- Prevent referral abuse.
- Support cross-country referrals.
- Integrate with the Rewards Engine.
- Enable AI-assisted referral insights.
- Maintain complete audit trails.
- Support future incentive programs.
- Ensure regulatory compliance.

# Referral Philosophy

## Customers Join for Value

Individuals should join AsBeez because they want to:

- Purchase products.
- Purchase services.
- Access digital goods.
- Participate in the Marketplace.

Referrals should introduce value—not pressure.

---

## Sponsorship Is Permanent

Once a sponsor relationship is established and accepted, it becomes part of the customer's permanent historical record.

This historical relationship should never be silently modified.

Administrative corrections require a fully documented audit trail.

---

## Referrals Should Be Honest

Sponsors should:

- Present accurate information.
- Avoid misleading claims.
- Respect customer decisions.
- Encourage informed participation.

Ethical referrals strengthen long-term platform growth.

---

## Marketplace Before Membership

Referral discussions should focus on:

- Products
- Services
- Marketplace value

Membership qualification occurs naturally through participation.

---

# Core Definitions

## Referrer

A Referrer is any eligible individual who introduces a new Customer to AsBeez.

Depending on platform policy, referrals may be initiated by:

- Qualified Members
- Promotional campaigns
- Strategic partners
- Vendors (future)

---

## Sponsor

A Sponsor is the Member permanently associated with a referred Customer.

The Sponsor relationship becomes active upon successful registration.

---

## Referral

A Referral is the relationship between:

Sponsor

↓

Customer

This relationship may later evolve into:

Sponsor

↓

Member

# Registration Under a Sponsor

During registration:

The system should:

1. Validate sponsor.
2. Validate referral code.
3. Check eligibility.
4. Prevent self-referrals.
5. Record referral source.
6. Create audit records.

---

# Sponsor Eligibility

To become a Sponsor, an individual generally must:

- Be a Qualified Member.
- Be in good standing.
- Not be suspended.
- Comply with platform policies.

Future programs may introduce additional sponsor requirements.

---

# Cross-Country Referrals

Cross-country referrals are fully supported.

Example:

```text
USA Member

↓

Refers Customer

↓

Canada Customer
```

Country membership affects regulatory and Rewards processing, but does not invalidate the referral relationship.

---

# Self-Referral Prevention

The platform should prevent:

- Self-registration using personal referral codes.
- Duplicate accounts.
- Circular referrals.
- Referral manipulation.

Detection methods include:

- Identity matching.
- Device fingerprinting.
- IP analysis.
- AI fraud detection.

---

# Referral Ownership

A referral belongs to the Sponsor who successfully referred the Customer.

Ownership remains historically accurate even if:

- The Sponsor changes country.
- The Customer qualifies later.
- Membership status changes.
- The Sponsor becomes inactive.

Historical relationships should never be silently reassigned.

# Summary

The Referrals & Sponsorship module provides a transparent and ethical framework for growing the AsBeez ecosystem through genuine marketplace recommendations rather than recruitment-driven practices. By establishing permanent sponsor relationships, supporting multiple referral methods, enabling cross-country participation, protecting customer privacy, and integrating with the Membership, Rewards, Marketplace, AI, and Analytics Engines, the module creates a scalable foundation for sustainable growth. Its emphasis on fairness, fraud prevention, and customer value ensures that referrals strengthen the ecosystem while preserving trust among Customers, Members, Sponsors, and the broader AsBeez community.
