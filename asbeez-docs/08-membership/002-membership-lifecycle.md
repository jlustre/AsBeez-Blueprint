# Membership Overview

## Introduction

The **Membership Engine** is the heart of the AsBeez ecosystem. While the Marketplace enables commerce and the Rewards Engine manages incentives, the Membership Engine establishes long-term relationships between individuals and the platform by governing qualification, participation, privileges, responsibilities, and ownership of membership assets.

Unlike conventional e-commerce platforms where users remain customers indefinitely, AsBeez introduces a progression model that allows customers to become members after meeting defined qualification requirements. This distinction creates a sustainable ecosystem where commerce, rewards, referrals, and community participation work together.

The Membership Engine is responsible for defining who qualifies as a member, what benefits they receive, how they participate in the AsBeez Business Cell (ABC) network, how sponsorship relationships are maintained, and how transferable membership assets are protected throughout the member's lifetime.

---

# Purpose

The Membership Engine exists to:

- Establish a clear distinction between customers and members.
- Govern the complete membership lifecycle.
- Protect member rights and transferable assets.
- Support ethical referral relationships.
- Enable long-term participation in the AsBeez ecosystem.
- Manage membership qualification.
- Maintain regulatory compliance.
- Support global operations.
- Integrate with all core platform engines.

# Membership Philosophy

The Membership Engine is built on several foundational philosophies.

## Membership Is Earned

Membership is not granted merely by registering an account.

Instead, membership is earned by fulfilling the platform's qualification requirements, beginning with the successful creation of at least one **AsBeez Business Cell (ABC)**.

This approach ensures that membership reflects genuine participation in the AsBeez ecosystem.

---

## Commerce Comes First

Every individual begins as a customer.

Customers are encouraged to:

- Purchase products.
- Purchase services.
- Explore the marketplace.
- Earn Reward Points.
- Experience the platform.

Membership naturally follows active participation rather than requiring an upfront enrollment fee.

---

## Long-Term Participation

The Membership Engine encourages sustainable engagement rather than short-term transactions.

Members are expected to:

- Continue participating in the marketplace.
- Support ethical business practices.
- Build customer relationships.
- Grow responsibly.
- Contribute to the overall health of the ecosystem.

---

## Global Yet Local

The Membership Engine supports global participation while respecting:

- Country-specific regulations
- Tax laws
- Privacy laws
- Local business practices
- Regional reward configurations

Each member maintains one global identity while participating under one active country membership at a time.

# Customer vs Member

One of the most important concepts within AsBeez is understanding the difference between a customer and a member.

## Customer

A customer is an individual who:

- Has registered an account.
- May purchase products.
- May purchase services.
- May earn Reward Points.
- Does not yet own an ABC.
- Has not qualified for membership.

Customers enjoy the marketplace but do not yet participate in the Membership Engine.

---

## Member

A member is a customer who has successfully qualified by creating at least one ABC.

Members gain access to:

- Membership privileges
- Reward participation
- Sponsor rights
- Referral benefits
- Beneficiary designation
- Country membership
- Member dashboard
- Additional platform opportunities

---

# Membership Qualification

Qualification is intentionally straightforward.

A customer becomes a member when:

- Qualification requirements are satisfied.
- At least one ABC has been successfully created.
- Identity verification requirements have been met (where applicable).
- Platform policies have been accepted.

Membership begins immediately after successful qualification.
