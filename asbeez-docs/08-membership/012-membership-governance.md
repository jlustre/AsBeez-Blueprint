# Membership Governance

## Introduction

The **Membership Governance** module serves as the constitutional framework of the AsBeez Membership Engine. It defines the rights, responsibilities, ethical standards, policies, disciplinary procedures, and governance principles that apply to every Customer and Member participating in the AsBeez ecosystem.

Governance ensures that AsBeez remains a fair, transparent, secure, and sustainable platform where every participant is treated consistently regardless of country, financial status, referral network, or tenure.

Rather than relying solely on software rules, the Governance framework establishes the human principles that guide platform decisions, dispute resolution, policy enforcement, and long-term ecosystem evolution.

---

# Objectives

The Membership Governance module aims to:

- Protect member rights.
- Define member responsibilities.
- Promote ethical participation.
- Maintain platform integrity.
- Support transparent decision-making.
- Prevent fraud and abuse.
- Establish disciplinary procedures.
- Protect membership assets.
- Support international compliance.
- Enable long-term ecosystem sustainability.

---

# Governance Principles

The Membership Governance framework is built upon the following principles.

---

## Fairness

Every Customer and Member shall be treated fairly and consistently.

No person shall receive preferential treatment because of:

- Wealth
- Country
- Position
- Sponsor
- Referral size
- Length of membership

Platform rules apply equally to everyone.

---

## Transparency

Policies should be:

- Clearly documented.
- Publicly available.
- Easy to understand.
- Consistently enforced.

Members should never be surprised by hidden rules.

---

## Accountability

Every participant is responsible for:

- Their own actions.
- Compliance with policies.
- Accurate information.
- Ethical conduct.

Administrators are equally accountable for enforcing policies consistently.

---

## Integrity

Integrity is more valuable than short-term growth.

The platform prioritizes:

- Honest business.
- Ethical referrals.
- Accurate communication.
- Respectful interactions.

---

## Security

Member information and assets must always be protected through:

- Strong authentication.
- Secure infrastructure.
- Privacy controls.
- Continuous monitoring.

---

## Sustainability

Policies should support the long-term health of the ecosystem rather than short-term gains.

---

# Member Rights

Every Qualified Member has the right to:

- Participate in the Marketplace.
- Own eligible ABCs.
- Earn rewards according to platform policies.
- Access Membership benefits.
- Designate beneficiaries.
- Receive transparent information.
- Request support.
- Appeal administrative decisions.
- Protect personal information.
- Be treated fairly and respectfully.

Rights remain subject to applicable laws and platform policies.

---

# Member Responsibilities

Members are responsible for:

- Maintaining accurate profile information.
- Protecting login credentials.
- Complying with platform policies.
- Conducting business ethically.
- Respecting other Members.
- Reporting suspected fraud.
- Keeping beneficiary information current.
- Completing required verification.
- Following applicable laws.

---

# Customer Responsibilities

Customers should:

- Use the Marketplace responsibly.
- Provide accurate registration information.
- Respect vendors.
- Follow platform rules.
- Avoid fraudulent activities.

---

# Code of Conduct

Every participant agrees to:

- Act honestly.
- Respect other users.
- Avoid harassment.
- Avoid discrimination.
- Avoid abusive behavior.
- Respect intellectual property.
- Follow applicable laws.
- Protect confidential information.

---

# Prohibited Activities

The following activities are prohibited.

## Fraud

Examples:

- Identity fraud.
- Payment fraud.
- Reward manipulation.
- ABC manipulation.
- Fake referrals.

---

## Misrepresentation

Examples:

- False income claims.
- False product claims.
- Misleading marketing.
- Impersonation.

---

## Multiple Accounts

Unless specifically authorized:

One individual may maintain only one Global Identity.

Creating duplicate accounts to gain advantages is prohibited.

---

## Automated Abuse

Examples:

- Bots
- Automated registrations
- Referral farming
- Fake traffic

---

## Harassment

Examples:

- Threats
- Bullying
- Hate speech
- Offensive conduct

---

## Unauthorized Access

Members may not:

- Access another account.
- Circumvent security.
- Reverse engineer protected systems.
- Exploit software vulnerabilities.

---

# Membership Asset Protection

Members are expected to protect:

- Reward Points
- ABC ownership
- AHC balances
- Wallet balances
- Login credentials
- Recovery information

Failure to follow reasonable security practices may affect account recovery processes.

---

# Sponsor Responsibilities

Sponsors should:

- Present accurate information.
- Respect customer decisions.
- Avoid high-pressure sales tactics.
- Support referred customers.
- Encourage ethical participation.

Sponsors should prioritize long-term relationships over short-term rewards.

---

# Vendor Responsibilities

Members operating as Vendors should:

- Deliver products honestly.
- Fulfill orders promptly.
- Honor published policies.
- Maintain accurate listings.
- Respond professionally to customers.

Vendor obligations are further defined within the Marketplace documentation.

---

# Administrative Responsibilities

Administrators must:

- Enforce policies consistently.
- Protect member information.
- Maintain confidentiality.
- Document administrative actions.
- Avoid conflicts of interest.
- Preserve audit trails.

Administrative authority should never be abused.

---

# Compliance

Members must comply with:

- Platform Terms of Service.
- Privacy Policy.
- KYC requirements.
- AML regulations.
- Tax obligations.
- Country-specific regulations.

Failure to comply may result in administrative action.

---

# Investigations

Investigations may occur when:

- Fraud is suspected.
- Identity concerns arise.
- Policy violations are reported.
- Security incidents occur.
- Court orders are received.

Investigations should remain confidential whenever possible.

---

# Disciplinary Actions

Administrative actions may include:

## Warning

Minor violations.

---

## Temporary Restriction

Temporary loss of selected privileges.

---

## Suspension

Temporary loss of Membership capabilities pending investigation or corrective action.

---

## Permanent Termination

Reserved for severe violations.

Examples:

- Proven fraud.
- Criminal activity.
- Repeated abuse.
- Serious security violations.

---

# Appeals Process

Members have the right to request review of certain administrative decisions.

Suggested process:

```text
Administrative Decision

↓

Appeal Submitted

↓

Review

↓

Decision

↓

Final Resolution
```

Appeals should be reviewed by authorized personnel who were not directly involved in the original decision whenever practical.

---

# Conflict Resolution

Disputes may involve:

- Customers
- Members
- Vendors
- Sponsors
- Beneficiaries

Whenever possible:

1. Direct resolution.
2. Administrative mediation.
3. Formal review.
4. Legal process (if required).

---

# Privacy

The platform is committed to protecting personal information.

Principles include:

- Data minimization.
- Consent.
- Encryption.
- Limited access.
- Secure retention.
- Compliance with applicable privacy laws.

---

# Intellectual Property

Members should respect:

- Platform trademarks.
- Copyright.
- Software.
- Documentation.
- Vendor content.
- User-generated content.

Unauthorized use is prohibited.

# Audit & Accountability

Every governance-related action should generate immutable audit records.

Examples:

- Policy acceptance.
- Warning issued.
- Suspension.
- Appeal.
- Administrative decision.
- Beneficiary approval.
- Country change approval.

Audit records should include:

- Timestamp
- User
- Action
- Reason
- Supporting references

# AI Governance

Artificial Intelligence may assist governance by:

- Detecting fraud.
- Identifying suspicious behavior.
- Recommending policy reviews.
- Monitoring abuse patterns.
- Prioritizing investigations.

AI recommendations are advisory only.

Final governance decisions remain the responsibility of authorized human administrators.

Reports may include:

# Best Practices

- Keep governance policies simple and transparent.
- Treat all Members consistently.
- Preserve complete audit trails.
- Clearly separate rights from privileges.
- Encourage education before enforcement whenever appropriate.
- Protect Member privacy.
- Use AI responsibly with human oversight.
- Regularly review governance policies.
- Align governance with applicable laws.
- Design policies for long-term ecosystem sustainability.
