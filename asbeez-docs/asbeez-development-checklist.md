# Architecture Notes

- Customer, member, vendor, partner, and administrator experiences should use separate navigation shells while sharing a common design system.
- A customer becomes a qualified member only after generating at least one ABC.
- Customer store credit, member rewards, vendor earnings, and partner compensation require separate wallet and ledger accounts.
- Country-specific rules must be configuration-driven rather than hard-coded into pages.
- Rewards calculations, placement events, and ledger transactions must maintain immutable audit trails.
- Sensitive financial, identity, tax, and matrix information must use permission-based access controls.
- All placeholder statistics, testimonials, income examples, and performance claims must be replaced by verified data before publication.
- Rewards pages must use qualification-based language and must not promise earnings or financial results.
- Public routes should be optimized for SEO, accessibility, mobile devices, and internationalization.
- Dashboard pages should include consistent loading, empty, error, success, permission-denied, and maintenance states.
