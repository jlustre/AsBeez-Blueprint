# Purpose

This document provides a comprehensive overview of the AsBeez business model.

It defines the nature of the company, the participants within the ecosystem, the value created for each stakeholder, and the long-term direction of the enterprise.

This document serves as the foundation for every business, legal, operational, technical, and financial decision made within AsBeez.

---

# What is AsBeez?

AsBeez is a community-powered digital commerce ecosystem designed to connect customers, members, vendors, partners, and investors through one integrated online marketplace.

Unlike traditional e-commerce platforms that focus primarily on facilitating transactions, AsBeez seeks to create lasting value by combining:

- Digital Commerce
- Customer Loyalty
- Vendor Growth
- Community Participation
- Enterprise Technology
- Long-Term Value Creation

into a single scalable ecosystem.

AsBeez is not merely an online store.

It is a digital economy built upon genuine commerce.

# Business Classification

AsBeez operates as multiple businesses within one ecosystem.

## Digital Marketplace

A multi-vendor marketplace where businesses sell products and services.

---

## SaaS Platform

Technology that enables vendors to manage their stores, products, customers, promotions, and analytics.

---

## Loyalty Platform

A marketplace that rewards long-term participation through a structured loyalty system.

## Community Platform

A platform where members help expand the marketplace through genuine commerce and referrals.

## Advertising Platform

Businesses may promote products and services through advertising opportunities within the marketplace.

# The AsBeez Ecosystem

The ecosystem consists of six primary participants.

## Customers

Purchase products and services.

Customers may purchase without creating an account.

Customers may later become Members.

---

## Members

Members participate in the loyalty ecosystem.

Members may:

- Purchase products
- Refer others
- Build ABCs
- Earn Hive Credits according to platform rules
- Upgrade to Vendor

Membership is free.

---

## Vendors

Businesses that sell products or services through AsBeez.

Every Vendor receives:

- Storefront
- Product Management
- Sales Analytics
- Promotions
- Coupons
- Order Management
- Digital Delivery
- Customer Insights

---

## Partners

Organizations that provide services supporting the ecosystem.

Examples include:

- Payment Providers
- Logistics Providers
- Marketing Agencies
- Educational Partners
- Technology Partners

---

## Employees

Employees build, maintain, and improve the AsBeez platform while supporting customers, vendors, and Members.

---

## Investors

Investors provide capital, strategic guidance, and governance that support the long-term growth of AsBeez.
