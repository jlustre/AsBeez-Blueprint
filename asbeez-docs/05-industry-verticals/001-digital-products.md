# Digital Products Marketplace

## Purpose

The **Digital Products Marketplace** enables creators, businesses, organizations, and developers to buy, sell, license, distribute, and manage digital products within the AsBeez ecosystem. It provides a secure, scalable, and AI-powered platform for the global distribution of digital goods while integrating seamlessly with the Membership, Commerce, Rewards, Financial, AI, and Vendor Engines.

Unlike physical products, digital products are delivered electronically, allowing instant fulfillment, global accessibility, lower operational costs, and unlimited scalability.

---

# Overview

The Digital Products Marketplace serves as one of the primary industry verticals of AsBeez.

It allows qualified sellers to publish digital products that can be purchased, downloaded, streamed, licensed, or accessed online by customers worldwide.

The marketplace supports both one-time purchases and recurring subscription models while leveraging AI to improve product discovery, recommendations, customer engagement, and fraud detection.

---

# Objectives

The Digital Products Marketplace aims to:

- Enable creators to monetize digital content.
- Provide customers with instant digital delivery.
- Support multiple licensing and pricing models.
- Expand the global reach of digital entrepreneurs.
- Generate recurring platform revenue.
- Encourage repeat purchases through the Rewards Engine.
- Promote trust through seller verification and buyer protection.
- Leverage AI to improve the buying experience.
