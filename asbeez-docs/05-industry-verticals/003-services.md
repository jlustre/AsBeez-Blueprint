# Services Marketplace

## Purpose

The **Services Marketplace** enables individuals, professionals, businesses, agencies, and organizations to offer, discover, book, manage, and deliver services through the AsBeez ecosystem. It provides a secure, AI-powered platform that connects service providers with customers while supporting quotations, scheduling, contracts, payments, milestones, reviews, and long-term customer relationships.

Unlike product marketplaces, services involve the delivery of expertise, labor, consultation, or professional assistance rather than tangible goods. The Services Marketplace is designed to manage the complete service lifecycle from discovery to successful completion.

---

# Overview

The Services Marketplace is one of the core industry verticals of AsBeez.

It enables providers to showcase their expertise while allowing customers to search, compare, communicate, schedule, and purchase services with confidence.

The marketplace supports both online and on-site services and integrates seamlessly with:

- Commerce Engine
- Membership Engine
- Vendor Engine
- Financial Engine
- Rewards Engine
- Calendar & Scheduling Services
- Notification Engine
- AI Engine

---

# Objectives

The Services Marketplace aims to:

- Connect qualified service providers with customers.
- Simplify service discovery and booking.
- Support secure online transactions.
- Manage quotations and contracts.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
- Encourage repeat business through loyalty rewards.
- Provide AI-powered recommendations.
- Create sustainable recurring platform revenue.
