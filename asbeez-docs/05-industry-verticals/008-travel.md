# Travel Marketplace

## Purpose

The **Travel Marketplace** enables travelers, travel agencies, tour operators, hotels, airlines, cruise lines, transportation providers, attractions, event organizers, travel insurance companies, and destination management organizations to offer, discover, book, and manage travel-related products and services through the AsBeez ecosystem.

The marketplace provides an AI-powered, end-to-end travel platform that supports trip planning, bookings, itinerary management, payments, loyalty rewards, travel assistance, and post-trip engagement.

Rather than serving as a simple booking website, the Travel Marketplace functions as a comprehensive digital travel ecosystem connecting every participant in the travel industry.

---

# Overview

Travel planning often requires customers to use multiple websites for flights, hotels, transportation, tours, attractions, insurance, and activities.

The AsBeez Travel Marketplace consolidates these services into a unified platform where travelers can plan complete trips while suppliers manage their offerings from a centralized system.

The marketplace integrates with:

- Commerce Engine
- Vendor Engine
- Membership Engine
- Financial Engine
- Rewards Engine
- AI Engine
- CRM Engine
- Calendar Engine
- Notification Engine
- Document Management
- Identity Engine

# Business Rules

- Suppliers are responsible for maintaining accurate availability and pricing.
- Travelers must provide accurate booking information.
- Cancellation policies are determined by individual suppliers.
- Refunds follow supplier agreements and applicable laws.
- Financial transactions are recorded by the Financial Engine.
- Rewards are governed by the Rewards Engine.
- Travel documentation remains the responsibility of the traveler unless visa assistance services are purchased.
