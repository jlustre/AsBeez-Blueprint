# Insurance Marketplace

## Purpose

The **Insurance Marketplace** enables insurance carriers, managing general agencies (MGAs), brokers, independent agents, financial advisors, employers, affinity organizations, and consumers to discover, compare, quote, purchase, renew, and manage insurance products through the AsBeez ecosystem.

The marketplace provides a secure, AI-powered platform that streamlines the entire insurance lifecycle—from lead generation and needs analysis to policy issuance, servicing, claims assistance, renewals, and customer retention.

Unlike a traditional insurance comparison website, the AsBeez Insurance Marketplace is designed as a complete digital insurance ecosystem that supports multiple carriers, product lines, distribution channels, and regulatory requirements across different countries.

Insurance is one of the largest global financial industries. Consumers often struggle to compare products, understand coverage, and find trustworthy advisors.

The Insurance Marketplace solves these challenges by providing:

# Business Rules

- Insurance products may only be sold by appropriately licensed individuals or organizations where required.
- Carrier-specific underwriting rules always take precedence.
- Customer information must remain confidential.
- Applications must be complete before submission.
- Electronic signatures must comply with applicable laws.
- All financial transactions are recorded by the Financial Engine.
- Customer recommendations should be based on documented needs analysis and applicable suitability requirements.
- Rewards and promotional programs must comply with all applicable insurance regulations.
