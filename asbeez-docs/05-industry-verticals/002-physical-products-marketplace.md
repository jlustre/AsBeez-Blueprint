# Physical Products Marketplace

## Purpose

The **Physical Products Marketplace** enables businesses, merchants, manufacturers, wholesalers, distributors, retailers, and individual sellers to buy and sell physical goods through the AsBeez ecosystem. It provides a secure, AI-powered, and scalable commerce platform supporting product discovery, inventory management, order fulfillment, shipping, payments, rewards, and customer engagement.

The marketplace is designed to support both small independent merchants and large enterprise sellers while providing customers with a seamless shopping experience across multiple industries.

---

# Overview

The Physical Products Marketplace is one of the primary industry verticals of AsBeez.

Unlike digital products, physical products require inventory management, warehousing, shipping, delivery confirmation, returns processing, and logistics coordination.

The platform integrates with multiple internal engines including:

- Commerce Engine
- Vendor Engine
- Membership Engine
- Financial Engine
- Rewards Engine
- Logistics Services
- Notification Engine
- AI Engine

Together these systems create a complete end-to-end commerce solution.

---

# Objectives

The Physical Products Marketplace is designed to:

- Enable businesses to sell products globally.

# Order Lifecycle

Every physical product order follows a defined workflow.

```text
Order Placed
      │
      ▼
Payment Confirmed
      │
      ▼
Inventory Reserved
      │
      ▼
Processing
      │
      ▼
Packed
      │
      ▼
Shipped
      │
      ▼
In Transit
      │
      ▼
Delivered
      │
      ▼
Completed
```
