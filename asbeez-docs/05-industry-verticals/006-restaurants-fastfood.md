# Restaurants & Fast Food Marketplace

## Purpose

The **Restaurants & Fast Food Marketplace** enables restaurants, cafés, food trucks, bakeries, cloud kitchens, catering companies, grocery vendors, and food entrepreneurs to market, sell, deliver, and manage food and beverage services through the AsBeez ecosystem.

The marketplace provides a complete digital commerce solution supporting dine-in reservations, takeout, delivery, catering, subscriptions, loyalty programs, AI-powered recommendations, and restaurant operations management.

---

# Overview

The Restaurants & Fast Food Marketplace serves as the centralized platform for food-related businesses within AsBeez. It enables customers to easily discover nearby restaurants, browse menus, place orders, make reservations, schedule deliveries, and earn rewards.

For restaurant owners, it provides a comprehensive platform to manage menus, inventory, pricing, promotions, orders, reservations, customer engagement, and business analytics.

The marketplace integrates with:

- Commerce Engine
- Vendor Engine
- Membership Engine
- Financial Engine
- Rewards Engine
- AI Engine
- CRM Engine
- Notification Engine
- Calendar & Reservation Engine
- Delivery & Logistics Engine

---

The marketplace aims to:
