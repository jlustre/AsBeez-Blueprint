# Reward Points (RP)

## Introduction

**Reward Points (RP)** are the foundational unit of value within the AsBeez Rewards & Loyalty Engine. Every qualified transaction within the AsBeez ecosystem has the potential to generate Reward Points, making RP the entry point into the platform's long-term value creation model.

Unlike conventional loyalty points that are primarily redeemed for discounts or free merchandise, Reward Points in AsBeez serve a much greater purpose. They are the building blocks that enable Members to create **AsBeez Business Cells (ABC)**, which subsequently participate in the Beehive Matrix and generate **AsBeez Hive Credits (AHC)**.

In essence, RP transforms customer loyalty into digital business ownership.

---

# Purpose

The Reward Points system is designed to:

- Reward marketplace participation.
- Encourage repeat purchases.
- Increase customer retention.
- Incentivize vendor promotions.
- Support referral campaigns.
- Enable ABC generation.
- Measure customer engagement.
- Fuel loyalty programs.
- Promote ecosystem growth.
- Create long-term member value.

---

# Vision

Reward every meaningful marketplace interaction with a transparent, measurable, and sustainable digital asset that encourages continued participation in the AsBeez ecosystem.

---

# Core Philosophy

Reward Points are **earned**, not purchased.

They are generated through legitimate marketplace activities and represent the customer's contribution to the growth of the ecosystem.

Reward Points should never become speculative financial instruments.

Instead, they serve as a measurable record of customer engagement and a gateway to creating digital business assets.

# Reward Status

Every Reward Point balance may exist in one of several states.

## Pending

Waiting for:

- payment verification
- return period expiration
- fraud checks

---

## Available

Eligible for:

- accumulation
- ABC qualification
- reporting

---

## Reserved

Temporarily locked due to:

- disputes
- investigations
- pending conversion

---

## Converted

Already used to generate an ABC.

Historical records remain preserved.

---

## Expired

Only applicable if expiration policies are enabled.

Default recommendation:

Reward Points do **not** expire.

---

## Reversed

Removed due to:

- refunds
- fraud
- administrative correction
