# AsBeez Hive Credits (AHC)

## Introduction

**AsBeez Hive Credits (AHC)** are the primary digital value units distributed throughout the AsBeez ecosystem after the successful creation and placement of an **AsBeez Business Cell (ABC)** within the **Beehive Matrix**.

AHC represents the measurable value earned by qualified Business Cells as new Business Cells are added beneath them through legitimate marketplace activity.

Unlike **Reward Points (RP)**, which are earned directly through commerce and converted into Business Cells, AHC represents the long-term value generated by Business Cell participation within the Beehive Matrix.

AHC serves as the foundation of the Member's digital business portfolio and eventually becomes available within the Wallet System according to platform policies.

# Summary

The AsBeez Hive Credits (AHC) system is the economic foundation of the AsBeez Rewards & Loyalty Engine, converting Business Cell participation into measurable digital value through transparent, commerce-driven reward distribution. By separating marketplace-generated Reward Points from long-term Hive Credit earnings, the platform creates a sustainable model where customer loyalty evolves into digital business ownership. Supported by immutable ledgers, AI-assisted fraud detection, configurable financial policies, global compliance, and seamless integration with the Wallet and Financial Engines, AHC provides the long-term value mechanism that powers the entire AsBeez ecosystem.
