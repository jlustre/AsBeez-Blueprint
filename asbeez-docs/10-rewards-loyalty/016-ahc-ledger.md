# AHC Ledger

## Introduction

The **AsBeez Hive Credit (AHC) Ledger** is the official financial ledger that records every movement of **AsBeez Hive Credits (AHC)** throughout the AsBeez ecosystem. It serves as the authoritative source of truth for all Hive Credit transactions, including earnings, adjustments, transfers, reservations, releases, settlements, withdrawals, reversals, and administrative actions.

The AHC Ledger is an **immutable, append-only financial ledger** designed to ensure complete transparency, auditability, compliance, and financial integrity.

Unlike the **ABC Ledger**, which records Business Cell lifecycle events, the **AHC Ledger** records the financial activities generated by those Business Cells.

---

# Purpose

The AHC Ledger exists to:

- Record every Hive Credit transaction.
- Preserve financial integrity.
- Support accounting reconciliation.
- Maintain immutable audit history.
- Enable financial forecasting.
- Support withdrawals.
- Track outstanding liabilities.
- Prevent fraud.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Serve as the single source of truth for AHC movements.

---

# Vision

To provide a globally scalable, immutable financial ledger capable of accurately recording billions of Hive Credit transactions while maintaining complete transparency, auditability, and trust.

---

# Core Principles

The AHC Ledger follows several guiding principles.

---

## Immutability

Ledger entries are never modified.

Corrections are performed using adjustment entries.

---

## Append-Only

Every financial event creates a new ledger record.

Historical records remain untouched.

---

## Double-Entry Ready

Although internally implemented as an operational ledger, the AHC Ledger should be compatible with future double-entry accounting integration.

---

## Deterministic

The current balance can always be reconstructed from ledger entries.

---

## Fully Auditable

Every transaction contains:

- source
- destination
- reason
- timestamp
- approval information

# Transaction Types

The ledger supports multiple transaction categories.

---

```text

↓

↓
```

---

## Reserved

Temporarily unavailable due to:

- fraud review
- compliance review
- disputes
- settlement processing

---

## Released

Reserved Hive Credits become available.

---

## Withdrawn

Hive Credits converted into monetary settlement.

---

## Reversed

Previous transaction reversed.

Requires approval.

---

## Adjusted

Administrative correction.

Requires complete audit information.

---

## Expired

Not recommended.

Only supported if required by future regulations.

---

## Promotional Bonus

Additional Hive Credits awarded through campaigns.

---

## Migration

Credits migrated during system upgrades or country-specific migrations.

# Balance Calculation

The current balance is derived entirely from ledger entries.

Example:

```text
Credits

10,000

-

Debits

2,500

=

Current Balance

7,500
```

Balances should never be stored as the sole source of truth.

---

# Available Balance

Available balance excludes:

- reserved credits
- pending settlements
- disputed transactions

Example:

```text
Current Balance

10,000

Reserved

500

Pending

300

↓

Available

9,200
```

---

# Reserved Balance

# Summary

The AHC Ledger is the immutable financial backbone of the AsBeez Rewards & Loyalty Engine, recording every Hive Credit movement from initial distribution through settlement and withdrawal. By maintaining append-only transaction history, deterministic balance reconstruction, comprehensive audit trails, AI-assisted fraud detection, and seamless integration with the Wallet, Financial, Analytics, and Compliance Engines, the AHC Ledger ensures complete transparency, financial accountability, and long-term scalability for the entire AsBeez ecosystem.
