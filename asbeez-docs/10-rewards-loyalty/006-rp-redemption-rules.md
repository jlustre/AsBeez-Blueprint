# RP Redemption Rules

## Introduction

The **Reward Point (RP) Redemption Rules** define how accumulated Reward Points may be used throughout the AsBeez ecosystem. These rules govern the eligibility, validation, authorization, and processing of every redemption event while ensuring financial integrity and long-term sustainability.

Unlike traditional loyalty systems where points are immediately redeemed for discounts or merchandise, the AsBeez platform treats Reward Points as strategic digital assets. Their primary purpose is the creation of **AsBeez Business Cells (ABC)**, although additional redemption options may be introduced as the ecosystem evolves.

Every redemption follows standardized validation rules, immutable ledger recording, AI-assisted fraud detection, and comprehensive audit logging.

---

The RP Redemption Rules exist to:

# Redemption Philosophy

Reward Points represent accumulated customer participation.

They should primarily be used to create long-term value rather than short-term consumption.

Current priority:

```text

↓

↓

Hive Credits (AHC)

↓

Long-Term Value
```

Future redemption channels may supplement—but should not diminish—this primary objective.

# Eligibility Requirements

## Available Balance

Only Available RP may be redeemed.

Pending RP cannot be redeemed.

Reserved RP cannot be redeemed.

- AML

# Redemption Expiration

Default recommendation:

Reward Points do **not** expire.

If expiration is enabled in specific jurisdictions:

- oldest RP redeemed first (FIFO)
- members receive advance notifications
- expired RP remains in historical ledgers

---

# FIFO vs LIFO

Recommended policy:

```text
FIFO

↓

First Redeemed
```

Advantages:

- easier auditing

# Summary

The RP Redemption Rules govern the secure, transparent, and sustainable use of Reward Points throughout the AsBeez ecosystem. By prioritizing the conversion of RP into AsBeez Business Cells (ABC) while supporting future redemption channels, the framework ensures that every redemption is validated, audited, financially accountable, and aligned with the platform's long-term vision. Through configurable policies, immutable ledgers, AI-assisted fraud detection, and seamless integration with every major AsBeez engine, the redemption process transforms accumulated customer loyalty into lasting digital value.
