# Overview

Welcome to the AsBeez Enterprise Documentation System (AEDS).

AEDS serves as the official source of truth for every aspect of AsBeez, including its vision, business model, legal framework, product requirements, compensation system, software architecture, operations, marketing, and investor relations.

Every software feature, business decision, legal agreement, operational process, and strategic initiative should trace back to the documentation contained within this repository.
