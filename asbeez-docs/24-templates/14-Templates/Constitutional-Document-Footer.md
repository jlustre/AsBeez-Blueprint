# The Founder's Challenge

Before making any decision related to this subject, ask yourself:

- Does this strengthen the customer experience?
- Does this help vendors succeed?
- Does this create long-term value?
- Does this strengthen the Hive?
- Does this align with the AsBeez Constitution?
- Would I still make this decision if AsBeez existed 50 years from now?

If the answer to any question is **No**, continue improving the idea.

# Questions for Future Leaders

This document is intended to guide future leaders of AsBeez.

As the company evolves, periodically ask:

- Is this philosophy still being honored?
- Has technology distracted us from our purpose?
- Are we still serving people first?
- Are we strengthening the Hive?
- What would the Founder have wanted us to protect?
