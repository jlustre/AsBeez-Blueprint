# Vendor Dashboard

## Introduction

The **Vendor Dashboard** is the centralized operational workspace for Vendors within the AsBeez Marketplace. It provides a real-time, customizable, AI-assisted command center where Vendors can monitor business performance, manage daily operations, track customer activity, oversee inventory, review financial performance, respond to customer needs, and receive intelligent business recommendations.

Unlike a traditional administrative dashboard, the AsBeez Vendor Dashboard is designed as a **Business Control Center** that surfaces the most important information, highlights opportunities, identifies risks, and recommends actions based on real-time marketplace activity.

The dashboard integrates seamlessly with every major engine within the AsBeez ecosystem including Products, Orders, Inventory, Customers, Payments, Rewards, CRM, Marketing, AI, Analytics, Marketplace, Reputation, Notifications, and Membership.

# Dashboard Philosophy

A Vendor should immediately understand the health of their business within seconds of opening the dashboard.

The dashboard should answer questions such as:

- What requires my attention?
- What changed today?
- What opportunities exist?
- What problems need immediate action?
- What recommendations does AI have?

The dashboard should prioritize action over information overload.

# Summary

The Vendor Dashboard serves as the intelligent operational command center of the AsBeez Vendor Engine by consolidating real-time business performance, operational workflows, customer engagement, financial metrics, inventory management, marketing activities, reputation monitoring, and AI-powered recommendations into a single, highly configurable workspace. Through seamless integration with every major AsBeez engine, it enables Vendors to make informed decisions, respond quickly to changing business conditions, improve operational efficiency, and successfully manage every aspect of their marketplace business from one centralized interface.
