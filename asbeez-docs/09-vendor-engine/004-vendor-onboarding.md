# Vendor Onboarding

## Introduction

The **Vendor Onboarding** process defines how an individual, business, or organization becomes an active seller within the AsBeez Marketplace. It is designed to be simple for new vendors while remaining robust enough to support enterprise-grade verification, compliance, and operational readiness.

Unlike many marketplace platforms that stop after collecting registration information, the AsBeez Vendor Onboarding process is a guided business setup experience. The platform assists vendors from initial registration through verification, storefront configuration, payment setup, product publication, and first sale.

Artificial Intelligence plays an important role throughout onboarding by reducing friction, recommending best practices, identifying missing information, and helping vendors launch successfully.

---

---

# Step 1 — Become a Vendor

A registered Member or eligible Customer chooses to become a Vendor.

Entry points may include:

- Vendor Landing Page
- Marketplace
- Dashboard
- Promotional Campaigns
- AI Recommendations

The platform explains:

- Benefits
- Responsibilities
- Policies
- Fees (if applicable)

---

# Step 2 — Eligibility Validation

Before onboarding begins, the system verifies eligibility.

Checks may include:

- Active account
- Email verification
- Phone verification
- Membership status (if required)
- Country availability
- Marketplace restrictions

If requirements are not met, the system guides the applicant toward resolution.

---

# Step 3 — Vendor Profile

Basic information is collected.

Examples:

- Business name
- Owner name
- Display name
- Business category
- Business description
- Contact email
- Phone number
- Website
- Social media links

AI may suggest business descriptions and category selections.

---

# Step 4 — Business Classification

The Vendor selects the appropriate business type.

Examples:

- Individual
- Professional
- Small Business
- Enterprise
- Manufacturer
- Distributor
- Digital Creator
- Educational
- Non-Profit

This classification influences later workflows and available features.

---

# Step 5 — Business Verification

Verification builds trust within the marketplace.

Depending on country and vendor type, verification may include:

- Email verification
- Phone verification
- Government-issued identification
- Business registration
- Tax identification
- Banking verification
- Address verification

Verification requirements should be configurable by jurisdiction.

---

# Step 6 — Storefront Creation

The Vendor creates a public storefront.

Required information may include:

- Store name
- Store URL
- Logo
- Banner image
- Store description
- Business hours
- Contact methods

Example:

```
https://asbeez.com/store/green-garden
```

AI may evaluate branding consistency and completeness.

---

# Step 7 — Payment Configuration

The Vendor configures payment methods.

Supported options may include:

- Bank account
- ACH
- Wire transfer
- PayPal
- Stripe
- Local payment providers
- Digital wallets

Future integrations should remain modular.

---

# Step 8 — Shipping Configuration

For physical products, Vendors configure:

- Shipping zones
- Shipping providers
- Delivery methods
- Pickup options
- Estimated delivery times
- Shipping rates

Digital-only vendors may skip this step.

---

# Step 9 — Tax Configuration

Tax configuration depends on country.

Examples:

- Sales Tax
- VAT
- GST
- Provincial taxes
- Local taxes

The Vendor Engine should automatically recommend applicable tax settings.

---

# Step 10 — Product Creation

The Vendor publishes initial products or services.

Supported offerings include:

- Physical products
- Digital products
- Services
- Appointments
- Memberships
- Subscriptions

AI assists with:

- Product titles
- Descriptions
- SEO keywords
- Category selection
- Suggested pricing
- Image recommendations

---

# Step 11 — Store Review

Before launch, the platform performs an automated readiness review.

The review checks:

- Missing information
- Product completeness
- Branding consistency
- Policy compliance
- Verification status
- Tax configuration
- Payment configuration

AI assigns a Store Readiness Score.

Example:

| Category | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Profile | ✓ |
| Verification | ✓ |
| Products | ✓ |
| Shipping | ✓ |
| Payments | ✓ |
| Branding | 90% |

---

# Step 12 — Marketplace Launch

Once approved, the storefront becomes publicly available.

Capabilities become active:

- Search indexing
- Product discovery
- Customer reviews
- Order processing
- Rewards generation
- Analytics

The Vendor officially joins the marketplace.

---

# Step 13 — First Sale Experience

The first successful order triggers onboarding completion.

The system may:

- Congratulate the Vendor.
- Unlock advanced tutorials.
- Provide business insights.
- Recommend growth opportunities.
- Introduce advanced analytics.

This milestone marks the beginning of continuous business growth.

# Summary

The Vendor Onboarding process provides a structured, AI-assisted pathway for transforming prospective sellers into successful marketplace vendors. By combining guided registration, configurable verification, intelligent business setup, storefront creation, payment and tax configuration, and launch readiness assessments, the onboarding experience ensures that vendors enter the AsBeez Marketplace fully prepared to deliver exceptional customer experiences. The process establishes the foundation for long-term business growth while maintaining marketplace quality, trust, compliance, and global scalability.
