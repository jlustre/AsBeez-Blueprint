# Vendor Reviews & Ratings

## Introduction

The **Vendor Reviews & Ratings** module enables customers to provide transparent feedback about Vendors, products, services, fulfillment, and overall purchasing experiences within the AsBeez Marketplace.

Customer reviews are among the most influential factors in purchasing decisions. This module is designed to build trust, encourage accountability, reward excellent service, and provide Vendors with actionable insights for continuous improvement.

Unlike a simple star-rating system, the AsBeez Reviews & Ratings module incorporates AI-assisted moderation, fraud detection, sentiment analysis, customer verification, reputation scoring, and business intelligence to maintain a fair and trustworthy marketplace.

The module integrates closely with the Customer Management, Orders, Products, CRM, Reputation, AI, Analytics, Marketplace, Rewards, and Notification Engines.
