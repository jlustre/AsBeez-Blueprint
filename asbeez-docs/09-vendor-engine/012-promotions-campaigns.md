# Promotions & Campaigns

## Introduction

The **Promotions & Campaigns** module provides Vendors with powerful marketing capabilities to increase visibility, attract customers, improve conversion rates, encourage repeat purchases, and grow long-term revenue within the AsBeez Marketplace.

While the **Pricing & Discounts** module determines how products are priced, the Promotions & Campaigns module determines **when**, **where**, **to whom**, and **under what conditions** promotional offers are presented.

This module supports simple discount campaigns as well as sophisticated multi-stage marketing initiatives driven by customer behavior, seasonal events, AI recommendations, loyalty programs, and marketplace-wide promotions.

The Promotions & Campaigns module integrates deeply with the Product, Pricing, Order, Rewards, CRM, AI, Analytics, Marketplace, Financial, and Notification Engines.

The Promotions & Campaigns module provides the marketing intelligence layer of the AsBeez Vendor Engine by enabling configurable promotions, personalized campaigns, customer segmentation, coupon management, automated marketing workflows, AI-assisted optimization, and comprehensive campaign analytics. Through deep integration with the Product, Pricing, Order, Financial, Rewards, CRM, Marketplace, AI, Analytics, and Notification Engines, it empowers Vendors to increase visibility, strengthen customer relationships, maximize revenue, and build sustainable long-term business growth throughout the AsBeez Marketplace.
