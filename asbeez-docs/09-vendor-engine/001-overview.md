# Vendor Overview

## Introduction

The **Vendor Engine** is one of the core business engines of the AsBeez ecosystem. It enables individuals, entrepreneurs, businesses, organizations, and enterprises to establish digital storefronts, sell products and services, manage customers, and grow sustainable businesses within the AsBeez Marketplace.

Unlike conventional e-commerce platforms, the Vendor Engine is deeply integrated with every major AsBeez engine. Every vendor transaction contributes not only to commerce but also to the Membership, Rewards, Financial, CRM, AI, Analytics, Notification, and Partner ecosystems.

The Vendor Engine is designed around the philosophy that **vendor success drives ecosystem growth**. As vendors prosper, customers enjoy a broader selection of quality products and services, Reward Point (RP) generation increases, AsBeez Business Cells (ABC) expand, AsBeez Hive Credits (AHC) circulate, and the overall value of the platform compounds.

The Vendor Engine is designed to scale from a single individual selling digital downloads to multinational organizations operating thousands of products across multiple countries.
