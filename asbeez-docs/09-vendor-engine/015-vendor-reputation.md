# Vendor Reputation

## Introduction

The **Vendor Reputation** module measures, manages, and continuously improves the overall trustworthiness, reliability, and quality of every Vendor operating within the AsBeez Marketplace.

While the **Reviews & Ratings** module captures customer feedback, the Vendor Reputation module transforms hundreds of operational metrics into a comprehensive reputation profile that reflects a Vendor's long-term performance and marketplace standing.

The module evaluates much more than star ratings. It analyzes fulfillment performance, customer satisfaction, response times, refund history, dispute resolution, policy compliance, product quality, service consistency, and AI-generated behavioral insights to produce an accurate and dynamic reputation score.

The Vendor Reputation module integrates deeply with Reviews, Orders, Shipping, Payments, Customer Management, CRM, Analytics, AI, Financial, Membership, Marketplace, and Notification Engines.

Possible classifications:

|--------|----------------|
| 900–1000 | Elite Vendor |
| 800–899 | Excellent |
| 700–799 | Trusted |
| 600–699 | Established |
| Below 600 | Needs Improvement |

The scoring model remains configurable.
