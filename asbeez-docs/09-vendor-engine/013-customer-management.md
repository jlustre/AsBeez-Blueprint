# Customer Management

## Introduction

The **Customer Management** module is the relationship hub of the AsBeez Vendor Engine. It provides Vendors with a centralized, intelligent, and comprehensive view of every customer throughout their entire lifecycle—from their first visit to long-term loyalty and advocacy.

Unlike a traditional customer database, the AsBeez Customer Management module continuously captures customer interactions, purchasing behavior, communication history, preferences, loyalty activities, support cases, referrals, and engagement metrics to help Vendors build meaningful, long-lasting relationships.

The module integrates seamlessly with CRM, Orders, Products, Rewards, Financial, Promotions, Analytics, AI, Notifications, Membership, and Marketplace Engines to deliver a complete 360-degree customer profile.
