# Vendor Research

## Purpose

Vendor research evaluates seller needs, product supply, onboarding friction, operational capability, economics, support burden, retention, and readiness for marketplace participation.

## Questions

What acquisition channels and tools do vendors use? What fees, settlement timing, tax support, analytics, promotions, fulfillment, customer service, privacy, and payout capabilities do they need? What prevents onboarding or repeat participation? Which products/services create compliance, support, fraud, or operational risk?

## Methods

Use interviews, applications, onboarding observation, product tests, vendor-support analysis, cohort/retention analysis, pilot marketplace data, and competitive comparison. Protect vendor/customer information and record country/entity context.

## Financial Interpretation

Vendor sales, gross order value, vendor entitlement, platform fees, tax, reserves, settlement, payout, and AsBeez recognized revenue are separate. Vendor interest or forecast sales do not prove revenue or liquidity.

## Deliverables

Maintain vendor personas, journey/friction map, qualification criteria, pilot findings, product requirements, support/runbooks, risk register, compliance actions, unit economics, and decision links.
