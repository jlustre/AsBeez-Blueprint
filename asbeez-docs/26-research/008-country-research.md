# Country Research

## Purpose

Country research evaluates launch readiness, market demand, legal entity, tax, payments, banking, currency, privacy, data residency, compliance, vendor supply, support, language, operations, and recovery for each country.

## Country Profile

Record target segment, market evidence, entity/registration, accounting calendar, currency/FX, payment/payout providers, banking, tax/nexus, KYC/KYB/AML/sanctions, consumer rules, privacy/residency, rewards/direct-selling classification, vendor requirements, support, incident/DR, reporting, and owner.

## Launch Gate

No country launches on market size alone. Require dated legal/tax/compliance approval, provider capability, country configuration, chart/account mapping, liquidity/reserve plan, support/runbooks, security/privacy, testing, reconciliation/close, monitoring, and rollback/pause plan.

## Historical Integrity

Country assignment and migration are platform rules, not proof of legal/tax residence. Historical records retain original country, currency, policy, and evidence; future activity follows approved effective policy.
