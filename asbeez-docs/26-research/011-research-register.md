# Research Register

## Purpose

The research register is the canonical index of research questions, studies, evidence, findings, assumptions, decisions, and review obligations.

## Required Fields

Research ID, title, domain, type, question, owner, decision owner, status, priority, country/entity, audience, method, source/evidence links, start/completion date, assumptions, limitations, confidence, findings, recommendation, affected documents, ADR/business-rule/product link, legal/privacy classification, review date, and supersession.

## Statuses

Proposed, approved, in progress, evidence collected, analysis complete, decision pending, accepted, rejected, superseded, archived, or requires revalidation.

## Governance

No material strategy, product, country, architecture, financial, legal, or AI decision relies on uncited research. Findings that alter policy or architecture link to the approved decision/rule and preserve the original study. Conflicting findings remain visible until resolved by the responsible authority.
