# Research Roadmap

## Near Term

- establish the research register, methodology, privacy/ethics review, source standards, and decision-linkage process;
- validate initial customer/vendor problems, marketplace supply/demand, pricing, acquisition, retention, support, and accessibility; and
- complete priority country legal/tax/payment/provider and financial-model assumptions.

## Medium Term

- research provider economics, treasury/liquidity, rewards liability, fraud/loss, operational capacity, AI use cases, architecture alternatives, and scalability;
- run pilots and experiments with measurable hypotheses, safe test data, and documented go/no-go gates; and
- refresh research after product, provider, legal, country, or market changes.

## Long Term

- maintain continuous market, customer, vendor, country, technology, AI, financial, risk, and regulatory intelligence;
- connect evidence to strategy, roadmap, ADRs, business rules, product requirements, and investment materials; and
- preserve reproducible, privacy-safe, auditable research history.

## Roadmap Gate

Research cannot be used to present estimates as facts, bypass legal/financial approval, expose sensitive data, or justify an untested financial or customer-impacting change.
