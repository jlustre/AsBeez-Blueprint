## Authority

Research informs decisions but does not replace legal advice, accounting policy, approved business rules, architecture decisions, financial reports, signed agreements, or product acceptance. Material claims identify evidence, date, methodology, assumptions, limitations, confidence, and owner.

## Research Lifecycle

Question -> scope/ethics -> method -> evidence -> analysis -> findings/confidence -> recommendation -> decision/approval -> implementation measure -> revalidation or supersession.

## Global Rules

Protect participants and sensitive data; distinguish facts from estimates and opinions; cite primary sources where possible; preserve conflicting evidence; avoid unsupported income/investment/financial claims; link findings to affected documents and decisions; and revalidate research after material market, legal, country, provider, product, architecture, or policy changes.
