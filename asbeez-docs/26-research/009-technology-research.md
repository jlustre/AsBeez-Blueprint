# Technology Research

## Purpose

Technology research reduces uncertainty in architecture, frameworks, databases, APIs, events, providers, security, AI, performance, operations, and recovery.

## Required Evaluation

Record problem, alternatives, constraints, context ownership, security/privacy, data residency, performance, scale, cost, reliability, migration, vendor lock-in, observability, testing, recovery, legal/compliance, and exit criteria.

## Financial Safety

Technology choices must preserve immutable balanced journals, bounded-context ownership, idempotency, concurrency, reconciliation, audit, country/entity scope, and provider evidence. A faster or cheaper option is not acceptable if it weakens correctness or control.

## Evidence

Use prototypes, benchmarks, threat models, failure tests, contract tests, operational exercises, dependency reviews, and dated sources. Record assumptions, limitations, results, recommendation, rejected alternatives, decision owner, and ADR linkage.
