# Customer Research

## Purpose

Customer research identifies customer problems, behaviors, trust needs, purchase decisions, support expectations, accessibility needs, and reasons for adoption or abandonment.

## Methods

Use interviews, usability tests, surveys, support analysis, funnel/cohort analysis, diary studies, product experiments, accessibility testing, and observed behavior. Record recruitment, consent, sample, screening, incentives, country/language, method, date, and limitations.

## Questions

What problem is the customer solving? How do they discover and evaluate products? What creates trust? What prevents checkout? How are payment, tax, delivery, refund, dispute, privacy, and support expectations formed? What does loyalty mean without implying income or investment?

## Interpretation

Do not generalize a small or biased sample. Separate stated preference from observed behavior, usability issue from market demand, and customer benefit from financial outcome. Sensitive data is minimized and protected.

## Deliverables

Produce participant-safe findings, journey maps, usability issues, prioritized hypotheses, acceptance criteria, accessibility actions, support implications, and links to product/UI/API decisions.
