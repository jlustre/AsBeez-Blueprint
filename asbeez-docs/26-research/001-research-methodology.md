# Research Methodology

## Purpose

This document defines how AsBeez research is proposed, conducted, evaluated, recorded, linked to decisions, and retired.

## Research Types

Use exploratory research to understand problems; descriptive research to measure users, markets, and behavior; evaluative research to test products and workflows; comparative research to assess alternatives; legal/regulatory research to identify obligations; financial research to test economics; and technical research to reduce architecture and delivery uncertainty.

## Required Research Record

Every study identifies research ID, question, decision owner, domain, scope, geography/country, audience, date, source, method, sample or evidence basis, assumptions, limitations, findings, confidence, conflicts, recommendation, decision linkage, owner, review date, and status.

## Evidence Quality

Separate primary evidence, secondary research, expert interpretation, estimate, forecast, opinion, and unresolved assumption. Record source date and access date. Do not present a small sample, vendor claim, model output, or AI summary as validated market truth without qualification.

## Ethics and Privacy

Use lawful purpose, informed consent where required, minimization, anonymization, secure storage, access control, retention, and participant protection. Do not collect unnecessary payment, identity, health, financial, or sensitive behavioral data.

## Decision Linkage

Material findings link to an architecture decision, business rule, product requirement, risk register, legal review, financial model, roadmap, or explicit unresolved question. Superseded findings remain traceable.
