# AI Research

## Purpose

AI research evaluates models, providers, prompts, retrieval, agents, automation, fraud/risk, reporting, forecasting, cost, safety, and human-workflow impact.

## Evaluation Dimensions

Accuracy, calibration, relevance, citation, uncertainty, fairness, drift, robustness, prompt-injection resistance, privacy, leakage, security, latency, cost, availability, data residency, provider terms, observability, human override, appeal, and incident recovery.

## Authority Boundary

AI research may recommend capabilities but cannot authorize direct posting, approval, payment, balance change, release/freeze, certification, close, or final legal/compliance decisions. Financial, privacy, security, and country owners approve production use.

## Evidence

Record use case, risk tier, model/provider/prompt version, data scope, evaluation set, limitations, human review, results, failure examples, monitoring, rollback, retention, and decision/approval. Treat provider and model claims as claims until validated.
