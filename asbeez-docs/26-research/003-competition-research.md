# Competition Research

## Purpose

Competition research compares marketplaces, loyalty programs, vendor platforms, community-commerce models, payment experiences, analytics tools, and relevant financial/platform services.

## Comparison Dimensions

Compare customer and vendor proposition, target segment, product/category, geographic scope, pricing/fees, payments, tax, fulfillment, loyalty/rewards, referral claims, vendor tools, trust/support, privacy/security, technology, integrations, unit economics, funding, traction evidence, legal posture, and differentiation.

## Evidence Rules

Distinguish observed behavior, public claim, customer/vendor feedback, analyst interpretation, estimate, and unverified assertion. Record source, date, country, version, limitations, and confidence. Do not copy protected content or treat competitor marketing as verified fact.

## Strategic Use

Use competition research to identify customer/vendor gaps, capability priorities, pricing hypotheses, provider choices, compliance risks, and differentiation tests. Do not assume a competitor's legal or financial treatment applies to AsBeez.

## Outputs

Maintain comparison matrix, evidence links, unresolved questions, risks, opportunity hypotheses, experiment plan, and decision references. Review after material market, provider, regulatory, or product changes.
