# Market Research

## Purpose

Market research evaluates the size, structure, growth, behavior, economics, regulation, and accessibility of markets relevant to AsBeez marketplace commerce and platform services.

## Required Questions

What customer/vendor problem exists? Who experiences it? How is it solved today? What alternatives and switching costs exist? What market segment is initially reachable? What evidence supports demand, willingness to pay, repeat behavior, and vendor supply? What country, tax, payment, privacy, and consumer constraints apply?

## Measures

Track addressable and serviceable segments, category demand, order frequency, average order value, conversion, repeat purchase, vendor density, acquisition cost, retention, refund/complaint rates, contribution economics, payment costs, and competitive alternatives. Definitions, periods, currency, source, and methodology are mandatory.

## AsBeez Interpretation

Market size is not revenue. GMV is not recognized revenue. Customer interest is not a committed order. Vendor applications are not activated supply. Forecasts are assumptions until validated by operating evidence.

## Deliverables

Maintain source register, segment definitions, competitor context, country assumptions, evidence quality, confidence, risks, open questions, and decision implications. Link findings to strategy, product requirements, launch gates, and financial model assumptions.
