# Legal and Regulatory Research

## Purpose

This research identifies legal, regulatory, tax, consumer, privacy, payments, marketplace, direct-selling, rewards, employment, AI, and country obligations relevant to AsBeez.

## Required Scope

Record jurisdiction, legal entity, product/capability, activity, parties, data, payment/money movement, reward classification, tax/nexus, licensing, disclosures, contracts, regulator/source, effective date, interpretation, confidence, counsel/owner, and open questions.

## Decision Rules

Research is not legal advice or approval. Launch, country, provider, reward, wallet, payout, promotion, AI, and data decisions require appropriate legal/compliance review and documented approval. When uncertainty is material, restrict, hold, stage, or block capability.

## Evidence

Prefer primary law/regulator guidance and dated counsel analysis; distinguish secondary commentary, provider claims, and assumptions. Retain source copies/links, access dates, jurisdiction, version, analysis, conclusion, exceptions, and review date.
