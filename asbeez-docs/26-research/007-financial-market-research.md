# Financial and Market Economics Research

## Purpose

This research tests market economics, pricing, payment costs, vendor economics, customer acquisition, retention, liquidity, reserves, tax, rewards liability, provider costs, and financial model assumptions.

## Required Distinctions

Separate GMV, orders, customer collections, recognized revenue, vendor/partner liabilities, fees, commissions, tax, refunds, chargebacks, rewards, reserves, cash, expenses, forecasts, and profit. State period, entity/country, currency/FX, accounting basis, source, methodology, and confidence.

## Research Questions

What drives conversion and repeat purchase? What is acquisition and service cost? What provider/tax/refund/dispute costs apply? What settlement and payout timing affects liquidity? What reward funding and maximum liability exist? Which assumptions are sensitive or unsupported?

## Controls

Reconcile actuals to certified reporting/ledger where available. Label estimates and scenarios. Do not use research to present a forecast as actual, promise return, or hide liabilities/reserves/risks. Finance, tax, treasury, legal, and audit review material conclusions.
