# Marketplace

## Introduction

The **Marketplace** is the commercial heart of the AsBeez ecosystem. It is where buyers discover products and services, vendors build and manage their digital storefronts, and every successful transaction contributes to the growth of the entire platform.

Unlike a traditional e-commerce platform, the AsBeez Marketplace is designed as an **AI-native, multi-vendor, multi-category, event-driven commerce ecosystem**. Every purchase, subscription, booking, or service engagement integrates seamlessly with the Rewards Engine, Membership Engine, Financial Engine, AI Engine, CRM, Analytics, and other core platform services.

The Marketplace supports a wide variety of business models—from digital downloads and physical goods to professional services, subscriptions, event tickets, real estate, automotive listings, affiliate products, and future marketplace innovations.

Its modular architecture allows new marketplace categories to be introduced without disrupting existing functionality, ensuring long-term scalability and adaptability.

The Marketplace exists to:
