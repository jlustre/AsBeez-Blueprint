# AI Capabilities

## Introduction

The **AI Capabilities** module defines how Artificial Intelligence is embedded throughout the AsBeez Marketplace. Unlike traditional marketplaces where AI is treated as an optional feature, AsBeez is designed as an **AI-Native Marketplace**, meaning Artificial Intelligence is integrated into every major business process to improve customer experience, vendor success, operational efficiency, security, and long-term marketplace growth.

Rather than existing as a single standalone component, AI functions as an intelligent layer that continuously observes marketplace activity, analyzes patterns, predicts outcomes, automates repetitive work, and assists customers, vendors, administrators, and partner systems.

The AI Engine operates across all Marketplace modules while respecting privacy, security, explainability, and human oversight.

# Best Practices

- Use AI to assist—not replace—human judgment.
- Keep AI recommendations transparent and explainable.
- Continuously monitor model quality.
- Respect customer privacy preferences.
- Provide vendors with control over AI-generated content.
- Audit AI decisions affecting financial or compliance outcomes.
- Retrain models regularly using high-quality data.
- Monitor for bias and unintended outcomes.
- Design AI services as modular, reusable components.
- Measure business impact alongside technical performance.
