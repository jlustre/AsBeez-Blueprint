## Drivers

Document customers, members, vendors, orders, conversion, repeat purchase, pricing, fees, payments, refunds, disputes, tax, rewards, reserves, payouts, staffing, infrastructure, providers, and countries.

## Statements and Cash

Separate GMV, collections, recognized revenue, vendor/partner liabilities, tax, fees, commissions, rewards, reserves, cash, expenses, profit, working capital, commitments, and liquidity. Identify recognition, settlement, and cash timing.

## Assumptions and Scenarios

Record assumption, source, date, owner, confidence, sensitivity, base/downside/upside/stress case, limitations, and trigger for reforecast.

## Controls

Reconcile actuals to approved reporting/ledger where available; label estimates; document methodology/exclusions; preserve formulas/version; review tax/legal/finance; and do not present forecasts as guarantees or investment returns.
