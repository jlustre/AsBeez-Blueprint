# Templates

## Purpose

This domain provides reusable templates for creating consistent AsBeez documentation, product artifacts, decisions, research, risks, architecture, APIs, business rules, assets, and financial models.

## Canonical Location

Templates are stored under [14-Templates](14-Templates/). Copy a template into its owning domain, then complete all required metadata, authority, links, evidence, approval, and revision fields.

## Template Rules

Templates are starting structures, not authority. Domain policies, legal requirements, accounting policy, General Ledger records, API/event contracts, approved ADRs, and business rules control behavior. Do not copy placeholder text into published material without replacing it with evidence or an explicit unresolved decision.
