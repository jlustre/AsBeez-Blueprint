# Partner Onboarding

## Introduction

The **Partner Onboarding** process is the structured journey that transforms a qualified organization into a fully operational partner within the AsBeez ecosystem. It ensures that every partner—regardless of size, industry, or geography—is introduced to the platform in a consistent, secure, compliant, and efficient manner.

Effective onboarding is more than simply creating an account. It includes legal agreements, identity verification, technical integrations, configuration, training, certifications, testing, and activation.

The goal is to provide partners with everything they need to begin delivering value while minimizing implementation time, reducing operational risk, and ensuring compliance with AsBeez standards.

---

# Objectives

The onboarding process aims to:

- Provide a consistent onboarding experience.
- Reduce partner activation time.
- Ensure legal and regulatory compliance.
- Verify organizational legitimacy.
- Configure partner environments.
- Prepare technical integrations.
- Train partner personnel.
- Establish communication channels.
- Build long-term relationships.
- Enable a successful launch.

---

Every onboarding process should be:

- Secure

# Phase 1 — Welcome

Once a partner has been approved, AsBeez initiates the onboarding process.

The Welcome phase includes:

- Welcome email
- Assigned Partner Manager
- Introduction package
- Partner Portal access instructions
- Implementation timeline
- Contact information
- Kickoff meeting invitation

Deliverables include:

- Welcome Kit
- Implementation Checklist
- Partner Handbook
- Documentation Links
- Support Contacts

---

# Phase 2 — Agreement Execution

Before platform access is granted, all required agreements must be completed.

Possible agreements include:

- Master Partnership Agreement
- Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
- Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Revenue Sharing Agreement
- API Usage Agreement
- Data Processing Agreement
- Compliance Agreement
- Branding Guidelines Acceptance
- Marketing Agreement

Electronic signatures should be supported whenever legally permitted.

---

# Phase 3 — Identity & Organization Verification

To protect the platform, AsBeez verifies both the organization and its authorized representatives.

## Organization Verification

Examples include:

- Business registration
- Corporate documents
- Tax identification
- Business licenses
- Insurance certificates
- Financial information

## Representative Verification

Examples include:

- Government-issued identification
- Email verification
- Phone verification
- Role confirmation
- Authorization letter

---

# Phase 4 — Risk & Compliance Review

Compliance requirements vary depending on partner type and jurisdiction.

Possible reviews include:

- KYB (Know Your Business)
- AML screening
- Sanctions screening
- PEP screening
- Credit assessment
- Industry certifications
- Regulatory compliance
- Data privacy review
- Security assessment

AI may assist by identifying incomplete or inconsistent information before manual review.

---

# Phase 5 — Partner Workspace Creation

Once verification is complete, AsBeez provisions the partner's digital workspace.

The workspace includes:

- Organization profile
- Dashboard
- Team management
- Document repository
- Notification preferences
- Analytics dashboard
- Support center
- Knowledge base
- Billing settings
- API management console

Each workspace is isolated to ensure tenant security and data privacy.

---

# Phase 6 — User & Role Provisioning

Partner administrators invite team members into the platform.

Typical roles include:

- Organization Owner
- Executive
- Partner Administrator
- Technical Lead
- Finance Manager
- Operations Manager
- Sales Manager
- Marketing Manager
- Support Representative
- API Developer
- Read-Only Auditor

Permissions are managed through Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).

---

Configuration options include:

- Multi-Factor Authentication
- Password policies
- IP restrictions

# Phase 8 — API & Integration Setup

Technical partners may integrate directly with the AsBeez platform.

Possible integrations include:

- REST APIs
- GraphQL
- OAuth
- API Keys
- Webhooks
- ERP systems
- CRM systems
- Accounting systems
- Payment gateways
- Shipping providers

The onboarding wizard should guide partners through each required integration step.

---

# Phase 9 — Sandbox Environment

Before production access is granted, partners receive a secure sandbox environment.

Sandbox capabilities include:

- Test APIs
- Sample transactions
- Webhook testing
- Error simulation
- Authentication testing
- SDK examples
- Mock services

Production credentials should only be issued after successful testing.

---

# Phase 10 — Training & Certification

Training ensures that partner personnel understand both the business and technical aspects of the platform.

Training modules may include:

- Platform Overview
- Partner Portal
- Security
- Compliance
- APIs
- Financial Processes
- Support Procedures
- Analytics
- Branding
- Best Practices

Certification levels may include:

- Certified Partner
- Silver
- Gold
- Platinum
- Enterprise

Certificates may have expiration dates requiring periodic renewal.

---

# Phase 11 — Readiness Assessment

Before activation, AsBeez verifies that the partner is prepared for production.

Checklist includes:

- Agreements completed
- Verification approved
- Integrations tested
- Training completed
- Certifications obtained
- Security review passed
- Support contacts assigned
- Financial setup complete

If any item fails validation, activation is delayed until resolved.

---

# Phase 12 — Production Activation

After successful validation, the partner becomes operational.

Activation tasks include:

- Enable production APIs
- Activate dashboards
- Enable reporting
- Start financial processing
- Enable notifications
- Activate monitoring
- Schedule first business review

The activation date is recorded for lifecycle reporting.

# Summary

The Partner Onboarding process establishes the operational foundation for every successful partnership within the AsBeez ecosystem. By combining structured workflows, configurable automation, compliance verification, technical provisioning, AI-assisted guidance, and comprehensive training, AsBeez enables organizations to become productive partners quickly while maintaining high standards of security, governance, and operational excellence. A well-designed onboarding experience not only accelerates activation but also lays the groundwork for long-term collaboration, mutual growth, and partner success.
