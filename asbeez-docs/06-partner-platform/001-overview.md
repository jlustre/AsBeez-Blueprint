# Partner Platform Overview

## Introduction

The **Partner Platform** is the business-to-business (B2B) collaboration framework of the AsBeez ecosystem. It enables external organizations to establish trusted relationships with AsBeez by providing standardized processes, governance, integrations, APIs, analytics, artificial intelligence, and operational tools.

While the **Marketplace Platform** enables commerce between buyers and sellers, and the **Vendor Platform** empowers merchants to sell products and services, the **Partner Platform** empowers organizations that contribute to the overall success, growth, and innovation of the AsBeez ecosystem.

Partners may provide technology, logistics, financial services, payment processing, education, consulting, marketing, legal services, insurance, government support, enterprise integrations, and countless other strategic capabilities.

Rather than treating every partnership as a custom business arrangement, AsBeez provides a unified platform where every partner follows consistent standards for onboarding, integration, collaboration, performance management, and continuous improvement.
