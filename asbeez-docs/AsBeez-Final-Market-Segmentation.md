# AsBeez Final Market Segmentation

**Decision date:** September 21, 2026  
**Status:** Recommended final platform taxonomy

## Final recommendation

AsBeez should use **seven top-level markets**:

1. Physical Products
2. Digital Products
3. Professional Services
4. Real Estate
5. Automotive
6. Insurance
7. Travel

The additional market should be **Automotive**. Automobile sales should not be placed under Physical Products because automotive has specialized listings, dealer onboarding, vehicle-identification data, financing, trade-ins, title requirements, lead workflows, inspections, and fee structures. It deserves its own market architecture.

Do not add more top-level markets at launch. New offerings should first be placed as categories or subcategories within these seven markets. A new top-level market should be created only when it requires a distinct transaction flow, partner type, regulatory framework, fee model, and customer experience.

## 1. Physical Products

**Purpose:** Tangible goods that can be purchased, shipped, delivered, or picked up.

### Recommended categories

- Electronics and Computers
- Home, Furniture, and Appliances
- Fashion, Shoes, and Accessories
- Beauty and Personal Care
- Health and Wellness Products
- Grocery and Specialty Food
- Baby, Kids, and Toys
- Sports and Outdoors
- Tools, Hardware, and Industrial Supplies
- Office and School Supplies
- Pet Supplies
- Arts, Crafts, and Handmade Goods
- Jewelry and Watches
- Books and Physical Media
- Collectibles
- Garden and Outdoor Living
- Local Products and Gifts
- Wholesale and Business Supplies

### Exclusions

- Downloadable items belong under Digital Products.
- Vehicles belong under Automotive.
- Property belongs under Real Estate.
- Bookable labor belongs under Professional Services.

## 2. Digital Products

**Purpose:** Products delivered electronically without physical fulfillment.

### Recommended categories

- E-books and Publications
- Online Courses and Training Materials
- Software and Applications
- Website Themes and Templates
- Graphics, Photos, Video, and Audio
- Documents, Forms, and Business Templates
- Music, Sound Effects, and Digital Media
- Memberships and Digital Subscriptions
- Licenses and Activation Keys
- Data, Research, and Reports
- AI Prompts and Automation Assets
- Printable Products
- Games and Digital Entertainment

### Classification rule

If the buyer receives a downloadable file, access right, license, subscription, or streamed content rather than a physical shipment, classify the offering as a Digital Product.

Live teaching, consulting, customization, or implementation remains a Professional Service even when delivered online.

## 3. Professional Services

**Purpose:** Work performed by an individual, professional, freelancer, contractor, agency, or local service business.

### Recommended category groups

#### Business and professional

- Accounting and Bookkeeping
- Legal Services
- Business Consulting
- Marketing and Advertising
- Web and Software Development
- Graphic and Creative Design
- Writing, Translation, and Administrative Support
- Human Resources and Recruiting
- IT Support and Cybersecurity

#### Home and local

- Cleaning
- Handyman and Repairs
- Construction and Renovation
- Plumbing, Electrical, and HVAC
- Landscaping and Gardening
- Moving and Delivery
- Automotive Repair and Maintenance
- Photography and Event Services

#### Personal and lifestyle

- Beauty and Wellness Services
- Fitness and Coaching
- Tutoring and Education
- Child, Senior, and Pet Care
- Personal Assistance
- Event Planning

### Classification rule

The market includes fixed-price projects, hourly work, appointments, retainers, milestones, recurring services, and qualified leads. Licensed professions must have additional credential and jurisdiction controls.

## 4. Real Estate

**Purpose:** Property listings, inquiries, professional connections, and related property services.

### Recommended categories

- Residential Property for Sale
- Commercial Property for Sale
- New Developments
- Land and Lots
- Residential Rentals
- Commercial Leasing
- Vacation and Investment Properties
- Real Estate Agents and Brokers
- Mortgage and Financing Connections
- Property Management
- Home Inspection
- Appraisal and Valuation
- Escrow, Title, and Closing Services
- Real Estate Legal Services

### Naming decision

Use **Real Estate**, not “Real Estate Sales.” The broader name accommodates rentals, leasing, property management, and related professionals without creating another top-level market.

### Launch boundary

Initially monetize through subscriptions, listings, advertising, and compliant qualified-lead fees. Do not charge a percentage of property value or closing-contingent fee unless AsBeez has an approved licensed arrangement in the applicable jurisdiction.

## 5. Automotive

**Purpose:** Vehicle sales, rentals, parts, and vehicle-related services.

### Recommended categories

- New Vehicles
- Used Vehicles
- Certified Pre-Owned Vehicles
- Private-Party Vehicles
- Motorcycles and Powersports
- Recreational Vehicles
- Commercial Vehicles
- Boats and Marine Vehicles
- Vehicle Parts and Accessories
- Vehicle Inspections and History Reports
- Auto Repair and Maintenance
- Detailing and Car Care
- Towing and Roadside Assistance
- Vehicle Financing Connections
- Warranties and Protection Products
- Local Car Rentals

### Naming decision

Use **Automotive**, not “Auto Sales.” This creates room for vehicle listings, dealer services, parts, repairs, roadside assistance, and local rentals.

Car rental inventory used primarily for travel may also appear inside Travel through cross-listing, but Automotive remains the system of record for vehicles and fleet partners.

## 6. Insurance

**Purpose:** Connect consumers and businesses with properly licensed insurance professionals, agencies, and approved products.

### Recommended categories

- Life Insurance
- Health Insurance
- Medicare Solutions
- Disability Insurance
- Long-Term Care Insurance
- Annuities
- Auto Insurance
- Homeowners and Renters Insurance
- Travel Insurance
- Business and Commercial Insurance
- Group and Employee Benefits
- Specialty Insurance
- Insurance Agents and Agencies
- Educational Resources and Needs Analysis

### Naming decision

Use **Insurance**, not “Insurance Sales.” This is clearer for consumers and does not frame every interaction as an immediate transaction.

### Launch boundary

AsBeez should begin as a technology, advertising, education, appointment, and consent-based lead platform. Compensation based on premium, policy issuance, or producer commission requires jurisdiction-specific licensing, appointment, disclosure, and legal approval.

## 7. Travel

**Purpose:** Search, compare, reserve, and manage travel inventory and travel-related services.

### Recommended categories

- Hotels and Resorts
- Vacation Rentals
- Flights
- Car Rentals
- Cruises
- Tours and Activities
- Travel Packages
- Airport Transfers
- Rail and Bus Travel
- Travel Agents and Advisors
- Corporate Travel
- Group Travel
- Travel Insurance
- Visa and Travel Document Assistance

### Naming decision

Use one **Travel** market rather than separate top-level markets for hotels, flights, and car rentals. Customers naturally plan these together, and a unified market supports packages, itinerary management, cross-selling, and a single travel search experience.

Each travel submarket still needs its own fee rules, supplier contracts, cancellation policies, and transaction workflow.

## Markets not recommended as separate top-level segments at launch

| Candidate | Decision | Placement |
|---|---|---|
| Jobs and Careers | Defer | Job board or talent feature inside Professional Services |
| Education | Do not separate initially | Courses under Digital Products; tutoring/coaching under Professional Services |
| Events and Experiences | Do not separate initially | Local services or Travel activities |
| Food and Restaurants | Defer as a dedicated market | Packaged goods under Physical Products; catering under Services |
| Healthcare | Defer due to regulatory complexity | Wellness products under Physical Products; permitted providers under Professional Services |
| Financial Services | Defer due to licensing and risk | Insurance remains separate; other financial products require a future study |
| B2B Wholesale | Treat as a channel, not a market | Wholesale categories inside Physical and Digital Products |
| Classifieds | Do not use as a market | A listing format spanning Real Estate, Automotive, and selected Products |
| Rentals | Do not use as a top-level market | Place by asset type: property in Real Estate, vehicles in Automotive/Travel, equipment in Products or Services |

## Final customer-facing navigation

Use short, familiar labels:

1. **Shop** — Physical Products
2. **Digital** — Digital Products
3. **Services** — Professional Services
4. **Real Estate** — Property and related professionals
5. **Automotive** — Vehicles, dealers, parts, repairs, and rentals
6. **Insurance** — Insurance professionals and solutions
7. **Travel** — Stays, flights, rentals, cruises, and experiences

The internal canonical names can remain `physical_products`, `digital_products`, `professional_services`, `real_estate`, `automotive`, `insurance`, and `travel`.

## Database hierarchy

Use a stable four-level taxonomy:

`Market → Category → Subcategory → Listing/Offering Type`

Example:

`Travel → Hotels and Resorts → Extended-Stay Hotels → Room Booking`

Every offering should have one primary market and may have approved secondary discovery placements. Cross-listing must not duplicate inventory, reviews, bookings, fees, or analytics.

## Partner-type mapping

| Market | Primary partner types |
|---|---|
| Physical Products | Vendor, merchant, manufacturer, wholesaler, artisan |
| Digital Products | Creator, publisher, software vendor, educator |
| Professional Services | Freelancer, contractor, professional, agency, local business |
| Real Estate | Agent, broker, property owner, manager, developer, approved service provider |
| Automotive | Dealer, private seller, fleet operator, repair shop, parts vendor |
| Insurance | Licensed producer, agency, carrier, IMO/FMO, approved educator |
| Travel | Hotel, host, airline/aggregator, rental supplier, tour operator, travel agency |

## Final platform rule

A new top-level market should be approved only when it meets at least four of these five tests:

1. It serves a distinct buyer intent.
2. It requires a distinct partner onboarding process.
3. It uses a materially different transaction or booking workflow.
4. It requires a different fee or commission structure.
5. It has meaningful regulatory or compliance requirements.

Under this rule, Automotive qualifies as the seventh market. Education, events, food, jobs, wholesale, and classifieds do not currently qualify because they can be handled cleanly within the seven-market structure.

## Final decision

The finalized AsBeez market segmentation is:

> **Shop | Digital | Services | Real Estate | Automotive | Insurance | Travel**

This structure is broad enough to support AsBeez's long-term marketplace vision, but focused enough to keep navigation, onboarding, commissions, reporting, and development manageable.
