# Member Portal

## Purpose

The member portal supports membership profile, country status/migration request, orders, referrals, loyalty activity, RP/ABC/AHC, wallet views, eligible redemptions/payouts, statements, support, privacy, and agreements.

## Required Experiences

Show member status, assigned country and review state, qualifying activity, reward balances/types, eligibility, expiry, reversals, holds, wallet monetary balances by currency, payment/payout status, terms version, disclosures, and support/escalation paths.

## Financial and Legal Clarity

RP/ABC/AHC are visibly separate from money and must not be presented as income, ownership, investment, currency, or guaranteed return. Country assignment is platform state subject to evidence and review, not proof of residence. Rewards, wallet, payout, and referral actions disclose restrictions and return authoritative pending/failed/unknown states.

## Controls

Use member authentication, resource scope, privacy masking, MFA/step-up for sensitive actions, idempotent commands, confirmation, accessible content, localization, audit, appeals, and legal/consumer disclosures.
