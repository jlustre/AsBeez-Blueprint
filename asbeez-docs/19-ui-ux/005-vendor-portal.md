# Vendor Portal

## Purpose

The vendor portal supports onboarding, verification, storefront, products/services, pricing, promotions, orders, fulfillment, customer support, earnings, fees, tax, reserves, statements, settlement, and payouts.

## Required Experiences

Show vendor agreement/status, KYB/compliance tasks, product approval, order and fulfillment state, gross/net amounts, fees, tax, reserves/holds, available/pending/settled earnings, payout eligibility/status, reconciliation exceptions, and support contacts.

## Financial Clarity

Vendor entitlement, customer payment, platform revenue, fees, tax, reserves, settlement, and payout are separate. Projections identify freshness/certification; provider status is not assumed final. The portal cannot edit balances, mark settlement/payout complete, release reserves, or change tax/accounting treatment directly.

## Controls

Enforce organization/entity/country/role scope, agreement acceptance, MFA, approval thresholds, privacy, accessible workflows, idempotency, audit, dispute/refund communication, and legal/prohibited-product controls.
