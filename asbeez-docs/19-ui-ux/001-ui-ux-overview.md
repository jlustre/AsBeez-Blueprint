# UI/UX Overview

## Purpose

AsBeez UI/UX translates marketplace, membership, vendor, partner, financial, administration, support, and platform capabilities into understandable, accessible, trustworthy experiences.

## Experience Principles

- design around user goals and role permissions;
- make commerce, money, rewards, tax, fees, refunds, payouts, and risk states understandable;
- distinguish actual, estimated, pending, provider-reported, failed, reversed, held, exception, and unknown states;
- minimize and protect personal/financial data;
- support keyboard, screen reader, contrast, localization, responsive layouts, and assistive technology; and
- provide confirmation, idempotency/retry safety, undo/compensation guidance, support, and audit-friendly history.

## Financial Trust

UI may display projections and recommendations but cannot make them authoritative. Monetary actions use authorized APIs, show calculated values before confirmation, require appropriate step-up/approval, and present the resulting operation status. RP/ABC/AHC remain visibly separate from monetary wallet balances.
