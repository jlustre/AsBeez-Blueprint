# Customer Website

## Purpose

The customer website supports discovery, product/service evaluation, vendor information, cart/order, payment, delivery, support, reviews, refunds, and privacy/terms journeys.

## Required Experiences

Show price, currency, taxes/fees where required, availability, vendor identity, terms, privacy, refund/return conditions, delivery/digital access, consent, accessibility, and support. Checkout confirms server-calculated order/payment/tax values and prevents duplicate submission.

## Trust and State

Clearly distinguish order created, payment pending/authorized/captured/failed/unknown, fulfillment pending/completed, refund requested/processing/completed/failed, dispute, and cancellation. Never show a successful purchase, refund, or delivery solely from a client response or provider callback.

## Privacy and Safety

Collect only necessary data, mask payment/identity values, provide consent and policy links, scope account/order access, protect children/vulnerable users where applicable, and offer accessible error/support paths.
