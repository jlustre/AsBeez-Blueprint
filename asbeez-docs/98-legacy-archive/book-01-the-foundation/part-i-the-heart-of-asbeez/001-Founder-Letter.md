# Founder's Letter

Welcome to AsBeez.

If you are reading this, it means our paths have crossed for a reason.

Whether you joined as a customer, a member, a vendor, an employee, an investor, or simply someone curious about what we are building, I want to personally welcome you to our journey.

AsBeez did not begin with software.

It did not begin with an online marketplace.

It began with a simple question.

**Why does loyalty end at checkout?**

For years I watched online shopping become part of everyday life.

People purchased books.

Electronics.

Software.

Groceries.

Household products.

Almost everything imaginable.

The convenience was incredible.

Companies like Amazon transformed commerce forever.

But I couldn't stop asking myself one question.

"What happens after the purchase?"

Customers remained loyal.

Vendors continued paying to acquire those same customers again and again.

Communities formed around products.

Yet the purchase itself rarely created lasting opportunity.

I believed technology could do more.

I believed shopping could become something greater than a transaction.

That belief became AsBeez.

The name AsBeez comes from one of nature's greatest examples of teamwork—the hive.

A bee never builds a hive alone.

Every bee contributes.

Every bee has purpose.

Every bee benefits when the hive becomes stronger.

That is the future I hope AsBeez represents.

A place where commerce strengthens communities.

Where technology creates opportunity.

Where loyalty is valued.

Where success is shared responsibly.

Throughout my career in software development, I have learned that technology is only valuable when it improves people's lives.

That principle guides every decision we make.

AsBeez will continuously evolve.

Our technology will improve.

Our marketplace will expand.

Our products will change.

But I hope one thing never changes.

Our commitment to people.

If you become an employee, remember that every line of code affects real families.

If you become a vendor, know that we are committed to helping your business grow.

If you become a member, know that your participation matters.

If you become an investor, know that we are building for generations—not for quick wins.

My hope is that one day AsBeez will be remembered not only as a successful company, but as a company that treated people with honesty, transparency, innovation, and respect.

Thank you for becoming part of this journey.

Together, we will build something greater than any one person could build alone.

Together,

we build the Hive.

---

**Joey Lustre**

Founder & Chief Architect

AsBeez
