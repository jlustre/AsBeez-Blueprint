# AI Services Overview

## Purpose

AI Services provide shared platform capabilities for assistants, agents, automation, workflows, search, recommendations, fraud/risk analysis, reporting insights, provider access, prompts, and retrieval-augmented generation.

## Platform Boundary

AI Services provide inference, retrieval, orchestration, evaluation, policy enforcement, and observability. Product and domain contexts own business meaning, authorization, source data, workflow decisions, and financial effects. AI Services do not become a source of truth.

## Common Rules

Use explicit tenant/user/entity/country scope, least privilege, data minimization, model/prompt/provider version, source lineage, confidence/uncertainty, audit correlation, human review, rate/cost limits, privacy, retention, and incident controls. AI may assist, explain, forecast, detect, prioritize, and recommend; financial commands re-enter normal authorized APIs.
