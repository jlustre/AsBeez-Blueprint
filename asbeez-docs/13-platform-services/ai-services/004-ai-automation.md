# AI Automation

## Purpose

AI automation applies models or rules to classify, route, summarize, prioritize, monitor, retrieve evidence, and recommend actions across platform workflows.

## Automation Tiers

Read-only observation; recommendation; approval-gated workflow initiation; and narrowly scoped safe controls such as queueing, rate limiting, retry backoff, or isolation. Monetary posting, payout, release, certification, close, and policy changes remain domain-authorized operations.

## Requirements

Define trigger, preconditions, scope, owner, model/rule version, idempotency, timeout, retry, compensation, stop condition, human escalation, monitoring, rollback, cost limit, and evidence. Automation preserves pending/unknown/exception states and never assumes provider success.
