# AI Provider Management

## Purpose

AI provider management selects, integrates, routes, evaluates, monitors, and retires model providers while protecting data, reliability, cost, portability, and decision quality.

## Selection Criteria

Capability, accuracy, calibration, safety, fairness, latency, availability, capacity, cost, rate limits, geography, residency, retention/training terms, subprocessors, security, audit, contract/SLA, incident response, exportability, and fallback.

## Routing and Fallback

Routing is policy/version scoped by use case, data class, country/entity, sensitivity, cost, and risk. Fallback preserves prompt/schema/version, confidence, source lineage, user disclosure, idempotency, and audit. Provider failure or disagreement becomes uncertainty/review, not silent substitution for a financial decision.

## Vendor Governance

Maintain provider inventory, contract/data terms, risk assessment, evaluation results, model versions, monitoring, incident history, renewal/exit plan, and portability tests. Providers cannot directly access or mutate authoritative AsBeez financial records.
