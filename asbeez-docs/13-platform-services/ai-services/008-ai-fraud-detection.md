# AI Fraud Detection

## Purpose

AI Fraud Detection provides shared signals, anomaly detection, graph analysis, risk-score suggestions, case prioritization, evidence retrieval, and loss/exposure forecasting for payment, wallet, payout, refund, chargeback, reward, vendor, partner, and account-risk workflows.

## Output

Return signal type, affected scope, related entities/transactions, feature/data window, candidate score/range, reason, confidence, model/rule/version, fairness/drift indicators, limitations, and recommended human review path.

## Prohibited Decisions

AI must not independently label fraud, deny lawful rights, set final thresholds, freeze/release funds, deny payout/refund, alter accounting, close cases, fabricate evidence, or expose sensitive data. Deterministic controls and authorized risk/compliance personnel decide.
