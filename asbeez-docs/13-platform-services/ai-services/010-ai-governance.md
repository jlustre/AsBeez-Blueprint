# AI Governance

## Purpose

AI governance controls the lifecycle, risk, accountability, transparency, evaluation, deployment, monitoring, incident response, and retirement of platform AI services.

## Required Record

Use case, owner, business purpose, risk tier, affected users/data, jurisdiction, model/provider/prompt/rule version, training/evaluation scope, limitations, decision boundary, tools, human owner, approval, deployment date, monitoring, security/privacy review, incidents, and retirement plan.

## Lifecycle Controls

Assess, approve, test, deploy gradually, monitor accuracy/calibration/fairness/drift/robustness/privacy/cost, review overrides and appeals, investigate incidents, revalidate material changes, and retire with data/contract/retention review.

## Non-Negotiable Boundary

AI may explain, forecast, detect, prioritize, retrieve, classify, summarize, and recommend. It may not directly post, approve, pay, alter balances, release/freeze funds, certify, close, make final legal/compliance decisions, or bypass controls.
