# AI Assistants

## Purpose

AI assistants provide conversational, role-aware help for customers, members, vendors, partners, operators, support, finance, compliance, and administrators.

## Capabilities

Answer questions from authorized sources, explain status, summarize records, retrieve policy/evidence, draft content, translate, classify requests, recommend next steps, and prepare workflow requests without executing unauthorized effects.

## Interaction Rules

Show source, freshness, scope, confidence, assumptions, and whether an answer is fact, estimate, inference, or recommendation. Ask clarification for ambiguity and refuse out-of-scope or sensitive requests. Actions become separately authorized commands with current validation, idempotency, approval, and audit.

## Prohibited Actions

Assistants cannot impersonate users, expose another party's data, write authoritative records, approve/pay/release funds, alter financial history, certify/close, make final legal/compliance decisions, or bypass controls.
