# AI Reporting Insights

## Purpose

AI Reporting Insights summarizes trends, explains metrics, detects anomalies, drafts narratives, assists forecasting, and supports decisions across business, financial, operational, risk, compliance, and platform domains.

## Output Contract

Identify source datasets/snapshots, metric definition, query/model/prompt version, period, entity/country/currency/FX/tax basis, filters, freshness, certification, assumptions, uncertainty, exclusions, and citations. Distinguish actual, posted, projected, forecast, provider-evidence, and estimate.

## Controls

AI cannot invent figures, alter source data, certify reports, conceal exceptions, present estimates as posted actuals, or expose unauthorized data. Generated narratives are reviewed before management, customer, regulatory, or financial use.
