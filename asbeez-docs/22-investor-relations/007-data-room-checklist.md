# Data Room Checklist

## Purpose

The data room provides controlled, current, traceable diligence materials for authorized investors, advisors, lenders, partners, auditors, and legal counsel.

## Corporate and Legal

Entity/ownership/governance; formation and registrations; material contracts; vendor/member/partner agreements; IP; privacy/terms; licenses; country/legal analyses; litigation/claims; insurance; compliance policies; risk register; and approvals.

## Business and Product

Product/architecture; roadmap; marketplace/vendor/customer/member metrics; acquisition/cohort definitions; operations/support; security/privacy; incidents; provider contracts; and product acceptance evidence.

## Financial and Tax

Financial statements/reports; chart/ledger policy; bank/cash; budgets/forecasts; financial model; revenue/expense recognition; tax registrations/filings; debt/commitments; reserves/liabilities; payment/payout/refund/dispute; reconciliation/close; and audit materials.

## Data Controls

Use access roles, confidentiality agreements, data minimization, redaction, watermarking, version/date/source, country/residency restrictions, download controls, retention/legal holds, request logs, Q&A ownership, and correction/withdrawal process. Do not place secrets, unnecessary personal data, or unapproved sensitive financial evidence in the room.

## Disclosure Gate

Legal, finance, tax, compliance, security, and business owners approve material materials. Actuals, estimates, forecasts, GMV, revenue, cash, liabilities, rewards, reserves, and projections are labeled distinctly; no item implies guaranteed income, investment return, ownership, or valuation.
