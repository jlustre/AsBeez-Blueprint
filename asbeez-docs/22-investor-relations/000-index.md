# Investor Relations

## Purpose

This domain defines investor communication, thesis, pitch structure, financial model, use of funds, investor FAQ, and data-room readiness for AsBeez.

## Authority

Investor materials summarize approved business and financial information; they do not replace audited/certified reports, legal advice, accounting policy, contracts, or regulatory filings. Actuals, estimates, forecasts, scenarios, GMV, revenue, cash, liabilities, rewards, reserves, and profit must be clearly distinguished.

## Disclosure Rules

Do not promise income, investment return, valuation, appreciation, guaranteed growth, guaranteed rewards, ownership, payout, or financial outcome. Claims require source, period, methodology, assumptions, exclusions, jurisdiction, review owner, and approval. Confidential information is shared only under appropriate access and confidentiality controls.
