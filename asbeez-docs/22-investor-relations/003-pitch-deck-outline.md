# Pitch Deck Outline

## Purpose

The pitch deck presents the approved AsBeez problem, commerce-first solution, market, product, traction, business model, strategy, team, risks, and capital plan without unsupported promises.

## Suggested Sequence

1. Mission and commerce problem.
2. Customer/vendor pain and market context.
3. AsBeez marketplace and community-commerce solution.
4. Product experience and participant roles.
5. Vendor/customer/member value and operating model.
6. Market and country expansion assumptions.
7. Business model and revenue streams with definitions.
8. Traction and metrics with period/source/methodology.
9. Financial model: actuals, forecast, scenarios, risks, and cash.
10. Technology, compliance, security, operations, and scalability.
11. Team, governance, use of funds, milestones, and risks.
12. Clear disclosure, terms, contacts, and diligence path.

## Presentation Controls

Label GMV, revenue, profit, cash, forecast, estimate, user/member/vendor counts, and cohort metrics. Disclose exclusions, one-time items, currency/FX, period, source, methodology, material risks, reward restrictions, and unresolved legal/classification matters. Never use testimonials or charts to imply guaranteed income or returns.
