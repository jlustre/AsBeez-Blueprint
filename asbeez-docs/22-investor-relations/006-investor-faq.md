# Investor FAQ

## What is AsBeez?

AsBeez is a community-powered digital commerce ecosystem combining marketplace commerce, vendor tools, customer value, voluntary loyalty participation, and platform technology.

## How does it make money?

Potential streams include marketplace/platform fees, vendor services, advertising, subscriptions, premium tools, and approved enterprise services. Actual recognition depends on contracts, performance, accounting policy, tax, and reconciliation.

## Are GMV and revenue the same?

No. GMV is gross transaction value; revenue is recognized under applicable policy. Payments, taxes, vendor/partner obligations, rewards, reserves, and cash are separately modeled.

## Are RP, ABC, or AHC investments or money?

They are platform-defined program units and are not presented as money, currency, equity, ownership, securities, investments, or guaranteed returns unless jurisdiction-specific legal analysis expressly permits that treatment.

## Is income or return guaranteed?

No. AsBeez must not promise income, profit, payout, appreciation, valuation, rewards, placement, or withdrawal. Results and availability depend on activity, rules, country, refunds, fraud controls, provider capability, and approved programs.

## What evidence supports claims?

Use approved reports, source data, periods, methodology, assumptions, exclusions, financial policy, risk disclosures, and legal/finance approval. Forecasts and scenarios are labeled and are not guarantees.

## What are major risks?

Marketplace liquidity, acquisition cost, vendor/customer retention, provider dependence, fraud, refunds/chargebacks, tax/compliance, rewards classification/liability, privacy/security, operations, liquidity, and execution.
