# Use of Funds

## Purpose

Use-of-funds materials connect approved capital deployment to measurable milestones, operating capacity, risk controls, and runway without promising an investor outcome.

## Potential Categories

Product and engineering; marketplace/vendor/customer acquisition; content and support; infrastructure/security/privacy; legal/tax/compliance; finance/treasury/audit; operations; data/analytics; working capital/reserves; and contingency. Actual allocation depends on approved budget, entity, instrument, restrictions, and law.

## Allocation Framework

For each category document amount/range, timing, owner, purpose, milestone, dependency, expected operating metric, risk, approval, accounting treatment, and reporting cadence. Restricted funds, reserves, customer/vendor funds, tax, and liabilities are not available capital.

## Governance

Board/management and applicable financing documents govern authorization. Track budget versus actual, commitments, variance, liquidity, runway, country/entity, procurement, conflicts, related parties, controls, and reporting. Material changes require approval and disclosure where required.
