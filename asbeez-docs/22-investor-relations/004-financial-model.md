# Financial Model

## Purpose

The financial model describes historical actuals, operating assumptions, forecasts, scenarios, cash needs, unit economics, and funding milestones for AsBeez.

## Required Model Layers

- operating drivers: vendors, customers, members, orders, conversion, repeat purchase, channels, countries, and products;
- revenue: marketplace/platform fees, vendor services, advertising, subscriptions, premium services, and other approved streams;
- direct costs: payment/provider, fulfillment/service, refunds/chargebacks, rewards/commissions, tax, support, infrastructure, and operations;
- balance/liquidity: receivables, payables, vendor/partner liabilities, reserves, wallet liabilities, cash, settlement timing, payout obligations, and FX; and
- scenarios: base, downside, upside, provider/country delay, liquidity stress, fraud/loss, and delayed revenue recognition.

## Model Rules

Separate GMV from recognized revenue, customer collections from cash available, vendor/partner entitlement from AsBeez revenue, tax from revenue, reserves from free cash, reward units from money, and forecast from actual. Use explicit period, entity/country, currency/FX, accounting policy, source, assumptions, version, and owner.

## Controls

Reconcile actuals to ledger/reporting, preserve formulas and source data, document methodology/exclusions, review sensitivities, test circularity/errors, obtain finance/legal review, and label estimates. The model is not a guarantee, valuation, or offer of securities/investment return.
